Bolivia - Cup Tournaments

Copa Simón Bolívar

Until 1976 this tournament was played for the national championship and qualification for the Copa Libertadores; since 1989 the winners are promoted to the first division. It was replaced by the Copa Bolivia after the 2010 edition.
