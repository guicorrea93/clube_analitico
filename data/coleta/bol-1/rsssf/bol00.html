Second Level (Copa Simon Bolivar) 2000

Cup winners promoted replacing team who finish 12th in league, runner-up 
play team who finish 11th in league in a relegation/promotion play-off. In 
first three rounds winners plus losers with "best" record qualify. 

Bolivia is politically divided into 9 departments or counties. Each one 
enters two teams into the cup. A list of them with their capitals follows:
