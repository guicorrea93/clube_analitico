The '''1952 [[Liga de Fútbol Profesional Boliviano|Bolivian Primera División]]''', the first division of Bolivian [[football (soccer)]], was played by 8 teams, all hailing from [[La Paz]] and played their matches at the [[Hernando Siles Stadium]]. The champion was [[The Strongest]].
