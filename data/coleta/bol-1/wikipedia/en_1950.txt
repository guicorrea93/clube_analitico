The '''1950 [[Liga de Fútbol Profesional Boliviano|Bolivian Primera División]]''', the first division of Bolivian [[football (soccer)]], was played by 9 teams. The champion was [[Club Bolívar|Bolívar]]. It was the first season after the La Paz Football Association turned its first division professional.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://historiadelfutbolboliviano.com/2020/05/07/las-tablas-de-los-campeonatos-nacionales-1950-1976/|title= Historia del Futbol Boliviano |work=Carlos D. Mesa}}</ref>
All 9 teams were from [[La Paz]] and played their matches on the [[Hernando Siles Stadium]]
