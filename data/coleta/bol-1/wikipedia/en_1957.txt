The '''1957 [[Liga de Fútbol Profesional Boliviano|Bolivian Primera División]]''', the first division of Bolivian [[football (soccer)]], was played in two tournaments, The Torneo Integrado and Torneo Nacional Mixto. The champions were [[Club Always Ready|Always Ready]] and [[Club Jorge Wilstermann|Jorge Wilstermann]] respectively.
