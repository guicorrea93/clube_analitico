The '''1951 [[Liga de Fútbol Profesional Boliviano|Bolivian Primera División]]''', the first division of Bolivian [[football (soccer)]], was played by 8 teams. The champion was [[Club Always Ready|Always Ready]].

A total of 8 teams played, 1 down from 1950 as Northern was relegated and none was promoted. All teams hailed from [[La Paz]] and played at the [[Hernando Siles stadium]]
