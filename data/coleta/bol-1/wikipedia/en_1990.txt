The '''1990 [[Liga de Fútbol Profesional Boliviano|Bolivian Primera División]]''', the first division of Bolivian [[football (soccer)]], was played by 13 teams. The champion was [[Oriente Petrolero]].<ref>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablesb/bol90.html Bolivia 1990]</ref>
