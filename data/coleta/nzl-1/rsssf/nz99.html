A national 10-team will be played in 2000, with 7 teams from the NISL and
3 from the SISL (this distribution was decided just after the season had
finished!).  The 2000 season will start Mar 25.  The top-4 clubs after 18
rounds will play off for the title; the bottom club will play off against
the winners of the Federation/Regional Championship to remain in the top
division.   Bonus points will be awarded for scoring three or more goals
in one match *and* for winning by three or more goals.
