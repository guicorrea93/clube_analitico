Winfield Challenge Shield 1988

In recognition of the Auckland Football Association's centenary
celebrations, the Winfield company donated the Winfield Shield in 1987
for inter-association competition.
Auckland, as inaugural holder, accepted five challenges during the 1988
season, successfully repulsing four before bowing to Otago.
