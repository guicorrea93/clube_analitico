New Zealand - List of Chatham Cup Finals

The Chatham Cup was presented to the NZFA by the crew of HMS Chatham on December 14 1922 for the purpose of an New Zealand FA Cup style competition.

It had a predecessor in the form of the "Brown Shield" (The Association Football Challenge Trophy) which was contested between 1892 and 1925.

Notes

Ponsonby are now known as Mt Albert-Ponsonby after merging with Mt Albert in 1970.
Tramways merged with Manurewa and were called Tramurewa for a while before 
becoming Manurewa.
Hospital changed their name to Western Suburbs, Porirua, Porirua-Viard United 
and then back to Western Suburbs, by which they are now known.
Tech Old Boys in Dunedin are now known as Dunedin Technical.
Tech Old Boys of Christchurch are now known as Christchurch Technical.
Christchurch City merged into Christchurch United in 1972.
Wellington City merged with Wellington Diamond United in 1985 to become 
Wellington United.

1986/87 NZFA Challenge Trophy

Awarded to North Shore United as holders of the Chatham Cup at the end of the 
1986 season, the NZFA Challenge Cup operated (as the name suggests) on a 
challenge basis. 

The holder's National League home matches counted towards this competition.
Prize money was awarded to the winning team of each match in the series.

The NZFA Challenge Cup lasted only one season.
