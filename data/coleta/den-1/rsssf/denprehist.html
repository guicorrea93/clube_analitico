Denmark - København A-Raeken and National Playoffs 1889-1927

Note that until 1911/12 there was no national championship playoff and the København champions could be considered (unofficially) as Danish champions. Until 1903 the A-Raeken was organised by the DBU (the KBU were founded in 1903 only) and so in principle 'national'.

See also the list of Provincial Champions 1902-1927 and the list of final tables 1927-1998.
