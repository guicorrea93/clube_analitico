The Danish Domestic Cup is open for all clubs registered under DBU. All clubs
may only enter with one team. Prior to 1st Round, the Cup is administered by
DBU's six (6) regional FAs. By June, 48 teams are ready for 1st Round of the
Compaq Cup, now administered by DBU itself.

Round Teams Entrants

 1st   64   48 sub-divisional teams
            16 teams from Division Three (2. division) 1997/98

 2nd   40   32 winners from 1st Round
             8 teams from Division Two (1. division), ranked 9-16 in 1997/98

 3rd   28   20 winners from 2nd Round
             2 teams from Division One (Superligaen), ranked 11-12 in 1997/98
             6 teams from Division Two (1. division), ranked 3-8 in 1997/98

 4th   20   14 winners from 3rd Round
             4 teams from Division One (Superligaen), ranked 7-10 in 1997/98
             2 teams from Division Two (1. division), ranked 1-2 in 1997/98

 5th   16   10 winners from 4th Round
             6 teams from Division One (Superligaen), ranked 1-6 in 1997/98

 6th    8   Quarterfinals, 8 winners from 5th Round

 7th    4   Semifinals, 4 winners from 6th Round (Quarterfinals)

            Semifinals are played home and away. The aggregate winner in each
            Semifinal is qualified for the Final. The aggregate winner is
            found by these rules:
            1. The team that has most points after the two Semifinals.
            2. If equal points the team with best goal difference.
            3. If equal goal difference the team with most away goals.
            4. If equal away goals, an extra time of 2 times 15 minutes is
               played. If scored, but still equal score after extra time, the
               away team in the 2nd Semifinal is declared winner, according
               to the away goals rule.
            5. If not scored in extra time, the winner is found through a
               penalty shoot-out.

 8th    2   The Compaq Cup Final, 2 winners from 7th Round (Semifinals)

            If no Cup winner is found after full time, an extra time of 2
            times 15 minutes is played. If still no Cup winner after extra
            time, the Cup winner is found through a penalty shoot-out.

Home advantage is granted to the team with the lowest divisional status. If
both teams play at the same level, the home team is the first drawn.
