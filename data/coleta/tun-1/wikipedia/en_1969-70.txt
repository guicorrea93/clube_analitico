The '''1969–70 Tunisian National Championship''' was the fifteenth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1969/1970 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1969-1970 |access-date=2026-10-14 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished 2 points ahead of [[Club Africain]] in the final standings. It was Espérance's third league title since independence.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-14 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref> The defending champions, [[Club Sportif Sfaxien]], finished fourth.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref>

The league was contested by 14 clubs, each playing 26 matches. [[CS Hammam-Lif]] and [[Association Sportive Ariana]] were relegated at the end of the campaign, while [[El Makarem de Mahdia]] and [[Football Club Djerissa]] were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
