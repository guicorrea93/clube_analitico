The '''2006–07 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' was the 52nd season of top-tier [[Association football|football]] in [[Tunisia]]. It saw [[Étoile du Sahel]] win the championship. At the other end of the table, [[Etoile Olympique de la Goulette et Kram]] and [[ES Hammam-Sousse]] were relegated to [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 2|Ligue Professionnelle 2]].
