The 1955–56 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|Tunisian National Championship]] was the 1st season of top-tier football in Tunisia. It saw [[CS Hammam-Lif]] crowned as champions while '''Patriote de Sousse''' was relegated to [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 2|Ligue 2]].

== Participating Clubs ==
*[[CS Hammam-Lif]] (CSHL)
*[[Étoile du Sahel]] (ESS)
*[[Club Africain]] (CA)
*[[Espérance de Tunis]] (EST)
*[[CA Bizertin]] (CAB)
*[[Union Sportive Maritime de Ferryville|USM Ferryville]] (USMF)
*[[Stade Tunisien]] (ST)
*[[Club Sportif Sfaxien|Club Tunisien]] (CT)<ref>futur Club Sportif Sfaxien</ref>
*[[Sfax Railways Sports]] (SRS)
*Olympique Tunisien (OT)<ref>futur Union Sportive Musulmane Olympique</ref>
*Patriote Football Club de Bizerte (PFCB)
*Patriote de Sousse (PS)
