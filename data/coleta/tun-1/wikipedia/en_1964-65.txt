The '''1964–65 Tunisian National Championship''' was the tenth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Stade Tunisien]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1964/1965 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1964-1965 |access-date=2026-10-12 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished one point ahead of the defending champions [[Club Africain]] in the final standings. It was Stade Tunisien's fourth league title since independence.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-12 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref>

The league was contested by 12 clubs, each playing 22 matches. [[Union Sportive Tunisienne]] finished bottom and was relegated, while [[Club Olympique des Transports]] was promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
