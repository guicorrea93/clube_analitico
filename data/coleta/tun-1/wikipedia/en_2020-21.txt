The '''2020–21 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' (''Tunisian Professional League'') season was the 66th season of top-tier football in [[Tunisia]]. [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis|Espérance de Tunis]] were the champions, winning the title for the fifth consecutive season and the 31st time in their history (a national record).

==Teams==
A total of 14 teams contested the league.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Tunisian Ligue 1 Final standings, Teams, statistics - Flashscore |url=https://www.flashscore.com/football/tunisia/ligue-professionnelle-1-2020-2021/standings/SxVSqc9k/standings/overall/ |access-date=10 October 2026 |website=www.flashscore.mobi |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Résultats et classement Tunisie Ligue 1 2020/2021 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/ligue-1/2020-2021 |access-date=2026-10-10 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref>
