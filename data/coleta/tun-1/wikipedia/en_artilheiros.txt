Following [[Tunisian independence]], the newspaper ''Le Petit Matin'' took on the task of compiling the top-scorer rankings for the [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]. In 1961, the newspaper ''Al Amal'' took over this role; subsequently, ''[[L'Action Tunisienne]]'' (in collaboration with the weekly ''Le Sport'') formalized the rankings and starting in 1967 introduced an associated award. As media coverage of Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1 matches expanded, these rankings gained greater prominence.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Tunisia – List of Topscorers|url=https://www.rsssf.org/tablest/tuntops.html|access-date=2020-06-15|website=[[RSSSF]]}}</ref>
