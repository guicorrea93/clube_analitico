The '''1962–63 Tunisian National Championship''' was the eighth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1962/1963 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1962-1963 |access-date=2026-10-11 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who went through the season unbeaten and finished ahead of the defending champions [[Stade Tunisien]] in the final standings. It was Étoile du Sahel's second league title, after 1957–58.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-11 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref>

The league was contested by 12 clubs, each playing 22 matches. [[Stade Gabésien]] finished bottom and was relegated, while [[CS Hammam-Lif]], who finished 11th, lost a play-off against [[Club Athlétique Bizertin|CA Bizertin]] and was relegated as well. [[Sfax Railways Sports]] and CA Bizertin were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
