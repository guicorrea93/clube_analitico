The '''1972–73 Tunisian National Championship''' was the eighteenth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Club Africain]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1972/1973 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1972-1973 |access-date=2026-10-14 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished 4 points ahead of [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel]] in the final standings. It was Club Africain's third league title since independence.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-14 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref> The defending champions, [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel|Étoile du Sahel]], finished second.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref>

The league was contested by 14 clubs, each playing 26 matches. [[Union Sportive Maghrébine]] and [[US Monastirienne|Union Sportive Monastir]] finished level on 39 points at the bottom of the table and were relegated at the end of the campaign, while [[Club Sportif Cheminots]] and [[Stade Sportif Sfaxien]] were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
