The 1956–57 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|Tunisian National Championship]] was the 2nd season of [[Association football|football]] in [[Tunisia]], and the first to start after the country's [[Tunisian independence|independence]] in March 1956.

==Participating clubs==
*[[Stade Tunisien]] (ST)
*[[Espérance de Tunis]] (EST)
*[[Étoile du Sahel]] (ESS)
*[[Club Africain]] (CA)
*[[Jeunesse Sportive Methouienne]] (JSM)
*[[Club Sportif de Hammam-Lif]] (CSHL)
*[[Stade Africain Menzel Bourguiba|Union Sportive de Ferryville]] (USF)<ref>futur Union sportive musulmane Menzel Bourguiba (USMMB)</ref>
*[[Club Athlétique Bizertin]] (CAB)
*Olympique Tunisien (OT)<ref>soon to be Union Sportive Musulmane Olympique</ref>
*[[CS Sfaxien|Club Tunisien]] (CT)<ref>futur [[Club Sportif Sfaxien]]</ref>
*[[Patriote Football Club de Bizerte]] (PFCB)
*[[Sfax Railways Sports]] (SRS)
