The '''1980–81 Tunisian National Championship''' was the twenty-sixth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Club Sportif Sfaxien]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1980/1981 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1980-1981 |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished two points ahead of the defending champions [[Club Africain]] in the final standings. It was Club Sportif Sfaxien's fourth league title since independence.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref>

The league was contested by 14 clubs, each playing 26 matches. [[Club Olympique des Transports]] and [[Stade Gabésien]] were relegated at the end of the campaign, while [[Association Sportive Mégrine]] and [[El Makarem de Mahdia]] were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
