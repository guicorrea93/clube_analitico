The '''1985–86 Tunisian National Championship''' was the thirty-first season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel]], who finished six points ahead of the defending champions [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]] in the final standings.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1985/1986 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1985-1986 |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> It was Étoile du Sahel's fifth league title since independence.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref>

The league was contested by 14 clubs, each playing 26 matches. [[STIA Sousse]] finished bottom and was the only club relegated, while [[Club Olympique des Transports]] was promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
