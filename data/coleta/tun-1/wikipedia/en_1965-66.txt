The '''1965–66 Tunisian National Championship''' was the eleventh season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1965/1966 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1965-1966 |access-date=2026-10-12 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who lost only one match and finished nine points ahead of [[Club Sportif Sfaxien]] in the final standings. It was Étoile du Sahel's third league title since independence.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-12 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref> The defending champions, [[Stade Tunisien]], finished fourth.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref>

The league was contested by 12 clubs, each playing 22 matches. [[Club Olympique des Transports]] and [[El Ahly Mateur|En Nadi Ahly Mateur]] were relegated at the end of the campaign, while [[Club Sportif Cheminots]] and [[US Monastirienne|Union Sportive Monastir]] were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />

== Top scorers ==

[[Mongi Dalhoum]] of [[Club Sportif Sfaxien|CS Sfaxien]] was the league's top scorer with 18 goals, after also finishing top in 1963–64.<ref name="RSSSF scorers">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Topscorers |url=https://www.rsssf.org/tablest/tuntops.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref>
