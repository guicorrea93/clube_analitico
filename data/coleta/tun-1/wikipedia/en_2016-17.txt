The '''2016–17 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' (''Tunisian Professional League'') season was the 62nd season of top-tier football in [[Tunisia]]. The competition started on 8 September 2016.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.kawarji.com/actu-31853-ligue_1_la_ftf_communique_la_date_du_coup_d_envoi_de_la_saison_2016_2017.html|title=Ligue 1 : La FTF communique la date du coup d'envoi de la saison 2016-2017}}</ref> The defending champions from the previous season were [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel|Étoile du Sahel]]. 

[[Esperance de Tunis]], the champions, drew the highest average home attendance of all clubs in the league with 11,000.

==Teams==
A total of 16 teams contested the league, including 13 sides from the [[2015–16 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|2015–16 season]] and three promoted from the [[2015–16 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 2|2015–16 Ligue 2]]. [[Avenir Sportif de Gabès|Avenir de Gabès]] was the first to obtain promotion, followed by [[Olympique Béja]] and finally [[Union Sportive de Tataouine|Union de Tataouine]]. The three teams replaced [[El Gawafel Sportives de Gafsa|El Gawafel de Gafsa]], [[Avenir Sportif de Kasserine|Avenir de Kasserine]] and [[Stade Tunisien]] who were relegated to [[2016–17 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 2|2016–17 Tunisian Ligue 2]]. <br>The teams were drawn in two groups of 8 each. At the end of the first part of the season, the first 3 qualified to the Championship Group, the 4th to 7th contested the Relegation Group and the 8th of each group were relegated to the [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 2|Ligue 2]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.kawarji.com/actu-32026-ligue_1_2016_2017_la_ftf_choisit_un_championnat_a_deux_poules_voici_leur_composition.html|title=Ligue 1 2016-2017 : La FTF choisit un championnat ? Deux poules, voici leur composition}}</ref> [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel|Étoile du Sahel]] were the defending champions from the [[2015–16 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|2015–16 season]].
