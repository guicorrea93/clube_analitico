The '''2021–22 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' (''Tunisian Professional League'') season was the 67th season of top-tier football in [[Tunisia]].

[[Espérance Sportive de Tunis|Espérance de Tunis]] were the defending champions and successfully defended their title, winning their record-extending 6th consecutive title and 32nd title overall.

The VAR was used in the [[#Playoff|playoff]].

==Teams==
A total of 16 teams contested the league.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Résultats et classement Tunisie Ligue 1 - Gr A 2021/2022 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/ligue-1/2021-2022 |access-date=2026-10-10 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=TUNISIA: Ligue Professionnelle 1 |url=https://www.flashscore.com/standings/CpF0dRJf/0UZQsVUO/ |access-date=2026-10-10 |website=www.flashscore.com |language=en}}</ref>

====Group B====
=====The Club Africain v Croissant Chebbien match case=====
On 18 April 2022, the match of the last round was held between [[Club Africain]] and [[Croissant Sportif Chebbien|Croissant Chebbien]] at [[Stade Olympique Hammadi Agrebi|Stade Hammadi Agrebi]], [[Tunis]] and ended with a 1–0 win for the hosts, a result that made [[Croissant Sportif Chebbien|Croissant Chebbien]] relegated to the [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 2|Ligue 2]], but [[Croissant Sportif Chebbien|Croissant Chebbien]] protested the presence of [[Club Africain]] president, Youssef Elmi, on the bench despite his suspension from punishment by the [[Tunisian Football Federation|Football Federation]], and demanded that they would be given a penal victory, and they decided to take the case to [[Court of Arbitration for Sport|CAS]].

On 29 September, the [[Court of Arbitration for Sport|CAS]] decided to reintegrate [[Croissant Sportif Chebbien|Croissant Chebbien]] in the [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|Ligue 1]], which forced the federation to add a 16th team for [[2022–23 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|2022–23]] season so that the number of teams would be even, and the identity of this team was determined by two playoff matches, the first between [[Club Sportif de Hammam-Lif|Club de Hammam-Lif]] and [[Espérance Sportive de Zarzis|Espérance de Zarzis]], and the second between the winner of the first match and [[ES Métlaoui|Étoile de Métlaoui]].

On 27 March 2023, the [[Court of Arbitration for Sport|CAS]] decided to give [[Croissant Sportif Chebbien|Croissant Chebbien]] a 2–0 penal victory. Since this decision affects the arrangement of the table in the first round and the added points for the coronation [[#Playoff|playoff]] and [[#Playout|playout]], and since this decision came late and after the start of the new season, the effect of this result in the second round was not calculated.
