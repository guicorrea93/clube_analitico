The '''1975–76 Tunisian National Championship''' was the twenty-first season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by the defending champions [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]], who finished level on 62 points with [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel]]<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1975/1976 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1975-1976 |access-date=2026-10-14 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> and won the title after a play-off between the two.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-14 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref> It was Espérance's fifth league title since independence and their second in a row.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref>

The league was contested by 14 clubs, each playing 26 matches. [[El Makarem de Mahdia]] and [[Club Sportif Cheminots]] were relegated at the end of the campaign. [[Stade Tunisien]], who finished 12th, won a play-off against [[Stade Africain de Menzel Bourguiba]] and stayed in the division. [[Association Sportive Mégrine]] and [[US Monastirienne|Union Sportive Monastir]] were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
