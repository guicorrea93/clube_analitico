The '''2014–15 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' (''Tunisian Professional League'') season was the 60th season of top-tier football in [[Tunisia]]. The competition began on 13 August 2014 and ended on 2 June 2015. The defending champions from the previous season were [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis|Espérance de Tunis]].

==Teams==
A total of 16 teams contested the league, including 13 sides from the [[2013–14 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|2013–14 season]] and three promoted from the [[2013–14 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 2]]. [[Association Sportive de Djerba|Association de Djerba]] was the first to obtain promotion, followed by [[Avenir Sportif de Gabès|Avenir de Gabès]] and finally [[Espérance Sportive de Zarzis|Espérance de Zarzis]] one game before the end of the promotion group league. The three teams made their comeback to the [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]] after finishing in the 3 first places of the six-team promotion group. On the other hand, [[Olympique Béja]], Palme de Tozeur and [[Grombalia Sports]] were the 3 last teams of the [[2013–14 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|2013–14 season]] and played in the [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 2]] during the [[2014–15 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 2|2014–15 season]]. [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis|Espérance de Tunis]] were the defending champions from the [[2013–14 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|2013–14 season]].

In the 2014-15 season, [[Club Africain]] drew an average home attendance of 12,478 in 15 home games. It was the highest average home attendance in the Tunisian top-flight football league, followed by [[ES Tunis]] with 7,365.
