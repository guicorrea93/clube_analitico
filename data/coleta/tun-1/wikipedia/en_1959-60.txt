The '''1959–60 Tunisian National Championship''' was the fifth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by the defending champions [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1959/1960 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1959-1960 |access-date=2026-10-11 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished ahead of [[Stade Tunisien]] in the final standings. It was Espérance's third league title overall and their second in a row.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref>

The league was contested by 13 clubs, each playing 24 matches. [[Sfax Railways Sports]] and [[Jeunesse Sportive Méthouienne]] were relegated at the end of the campaign, while [[El Makarem de Mahdia]] was promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />

== Top scorers ==
