The '''1982–83 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' (''Tunisian National Championship'')  season was the 28th season of top-tier [[football in Tunisia]]. The competition began on 26 September 1982, and ended on 29 May 1983. The defending champions from the [[2010–11 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|previous season]] are [[Espérance de Tunis]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://footballvintage.net/index.php/etranger/tunisie/1982-83/les-resultats |title=Tunisie 82/83 Les résultats |website=footballvintage.net |accessdate=10 July 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.rsssf.org/tablest/tun83.html |title=Tunisia 1982/83 |website=[[RSSSF]] |accessdate=10 July 2020}}</ref>
