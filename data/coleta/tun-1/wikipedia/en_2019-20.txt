The '''2019–20 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' (''Tunisian Professional League'') season was the 65th season of top-tier football in [[Tunisia]].

The season was suspended in March 2020, due to [[COVID-19 pandemic in Tunisia]], then resumed in July 2020.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.goal.com/en-tza/news/tunisian-2019-20-ligue-1-season-could-resume-in-august-ftf/uoxh5ecwdrka1lu0zthwrvous |title=Tunisian 2019-20 Ligue 1 season could resume in August – FTF |website=Goal.com |date=8 May 2020 }}</ref>

==Teams==
A total of 14 teams contested the league.
