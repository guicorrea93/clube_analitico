The '''1967–68 Tunisian National Championship''' was the thirteenth season{{Clarify|reason=counting from 1956–57, the first season after independence, this would be the 12th; is 1955–56 included?|date=October 2026}} of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Sfax Railways Sports]], who finished four points ahead of the defending champions [[Club Africain]] in the final standings.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1967/1968 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1967-1968 |access-date=2026-10-12 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> It was Sfax Railways Sports' first league title since independence.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-12 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref>

The league was contested by 12 clubs, each playing 22 matches. There were no relegations this season, and [[Club Sportif Cheminots]] and [[Stade Sportif Sfaxien]] were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
