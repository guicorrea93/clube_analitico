The '''1970–71 Tunisian National Championship''' was the sixteenth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Club Sportif Sfaxien]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1970/1971 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1970-1971 |access-date=2026-10-14 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished 5 points ahead of [[Club Africain]] in the final standings. It was Club Sportif Sfaxien's second league title since independence.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-14 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref> The defending champions, [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]], were dissolved and do not appear in the final table.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref>

The league was contested by 13 clubs, each playing 24 matches. [[Union Sportive Tunisienne]] and [[Football Club Djerissa]] were relegated at the end of the campaign, while [[Union Sportive Maghrébine]] and [[Stade Sportif Sfaxien]] were promoted for the following season. Étoile du Sahel had one point deducted for a forfeit against [[US Monastirienne|Union Sportive Monastir]].<ref name="RSSSF" />
