The '''2023–24 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' (''Tunisian Professional League'') season was the 69th season of top-tier football in [[Tunisia]].

[[Étoile Sportive du Sahel|Étoile du Sahel]] were the defending champions from the previous season. On 15 June 2024, [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis|Espérance de Tunis]] were officially confirmed as champions with one game to spare following their 2–0 win against [[US Monastir (football)|US Monastir]], securing a record-extending 33rd title.

==Teams==
