The '''2024–25 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' (''Tunisian Professional League'') season was the 70th season of top-tier football in [[Tunisia]].

[[Espérance Sportive de Tunis|Espérance de Tunis]] were the defending champions and successfully defended their title, winning their record-extending 34th title with one game left.

==Teams==
16 teams contested the league.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Résultats et classement Tunisie Ligue 1 2024/2025 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/ligue-1/2024-2025 |access-date=2026-10-10 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=TUNISIA: Ligue Professionnelle 1 |url=https://www.flashscore.com/standings/6wHAFgR7/CYDyIQ6O/ |access-date=2026-10-10 |website=www.flashscore.com |language=en}}</ref>
