The '''2007–08 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1''' was the 53rd season of top-tier [[Association football|football]] in [[Tunisia]]. It saw [[Club Africain]] crowned as champions. [[Stade Gabésien]] and [[Espérance Sportive de Zarzis]] were relegated to [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 2|Ligue Professionnelle 2]].

It was played in a traditional [[Sports league|league]] format, with 14 teams playing 26 rounds, two matches against every opposing team.

[[Club Sportif Sfaxien]] had two points deducted from their end of season total due to walking off field before completion of their round 15 game against [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]].<ref>{{cite web|title=CSS points deduction |url=https://www.rsssf.org/tablest/tun08.html |access-date=9 October 2010 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100330084014/http://www.rsssf.com/tablest/tun08.html |archive-date=30 March 2010 }}</ref>
