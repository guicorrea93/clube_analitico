The '''2011–12 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' (''Tunisian Professional League'')  season was the 57th season of top-tier [[football in Tunisia]]. The competition began on 4 November 2011, and ended on 30 September 2012. The defending champions from the [[2010–11 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|previous season]] are [[Espérance de Tunis]].

This is the first full season held after the [[Tunisian Revolution]].
