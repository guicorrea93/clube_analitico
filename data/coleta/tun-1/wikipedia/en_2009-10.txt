The '''2009–10 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]'''(''Tunisian Professional League'')  season was the 55th season of top-tier [[football in Tunisia]]. The competition began on 27 July 2009, and concluded on 15 May 2010. The defending champions from the [[2008–09 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|previous season]] are [[Espérance de Tunis]].
