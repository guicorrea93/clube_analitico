The '''2018–19 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' (''Tunisian Professional League'') season was the 64rd season of top-tier football in [[Tunisia]].<br />
The defending champions from the previous season are [[Espérance de Tunis]]. The competition began on 18 August.<ref>{{Cite web | url=https://www.kawarji.com/resultats/ligue1/2018-2019/1 | title=Résultats et classement Tunisie Ligue 1 2018/2019 }}</ref>

==Teams==
A total of 14 teams contested the league.
