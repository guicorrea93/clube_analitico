The '''1963–64 Tunisian National Championship''' was the ninth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Club Africain]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1963/1964 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1963-1964 |access-date=2026-10-11 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished ahead of [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]] in the final standings. It was Club Africain's first league title since independence.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-11 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref> The defending champions, [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel]], finished sixth.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref>

The league was contested by 12 clubs, each playing 22 matches. [[US Monastirienne|Union Sportive Monastir]] finished bottom and was relegated, while [[Stade Soussien]], who finished 11th, won a play-off against [[Stade Sportif Sfaxien]] and stayed in the division. [[CS Hammam-Lif]] was promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
