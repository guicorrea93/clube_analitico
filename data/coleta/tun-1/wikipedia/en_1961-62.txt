The '''1961–62 Tunisian National Championship''' was the seventh season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by the defending champions [[Stade Tunisien]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1961/1962 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1961-1962 |access-date=2026-10-11 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished ahead of [[Stade Soussien]] in the final standings. It was Stade Tunisien's third league title overall and their second in a row.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-11 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref>

The league was contested by 12 clubs, each playing 22 matches. [[Club Athlétique Bizertin|CA Bizertin]] and [[El Makarem de Mahdia]] were relegated at the end of the campaign, while [[En Nadi Ahly Mateur]] was promoted for the following season. [[Stade Gabésien]] and [[US Monastirienne|Union Sportive Monastir]], promoted the previous season, both stayed up. RSSSF also notes the re-establishment of [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel]] during this period.<ref name="RSSSF" />
