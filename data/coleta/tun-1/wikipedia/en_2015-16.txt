The '''2015–16 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' (''Tunisian Professional League'') season was the 61st season of top-tier football in [[Tunisia]]. The competition began on 12 September 2015 and ended on 12 June 2016.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ftf.org.tn/fr/calendrier-general-des-competitions-20152016/|title = Calendrier Général des Compétitions 2015/2016 - Fédération Tunisienne de Football}}</ref> The defending champions from the previous season were [[Club Africain]].

==Teams==
A total of 16 teams contested the league, including 13 sides from the [[2014–15 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|2014–15 season]] and three promoted from the [[2014–15 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 2|2014–15 Ligue 2]]. [[Union Sportive de Ben Guerdane|Union de Ben Guerdane]] was the first to obtain promotion, followed by [[Avenir Sportif de Kasserine|Avenir de Kasserine]] and finally [[Olympique Sidi Bouzid]]. The three teams replaced [[Union Sportive Monastirienne (football)|Union Monastirienne]], [[Avenir Sportif de Gabès|Avenir de Gabès]] and [[Association Sportive de Djerba|Association de Djerba]] who were relegated to [[2015–16 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 2|2015–16 Tunisian Ligue 2]]. [[Club Africain]] were the defending champions from the [[2014–15 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|2014–15 season]].
