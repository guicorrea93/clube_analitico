The '''1957–58 Tunisian National Championship''' was the third season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel]], who finished three points ahead of [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]] in the final standings. It was Étoile du Sahel's first league title since independence.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref> [[Stade Tunisien]], the previous season's champions, finished third.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref>

The league was contested by 15 clubs, each playing 28 matches. [[USM Menzel Bourguiba]], [[Patrie Football Club Bizertin]] and [[US Musulmane Olympique]] were relegated at the end of the campaign, while [[Olympique du Kef]] and [[Stade Gabésien]] were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
