The '''1996–97 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' season was the 42nd season of top-tier [[Association football|football]] in [[Tunisia]].

[[Étoile du Sahel]] finished the season as champions.<ref>{{Cite web |date=1999-08-02 |title=Tunisia 1996/97 |url=https://www.rsssf.org/tablest/tun97.html |access-date=2026-08-20 |website=[[RSSSF]]}}</ref>
