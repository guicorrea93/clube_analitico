The '''1983–84 Tunisian National Championship''' was the twenty-ninth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[CA Bizertin]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1983/1984 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1983-1984 |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished level on 62 points with [[Stade Tunisien]] and won the title on the goal difference of the first-leg ("aller") matches. It was CA Bizertin's first league title since independence.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref> The defending champions, [[Club Sportif Sfaxien]], finished eighth.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref>

The league was contested by 14 clubs, each playing 26 matches. [[Stade Gabésien]] finished bottom and was relegated. [[Stade Soussien]] and [[US Monastirienne|Union Sportive Monastir]] finished level on 45 points, and Stade Soussien was relegated on the goal difference of the first-leg matches (−9 against −4). [[Club Olympique des Transports]] and [[Avenir Sportif de Gabès]] were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
