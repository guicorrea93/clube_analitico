The '''1987–88 Tunisian National Championship''' was the thirty-third season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1987/1988 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1987-1988 |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished level on 81 points with [[Club Olympique des Transports]] and won the title on their head-to-head record against them. It was Espérance's eighth league title since independence.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref> The defending champions, [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel]], finished fifth.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref>

The league was contested by 14 clubs, each playing 26 matches. [[Olympique du Kef]] and [[Grombalia Sports]] finished at the bottom and were relegated. [[CS Hammam-Lif]], who finished 12th, lost a play-off against [[Sfax Railways Sports]] and were relegated as well. [[Avenir Sportif de La Marsa]], [[Océano Club de Kerkennah]] and Sfax Railways Sports were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
