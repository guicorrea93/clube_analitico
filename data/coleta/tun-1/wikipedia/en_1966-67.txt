The '''1966–67 Tunisian National Championship''' was the twelfth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Club Africain]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1966/1967 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1966-1967 |access-date=2026-10-12 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who lost only one match and finished eight points ahead of the defending champions [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel]] in the final standings. It was Club Africain's second league title since independence, after 1963–64.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-12 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref>

The league was contested by 12 clubs, each playing 22 matches. [[Stade Soussien]] and [[Club Sportif Cheminots]] were relegated at the end of the campaign, while [[Club Olympique des Transports]] and [[Union Sportive Maghrébine]] were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
