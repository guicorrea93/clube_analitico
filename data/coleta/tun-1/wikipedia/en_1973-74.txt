The '''1973–74 Tunisian National Championship''' was the nineteenth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by the defending champions [[Club Africain]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1973/1974 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1973-1974 |access-date=2026-10-14 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished three points ahead of [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]] in the final standings. It was Club Africain's fourth league title since independence and their second in a row.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-14 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref>

The league was contested by 14 clubs, each playing 26 matches. [[JS Kairouan]] and [[Stade Sportif Sfaxien]] were relegated at the end of the campaign, while [[Stade Africain de Menzel Bourguiba]] and [[Patriote de Sousse]] were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
