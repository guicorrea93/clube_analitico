The '''1984–85 Tunisian National Championship''' was the thirtieth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]], who finished one point ahead of [[Club Africain]] in the final standings.<ref>{{Cite web |title=الإنجازات {{!}} Espérance sportive de tunis |url=https://www.est.org.tn/ar/alanjazat |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=www.est.org.tn}}</ref> It was Espérance's seventh league title since independence.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref> The defending champions, [[CA Bizertin]], finished ninth.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref>

The league was contested by 14 clubs, each playing 26 matches. [[Stade Sportif Sfaxien]] and [[Avenir Sportif de Gabès]] were relegated at the end of the campaign, while [[Olympique de Béja]] and [[STIA Sousse]] were promoted for the following season. RSSSF notes that [[Océano Club de Kerkennah]] played in the top division the following season in place of [[Club Olympique des Transports]].<ref name="RSSSF" />
