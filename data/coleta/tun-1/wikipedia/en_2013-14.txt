The '''2013–14 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' (Tunisian Professional League) season was the 59th season of top-tier football in Tunisia. The competition began on 15 September 2013 and ended on May 13, 2014. The defending champions from the previous season were [[Club Sportif Sfaxien]]. In the 2013-14 league season, Club Africain drew the highest average home attendance with 9,000, followed by ES Tunis with 8,273.<ref>{{cite web| url = https://www.worldfootball.net/competition/co1158/| title = Ligue 1 2021/2022}} </ref>
