The '''1968–69 Tunisian National Championship''' was the fourteenth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Club Sportif Sfaxien]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1968/1969 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1968-1969 |access-date=2026-10-14 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished 5 points ahead of [[Club Africain]] in the final standings. It was Club Sportif Sfaxien's first league title since independence. The defending champions, [[Sfax Railways Sports]], finished fourth.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-14 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref>

The league was expanded to 14 clubs, each playing 26 matches, after there were no relegations the previous season. [[Stade Sportif Sfaxien]] and [[Union Sportive Maghrébine]] were relegated at the end of the campaign, while [[Association Sportive Ariana]] and [[Union Sportive Tunisienne]] were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
