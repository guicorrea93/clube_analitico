The '''1960–61 Tunisian National Championship''' was the sixth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Stade Tunisien]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1960/1961 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1960-1961 |access-date=2026-10-11 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished ahead of the defending champions [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]] in the final standings. It was Stade Tunisien's second league title, after 1956–57.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-11 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref>

The league was contested by 11 clubs, each playing 20 matches. [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel]] was dissolved and does not appear in the final table. [[El Makarem de Mahdia]], promoted the previous season, took part, and [[Stade Populaire]] finished last and was relegated. [[Stade Gabésien]] and [[US Monastirienne|Union Sportive Monastir]] were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
