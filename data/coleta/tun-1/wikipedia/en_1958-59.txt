The '''1958–59 Tunisian National Championship''' was the fourth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1958/1959 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1958-1959 |access-date=2026-10-11 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished ahead of the defending champions [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel]] in the final standings. It was Espérance's second league title overall and their first since independence.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref>

The league was contested by 14 clubs, each playing 26 matches. [[Stade Gabésien]] and [[Olympique du Kef]], both promoted the previous season, were relegated at the end of the campaign, while [[Avenir Sportif de La Marsa|Avenir Musulman]] was promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
