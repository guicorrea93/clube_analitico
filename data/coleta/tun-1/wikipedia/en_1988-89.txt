The '''1988–89 Tunisian National Championship''' was the thirty-fourth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by the defending champions [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1988/1989 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1988-1989 |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished 13 points ahead of [[Club Africain]] in the final standings. It was Espérance's ninth league title since independence and their second in a row.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref>

The league was contested by 14 clubs, each playing 26 matches. [[JS Kairouan]] and [[Océano Club de Kerkennah]] finished at the bottom and were relegated. [[CA Bizertin]], who finished 12th, lost a play-off against [[CS Hammam-Lif]] on penalties and were relegated as well. [[Club Sportif Cheminots]], [[Avenir Sportif Oued Ellil]] and CS Hammam-Lif were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
