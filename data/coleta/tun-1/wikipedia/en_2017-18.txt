The '''2017–18 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' (''Tunisian Professional League'') season was the 63rd season of top-tier football in [[Tunisia]]. The season calendar was drawn on July 24.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.kawarji.com/actu-39459-ligue_1_la_lnfp_communique_la_date_du_tirage_au_sort_du_calendrier.html|title=Ligue 1 : La LNFP communique la date du tirage au sort du calendrier}}</ref> The competition started on August 15.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.kawarji.com/actu-39815-ligue_1_programme_de_la_premiere_journee.html|title = Ligue 1 : Programme de la première journée}}</ref>

==Teams==
A total of 14 teams contested the league, including 11 sides from the [[2016–17 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|2016–17 season]] and three promoted from the [[2016–17 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 2|2016–17 Ligue 2]]. [[Union Sportive Monastirienne (football)|Union Monastirienne]] and [[Stade Tunisien]] obtained promotion after winning their penultimate game of the season, while [[Club Olympique de Médenine|Olympique Médenine]] won the promotion playoff against [[Avenir Sportif de La Marsa|Avenir de Marsa]]. The three teams replaced the five teams that were relegated to [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 2|2017–18 Tunisian Ligue 2]] in order to reduce the number of teams from 16 to 14.
