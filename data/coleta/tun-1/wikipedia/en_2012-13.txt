The '''2012–13 [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1]]''' (''Tunisian Professional League'')  season was the 58th season of top-tier [[football in Tunisia]]. The competition began on 10 November 2012 and was scheduled to conclude in June 2013. The defending champions from the [[2011–12 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|previous season]] were [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Season 2011-12|url=https://int.soccerway.com/national/tunisia/ligue-1/2011-2012/regular-season/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130212181305/http://int.soccerway.com/national/tunisia/ligue-1/2011-2012/regular-season/|url-status=dead|archive-date=February 12, 2013|publisher=Soccerway|access-date=5 November 2012}}</ref>

==Team and format changes==
[[2011–12 Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 1|Last season]] two teams were relegated as usual ([[AS Gabès]] and [[ES Beni-Khalled]]), with two teams promoted from the [[Tunisian Ligue Professionnelle 2]] to replace them ([[Olympique du Kef]] and [[Stade Gabèsien]]). However, in a change from previous seasons, the league was split into two groups of 8 teams.<ref>{{cite web|title=Season 2012-13|url=https://int.soccerway.com/national/tunisia/ligue-1/20122013/regular-season/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130207124131/http://int.soccerway.com/national/tunisia/ligue-1/20122013/regular-season/|url-status=dead|archive-date=February 7, 2013|publisher=Soccerway|access-date=5 November 2012}}</ref>
