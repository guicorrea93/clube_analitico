The '''1989–90 Tunisian National Championship''' was the thirty-fifth season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by [[Club Africain]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1989/1990 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1989-1990 |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> who finished level on 83 points with [[Espérance Sportive de Tunis]] and won the title on their head-to-head record against them. It was Club Africain's seventh league title since independence.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref> The defending champions, Espérance, finished second.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref>

The league was contested by 14 clubs, each playing 26 matches. [[Club Sportif Cheminots]] and [[Avenir Sportif de Kasserine]] were relegated at the end of the campaign, while [[CA Bizertin]] and [[JS Kairouan]] were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
