The '''1986–87 Tunisian National Championship''' was the thirty-second season of the Tunisian national football championship following the country's independence in 1956. The competition was won by the defending champions [[Étoile Sportive du Sahel]], who finished five points ahead of [[Club Africain]] in the final standings.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Classement Tunisie Ligue 1 1986/1987 |url=https://www.kawarji.com/classement/ligue1/1986-1987 |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=www.kawarji.com}}</ref> It was Étoile du Sahel's sixth league title since independence and their second in a row.<ref name="RSSSF">{{cite web |title=Tunisia – List of Final Tables |url=https://rsssf.org/tablest/tunhist.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |access-date=11 October 2026}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligue 1 palmarès: tous les vainqueurs |url=https://www.mondefootball.fr/competition/co1158/tunisie-ligue-1/records-winners-list/ |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=mondefootball.fr |language=fr}}</ref>

The league was contested by 14 clubs, each playing 26 matches. [[Sfax Railways Sports]] and [[Avenir Sportif de La Marsa]] finished at the bottom and were relegated. [[Océano Club de Kerkennah]], who finished 12th, lost a play-off against [[Grombalia Sports]] on the away-goals rule and were relegated as well. [[Olympique du Kef]], [[Avenir Sportif de Kasserine]] and Grombalia Sports were promoted for the following season.<ref name="RSSSF" />
