Tunisia 1952/53

Championship

NB: played in five regional groups:

northern zone:
Tunis-Nord 1 (10 clubs): qualified: Olympique de Tunis, US Ferryville
Tunis-Nord 2 ( 8 clubs): qualified: Vaillante-Jeunesse de Hammam-Lif
Nord-Ouest   ( 4 clubs): qualified: FC Jérissa
southern zone:
Centre-Sud   ( 3 clubs): qualified: Sfax Railway Sports
Sud-Ouest    ( 6 clubs): qualified: Khanfous Club (Redeyef)

NB: the tournament was boycotted by nationalist and muslim clubs

Playoff

Round 1 (northern zone only)
Olympique de Tunis          bt  US Ferryville
FC Jérissa                  bt  Vaillante-Jeunesse de Hammam-Lif

Semifinals
Olympique de Tunis          6-0 FC Jérissa                  
Sfax Railway Sports         2-0 Khanfous Club (Redeyef)     

Final   
Sfax Railway Sports         2-1 Olympique de Tunis
