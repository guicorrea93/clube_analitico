Tunisia 1907

First club created in Tunisia was Football Club de Tunis, on January 19, 1906;
it was renamed Racing Club de Tunis soon after;
in 1907, a second club was created, also called Football Club de Tunis (F.C.T.). 

First Match [Jun 9, 1907; organised by Racing]
Collège Alaoui    1-1 Lycée Carnot 



First Championship of Tunisia


Participants (5):
Racing Club de Tunis
Football Club de Bizerte [aka Stade maritime bizertin] 
Sporting Club de Ferryville 
Lycée Carnot de Tunis 
Collège Sadiki

Final
Racing de Tunis   2-0 FC Bizerte
