[1] Sete de Setembro won the points of one of their defeats against Renascença
because the latter fielded an irregular player. Therefore, Renascença was forced
to play a tiebreak with Bela Vista in order to decide who would remain in the top
flight and who would play-off against Itaú for a spot in the top division.

Tiebreak playoffs (at neutral venues):

[May 13, 1962]
Renascença   2-3   Bela Vista   [at América]
 [Zeca, Rafael; Ica (2), Nenenzão]

[May 17]
Bela Vista   1-4   Renascença   [at Atlético]
 [Jair; Jair, Robson, Guido (2)]

[May 19]
Renascença   2-0   Bela Vista   [at Sete de Setembro]
 [Jair, Guido]

Renascença remained in Extra Division. Bela Vista qualified for the relegation playoff
against Itaú, champions of 1961 First Division.
