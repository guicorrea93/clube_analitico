** Palestra Itália are Minas Gerais' unbeaten champions of 1929 **

*** Topscorer: Ninão (Palestra Itália), 33 goals ***

In 1930, a general assembly changed the league's statutes. Therefore, Santa Cruz and Alves Nogueira
were relegated to the newly structured second division, Fluminense (1929 second division winners)
were not granted promotion to 1930 first division and Villa Nova was readmitted in first division,
as it was approved that Villa Nova could play their home matches in their own stadium.
