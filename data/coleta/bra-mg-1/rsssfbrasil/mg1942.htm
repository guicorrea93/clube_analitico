[1] In January 30, 1942, Palestra Itália had to change their name to
"Sociedade Esportiva Palestra Mineiro" because of a decree by president
Getúlio Vargas banning names referring to countries of the Axis. Throughout
the year, the small name change was insufficient to cease the animosities
with the club and its ascendence. Then, in October 2, Palestra president
Ennes Ciro Poni called the club's General Assembly, which decided to change
the name to Esporte Clube Ypiranga. The only match under the name was a 2-1 defeat to
Atlético. Later, in October 7, another General Assembly discussed and decided
for the name "Cruzeiro Esporte Clube".
[2] After the 1st Stage, Aeroporto abandoned the Professional League and
therefore their matches were annulled.
