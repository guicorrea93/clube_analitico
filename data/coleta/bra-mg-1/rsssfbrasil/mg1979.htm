(+) Promoted from 1978's Second Level Championship.
(++) The Main Division of Minas Gerais League was planned to have 14 teams in 1979,
the 12 teams of 1978, with relegation having been cancelled, and the two promoted
from 1978's Second Level.
However, a new league expansion was approved and Guaxupé and Ateneu were invited. Guaxupé was
invited for having finished the Second Level just behind the promoted teams, while
Ateneu was invited under the argument of having the best revenues of 1978.
