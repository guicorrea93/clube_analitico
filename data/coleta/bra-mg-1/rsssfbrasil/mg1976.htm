Rules:
- Tournament divided in two cups: Taça Minas Gerais (Minas Gerais Cup)
  and Taça Governador do Estado (State Governor Cup).
- Due to participation in Copa Libertadores, Cruzeiro are byes to
  Taça Minas Gerais' first stage. They'll enter directly in semifinals.
- The best group champion qualifies to Taça Minas Gerais' final match.
- The worst group champion qualifies to semifinal match with Cruzeiro.
- Cruzeiro, Atlético, América, Caldense, as championship finalists in
  1975, and other top 8 clubs overall in Taça Minas Gerais qualify to
  Taça Governador do Estado. The other 11 teams will play Torneio
  Paralelo I (Parallel Tournament I).
