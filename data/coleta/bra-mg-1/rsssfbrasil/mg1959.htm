"Sertão" Zone Tiebreaking Playoff

1st leg [Sep 27]
Guarani		  3-1  Curvelo
 [Wander, Ticrim (2); Bacalhau]

Second leg was scheduled to October 4, but Guarani claimed that members of the
team staff were threatened by locals in the hotel and walked-over claiming lack
of warranties. The case went to the state sports court which rescheduled the
match to October 31 in a neutral venue. However, Curvelo filed an injunction in
the Superior Sports Court suspending the upcoming match, until the issue was
judged by that court. In the following week, the teams reached an agreement to
reschedule the match to November 15, in Curvelo.

2nd leg [Nov 15]
Curvelo		  1-1  Guarani
 [Joãosinho; Wander]

Guarani qualified to the league championship.
