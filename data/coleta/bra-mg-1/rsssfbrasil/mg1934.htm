Minas Gerais State League 1934

Campeonato Mineiro 1934

The league was divided in two championships: the Belo Horizonte and Juiz de Fora
city championships. The two champions would decide the title.

TUPYNAMBÁS Futebol Clube won the Juiz de Fora league. All other data of their
city championship of that year are still unknown.

LEAGUE FINALS
(Finais do campeonato)

Leg 1 [Dec 16]
Villa Nova   2-0  Tupynambás   [at América]

VILLA NOVA 2-0 TUPYNAMBÁS
Venue: Estádio da Alameda - Belo Horizonte, MG
Referee: José Pedro Rizzo
Goals: Perácio 4', Alfredo Bernardino 74'
VILLA NOVA: Geraldão, Chico Preto, Sérgio; Zezé Procópio, Neco, Geninho; Lêra,
Alfredo Bernardino, Campos, Perácio, Canhoto. Coaches: Zé de Deus and Gute Mello
TUPYNAMBÁS: Balim, Florindo, Muzza; Pereira, Lulu (Rizoca), Waldemiro; Buru
(Chiquinho), Nino, Cláudio, Lessa, Chiquinho (Toschi).

The three-leg finals were reduced to just one because the Juiz de Fora AMF sub-league
disbanded from the FBF (Federação Brasileira de Futebol), to which the central AMF was
affiliated, and returned to CBD (Confederação Brasileira de Desportos). Thus, the other
legs were cancelled and Villa Nova were declared state champions.

*** VILLA NOVA are Minas Gerais state champions of 1934 ***

Topscorer: Alfredo Bernardino (Villa Nova): 11 goals
