LEAGUE FINALS

Rules: each stage winner wins 10 points. In the finals, a victory is awarded
5 points and a tie, 2.5 points. The team that first reaches 25 points are declared
champions.

Atlético, as winner of one stage, carries 10 extra points. Cruzeiro, as winner
of the other two, carries 20 extra points.

Leg 1 [Apr 17, 1955]
Atlético	 2-0  Cruzeiro
 [Joel, Gastão]

Leg 2 [Apr 21]
Cruzeiro	 0-3  Atlético
 [Ubaldo (2), Joel]	 

Leg 3 [Apr 24]
Cruzeiro	 1-1  Atlético
 [Raimundinho; Ubaldo]	 

Before the fourth leg, as both teams were tied in 22.5 points, FMF declared that,
if both teams tied the match and the following extra time, they should share
the title.

Leg 4 [May 1]
Atlético	 2-0  Cruzeiro
 [Ubaldo 16’, Joel 88’]
