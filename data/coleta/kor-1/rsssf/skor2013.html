K League Challenge

This is the new second tier division introduced as a result of K League restructuring.  
The new arrangement introduces a promotion/relegation system between the K League Classic 
(first division) and the K League Challenge (second division).

The 2013 K League Challenge season involves eight teams:  
Bucheon FC 1995 (previously in the Challengers League), Chungju Hummel FC (previously in the N-League), 
FC Anyang (new entity which absorbed Goyang Kookmin Bank FC who were previously in the N-League), 
Goyang Hi FC (previously Ansan H FC in the N-League), Gwangju FC (relegated from the K-League), 
Police FC (previously in the Reserve League), Sangju Sangmu (relegated from the K-League) and  
Suwon FC (previously Suwon City FC in the N-League).
