K League Challenge

The league was called the Hyundai Oilbank K League Challenge 2014 for sponsorship reasons 
and was the second tier of the Korean league system. The league winner was promoted to 
the K League Classic for the 2015 season while the promotion play-off winner entered into 
a play-off with the 11th placed team in the K League Classic for an additional spot.
There was no relegation.
