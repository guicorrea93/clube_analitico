NOTE: Vila Nova did not show up for the Jun 24 match against Goiás as a protest for refereeing decisions. The FGF (Goiás FA) studied the case
and in Jul 5 considered it "forfeit of the league", therefore cancelling  all of Vila Nova's matches during the season. So the club ended the championship
as 14th, with 0 point won, and was consequently relegated.
