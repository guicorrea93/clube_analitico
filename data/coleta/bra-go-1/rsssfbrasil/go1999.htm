N.B. Santa Helena paid in the end of 1998 a referee fee of R$300,00 (which
     was then about U$270,00) with a check that bounced.  The state federation
     decided to punish the team with an 180 days suspension beginning Jan 1,
     1999.  So the team was excluded form 1st level and Anapolina was brought
     as the replacement.  That is at least the official explanation for the
     exclusion of the team of Santa Helena.
