Papua New Guinea - List of Cup Winners

PNGFA Cup

1988    Lae                2-0 Arawa
1990    Madang
1991    Port Moresby
2003    Madang             3-1 Lae   
2006    Finschhafen        1-1 Port Moresby            [aet, 4-2 pen]

Besta Cup

2011    Lae                3-1 Public Servants SA (Port Moresby)
2012    Lae                7-0 Bougainville             
2014    Manus              3-2 Lae
2015    Manus              1-0 Madang
2016      not known
2017    Madang             0-0 PMSA                    [aet, 5-4 pen]
2018    Morobe             3-0 Almami (Bogia)   
2019    Morobe             0-0 NCD PSSA                [aet, Morobe on pen]

NB: the 2011 edition of the Besta Cup was the first ever (none are missing above);
    the 2012 edition was reportedly the 12th (overall)
