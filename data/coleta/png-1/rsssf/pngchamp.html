Papua New Guinea - List of Champions

Football was first introduced in PNG in 1884 in Finschhafen, Morobe Province, by the missionaries of the Evangelical Lutheran Church of Germany.
During colonial times football was mostly played by Europeans.
An organized competition was started in the gold fields of Wau and Bulolo in 1946 and was called Wau Kik, depicting a foul kick or rouph play.
