NB: the NSL decided to scrap the final following the withdrawal of PS United,
    apparently for financial reasons, as "not all franchisees had paid their
    fees in full undermining the viability of staging the finals" while noting
    that "the on-going postponement of the finals had inconvenienced many
    stakeholders including football fans and sponsors".
