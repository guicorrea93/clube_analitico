National Club Championship 2008

Held in Port Moresby, Oct 31-Nov 2

NB: 7 participants; the Port Moresby clubs (Rapatona(?!) and Souths United)
    did not enter due to a power struggle among the PNGFA executive

Final [Nov 2]
Sunammad FC (Dami)      1-1 Poro FC (Lae)         [aet, 3-2 pen]
  [Jeffrey Sakie 1-1; Essau Nassa 0-1]

NB: Sunammad FC (Dami) represented the Kimbe FA; as winners, they will
    represented Papua New Guinea at the Arafura Games in May 2009.
    It was the first ever title for the West New Britain province and
    the New Guinea islands region.
