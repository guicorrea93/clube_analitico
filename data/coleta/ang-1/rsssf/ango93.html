     to Académica Petróleos Clube do Lobito due oil company Sonangol sponsoring.
(3)  Born as Sports Clube Portugal de Benguela, in 1975 changed to Sports Club Nacional de Benguela and

(6)  Born as Atlético Sport Aviação, later renamed Desportivo da TAAG and in 1992 renamed again as
     Atlético Sport Aviação (aka ASA).
(10) Founded in 1975 as a merger of three clubs: Juventude Unida do Bairro Alfredo (JUBA), Juventista 
     and Vaza.
(12) Founded in 1981 as a merger between Estrela Vermelha de Benguela and Grupo Desportivo 1º de Maio,
     initial name was África Têxtil 1º de Maio, later renamed Estrela Club 1º de Maio.
(13) Founded in 1980 as a merger between Clube Atlético de Luanda, Grupo Sonangol and Sport e Benfica.

NB: Due to civil war Benfica Huambo and Petro Huambo were replaced by Desportivo Nocal and Inter Huíla.
    FAF informed that Benfica Huambo and Petro Huambo will, automatically, play on 1994 championship if
    the war finish.

NB: On Feb 13 Grupo Desportivo da CUCA (Luanda) suspended professional football sections due to financial
    reasons.
