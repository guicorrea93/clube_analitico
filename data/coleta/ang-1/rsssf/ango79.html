Angola 1979

Girabola

NB: first ever edition of Girabola

Started on Dec 8 1979.
Ended on Mar 23 1980.

NB: Only edition with teams from all 16 provinces (on that time Angola was divided into 16 provinces:
later Lunda province was transformed in two, Lunda-Norte and Lunda-Sul, and Bengo Province was created later).

NB: 24 teams (4 groups x 6 teams each)

NB: Vitória Atlético Clube were a last minute replacement for Desportivo de Kamakupa, also from Bié.
				
(1) formely and later Clube Desportivo Construtores do Uíge.
(2) Born in 1970 as Académica da Chilimba, later renamed to Académica Clube do Lobito and in 1981
    to Académica Petróleos Clube do Lobito due oil company Sonangol sponsoring.
(3) Born as Sports Clube Portugal de Benguela, in 1975 changed to Sports Club Nacional de Benguela and
    in 1979 changed to Clube Nacional de Benguela.
(4)  Born as Sport Nova Lisboa e Benfica (aka Benfica de Nova Lisboa), in 1975 changed to Clube Desportivo 
     da Chela and later to Benfica Petróleos do Lubango (aka Benfica do Lubango).
(5) Born as Clube Ferroviário de Sá da Bandeira and in 1975 changed to Clube Ferroviário da Huíla.
(6) Born as Atlético Sport Aviação, later renamed Desportivo da TAAG and in 1992 renamed again as
    Atlético Sport Aviação (aka ASA).
(7) Born as Sporting Clube de Luanda, in 1975 renamed Diabos Verdes, later Leões de Luanda and in
    1990 renamed again as Sporting Clube de Luanda.
(8) Born as Sport Nova Lisboa e Benfica, in 1975 renamed Estrela Vermelha do Huambo, later renamed
    Mambroa SC and in 1990 renamed to Sport Huambo e Benfica.
(9) Born as Santa Rita, later renamed Grupo Desportivo Welwitschia and later renamed again Santa Rita.
