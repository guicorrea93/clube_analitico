Angola - List of Champions

Ferrovia Atlético Clube de Luanda were founded in 1912 or 1913 (and are probably the oldest club of the country); around 1915 a first annual championship (football league of Luanda) was created, for the Taça de Luanda (Luanda Cup); in 1916 two more clubs (Militar and Ideal) appeared. The first national championship (Angola Provincial championship) in colonial times was decided in 1941. Between 1922 and 1940 only provincial leagues were constested.

[*1] Sport Benguela e Benfica was founded in 1921 as Império Sport Clube;
     later changed name to Sport Lisboa e Benguela, and now called Sport
     Benguela e Benfica.
[*2] Ferroviário de Luanda was founded in 1915, linked to the Railways.
[*3] Lobito Sport Clube was founded in 1914, being one of the oldest in Angola.
[*4] Independente Sport Clube (Porto Alexandre) were the only club from the
     district of Moçâmedes to win the championship before independence
[*5] the final of the second division was played on Jan 12 1975:
     FC Luanda 3-0 Sporting do Huambo 
[*6] Recreativo da Caála were leading when the league was abandoned after
     12 rounds because of the political situation

NB: the town of Porto Alexandre is now called Tômbwa;
    the town of Nova Lisboa is now called Huambo;
    the town of Sá da Bandeira is now called Lubango;
    the district and town of Moçâmedes are now called Namibe;
    Sport Clube Portugal de Benguela were later renamed Nacional de Benguela;
