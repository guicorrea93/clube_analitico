[Apr 27]
The Angolan Football Federation (FAF) has confirmed the withdrawal of JGM
from the national championship, for financial reasons. In this sense, 
FAF Technical Council has withdrawn all points to the training that has 
already faced the JGM and awaits other decisions of the Disciplinary Board,
which should apply the appropriate sanctions (suspended from all official
competitions during two seasons).

[May 4]
Under guidance of the International Football Federation (FIFA), six points 
were withdrawn from Kabuscorp in the Girabola qualifying table2018, due to
the failure to pay the $ 750,000 debt to Rivaldo.
The measure can still be aggravated if the team does not pay, with relegation to the second division.
The FAF informs that it has received and implemented the orientation of the governing
body of world football after an extraordinary meeting of its discipline council.
The Brazilian represented Kabuscorp in the 2012 season.
