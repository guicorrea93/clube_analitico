NB: on Nov 7 the new direction of the FCF suggested to change the set-up for the
    ongoing season, ending the regular phase after 9 rounds, not have any clubs
    relegated, and adding a playoff between the top-4 (single round robin) to
    determine the championship and OFC Champions League qualifiers; this proposal
    was accepted before round 9 was played;
    the 2021 league will include 13 clubs (no clubs are relegated; 3 provincial
    champions 2020 will be promoted)
