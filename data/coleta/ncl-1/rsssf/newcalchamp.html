New Caledonia - List of Champions

Football arrived in Nouméa in 1910 thanks to a soldier called Tomasini, who formed three teams at the barracks; when the French cruiser "Wontcalm" visited the town, the best military selection, called "Stade militaire nouméen" played a team from the "Wontcalm" on Dec 25, 1910, winning 3-0.
