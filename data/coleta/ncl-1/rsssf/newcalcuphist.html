NB: until (presumably) 1993, the New Caledonian teams had to play off with those 
    from Tahiti and, at least occasionally (in 1975), the New Hebrides, for one
    berth in the Coupe de France; since then, New Caledonia have their own berth,
    which since 1998 is reserved for the winners of the Coupe de Calédonie.
