      between square brackets refer to punishments related to league matches as
      enumerated in the note above

[*] the worst club from Lifou is automatically relegated unless it finishes 7th
    or better (in which case it would have to play off against the Champions des
    Iles (AS Kirikitr) for a place in the league next season); as Qanono (who
    were sixth and ahead of Ne Drehu after the final round of matches) ended up
    in eighth place (and below Ne Drehu) after the point deductions, they are
    relegated automatically (and Kirikitr promoted automatically)

NB: only one club from Grande Ile to be relegated as top level is extended to
    12 clubs for the 2014 season
