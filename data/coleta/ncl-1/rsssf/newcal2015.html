[*] the worst placed of the two clubs "des Iles" (i.e. not from Grande Terre), viz.
    SC Ne Drehu and ES Wacaélé, is either relegated directly (if finishing 8th or lower)
    or has to play off against the Champion des Iles (if finishing 7th or higher);
    so, if the above final table would have been homologated, ES Wacaélé would have
    had to play off against Horizon Patho (Champions des Iles) and SC Ne Drehu would
    have remained at the top level.

NB: note that subtraction à la calédonienne apparently does not follow otherwise
    universally accepted arithmetical rules:

    58- 9 = 46 (Mont-Dore) [apparently 12 points were deducted instead of 9]
    54-10 = 45 (Lössi)     [apparently 9 points were deducted instead of 10]
    35-30 =  6 (Baco)      [possibly the original 1-point deduction was included among the 30]
    26-13 = 16 (Auteuil)   [possibly the original 3-points deduction was included among the 13]
