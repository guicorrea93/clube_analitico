Rio Grande do Sul -- List of State Topscorers

 * The criteria for choosing the top scorer of the competition was the total number of goals scored only in the final stage of the competition until the early 1940s.
 The list includes, in the 1940-1960 period, the topscorers of the Porto Alegre championship.
 From 1961 onwards, the championship ceased to be contested by regions, and included all professional teams from the State.
 * O critério para a escolha do goleador da competição era o somatório dos gols assinalados somente na fase
 final da competição até o início da década de 40. A lista tem, no período 1940-1960, os goleadores do certame de Porto Alegre.
