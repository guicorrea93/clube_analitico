Final [Jeddah, Youth Welfare Stadium, att: 55,000]
Al Nasr    3-1 Al Hilal
  [5' Abdullah, 30' Mohaysen Al-Jamaan, 66' Abdullah; 45' Mansour Al-Mowayneh]

NB: the Saudi FA rules stipulate both teams have to play away, so although
    both finalists are from Riyadh, the final was in Jeddah.
    Al Nasr had four coaches during the season. Youssouf Khamis was the
    lucky one to win the final, following the successive sackings of Henri
    Michel, Ivo Borkibo and Nasser Jawhar.
