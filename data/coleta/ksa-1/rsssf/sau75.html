Saudi Arabia 1974/75

Saudi Categorization League

NB: first and only edition; contested between the top-16 teams from the
    Western, Central, and Eastern regions, who were divided into 2 groups
    of 8 clubs; the top-4 of both qualified for the inaugural edition of
    the Saudi Premier League (1975/76) while the bottom-4 entered the
    1975/76 Saudi First Division
