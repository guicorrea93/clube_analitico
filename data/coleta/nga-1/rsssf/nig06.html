NB: The 2006/07 season is programmed to kick off on October 7 to begin 
    a new era of Nigerian league season calendar harmonized with those
    of most other countries, especially in Europe. This is, however, 
    subject to the approval of the NFL Congress billed to hold a day
    before the board elections in Abuja.

NB: NFL Board ratified at its Extraordinary Congress in Abuja that the 
    last two teams in the Premier League would be automatically be 
    relegated at the end of the football season while the first from 
    each zone of the Pro League gain promotion to the elite Globacom 
    League.

The Nigerian Football League (NFL) Congress has approved the abridged 
league format as proposed by the league board, breaking the Premier 
League into two and the Pro-League Division One into four for the 2006 
season only.

The format, which is a measure to harmonize the NFL calendar in line 
with others in the continent and will be only for the 2006 season being 
proposed to end by August, brings back a Super League competition at 
a neutral venue that will involve the top two winners in both groups 
in the Premier League fighting for the two CAF Champions League tickets 
as well as one of the two tickets into the African Confederation Cup 
competition.

National stage
--------------

NB: Round of 32 composed of seeded 20 Premier League clubs and 12 teams 
    who qualified from the zonal play-offs.
    For the first time in the history of the competition, the Nigeria 
    Football Association (NFA) and sponsor, Coca-Cola, agreed to use only 
    eight match venues from the Round of 32 through finals.
    The eight match venues are: Dan Anyiam Stadium, Owerri (Imo State); 
    Enyimba Stadium, Aba (Abia State); Gateway Stadium (Ogun State);
    Gombe Stadium, Gombe (Gombe State); Jos Township Stadium, Jos (Plateau
    State); Onikan Stadium, Lagos (Lagos State); Samuel Ogbemudia Stadium,
    Benin-City (Edo State); Sani Abacha Stadium, Kano (Kano State).
    At this stage of the competition, teams would play on neutral grounds.
