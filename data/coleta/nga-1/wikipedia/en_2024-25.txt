The '''2024–25 Nigeria Premier Football League''' was the 54th season of Nigeria's top-flight [[association football]] league, the 35th since attaining professionalism and the second under the current name. The season commenced early on 31 August 2024 with the defending champions, [[Enugu Rangers]] hosting [[El-Kanemi Warriors]].<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://dailypost.ng/2024/06/26/new-npfl-season-kicks-off-august-31/|location=Lagos, Nigeria|title=New NPFL season kicks off August 31|date=26 June 2024|newspaper=[[Daily Post (Nigeria)|Daily Post]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.brila.net/rangers-settle-for-point-against-el-kanemi-in-npfl-opener/|title=Rangers settle for point against El-Kanemi in NPFL opener|work=brila.net|date=1 September 2024}}</ref>

On 27 April 2025, [[Remo Stars]] defeated [[Niger Tornadoes]] to secure their first ever [[Nigeria Premier Football League]] title with 3 games to spare.<ref>{{Cite news |last1=Adewale |first1=Abiodun |title=Remo Stars clinch first NPFL title |url=https://punchng.com/breaking-remo-stars-beat-tornadoes-to-clinch-first-npfl-title/ |newspaper=[[The Punch]]|access-date=27 April 2025}}</ref> With this triumph, they became the first [[South West (Nigeria)|South-western]] club to win the title since [[Bridge F.C.|Julius Berger]] in 2000.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Abdulsalam |first1=Hassan |title=Remo Stars make history, break 25-year-old NPFL record |url=https://www.pulsesports.ng/football/story/remo-stars-make-history-break-25-year-old-npfl-record-2025042718303655579 |website=Pulse Sports |access-date=27 April 2025}}</ref>

This season also saw an increase of [[Naira|N]]50 million in prize money for the champions from the [[2023–24 Nigeria Premier Football League|previous season]], with the winners taking home [[Naira|N]]200 million.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://dailypost.ng/2024/07/16/npfl-increases-prize-money-for-2024-25-season-to-n200m/|location=Lagos, Nigeria|title=NPFL increases prize money for 2024-25 season to N200m|date=16 June 2024|newspaper=[[Daily Post (Nigeria)|Daily Post]]}}</ref> Additionally, the NPFL secured a sponsorship deal with British multinational television production company, [[IMG/JVco]] for broadcast of league games.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theathletic.com.ng/npfl-lands-new-sponsorship-deal/|title=Latest News: NPFL lands new sponsorship deal|last=Nsikak|first=Asuquo|work=The Athletic Nigeria|date=24 July 2024}}</ref>

==Teams==
=== Changes from previous season ===
Twenty teams, consisting of the top sixteen teams from the previous season and four promoted teams from the Nigeria National League, are competing in the league. The promoted teams are [[Nasarawa United]] and [[El-Kanemi Warriors]] who both got relegated [[2022–23 Nigeria Professional Football League|two seasons ago]], and newcomers [[Ikorodu City F.C.|Ikorodu City]] and [[Beyond Limits F.A.|Beyond Limits]]. Beyond Limits would be subsequently replaced by [[Heartland F.C.|Heartland]] who were initially relegated the previous season as Beyond Limits and [[Remo Stars]] share the same owners.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://dailypost.ng/2024/07/27/npfl-heartland-close-to-top-flight-return-after-beyond-limits-agreement/|location=Lagos, Nigeria|title=NPFL: Heartland close to top-flight return after Beyond Limits agreement|first=Mike|last=Oyebola|date=27 July 2024|newspaper=[[Daily Post (Nigeria)|Daily Post]]}}</ref>
