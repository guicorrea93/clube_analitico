The '''2015 Nigeria Professional Football League''' (referred to as the Nigerian [[Globacom|Glo]] premier league for sponsorship reasons) is the 44th season of the [[Nigeria Premier League]], the top Nigerian professional league for association football clubs, since its establishment in 1972. It is also the 25th season since the league was rebranded as the "Professional League."

[[Enyimba F.C.|Enyimba]] won their seventh league title and first since the [[2009–10 Nigeria Premier League|2009–10 season]]. They clinched with a week to go in the season with a scoreless draw with [[Warri Wolves F.C.|Warri Wolves]], who were six points behind. Warri Wolves finished in second place, four points behind Enyimba, and both will represent Nigeria in the [[2016 CAF Champions League]]. [[Nasarawa United F.C.|Nasarawa United]] placed third and qualified for the [[2016 CAF Confederation Cup]].

[[Bayelsa United F.C.|Bayelsa United]], [[Kwara United F.C.|Kwara United]], [[Sharks F.C.|Sharks]] and [[Taraba F.C.|Taraba]] each finished in the bottom four and will be relegated to the [[Nigeria National League]] for 2016. [[Akwa United F.C.|Akwa United]] escaped relegation, pulling out of the bottom four with a 1-0 win over [[Lobi Stars F.C.|Lobi Stars]] in their final match of the season.

==Clubs==
A total of 20 teams will contest the league including 16 teams from the [[2014 Nigeria Premier League|previous season]] and four teams promoted from the [[Nigeria National League]]. The four promoted teams replace Gombe United, Crown, Kaduna United and Nembe City who were all relegated to the National League at the end of the [[2014 Nigeria Premier League|previous season]]

==News== 
Five Kano Pillars players were injured during an armed robbery as the club was heading south for their opener against Heartland FC.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/0/football/31756677|title=Gunmen wound Kano Pillars players|work=BBC Sport}}</ref>
Their opening game was postponed indefinitely.

In June, the league forced several clubs to find new venues for Matchdays 12 and 13 while they make mandatory upgrades to their home grounds.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.npfl.ng/index.php/news-features/club-news/946-seven-clubs-settle-for-new-homes-lmc-awaits-giwa-nasarawa |title=Seven clubs settle for new homes, LMC awaits Giwa, Nasarawa |website=www.npfl.ng |access-date=15 January 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150621214302/http://www.npfl.ng/index.php/news-features/club-news/946-seven-clubs-settle-for-new-homes-lmc-awaits-giwa-nasarawa |archive-date=21 June 2015 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

In August, Kano Pillars' 12-year undefeated streak at home came to an end in a 2-1 loss to Nasarawa United.<ref>[https://www.bbc.com/sport/0/football/34035720Nigerian Premier League side's unbeaten record ends]{{Dead link|date=August 2021 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
