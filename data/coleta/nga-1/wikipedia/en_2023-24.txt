The '''2023–24 Nigeria Premier Football League''' was the 53rd season of Nigeria's top-flight [[association football]] league, the 34th since attaining professionalism and the first under the current name.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://dailypost.ng/2023/09/10/nff-approves-npfl-name-change/|location=Lagos, Nigeria|title=NFF approves NPFL name change|date=10 September 2023|newspaper=[[Daily Post (Nigeria)|Daily Post]]}}</ref> The season commenced on 30 September 2023 after initial delays.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://nationaltrailonline.com.ng/2023-2024-npfl-season-finally-set-to-begin-on-september-30/|title=2023–24 NPFL season finally set to begin on September 30 after delays|work=National Trial Online|access-date=4 October 2023}}</ref><ref name=Prize>{{cite web|url=https://www.primebusiness.africa/npfl-announces-september-30-kick-off-date-n150m-prize-money-for-champions/|title=NPFL announces September 30 kick off date, N150 million prize money for champions|work=Prime Business Africa|access-date=4 October 2023}}</ref>

[[Enyimba F.C.|Enyimba]] were the defending champions, after finishing top of the [[2022–23 Nigeria Professional Football League#Championship Round|Championship round]] at the previous season.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.channelstv.com/2023/06/11/npfl-enyimba-draw-with-rivers-utd-to-win-record-ninth-title/|title=NPFL: Enyimba Draw With Rivers Utd To Win Record Ninth Title – Channels Television}}</ref> On 17 June 2024, [[Enugu Rangers]] won their eighth NPFL title with a game to spare.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://punchng.com/enugu-rangers-win-eighth-npfl-title/|title=Enugu Rangers won eighth NPFL title|date=16 June 2024|newspaper=[[The Punch]]}}</ref>

This season sees the Interim Management Committee (IMC) entering into a partnership with technology firm GTI Investment, Propel Sports Africa and [[MTN Group|MTN]] to sponsor the league, with an exclusive broadcast right that shall cover eight live games every weekend and 300 throughout the season.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.premiumtimesng.com/news/top-news/627086-mtn-to-co-sponsor-nigerian-football-league-with-gti-propel-sports-africa.html|title=MTN to co-sponsor Nigerian football league with GTI, Propel Sports Africa|date=20 September 2023|newspaper=[[Premium Times]]}}</ref> Thus, this makes the NPFL the first African football league to deploy [[artificial intelligence]] at games.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://punchng.com/gti-unveils-ai-cameras-for-npfl-coverage/|title=GTI unveils AI cameras for NPFL coverage|access-date=4 October 2023|newspaper=[[The Punch]]}}</ref>

This season also sees an increase of [[Naira|N]]50 million in prize money for the champions.<ref name=Prize />
Also, The NPFL secured $1 Billion Broadcast sponsorship deal with Chinese satellite provider [[StarTimes]], for five years.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://thesun.ng/npfl-startimes-sign-n1-06b-5-year-television-sponsorship-deal/|location=Lagos, Nigeria|title=NPFL, StarTimes sign N1.6bn 5-year television sponsorship deal|date=3 November 2023|newspaper=[[The Sun (Nigeria)|The Sun]]}}</ref>

== Teams ==
Twenty teams are competing in the league – the top sixteen teams from the previous season and the four teams promoted from the [[Nigeria National League]]. The promoted teams are [[Heartland F.C.|Heartland]], [[Sporting Lagos]], [[Kano Pillars F.C.|Kano Pillars]] and [[Katsina United F.C.|Katsina United]]. This is Sporting Lagos's first season in the Nigeria Professional Football League. With their promotion, Sporting Lagos became the first team to have been promoted after the first season of their creation. Heartland, Katsina United and Kano Pillars also returned to the top-flight after getting relegated [[2021–22 Nigeria Professional Football League|two seasons ago]]. They replaced [[Wikki Tourists]], [[Nasarawa United]], [[El-Kanemi Warriors]] and [[Dakkada]], who were relegated to the [[Nigeria National League]].
