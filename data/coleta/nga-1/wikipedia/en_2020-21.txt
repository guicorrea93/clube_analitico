The '''2020–21 [[Nigeria Professional Football League]] (NPFL)''' was the 50th season of the top flight league in the [[Nigerian football league system|Nigeria football league system]] and the 31st since its rebranding. The league started on 27 December 2020 after much delay and concluded on 5 August 2021.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://m.guardian.ng/sport/lmc-affirms-december-27-date-for-league-kickoff/amp/|location=Lagos, Nigeria|title=LMC affirms December 27 date for league kickoff|newspaper=[[The Guardian (Nigeria)|The Guardian]]|accessdate=12 January 2021|date=20 December 2020}}</ref>

[[Enyimba FC|Enyimba]] were the defending champions from the [[2019 Nigeria Professional Football League|2019 season]] but failed to defend their title after finishing third on the table.<ref name=Covid >{{cite web|url=https://www.goal.com/en-ng/news/coronavirus-2019-20-npfl-season-cancelled-without-promotion/1kfn26pkx04qu1bnce40tqqipa|title=2019–20 NPFL season cancelled without promotion or relegation|website=[[Goal.com]]|date=10 July 2020}}</ref>

The season was initially scheduled to commence on 6 December 2020 but after much delay, the '''League Management Company''' announced that the league would start on 27 December 2020 with all matches played [[Behind closed doors (sports)|behind closed doors]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.aclsports.com/npfl-2020-21-season-to-start-on-december-6/|title=NPFL 2020/21 season to start on December 6|website=aclsports|date=9 November 2020}}</ref>

This season featured the 20 teams from the [[2019–20 Nigeria Professional Football League|2019–20]] season as no clubs were promoted nor relegated as the season was cancelled due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic in Nigeria|COVID-19 pandemic]]<ref name=Covid />

The League Management Company announced that only 13 stadiums across the country were eligible to host league matches as part of a broader club licensing regime, they were certified to have met minimum requirements to host games. Another 11 were recommended for varying degrees of upgrade and repair works before they can be certified<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.vanguardngr.com/2020/11/lmc-certifies-13-stadiums-ahead-of-2020-2021-npfl-season/|location=Lagos, Nigeria|title=LMC certifies 13 stadiums ahead of 2020–21 NPFL season|date=1 November 2020|newspaper=[[Vanguard (Nigeria)|Vanguard]]}}</ref>

This season saw a return of live NPFL matches. Selected matches were viewed live on [https://www.npfl.tv/football/npfl/league/1/home     NPFL TV] and on [[Nigerian Television Authority|NTA]].

On 1 August 2021, [[Akwa United]] won their first NPFL title after a convincing 5–2 home win over [[MFM F.C.|MFM]] with    a game left.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://punchng.com/npfl-akwa-united-beat-mfm-to-win-first-ever-nigeria-premier-league/|title=Akwa United beat MFM to win first ever Nigeria Premier League title|date=1 August 2021|newspaper=[[The Punch]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news|url=https://thenationonlineng.net/impressive-akwa-united-lift-first-npfl-title/|location=Lagos, Nigeria|title=Impressive Akwa United lift first NPFL title|date=2 August 2021|newspaper=[[The Nation (Nigeria)|The Nation]]}}</ref> Akwa United also set a league record with their 18-match unbeaten run, surpassing Enyimba in 2005 and Kano Pillars [[2019–20 Nigeria Professional Football League|2019–20]] 13-match unbeaten sequence.

[[Warri Wolves]], [[Jigawa Golden Stars]], [[Ifeanyiubah F.C.|Ifeanyiubah]] and [[Adamawa United]] were all confirmed relegated at the end of the campaign.

Nasarawa's Silas Nwankwo won the [[Eunisell]] golden boot award as the league topscorer for scoring lesser penalties having been tied with Akwa United's Charles Atshimene.<ref name=Eunisell>{{cite web|url= https://whogoz.ng/silas-nwankwo-wins-the-npfl-eunisell-golden-boot-award/|title=Silas Nwankwo wins the NPFL Eunisell golden boot|work=whogoz.ng|date=6 August 2021}}</ref>
