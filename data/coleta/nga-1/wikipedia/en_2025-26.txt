The '''2025–26 Nigeria Premier Football League''' was the 55th season of Nigeria's top-flight [[association football]] league, the 36th since attaining professionalism and the third under the current name. The season commenced on 22 August 2025 and ended on 24 May 2026<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://thenationonlineng.net/next-npfl-season-to-kick-off-august-22/|location=Lagos, Nigeria|title=
Next NPFL season to kick off August 22|date=14 June 2025|newspaper=[[The Nation (Nigeria)|The Nation]]}}</ref> with a one-week mid-season break from 30 December to 6 January and another two weeks break from 20 April to 2 May.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://en.africatopsports.com/2025/12/12/npfl-confirms-2025-26-season-will-continue-amid-afcon-2025-in-morocco/|title=NPFL confirms 2025–26 season will continue amid AFCON 2025 in Morocco|work=Africa Top Sports|date=12 December 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://sportsvillagesquare.com/2026/04/18/npfl-title-race-survival-battle-take-shape-as-season-enters-final-stretch/|title=NPFL title race, survival battle take shape as season enters final stretch|work=Sports Village Square|accessdate=20 April 2026}}</ref>

After an eventful final matchday, [[Enugu Rangers]] were crowned champions after defeating [[Ikorodu City F.C.|Ikorodu City]] away at [[Lagos]] to remain ahead of [[Rivers United F.C.|Rivers United]] on the table.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/05/breaking-enugu-rangers-crowned-champions-of-npfl-for-record-ninth-time/|title=Enugu Rangers crowned champions of NPFL for record ninth time|work=Vanguard Nigeria|date=24 May 2026}}</ref> Their win was their second in three seasons and their ninth overall, making them joint highest league winners along with [[Enyimba F.C.|Enyimba]].<ref name=Enyimba>{{Cite news|url=https://punchng.com/enugu-rangers-win-ninth-npfl-title-remo-relegated/|title=Enugu Rangers win ninth NPFL title, Remo Stars relegated|date=25 May 2026|newspaper=[[The Punch]]}}</ref>

Defending champions [[Remo Stars]] were relegated along with [[El-Kanemi Warriors]] on the final matchday, as they failed to win their games with Remo Stars relegating on [[goal difference]] against [[Kwara United]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://theeagleonline.com.ng/npfl-rangers-win-ninth-title-remo-stars-others-relegated/|work=The Eagle Online|title=NPFL: Rangers win ninth title, Remo Stars, others relegated|date=25 May 2026}}</ref> Remo Stars became the third defending champion to get relegated the following season, following [[Shooting Stars F.C.|Shooting Stars]] in 1999 and [[Bayelsa United]] in 2010.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://scorenigeria.com.ng/defending-champions-remo-stars-doomed-for-relegation/|title=Defending champions Remo Stars doomed for relegation|work=Score Nigeria|date=24 May 2026}}</ref> They joined [[Wikki Tourists]] and [[Bayelsa United]], who had been previously confirmed relegated two weeks prior.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://dailytrust.com/wikki-tourist-bayelsa-united-relegated-from-npfl/|title=Wikki Tourists, Bayelsa United relegated from NPFL|date=10 May 2026|newspaper=[[Daily Trust]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news|url=https://thenationonlineng.net/how-remo-stars-el-kanemi-bayelsa-united-wikki-tourists-suffered-npfl-relegation/|location=Lagos, Nigeria|title=How Remo Stars, Bayelsa United, Wikki Tourists suffered NPFL relegation|date=25 May 2026|newspaper=[[The Nation (Nigeria)|The Nation]]}}</ref><ref name=Enyimba/>

[[Shooting Stars F.C.|Shooting Stars]] qualified for a continental tournament for the first time since 1999, qualifying for the [[2026–27 CAF Confederation Cup]] ahead of Ikorodu City despite a loss at the final matchday.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://punchng.com/3sc-end-27-year-wait-for-continental-ticket/|title=
3SC end 27-year wait for continental ticket|date=25 May 2026|newspaper=[[The Punch]]}}</ref>

==Teams==
=== Changes from previous season ===
Twenty teams, consisting of the top sixteen teams from the previous season and four promoted teams from the Nigeria National League, are competing in the league. The promoted teams are [[Nigeria National League|NNL]] champions [[Warri Wolves]], [[Wikki Tourists]] who have played previously in the top flight, and newcomers [[Barau FC|Barau]] and [[Kun Khalifat FC|Kun Khalifat]].<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.thisdaylive.com/2025/07/13/warri-wolves-overpower-wikki-to-emerge-nnl-super-8-champions/|title=Warri Wolves overpower Wikki to emerge NNL Super 8 champions|accessdate=21 July 2025|newspaper=[[This Day]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.premiumtimesng.com/news/more-news/806355-wikki-tourist-barau-fc-warri-wolves-kun-khalifat-secure-promotion-to-npfl-2025-26-season.html|title=Wikki Tourist, Barau FC, Warri Wolves, Kun Khalifat secure promotion to NPFL|date=10 July 2025|newspaper=[[Premium Times]]}}</ref>
