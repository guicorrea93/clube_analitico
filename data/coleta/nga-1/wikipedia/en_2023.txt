The '''2022–23 [[Nigeria Professional Football League]]''' was the 52nd season of Nigeria's top-flight [[association football]] league and the 33rd since attaining professionalism. The regular season started on 8 January 2023 and concluded with the playoffs on 11 June 2023.

[[Bendel Insurance F.C.|Bendel Insurance]], [[Doma United F.C.|Doma United]], [[Bayelsa United F.C.|Bayelsa United]] and [[El-Kanemi Warriors F.C.|El-Kanemi Warriors]] were the four promoted clubs from the 2021–22 [[Nigeria National League]], replacing [[Kano Pillars F.C.|Kano Pillars]], [[Katsina United F.C.|Katsina United]], [[MFM F.C.|MFM]] and [[Heartland F.C.|Heartland]].

Following [[Enyimba F.C.|Enyimba]] draw against [[Rivers United F.C.|Rivers United]] on 11 June at the playoffs, Enyimba secured a record ninth NPFL title at the [[Mobolaji Johnson Arena|Onikan Stadium]].<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://punchng.com/enyimba-win-ninth-npfl-title-in-lagos/|title=Enyimba win ninth NPFL title in Lagos|date=12 June 2023|newspaper=[[The Punch]]}}</ref>

== Format ==
The clubs and league organizers decided to start the 2022–23 season on 8 January 2023 instead of 28 December 2022 as previously planned. The league will switch to an abridged format that will see the clubs split into two groups of ten each. The top three clubs from each group will qualify for a championship round, which will be known as the '''Super Six''', to determine the league winners.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://gazettengr.com/just-in-npfl-conducts-draws-fixes-2022-2023-league-season-kickoff-amid-clubs-opposition/|location=Abuja, Nigeria|title=IMC conducts draws, fixes 2022/2023 NPFL season kickoff amid clubs’ opposition|date=28 December 2022|newspaper=[[Peoples Gazette]]}}</ref>
