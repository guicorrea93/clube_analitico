The '''2021–22 [[Nigeria Professional Football League]]''' was the 51st season of Nigeria's top-flight [[association football]] league and the 32nd since attaining professionalism. The season started on 17 December 2021 and was concluded on 17 July 2022.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://thenationonlineng.net/npfl-2021-22-season-starts-december-17/|location=Lagos, Nigeria|title=NPFL 2021–22 season starts December 17|date=25 November 2021|newspaper=[[The Nation (Nigeria)|The Nation]]}}</ref>

This season saw [[Shooting Stars S.C.|Shooting Stars]] returning to the top-flight after a four-year hiatus, likewise [[Niger Tornadoes]] who won the [[2020–21 Nigeria National League|National League]] play-offs. They were also joined by [[Remo Stars]] and [[Gombe United]] after securing promotion from the second division.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.goal.com/en-ng/news/3sc-remo-stars-niger-tornadoes-gombe-united-promoted-to-npfl/|title=3SC, Remo Stars, Niger Tornadoes and Gombe United promoted to NPFL|work=[[Goal.com|Goal]]|first=Shina|last=Oludare|date=12 September 2021|accessdate=25 November 2021}}</ref> [[Akwa United]] were the defending champions.

On 25 June 2022, Rivers United won their first league title with 4 matches to spare.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://punchng.com/rivers-united-npfl-title-win-thrills-wike/|title=Rivers United NPFL title win thrills Wike|date=27 June 2022|newspaper=[[The Punch]]}}</ref>

[[Kano Pillars]] were relegated for the first time in 20 years.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.goal.com/en-ng/news/kano-pillars-four-time-npfl-kings-relegated-after-losing-appeal-against-dakkada-fc/blt2991e354dc7261d7|title=Kano Pillars four time NPFL kings relegated after losing appeal against Dakkada|work=[[Goal.com]]|date=16 July 2022}}</ref> This occurred as a result of the team suffering a six point deduction from the league committee and the club's poor form during the season,<ref name=NFF/><ref name=Kano/> they were joined by [[MFM F.C.|MFM]], [[Heartland F.C.|Heartland]] and [[Katsina United F.C.|Katsina United]]. [[Remo Stars F.C.|Remo Stars]] secured a continental ticket for the first time in the club's history.<ref name=Remo>{{Cite news|url=https://www.premiumtimesng.com/sports/football/543329-npfl-round-up-remo-stars-punch-continental-ticket-as-pillars-heartland-mfm-katsina-are-relegated.html|title=NPFL round up:Remo Stars punch continental ticket as Andy Okpe scores hattrick|date=17 July 2022|newspaper=[[Premium Times]]}}</ref>
