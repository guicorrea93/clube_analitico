Fluminense are the only club to have won both the first and third division 
championships.

América-MG, Bragantino, Brasiliense, Atlético-GO, Criciúma, Joinville, Sampaio Corrêa, Tuna Luso Brasileira and 
União São João are the clubs that have been both second and third division 
champions.
