The '''[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Serie C of the Brazilian Championship 2015]]''' is a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the third division. It is contested by 20 clubs.<ref>{{cite web |title=Série C 2015 |website=Soccerway |access-date=April 1, 2015 |url=https://int.soccerway.com/national/brazil/serie-c/2015/regular-season/r24208/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191023005127/https://int.soccerway.com/national/brazil/serie-c/2015/regular-season/r24208/|url-status=dead|archive-date=October 23, 2019}}</ref><ref>[http://globoesporte.globo.com/futebol/brasileirao-serie-c/ Brasileirão Série C]; Globo Esporte.
