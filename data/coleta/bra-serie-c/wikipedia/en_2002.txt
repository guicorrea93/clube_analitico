The football (soccer) '''Campeonato Brasileiro Série C 2002''', the third level of Brazilian National League, was played from August 24 to December 1, 2002. The competition had 65 clubs and two of them were promoted to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].

[[Brasiliense Futebol Clube|Brasiliense]] finished the final phase group with most points and was declared 2002 Brazilian Série C champions, claiming the promotion to the [[2003 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|2003 Série B]] along with [[Marília Atlético Clube|Marília]], the runners-up.
