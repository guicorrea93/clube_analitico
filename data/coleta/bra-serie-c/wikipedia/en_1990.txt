The '''Campeonato Brasileiro Série C 1990''', known as the '''Terceira Divisão''',<ref name="RSSSF"/> was a [[Association football|football]] series played from 29 September to 9 December 2020. It was the third level of the Brazilian National League. The competition had 30 clubs, and four of them were originally promoted to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].

In 1991, the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] declared that the 1990 Série C was a deficitary tournament, deciding to extinguish it and allowing 64 teams in the [[1991 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]].<ref name="RSSSF"/>
