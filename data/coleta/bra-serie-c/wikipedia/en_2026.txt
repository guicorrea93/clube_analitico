The '''2026 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the third division. It began on 4 April and ended on 24 October 2026.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.itatiaia.com.br/esportes/futebol/futebol-nacional/brasileirao-serie-c/serie-c-2026-tem-datas-de-inicio-e-fim-definidas-pela-cbf-veja|title=Série C 2026 tem datas de início e fim definidas pela CBF; veja|trans-title=2026 Série C have start and end dates set by the CBF; check out|publisher=Rádio Itatiaia|language=pt-BR|date=1 October 2025|accessdate=20 October 2025}}</ref>

Twenty teams competed in the tournament, twelve returning from the [[2025 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|2025]] season, four promoted from the [[2025 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]] ([[Barra Futebol Clube (SC)|Barra]], [[Associação Atlética Internacional (Limeira)|Inter de Limeira]], [[Maranhão Atlético Clube|Maranhão]] and [[Santa Cruz Futebol Clube|Santa Cruz]])<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pe/futebol/brasileirao-serie-d/noticia/2025/09/07/serie-d-veja-os-quatro-times-que-conquistaram-acesso-para-a-terceira-divisao.ghtml|title=Série D: veja os quatro times que conquistaram acesso para a terceira divisão|trans-title=Série D: check out the four teams who achieved promotion to the third division|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=7 September 2025|accessdate=20 October 2025}}</ref> and four relegated from the [[2025 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] ([[Amazonas Futebol Clube|Amazonas]], [[Associação Ferroviária de Esportes|Ferroviária]], [[Paysandu Sport Club|Paysandu]] and [[Volta Redonda FC|Volta Redonda]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pr/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/noticia/2025/11/23/serie-b-veja-quem-subiu-para-a-serie-a-e-quem-caiu-para-a-serie-c.ghtml|title=Série B: veja quem subiu para a Série A e quem caiu para a Série C|trans-title=Série B: check out who went up to the Série A and who went down to the Série B|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=23 November 2025|accessdate=23 November 2025}}</ref>

==Format==
The first stage had one group where each team played the other teams in a single [[round-robin tournament]]. The top eight teams advanced to the second stage, while the bottom two were relegated, as a part of the expansion planned for the division. In the second stage, the teams were divided into two groups of four teams each. Each group was played on a home-and-away round-robin basis. The top two teams of each group were promoted to the Série B, while the group winners advanced to the finals.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pe/futebol/brasileirao-serie-c/noticia/2025/10/01/serie-c-tera-28-clubes-a-partir-de-2028-entenda-mudancas.ghtml|title=Série C terá 28 clubes a partir de 2028; entenda mudanças|trans-title=Série C will have 28 clubs starting from 2028; understand changes|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=1 October 2025|accessdate=20 October 2025}}</ref>

==First stage==
In the first stage, each team played the other nineteen teams in a single [[round-robin tournament]]. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Fewest red cards; 5. Fewest yellow cards; 6. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 16).<ref name="regulations">{{cite web|url=https://stcbfsiteprdimgbrs.blob.core.windows.net/img-site/cdn/REC_Brasileiro_Serie_C_2026_d2552ddc00.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição - Brasileiro Série C 2026 |publisher=CBF |date=23 February 2026 |language=pt}}</ref>

The top eight teams advanced to the second stage, while the bottom two were relegated to [[2027 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D|Série D]].

==Second stage==
In the second stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie was only between two teams); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 20).<ref name="regulations"/>

The top two teams of each group were promoted to the Série B. Group winners advanced to the finals.

==Finals==
The finals were played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis, with the higher-seeded team hosting the second leg. If tied on aggregate, the [[away goals rule]] would not be used, [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played, and the [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] would be used to determine the champions (Regulations Article 21).<ref name="regulations"/>

The finalists were seeded according to their performance in the tournament. The teams were ranked according to overall points. If tied on overall points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Overall wins; 2. Overall goal difference; 3. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 22).<ref name="regulations"/>
