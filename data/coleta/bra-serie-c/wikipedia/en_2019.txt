The '''2019 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the third division. The competition started on 27 April and ended on 6 October 2019.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/201902/20190219183234_169.pdf |title=CAMPEONATO BRASILEIRO DA SÉRIE C TABELA BÁSICA / EDIÇÃO 2019 |date=19 February 2019 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

Twenty teams competed in the tournament, twelve returning from the [[2018 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|2018]] season, four promoted from the [[2018 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]] ([[Ferroviário Atlético Clube (CE)|Ferroviário]], [[Treze Futebol Clube|Treze]], [[Esporte Clube São José|São José]] and [[Sociedade Imperatriz de Desportos|Imperatriz]]),<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/serie-d-2018-tres-equipes-confirmam-acesso-a-serie-c-2019 |title=Série D 2018: três equipes confirmam acesso à Série C 2019 |date=9 July 2018 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/competicoes/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/2018/259 |title=Nos pênaltis, Ferroviário-CE conquista acesso à Série C de 2019 |date=9 July 2018 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180726233927/https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/competicoes/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/2018/259 |archive-date=26 July 2018 |url-status=dead}}</ref> and four relegated from the [[2018 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] ([[Paysandu Sport Club|Paysandu]], [[Sampaio Corrêa Futebol Clube|Sampaio Corrêa]], [[Esporte Clube Juventude|Juventude]] and [[Boa Esporte Clube|Boa Esporte]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/csa-e-avai-conquistam-acesso-a-serie-a-paysandu-sofre-o-rebaixamento |title=CSA e Avaí conquistam acesso à Série A de 2019 |date=24 November 2018 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

[[Associação Desportiva Confiança|Confiança]], [[Esporte Clube Juventude|Juventude]], [[Clube Náutico Capibaribe|Náutico]] and [[Sampaio Corrêa Futebol Clube|Sampaio Corrêa]] qualified for the semi-finals and were promoted to the [[2020 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/esportes/noticia/2019-09/juventude-nautico-confianca-e-sampaio-correa-sobem-para-serie-b |title=Juventude, Náutico, Confiança e Sampaio Corrêa sobem para a Série B |date=10 September 2019 |publisher=Agência Brasil-EBC |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

[[Clube Náutico Capibaribe|Náutico]] defeated [[Sampaio Corrêa Futebol Clube|Sampaio Corrêa]] 5–3 on aggregate in the finals to win their first title.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/ma/futebol/brasileirao-serie-c/jogo/06-10-2019/sampaio-correia-nautico.ghtml |title=Náutico empata com o Sampaio Corrêa e é campeão da Série C |date=6 October 2019 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

==Group stage==
In the group stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie is only between two teams). If tied on aggregate, the away goals rule would be used (except if both teams shared the same stadium); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 15).<ref name="regulations">{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/201903/20190319165949_951.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição - Campeonato Brasileiro da Série C 2019 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

The top four teams of each group advanced to the quarter-finals of the knockout stages.

==Final Stages==
Starting from the quarter-finals, the teams played a [[single-elimination tournament]] with the following rules:<ref name="regulations"/>
*Each tie was played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis, with the higher-seeded team hosting the second leg.
*If tied on aggregate, the [[away goals rule]] would not be used, [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played, and the [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] would be used to determine the winner (Regulations Article 16).

Starting from the semi-finals, the teams were seeded according to their performance in the tournament. The teams were ranked according to overall points. If tied on overall points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Overall wins; 2. Overall goal difference; 3. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 17).<ref name="regulations"/>
