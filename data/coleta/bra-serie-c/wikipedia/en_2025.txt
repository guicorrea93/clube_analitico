The '''2025 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the third division. It began on 12 April and ended on 25 October 2025.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/futebol/noticia/2024/11/12/calendario-do-futebol-brasileiro-em-2025-veja-as-datas.ghtml|title=Calendário do futebol brasileiro em 2025: veja as datas|trans-title=2025 Brazilian football calendar: see dates|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=12 November 2024|access-date=14 November 2024}}</ref>

Twenty teams competed in the tournament, twelve returning from the [[2024 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|2024]] season, four promoted from the [[2024 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]] ([[Anápolis Futebol Clube|Anápolis]], [[Associação Olímpica de Itabaiana|Itabaiana]], [[Maringá Futebol Clube|Maringá]] and [[Retrô Futebol Clube Brasil|Retrô]])<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pi/futebol/brasileirao-serie-d/noticia/2024/09/01/serie-c-2025-definidos-os-quatro-clubes-da-serie-d-que-sobem-de-divisao-confira.ghtml|title=Série C 2025: definidos os quatro clubes da Série D que sobem de divisão; confira|trans-title=2025 Série C: defined the four Série D clubs who are promoted; check out|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=1 September 2024|access-date=21 October 2024}}</ref> and four relegated from the [[2024 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] ([[Brusque Futebol Clube|Brusque]], [[Ituano FC|Ituano]], [[Guarani FC|Guarani]] and [[Associação Atlética Ponte Preta|Ponte Preta]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pi/futebol/brasileirao-serie-c/noticia/2024/11/17/ponte-preta-ituano-brusque-e-guarani-veja-todas-equipes-confirmadas-na-serie-c-em-2025.ghtml|title=Ponte Preta, Ituano, Brusque e Guarani: veja todas equipes confirmadas na Série C em 2025|trans-title=Ponte Preta, Ituano, Brusque and Guarani: check out all teams confirmed in the 2025 Série C|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=17 November 2024|access-date=18 November 2024}}</ref>

==Format==
The first stage had one group where each team played the other teams in a single [[round-robin tournament]]. The top eight teams advanced to the second stage, while the bottom four were relegated. In the second stage, the teams were divided into two groups of four teams each. Each group was played on a home-and-away round-robin basis. The top two teams of each group were promoted to the Série B, while the group winners advanced to the finals.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pb/futebol/brasileirao-serie-c/noticia/serie-c-muda-formato-para-2022-e-tera-turno-unico-com-os-20-times-se-enfrentando-na-1a-fase.ghtml |title=Série C muda formato para 2022 e terá turno único, com os 20 times se enfrentando na 1ª fase |publisher=Globo |language=pt |date=4 February 2022}}</ref>

==First stage==
In the first stage, each team played the other nineteen teams in a single [[round-robin tournament]]. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Fewest red cards; 5. Fewest yellow cards; 6. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 16).<ref name="regulations">{{cite web|url=https://objectstorage.sa-saopaulo-1.oraclecloud.com/n/grsa9ybqykir/b/portalcbf/o/REC%20-%20Brasileiro%20S%C3%A9rie%20C%202025.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição - Brasileiro Série C 2025 |publisher=CBF |date=27 February 2025 |language=pt}}</ref>

The top eight teams advanced to the second stage, while the bottom four were relegated to [[2026 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D|Série D]].

==Second stage==
In the second stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie was only between two teams); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 20).<ref name="regulations"/>

The top two teams of each group were promoted to the Série B. Group winners advanced to the finals.

==Finals==
The finals were played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis, with the higher-seeded team hosting the second leg. If tied on aggregate, the [[away goals rule]] would not be used, [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played, and the [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] would be used to determine the champions (Regulations Article 21).<ref name="regulations"/>

The finalists were seeded according to their performance in the tournament. The teams were ranked according to overall points. If tied on overall points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Overall wins; 2. Overall goal difference; 3. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 22).<ref name="regulations"/>
