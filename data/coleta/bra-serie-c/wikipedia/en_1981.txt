The '''1981 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C''', officially, the '''Taça de Bronze 1981''', was the first edition of the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]].The championship was performed by 24 clubs. The first phase was disputed by a two-legged knockout tournament, in which the winners qualified to the Second phase, that was disputed in the same way. The winners qualified to the second phase, in which the six teams were divided into two groups of tree teams, that played against the teams of their own groups twice.the first-placed teams of each group qualified to the finals. The winner qualified to the [[1982 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Taça de Prata of the following year]].
