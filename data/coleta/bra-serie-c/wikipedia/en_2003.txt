The football (soccer) '''Campeonato Brasileiro Série C 2003''', the third level of Brazilian National League, was played from September 17 to December 7, 2003. The competition had 95 clubs<ref>the withdrawal of [[Sociedade Esportiva Guaxupé|Guaxupé]] and [[Parnahyba Sport Club|Parnahyba]] reduced this number to 93.</ref> and two of them were promoted to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].

[[Ituano Futebol Clube|Ituano]] finished the final phase group with most points and was declared 2003 Brazilian Série C champions, claiming the promotion to the [[2004 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|2004 Série B]] along with [[Esporte Clube Santo André|Santo André]], the runners-up.
