The '''Campeonato Brasileiro Série C 1994''' was a football (soccer) series played from September 18 to December 9, 1994. It was the third level of the Brazilian National League. The competition had 42 clubs, and two of them were originally promoted to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].
