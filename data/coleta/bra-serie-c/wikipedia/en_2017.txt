The 2017 '''[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]]''', the third level of the Brazilian League, was contested by 20 clubs. The competition started on 14 May and ended on 21 October 2017.

[[Centro Sportivo Alagoano|CSA]], [[Fortaleza Esporte Clube|Fortaleza]], [[Sampaio Corrêa Futebol Clube|Sampaio Corrêa]] and [[Esporte Clube São Bento|São Bento]] qualified for the semi-finals and were promoted to the [[2018 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]].

[[Centro Sportivo Alagoano|CSA]] won the title after defeating [[Fortaleza Esporte Clube|Fortaleza]] in the final.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/csa-fatura-titulo-inedito-da-serie-c-do-brasileiro |title=CSA fatura título inédito da Série C do Brasileiro |language=pt |publisher=CBF |date=21 October 2017}}</ref>

==Group stage==
In the group stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie is only between two teams).  If tied on aggregate, the away goals rule would be used (except if both teams share the same stadium); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 14).<ref name="regulations">{{cite web |url=http://cdn.cbf.com.br/content/201703/20170313190033_0.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição - Campeonato Brasileiro da Série C 2017 |publisher=CBF |access-date=2017-09-12 |archive-date=2017-09-18 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170918230635/http://cdn.cbf.com.br/content/201703/20170313190033_0.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Final Stage==
In the final stage, each tie was played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis. If tied on aggregate, the [[away goals rule]] would be used. If still tied, [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played, and the [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] would be used to determine the winner (Regulations Article 15).<ref name="regulations"/>
