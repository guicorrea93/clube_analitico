The '''2022 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the third division. It began on 9 April and ended on 8 October 2022.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/202202/20220209104735_788.pdf |title=CAMPEONATO BRASILEIRO SÉRIE C TABELA BÁSICA / EDIÇÃO 2022 |publisher=CBF |language=pt|date=8 February 2022}}</ref>

Twenty teams competed in the tournament, twelve returning from the [[2021 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|2021]] season, four promoted from the [[2021 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]] ([[ABC Futebol Clube|ABC]], [[Associação Atlética Aparecidense|Aparecidense]], [[FC Atlético Cearense|Atlético Cearense]] and [[Campinense Clube|Campinense]])<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/abc-aparecidense-atletico-ce-e-campinense-garantem-acesso-a-serie-c |title=ABC, Aparecidense, Atlético-CE e Campinense garantem acesso à Série C |date=17 October 2021 |publisher=CBF |language=pt}}</ref> and four relegated from the [[2021 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] ([[Grêmio Esportivo Brasil|Brasil de Pelotas]], [[Associação Desportiva Confiança|Confiança]], [[Clube do Remo|Remo]] and [[Esporte Clube Vitória|Vitória]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/remo-empata-vitoria-perde-e-as-duas-equipes-vao-disputar-a-serie-c-em|title=Remo empata; Vitória perde e as duas equipes vão disputar a Série C em 2022; Londrina vence e permanece na Série B|trans-title=Remo draw; Vitória lose and both teams will play the Série C in 2022; Londrina win and stay in the Série B|publisher=CBF|language=pt-BR|date=28 November 2021|accessdate=29 November 2021}}</ref>

In the finals, [[Mirassol Futebol Clube|Mirassol]] won their first title after defeating [[ABC Futebol Clube|ABC]] 2–0 on aggregate.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/mirassol-conquista-titulo-inedito-da-serie-c |title=Mirassol coroa campanha da Série C com título inédito da competição |publisher=CBF |language=pt |date=8 October 2022}}</ref>

Mirassol, ABC, [[Botafogo Futebol Clube (SP)|Botafogo-SP]] and [[Esporte Clube Vitória|Vitória]] were promoted to the [[2023 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.umdoisesportes.com.br/futebol/mirassol-botafogo-abc-e-vitoria-garantem-acesso-a-serie-b/ |title=Mirassol, Botafogo, ABC e Vitória garantem acesso à Série B |publisher=UmDois Esportes |language=pt |date=25 September 2022}}</ref> while [[FC Atlético Cearense|Atlético Cearense]], [[Grêmio Esportivo Brasil|Brasil de Pelotas]], [[Campinense Clube|Campinense]] and [[Ferroviário Atlético Clube (CE)|Ferroviário]] were relegated to the [[2023 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/conheca-os-grupos-da-segunda-fase-da-serie-c |title=Série C: conheça os classificados, os rebaixados e os grupos da segunda fase |publisher=CBF |language=pt |date=13 August 2022}}</ref>

==Format changes==
Starting from this edition, the first stage had only one group where each team played the other teams in a single [[round-robin tournament]] instead of two groups played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The top eight teams advanced to the second stage, while the bottom four were relegated.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pb/futebol/brasileirao-serie-c/noticia/serie-c-muda-formato-para-2022-e-tera-turno-unico-com-os-20-times-se-enfrentando-na-1a-fase.ghtml |title=Série C muda formato para 2022 e terá turno único, com os 20 times se enfrentando na 1ª fase |publisher=Globo |language=pt|date=4 February 2022}}</ref>

==Format==
In the first stage, each team played on a single [[round-robin tournament]] against the other clubs. The top eight teams advanced to the second stage. In the second stage, the teams were divided into two groups of four teams each. Each group was played on a home-and-away round-robin basis. The top two teams of each group were promoted to the Série B, while the group winners advanced to the finals.

==First stage==
In the first stage, each team played the other nineteen teams in a single [[round-robin tournament]]. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Fewest red cards; 5. Fewest yellow cards; 6. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 16).<ref name="regulations">{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/202202/20220209104343_494.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição - Campeonato Brasileiro Série C - 2022 |publisher=CBF |date=8 February 2022 |language=pt}}</ref>

The top eight teams advanced to the second stage, while the bottom four were relegated to [[2023 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D|Série D]].

==Second stage==
In the second stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie was only between two teams); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 20).<ref name="regulations"/>

The top two teams of each group were promoted to the Série B. Group winners advanced to the finals.

==Finals==
The finals were played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis, with the higher-seeded team hosting the second leg. If tied on aggregate, the [[away goals rule]] would not be used, [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played, and the [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] would be used to determine the champions (Regulations Article 21).<ref name="regulations"/>

The finalists were seeded according to their performance in the tournament. The teams were ranked according to overall points. If tied on overall points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Overall wins; 2. Overall goal difference; 3. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 22).<ref name="regulations"/>
