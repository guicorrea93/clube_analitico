In 2009, the '''[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]]''', the third level of the Brazilian League, was contested by 20 clubs from May 24 to September 19, 2009. In an attempt to increase profits and interest on the competition, [[Brazilian Football Confederation|CBF]] decided to reduce the number of participating teams from 64 to 20 this season and establish regular membership, akin to Série A and Série B.

In the finals, [[América Futebol Clube (MG)|América Mineiro]], a traditional club from the city of [[Belo Horizonte]], defeated [[Agremiação Sportiva Arapiraquense|ASA]] after winning away and at home. Top four clubes, the ones which qualified to the quarterfinals, ascended to the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] to be contested in [[2010 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|2010]]: [[América Futebol Clube (MG)|América Mineiro]], [[Agremiação Sportiva Arapiraquense|ASA]], [[Associação Desportiva Recreativa Cultural Icasa|Icasa]] and [[Guaratinguetá Futebol Ltda|Guaratinguetá]]. Meanwhile, the bottom four clubs, the ones that finished in last place of each group, were relegated to [[2010 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D|Série D next season]]: [[Sampaio Corrêa Futebol Clube|Sampaio Corrêa]], [[Associação Desportiva Confiança|Confiança]], [[Mixto Esporte Clube|Mixto]] and [[Clube Náutico Marcílio Dias|Marcílio Dias]].

==Format==
* '''First Stage: '''The 20 teams are divided in four groups of 5, playing within them in a double round-robin format. The two best ranked in each group advance towards next stage. The last placed team in each group is relegated to [[2010 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D|Série D 2010]]
* '''Quarterfinals:''' Eight qualified teams play in two-leg format, home and away. Winners qualify to semifinals and are promoted to [[2010 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B 2010]].
* '''Semifinals:''' Quarterfinals winners play in two-leg format, home and away. Winners qualify to the Finals.
* '''Finals:''' Semifinals winners play in two-leg format, home and away. Winners are declared champions.
