The '''2021 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the third division. It began on 29 May and ended on 20 November 2021.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/cbf-divulga-tabela-da-serie-c-2021 |title=CBF divulga tabela da Série C 2021 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese |date=26 March 2021}}</ref>

Twenty teams competed in the tournament, twelve returning from the [[2020 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|2020]] season, four promoted from the [[2020 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]] ([[Associação Atlética de Altos|Altos]], [[Floresta Esporte Clube|Floresta]], [[Mirassol Futebol Clube|Mirassol]] and [[Grêmio Novorizontino|Novorizontino]])<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/altos-pi-floresta-ce-e-novorizontino-sp-sobem-para-a-serie-c |title=Altos-PI, Floresta-CE, Mirassol-SP e Novorizontino-SP sobem para a Série C do Brasileiro |date=10 January 2021 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref> and four relegated from the [[2020 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] ([[Botafogo Futebol Clube (SP)|Botafogo-SP]], [[Figueirense FC|Figueirense]], [[Oeste Futebol Clube|Oeste]] and [[Paraná Clube|Paraná]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.correiodopovo.com.br/esportes/vit%C3%B3ria-vence-escapa-do-rebaixamento-e-confirma-quedas-de-figueirense-botafogo-oeste-e-paran%C3%A1-1.561238 |title=Vitória vence, escapa do rebaixamento e confirma quedas de Figueirense, Botafogo, Oeste e Paraná |publisher=Correio do Povo |language=Portuguese |date=26 January 2021}}</ref>

[[Criciúma Esporte Clube|Criciúma]], [[Ituano FC|Ituano]], [[Grêmio Novorizontino|Novorizontino]] and [[Tombense Futebol Clube|Tombense]] were promoted to the [[2022 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/am/futebol/brasileirao-serie-c/noticia/serie-c-novorizontino-se-junta-a-tombense-ituano-e-criciuma-entre-os-classificados-para-a-serie-b.ghtml |title=Série C: Novorizontino se junta a Tombense, Ituano e Criciúma entre os classificados para a Série B |date=7 November 2021 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

[[Ituano FC|Ituano]] defeated [[Tombense Futebol Clube|Tombense]] 4–1 on aggregate in the finals to win their second title.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/competicoes/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/2021/206 |title=Ituano-SP vence Tombense-MG e conquista a Série C do Brasileiro 2021 |date=20 November 2021 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

==Format==
In the first stage, the teams were divided into two groups of ten teams each. Each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The top four teams of each group advanced to the second stage.

In the second stage, the teams were divided into two groups of four teams each. Each group was played on a home-and-away round-robin basis. The top two teams of each group were promoted to the Série B, while the group winners qualified for the finals.

==First stage==
In the first stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie is only between two teams); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 15).<ref name="regulations">{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/202103/20210330193020_383.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição - Campeonato Brasileiro Série C - 2021 |publisher=CBF |date=30 March 2021 |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

The top four teams of each group advanced to the second stage.

==Second stage==
In the second stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the criteria to determine the ranking would be the same as used in the first stage (Regulations Article 19).<ref name="regulations"/>

The top two teams of each group were promoted to the Série B. Group winners advanced to the finals.

==Finals==
The finals were played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis, with the higher-seeded team hosting the second leg. If tied on aggregate, the [[away goals rule]] would not be used, [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played, and the [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] would be used to determine the champions (Regulations Article 20).<ref name="regulations"/>

The finalists were seeded according to their performance in the tournament. The teams were ranked according to overall points. If tied on overall points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Overall wins; 2. Overall goal difference; 3. Overall goals scored; 4. Fewest red cards in the tournament; 5. Fewest yellow cards in the tournament; 6. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 21).<ref name="regulations"/>
