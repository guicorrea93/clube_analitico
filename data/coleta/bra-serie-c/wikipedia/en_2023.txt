The 2023 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]] was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the third division. It began on 2 May and ended on 22 October 2023.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/index/brasileirao-sera-paralisado-em-datas-fifa-e-copa-do-brasil-tera-finais |title=Calendário 2023: Brasileirão será paralisado em Datas FIFA pela primeira vez |publisher=CBF |language=pt|date=21 September 2022}}</ref>

Twenty teams competed in the tournament, twelve returning from the [[2022 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|2022]] season, four promoted from the [[2022 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]] ([[Amazonas Futebol Clube|Amazonas]], [[América Futebol Clube (RN)|América de Natal]], [[Pouso Alegre Futebol Clube|Pouso Alegre]] and [[São Bernardo Futebol Clube|São Bernardo]])<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/america-rn-amazonas-pouso-alegre-e-sao-bernardo-sobem-para-a-serie-c |title=América-RN, Amazonas, Pouso Alegre e São Bernardo sobem para a Série C |date=29 August 2022 |publisher=CBF |language=pt}}</ref> and four relegated from the [[2022 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] ([[Brusque Futebol Clube|Brusque]], [[Centro Sportivo Alagoano|CSA]], [[Clube Náutico Capibaribe|Náutico]] and [[Operário Ferroviário Esporte Clube|Operário Ferroviário]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.gazetaweb.com/noticias/esportes/alagoano/com-rebaixamento-decretado-csa-conhece-seus-adversarios-na-serie-c-de-2023/ |title=Com rebaixamento decretado, CSA conhece seus adversários na Série C de 2023 |date=7 November 2022 |publisher=Gazetaweb |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

Amazonas, Brusque, [[Operário Ferroviário Esporte Clube|Operário Ferroviário]] and [[Paysandu Sport Club|Paysandu]] were promoted to the [[2024 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/operario-vence-e-sobe-para-a-serie-b |title=Operário-PR vence e conquista o acesso à Série B |date=8 October 2023 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref> while [[Associação Atlética de Altos|Altos]], [[América Futebol Clube (RN)|América de Natal]], [[Manaus Futebol Clube|Manaus]], and [[Pouso Alegre Futebol Clube|Pouso Alegre]] were relegated to the [[2024 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/serie-c-sao-bernardo-e-sao-jose-garantem-ultimas-vagas-para-disputa-d |title=Série C: São Bernardo e São José garantem últimas vagas para disputa do acesso |date=26 August 2023 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

In the finals, Amazonas won their first title after defeating Brusque 2–1 on aggregate.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/amazonas-e-campeao-da-serie-c-do-brasileirao |title=Amazonas é campeão da Série C do Brasileirão |publisher=CBF |language=pt |date=22 October 2023}}</ref>

==Format==
The first stage had one group where each team played the other teams in a single [[round-robin tournament]]. The top eight teams advanced to the second stage, while the bottom four were relegated. In the second stage, the teams were divided into two groups of four teams each. Each group was played on a home-and-away round-robin basis. The top two teams of each group were promoted to the Série B, while the group winners advanced to the finals.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pb/futebol/brasileirao-serie-c/noticia/serie-c-muda-formato-para-2022-e-tera-turno-unico-com-os-20-times-se-enfrentando-na-1a-fase.ghtml |title=Série C muda formato para 2022 e terá turno único, com os 20 times se enfrentando na 1ª fase |publisher=Globo |language=pt|date=4 February 2022}}</ref>

==First stage==
In the first stage, each team played the other nineteen teams in a single [[round-robin tournament]]. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Fewest red cards; 5. Fewest yellow cards; 6. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 16).<ref name="regulations">{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/202303/20230303201552_960.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição - Brasileiro Série C 2023 |publisher=CBF |date=3 March 2023 |language=pt}}</ref>

The top eight teams advanced to the second stage, while the bottom four were relegated to [[2024 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D|Série D]].

==Second stage==
In the second stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie was only between two teams); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 20).<ref name="regulations"/>

The top two teams of each group were promoted to the Série B. Group winners advanced to the finals.

==Finals==
The finals were played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis, with the higher-seeded team hosting the second leg. If tied on aggregate, the [[away goals rule]] would not be used, [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played, and the [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] would be used to determine the champions (Regulations Article 21).<ref name="regulations"/>

The finalists were seeded according to their performance in the tournament. The teams were ranked according to overall points. If tied on overall points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Overall wins; 2. Overall goal difference; 3. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 22).<ref name="regulations"/>
