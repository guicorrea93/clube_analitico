The '''2018 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the third division. The competition started on 14 April and ended on 22 September 2018.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cdn.cbf.com.br/content/201802/20180207171521_0.pdf |title=CAMPEONATO BRASILEIRO DA SÉRIE C TABELA BÁSICA / EDIÇÃO 2018 |date=7 February 2018 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

Twenty teams competed in the tournament, twelve returning from the [[2017 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|2017]] season, four promoted from the [[2017 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]] ([[Operário Ferroviário Esporte Clube|Operário Ferroviário]], [[Globo Futebol Clube|Globo]], [[Atlético Acreano]] and [[Sociedade Desportiva Juazeirense|Juazeirense]]),<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/conheca-os-semifinalistas-da-serie-d-2017 |title=Conheça os semifinalistas da Série D 2017 |date=15 August 2017 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref> and four relegated from the [[2017 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] ([[Luverdense Esporte Clube|Luverdense]], [[Santa Cruz Futebol Clube|Santa Cruz]], [[ABC Futebol Clube|ABC]] and [[Clube Náutico Capibaribe|Náutico]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/sp/campinas-e-regiao/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/noticia/empate-dramatico-salva-o-guarani-rebaixa-o-luverdense-e-fecha-z-4-da-serie-b.ghtml |title=Empate dramático salva o Guarani, rebaixa o Luverdense e fecha Z-4 da Série B |date=17 November 2017 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

[[Botafogo Futebol Clube (SP)|Botafogo-SP]], [[Clube Atlético Bragantino|Bragantino]], [[Cuiabá Esporte Clube|Cuiabá]] and [[Operário Ferroviário Esporte Clube|Operário Ferroviário]] qualified for the semi-finals and were promoted to the 2019 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/os-quatro-times-que-subiram-para-a-serie-b-de-2019 |title=Série B de 2019: os quatro times que subiram por meio da Série C |date=28 August 2018 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

[[Operário Ferroviário Esporte Clube|Operário Ferroviário]] won the title after defeating [[Cuiabá Esporte Clube|Cuiabá]] in the final.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/pr/futebol/times/operario-pr/noticia/operario-pr-vence-o-cuiaba-consolida-ascensao-e-coroa-campanha-com-titulo-da-serie-c.ghtml |title=Operário-PR vence o Cuiabá, consolida ascensão e coroa campanha com título da Série C |date=22 September 2018 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

==Group stage==
In the group stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie is only between two teams). If tied on aggregate, the away goals rule would be used (except if both teams shared the same stadium); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 15).<ref name="regulations">{{cite web|url=https://cdn.cbf.com.br/content/201802/20180209174302_0.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição - Campeonato Brasileiro da Série C 2018 |publisher=CBF}}</ref>

The top four teams of each group advanced to the quarter-finals of the knockout stages.

==Final Stages==
Starting from the quarter-finals, the teams played a [[single-elimination tournament]] with the following rules:<ref name="regulations"/>
*Each tie was played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis, with the higher-seeded team hosting the second leg (Regulations Article 17).
*If tied on aggregate, the [[away goals rule]] would not be used, [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played, and the [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] was used to determine the winner (Regulations Article 16).

Starting from the semi-finals, the teams were seeded according to their performance in the tournament. The teams were ranked according to overall points. If tied on overall points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Overall wins; 2. Overall goal difference; 3. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 17).<ref name="regulations"/>
