The football (soccer) '''Campeonato Brasileiro Série C 2004''', the third tier of Brazilian National League, was played from August 1 to November 28, 2004. The competition had 64 clubs and two of them were promoted to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].

[[União Agrícola Barbarense Futebol Clube|União Barbarense]] finished the final phase group with most points and was declared 2004 Brazilian Série C champions, claiming the promotion to the [[2005 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|2005 Série B]] along with [[Sociedade Esportiva do Gama|Gama]], the runners-up.
