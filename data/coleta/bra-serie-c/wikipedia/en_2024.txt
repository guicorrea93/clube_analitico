The '''2024 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the third division. It began on 20 April and ended on 19 October 2024.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/index/campeonato-brasileiro-2024-tera-pela-primeira-vez-jogo-de-abertura |title=Campeonato Brasileiro 2024 terá pela primeira vez jogo de abertura |publisher=CBF |language=pt|date=31 October 2023}}</ref>

Twenty teams competed in the tournament, twelve returning from the [[2023 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|2023]] season, four promoted from the [[2023 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]] ([[Athletic Club (MG)|Athletic]], [[Sociedade Esportiva e Recreativa Caxias do Sul|Caxias]], [[Associação Ferroviária de Esportes|Ferroviária]] and [[Ferroviário Atlético Clube (CE)|Ferroviário]])<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/serie-d-conhece-os-quatro-semifinalistas-que-conseguiram-acesso-a-seri |title=Série D conhece os quatro semifinalistas que conseguiram acesso à Série C do Brasileirão |date=3 September 2023 |publisher=CBF |language=pt}}</ref> and four relegated from the [[2023 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] ([[ABC Futebol Clube|ABC]], [[Londrina Esporte Clube|Londrina]], [[Sampaio Corrêa Futebol Clube|Sampaio Corrêa]] and [[Tombense Futebol Clube|Tombense]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/esportes/tombense-sampaio-correa-londrina-e-abc-sao-rebaixados-a-serie-c/ |title=Tombense, Sampaio Corrêa, Londrina e ABC são rebaixados à Série C |date=25 November 2023 |publisher=CNN Brasil |language=pt}}</ref>

[[Athletic Club (MG)|Athletic]], [[Associação Ferroviária de Esportes|Ferroviária]], [[Clube do Remo|Remo]] and [[Volta Redonda Futebol Clube|Volta Redonda]] were promoted to the [[2025 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/detalhes/campeonato-brasileiro/athletic-e-ferroviaria-conseguem-acesso-a-serie-b |title=Athletic e Ferroviária conseguem acesso à Série B |date=5 October 2024 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref> while [[Associação Atlética Aparecidense|Aparecidense]], [[Ferroviário Atlético Clube (CE)|Ferroviário]], [[Sampaio Corrêa Futebol Clube|Sampaio Corrêa]], and [[Esporte Clube São José|São José]] were relegated to the [[2025 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/detalhes/jogosdehoje-campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/confira-como-ficaram-os-grupos-da-segunda-fase-da-serie-c |title=Confira como ficaram os grupos da segunda fase da Série C |date=24 August 2024 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

In the finals, [[Volta Redonda Futebol Clube|Volta Redonda]] won their first title after defeating [[Athletic Club (MG)|Athletic]] 3–0 on aggregate.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/detalhes/jogosdehoje-campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/volta-redonda-e-campeao-da-serie-c |title=Volta Redonda é campeão da Série C |publisher=CBF |language=pt |date=19 October 2024}}</ref>

==Format==
The first stage had one group where each team played the other teams in a single [[round-robin tournament]]. The top eight teams advanced to the second stage, while the bottom four were relegated. In the second stage, the teams were divided into two groups of four teams each. Each group was played on a home-and-away round-robin basis. The top two teams of each group were promoted to the Série B, while the group winners advanced to the finals.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pb/futebol/brasileirao-serie-c/noticia/serie-c-muda-formato-para-2022-e-tera-turno-unico-com-os-20-times-se-enfrentando-na-1a-fase.ghtml |title=Série C muda formato para 2022 e terá turno único, com os 20 times se enfrentando na 1ª fase |publisher=Globo |language=pt |date=4 February 2022}}</ref>

==First stage==
In the first stage, each team played the other nineteen teams in a single [[round-robin tournament]]. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Fewest red cards; 5. Fewest yellow cards; 6. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 16).<ref name="regulations">{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/202403/20240307214049_644.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição - Brasileiro Série C 2024 |publisher=CBF |date=7 March 2024 |language=pt}}</ref>

The top eight teams advanced to the second stage, while the bottom four were relegated to [[2025 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D|Série D]].

==Second stage==
In the second stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie was only between two teams); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 20).<ref name="regulations"/>

The top two teams of each group were promoted to the Série B. Group winners advanced to the finals.

==Finals==
The finals were played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis, with the higher-seeded team hosting the second leg. If tied on aggregate, the [[away goals rule]] would not be used, [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played, and the [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] would be used to determine the champions (Regulations Article 21).<ref name="regulations"/>

The finalists were seeded according to their performance in the tournament. The teams were ranked according to overall points. If tied on overall points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Overall wins; 2. Overall goal difference; 3. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 22).<ref name="regulations"/>
