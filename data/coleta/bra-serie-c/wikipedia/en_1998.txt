The football (soccer) '''Campeonato Brasileiro Série C 1998''', the third level of Brazilian National League, was played from August 8 to December 6, 1998. The competition had 66 clubs<ref group=n>Itumbiara was originally placed in Group 5. After playing three matches, Itumbiara was excluded from the competition for not paying referee fees.</ref> and two of them were promoted to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].
