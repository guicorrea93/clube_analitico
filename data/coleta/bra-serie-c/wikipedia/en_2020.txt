The '''2020 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the third division. The competition was originally scheduled to begin on 3 May and end on 8 November,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro/cbf-publica-calendario-de-2020-com-datas-fifas-livres-e-16-datas-para |title=CBF publica calendário de 2020 com Datas FIFA livres |date=3 October 2019 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref> however due to [[COVID-19 pandemic in Brazil|COVID-19 pandemic]] the tournament was rescheduled for 8 August 2020 – 30 January 2021.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/index/cbf-publica-calendario-revisado-da-temporada-2020 |title=CBF publica calendário revisado da temporada 2020 |date=9 July 2020 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

Twenty teams competed in the tournament, twelve returning from the [[2019 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|2019]] season, four promoted from the [[2019 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]] ([[Brusque Futebol Clube|Brusque]], [[Manaus Futebol Clube|Manaus]], [[Ituano FC|Ituano]] and [[Esporte Clube Jacuipense|Jacuipense]])<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/am/futebol/brasileirao-serie-d/noticia/brusque-manaus-jacuipense-ba-e-ituano-garantem-acesso-a-serie-c-confira-os-duelos-das-semis.ghtml |title=Brusque, Manaus, Jacuipense-BA e Ituano garantem acesso à Série C; confira os duelos das semis |date=21 July 2019 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref> and four relegated from the [[2019 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] ([[Londrina Esporte Clube|Londrina]], [[Esporte Clube São Bento|São Bento]], [[Criciúma Esporte Clube|Criciúma]] and [[Vila Nova Futebol Clube|Vila Nova]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/figueirense-empata-com-o-crb-al-e-garante-permanencia-na-serie-b |title=Figueirense empata com o CRB-AL e garante permanência na Série B |date=22 November 2019 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

The matches Treze v Imperatriz, scheduled for 9 August 2020 (Group A 1st round), and Imperatriz v Jacuipense, scheduled for 15 August 2020 (Group A 2nd round), were postponed after 14 Imperatriz players tested positive for COVID-19.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/nota-oficial-adiamento-de-partida-da-serie-c |title=Nota oficial - adiamento de partida da Série C do Campeonato Brasileiro |date=9 August 2020 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/nota-oficial-adiamento-de-partida-da-serie-c-campeonato-brasileiro |title=Nota oficial - adiamento de partida da Série C do Campeonato Brasileiro |date=13 August 2020 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

[[Brusque Futebol Clube|Brusque]], [[Londrina Esporte Clube|Londrina]], [[Clube do Remo|Remo]] and [[Vila Nova Futebol Clube|Vila Nova]] were promoted to the [[2021 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/pe/futebol/brasileirao-serie-c/noticia/serie-c-vila-nova-se-junta-a-brusque-remo-e-londrina-entre-os-classificados-para-a-2adivisao.ghtml |title=Série C: Vila Nova se junta a Brusque, Remo e Londrina entre os classificados para a 2ª divisão |date=17 January 2021 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

[[Vila Nova Futebol Clube|Vila Nova]] defeated [[Clube do Remo|Remo]] 8–3 on aggregate in the finals to win their third title.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/competicoes/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/2020/206 |title=Vila Nova volta a vencer o Remo e conquista a Série C 2020 |date=30 January 2021 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

==Format changes==
Starting from this edition, the quarter-finals and semi-finals were replaced by a second group stage played by eight teams. They were divided into two groups of four teams each. The top two teams of each group were promoted to the Série B, while the group winners qualified for the finals.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/pe/futebol/noticia/presidente-do-santa-revela-mudanca-no-formato-da-serie-c.ghtml |title=Com grupos definidos, CBF muda formato da Série C: sai mata-mata e entram quadrangulares |publisher=Globo |date=2 March 2020 |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

==First stage==
In the first stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie is only between two teams); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 15).<ref name="regulations">{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/202003/20200304101127_408.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição - Campeonato Brasileiro Série C de 2020 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

The top four teams of each group advanced to the second stage.

==Second stage==
In the second stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the criteria to determine the ranking were the same as used in the first stage (Regulations Article 19).<ref name="regulations"/>

The top two teams of each group were promoted to the Série B. Group winners advanced to the finals.

==Finals==
The finals were played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis, with the higher-seeded team hosting the second leg. If tied on aggregate, the [[away goals rule]] would not be used, [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played, and the [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] would be used to determine the champions (Regulations Article 20).<ref name="regulations"/>

The finalists were seeded according to their performance in the tournament. The teams were ranked according to overall points. If tied on overall points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Overall wins; 2. Overall goal difference; 3. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 21).<ref name="regulations"/>
