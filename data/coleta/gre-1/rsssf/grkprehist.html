1912/13 to 1918/19

No official championship was played during the Balkan War and World War I.

However, in March 1914, the Greek Olympic Committee organised "Pan-Hellenic
Soccer Games", in which the final match was played between Athinaikos Syllogos
Podosfairiseos (a temporary merger between P.O.A. and Goudi, often referred to
as Goudi although that club did not exist at the time) and Piraiki Enosis, won
by A.S.P.

The first ever city championship of Thessaloniki was held in Jan and Feb 1914
(shortly after the Second Balkan War had ended).
 
Champions: Iraklis
           other participants: Alliance                           (lost 1-3 to Iraklis)
                               Progrès Sportive                   (lost 1-5 to Iraklis)
                               French-German School Alumni Union  (lost to Iraklis)

Iraklis also won the second edition early 1915; no further editions were organised
until after World War I.  (Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraklis_F.C._(Thessaloniki))

1943/44

In February 1944 Panathinaikos created a "Panathinaikos Tournament", but 
this failed to end. There were 2 groups created, 1 in Athens and 1 in 
Pireas.  Not a single score is known.

decision to start the regional championships again. A tournament called
"Easter Cup" was started 22.4.1945, but failed to finish, because 
Panathinaikos were expelled from the tournament.
