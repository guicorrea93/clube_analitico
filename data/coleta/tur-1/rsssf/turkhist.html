1940

After four years of absence the Türkiye Futbol Şampiyonası was resumed in 1940 with a special format. The Millî Küme was also held. 
As a result there were two national championships from 1940 onward. The champions of the regional leagues not included in the National League 
competed in regional groups again, such as in the 1930's. The winners of these regional qualifying groups played against each other in a play-off, 
with the winners of that play-off qualifying for the final. The final was contested between 1940 National League champions Fenerbahçe and the 
winners of the qualification play-off Eskişehir Demirspor.

1942

The Türkiye Futbol Şampiyonası was held in a new format. The champions of the three major regional leagues of Istanbul, Ankara, and İzmir 
qualified directly for the Final Group, while the other regional champions competed in qualification groups, with the winners of those groups 
facing off in a play-off in order to decide the fourth participant of the Final Group. The Final Group was played in round-robin format.

Millî Küme was not held.

1956-57

Federasyon Kupası
The Federation Cup was organized by the Turkish Football Federation (TFF) to select a club to represent Turkey at the European Champions Cup, 
since the professional nationwide league was not introduced yet. The competition in 1956-57 consisted of two stages, the knockout stage 
and the Final Group. In the knockout stage the clubs were split into three groups with 10 clubs each. The second stage was contested in a Final Group, 
which was based on a double round-robin format. Beşiktaş won the tournament and thus qualified for the European Cup. 
However, since the TFF failed to register their name for the draw in time, they could not participate in the 1957-58 season of the European Cup after all.
