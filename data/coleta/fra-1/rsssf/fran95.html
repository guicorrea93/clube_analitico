    hit the crossbar on the following penalty. Howeve, the referee kept
    handling out the reds to Bordeaux players, sending off Gatean Huard for
    handball outside the area (was it really outside?), quite harsh
    IMHO. Until that point, Gaetan Huard have been playing great whilst
    Racing failed to take advantage on the numerous dominance. With two
    men short, Zinedine Zidane almost snatched the winner on a freekick.
    In extra-time, Bordeaxu failed to stay in the game with a Mostovoi goal
    on a Garde cross and then a dubious penalty after Keller slipped (wet
    pitch) in the area (unless you say Perez fouled him, which I wouldn't
    say). William Prunier was understandably upset, but his actions
    are still not defendable and was sent off, to. Quite miraculous
    that all Racing players stayed on the pitch, though.
