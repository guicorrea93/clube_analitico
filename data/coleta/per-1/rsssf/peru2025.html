Copa Perú 2025

Etapa Nacional

The tournament will feature 64 teams participating in a single-elimination format. Clubs will be divided 
into two zones (North and South), beginning in the round of 32 in both, and then advancing to the round 
of 16, quarterfinals, and semifinals. The champions of each zone will face each other in the Grand Final, 
a single match. The winner of the Grand Final will be promoted to Liga 2 2026, while the runner-up will 
play a playoff match against the Liga 3 runner-up for a second promotion spot to Liga 2 2026. In addition, 
the four highest-placed teams in the National Table will automatically qualify for Liga 3 2026.
