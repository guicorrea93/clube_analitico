Notes:

1. The top 6 teams from the First Stage qualified to the Championship Group
   (in Lima) carrying their whole season record.

2. As the First Division was reduced to 16 teams for 1976, no team was promoted
   and two teams were relegated. The criteria for relegation: Atlético Grau as
   the last placed team; Unión Tumán as the worst team from a Department
   (Lambayeque) with two teams (the other was Juan Aurich). This rule didn't
   apply for Lima clubs. 

3. A playoff match for 2nd place (qualifing for the Copa Libertadores) was
   necessary.
