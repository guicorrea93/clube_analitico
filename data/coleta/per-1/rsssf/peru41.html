Promoted: Centro Iqueño and Santiago Barranco

Note: 
No team was relegated, since the Promotion Liguilla (disputed between the last two of the First Division and 
the two best of the Lima and Callao 1941 Regional League) was annulled. Therefore, the champion 
Santiago Barranco and runner-up Centro Iqueño of the Regional League were invited to compete 
in the 1942 tournament.
