Notes:
1. First Division reduced to 8 teams for 1932.
2. Federación Universitaria and Ciclista Lima were both deducted 1 point because of walk-over defeats.
3. For the 1932 tournament, Lawn Tennis de la Exposición decided to disaffiliate from the Peruvian Football Federation.
4. For the 1932 tournament, Atlético Chalaco, Unión Buenos Aires y Alianza Frigorífico leave the League to form
   the Provincial Football League of Callao.
5. For the 1932 tournament, Hidroaviación disappeared after losing the support of the Escuela de Hidroaviación de Ancón.
6. Cilista Lima Association to First Division 1932 by disaffiliation of Lawn Tennis de la Exposición.
