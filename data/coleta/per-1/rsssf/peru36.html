Peru 1936

Amateur Tournament (“Division de Honor”) of Lima and Callao 
(non official) but organized by F.P.F.

NB: No official tournament took place, because of the participation of the
    Peruvian National Team in the 1936 Olympic Games soccer tournament.

NB: The sports spirit had much to do in the realization of the championship
    of 1936. It should be remembered that soccer in Peru was amateur, so that
    all football activity was more a social goal than an economic one. In the
    case of Lima, the performance of the main teams in the capital was missed
    for much of the year, so it was convenient to give activity to all players
    who were not considered in the squad that traveled to the Copa América of
    Argentina in 1937. In short, the tournament was basically played to generate
    domestic competition, and the title of champion was awarded as an incentive goal.
