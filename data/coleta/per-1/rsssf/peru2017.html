Primera División


NB: The season is divided into four phases, Torneo de Verano, Torneo Apertura,
    Torneo Clausura, and Supercopa. The first phase is the Torneo de Verano where
    all the teams are divided into two groups and play each team in their group
    twice at home and away. The winner of each group will qualify to a one match
    final which will be played on neutral ground. The second and third stages are
    two smaller Apertura and Clausura tournaments of 15 games each. Each team will
    play all other teams once during the Apertura tournament and during the Clausura
    tournament in a reversed order for a total of 30 matches. Points earned during
    the Apertura will not carry over during the Clausura. The winners of the Apertura
    and Clausura stages will qualify for the fourth stage of the tournament, La Supercopa.
    During La Supercopa the championship will be contested over two legs. If the teams
    are tied in points a third match on neutral ground will be played to decide the
    national champion. If a team wins both the Apertura and Clausura tournaments then
    it will be automatically declared the tournament champion and the runner-ups from 
    the Apertura and Clausura tournaments will play off to enter the Copa Libertadores
    2018 group stage. The two teams with the least amount of points at the end of the
    third stage will be relegated. The champions, runners-up, and Torneo de Verano
    champions advance to the Copa Libertadores.
