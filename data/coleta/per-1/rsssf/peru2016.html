Torneo Promoción y Reservas - Copa Modelo Centenario

The Copa Modelo Centenario 2016 is the first edition of this tournament. Participating in it are 
the Reserve teams of the 16 clubs that make up the First Peruvian Division. They were divided into 
four zones. This tournament is held after the Torneo de Promocion y Reserva 2016 and is organized 
by the F.P.F. (Federacion Peruana de Futbol) in coordination with the A.D.F.P. (Asociacion Deportivo 
de Futbol Profesional). The objective is that the reserve teams of the professional teams do not 
lose the competition as well as serve as a model for the accomplishment of competitions between 
the categories Sub-15 and Sub-17 from the following year among the clubs of the First Peruvian Division.
