Promoted: ADET.       
 
NB: Luis Angel Firpo take their name from that of a heavyweight boxer who
    challenged Jack Dempsey for the world heavyweight title in 1923. Firpo 
    was knocked down seven times, then sent Dempsey crashing through the
    ropes ... but the champ climbed back and knocked Firpo cold. All this
    in only five minutes!
