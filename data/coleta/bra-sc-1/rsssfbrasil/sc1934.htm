Santa Catarina State League 1934

The Civic Championship of Florianópolis 1934 was also valid for the State Championship.

Participants (5 clubs):

AVAÍ Futebol Clube  --  Florianópolis

Clube ATLÉTICO Catarinense  --  Florianópolis

CRUZEIRO Futebol Clube  --  Florianópolis

FIGUEIRENSE Futebol Clube  --  Florianópolis

ÍRIS Futebol Clube  --  Florianópolis

Matches

[not dates available]

Íris 3-2 Avaí

Final Classification

Champion: Atlético (Florianópolis)

     2nd: Íris (Florianópolis)

     3rd: Avaí

Champion’s campaign

The “Atlético” was champion unbeaten - 7 games, 6 victories and 1 tie (31 marked goals).

The cast of Atlético:

Moritz; Arnaldo, Fiúza, Santos, Borba, Santana and Oliveira;

Nanado, Féza, Leal and Piper.

The cast of Íris:

Metralha; Alfeu, Cruz, Dante, Carlos and Pinheiro;

Godinho, Malta, Nizeta, Silveira and Oliveira.

Source: Book "História do Futebol Catarinense" by César do Canto Machado,

        Insular publisher, first edition, 2000.
