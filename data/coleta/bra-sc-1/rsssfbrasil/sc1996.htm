Championship Final

First Leg
[Jul 6]
Joinville      2-0  Chapecoense

Second Leg
[Jul 13]
Chapecoense    dns  Joinville  

Joinville did not appear to play alleging lack of security for a match.  There
were complaints of Chapecoense fans making noises during the night before the 
match near the hotel where Joinville's players were thus forbidding them to sleep.
Also Joinville's staff complained of death threats.
