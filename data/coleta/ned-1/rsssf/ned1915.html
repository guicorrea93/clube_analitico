Netherlands 1914/15

Championship Playoff
District West | District Oost | District Zuid | District Noord 

Due to World War I an emergency competition was played, for which clubs
were distributed regionally to reduce travel time; there was no promotion
and relegation.

In districts West and Oost the nominal 1e Klasse clubs played together in
the same group (called A, as all groups were denominated by a letter);
the winners of those two groups played a so-called "extra competition" for
an unofficial national title, without a representative from district Zuid,
where the 1e Klasse clubs had been distributed over three groups and no
district championship was contested.

Because of the fact that the southern 1e Klasse clubs could not enter the
playoff for the national title, it was not considered official at the time
and the Dutch football federation yearbooks published until the start of
World War II all clearly stated that no national championship was awarded
for the 1914/15 season.  The same applied for the 1939/40 season (won by
Feĳenoord) but at some point after World War II it became usual to include
these titles in the totals for the two Rotterdam clubs.

The results 5*0 and 0*5 denote matches awarded to the home resp. away team.
Tie-breaker is goal average.

Extra-Competitie

NB: at the time, this edition was not official and no national title
    was awarded for the 1914/15 season; the official denomination for
    the series was "extra-competitie" (not "landskampioenschap") and
    Sparta's title was called "officieus" (unofficial) in the press;
    all federation yearbooks until the start of World War II clearly
    stated that in 1914/15 no national championship was contested.
