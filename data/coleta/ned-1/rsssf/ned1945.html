District West I | District West II | District Oost | District Zuid | District Noord
Saturday Football 

The German occupiers forbade football mid-September 1944;
by then no matches had yet been played in the districts
West I and Noord and just a few in West II, Oost and Zuid.

Below the division of clubs over the classes is given,
as well as the results of the 11 matches played in the
1e Klasse in district Zuid.
