Netherlands 1939/40

Championship Playoff
District West I | District West II | District Oost | District Zuid | District Noord 

Because of World War II, only an emergency competition was played.
Clubs were divided on a regional basis, ignoring class membership,
in order to reduce travel time.

Only the 1e Klasse clubs played together in group A of their district.
The winners played off for the national championship, but there was
no promotion or relegation.

Due to the German invasion in May 1940, most competitions were not
finished.

Cross-tables with results are given only for groups including clubs
from the 1e or 2e Klasse.
