Following pressure from the German occupiers, the KNVB (now renamed
NVB) merged, upon a request by the ministry of Onderwĳs, Kunsten en
Wetenschappen, during the summer of 1940 with three other football
federations.  The clubs of the protestant CNVB and the socialist
NASB enter below the district level (and thus do not feature in the
overview below), but clubs from the roman catholic IVCB were added
at the district level; those clubs are marked # below.

Cross-tables with results are given only for the 1e and 2e Klasse.
