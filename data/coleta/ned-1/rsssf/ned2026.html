Go Ahead        6-0 NAC             
  [NAC demanded a replay of this match, as Go Ahead had fielded Dean James, who
   had taken Indonesian citizenship, thereby losing his status as an EU citizen
   and requiring a work permit, which he did not have, rendering him ineligible;
   however, the Dutch FA refused to order a replay; NAC took the matter to court,
   where the FA argued that by ordering a replay, up to 133 matches might have
   to be replayed, a red herring as any complaints on player eligibility have to
   be filed within eight days, which NAC had done, but did not apply to any other
   matches; nevertheless, the court deemed that the FA's interest in completing
   the competition outweighed that of NAC (who were fighting relegation at the
   time, eventually unsuccessfully) in having the competition rules adhered to
   (the FA had admitted that James had been ineligible to play; that neither he
   nor his club had been aware of the effect of changing nationality would seem
   to be irrelevant); NAC decided to let the matter rest although other clubs in
   similar cases have successfully appealed to CAS in Geneva; also note that in
   many countries (and indeed in FIFA competitions such as World Cup qualifiers)
   fielding an ineligible player carries an automatic forfeit loss, not a replay]
