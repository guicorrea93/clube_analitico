'''The 2019–20 [[Ghana Premier League]]''' was the 64th season of top professional [[association football]] league in [[Ghana]]. The season started on 28 December 2019.<ref name="leaguestructure" /> 18 teams compete in the league with each club playing each other twice, home and away, with the three clubs at the bottom of the league relegated to the [[Division One League, Ghana|Division One League]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.ghanafa.org/premier-league/premier-league-fixtures|title=Premier League Fixtures|website=Ghana FA|accessdate=9 June 2020}}</ref>

==Season overview==
With 18 clubs participating in the league, this marked the first time since 1980 that more than 16 clubs competed in the top division of Ghanaian football.<ref name="leaguestructure" /> King Faisal and Great Olympics were readmitted after separate court cases over unhappiness about their relegation from the top-flight in previous seasons.<ref name="leaguestructure">{{cite web|url=https://www.goal.com/en-gh/news/ghana-resolve-to-keep-18-team-premier-league-beyond-2019-20/9ymi03fs7g7o1gre2nzjc8p9w|title=Ghana resolve to keep 18-team Premier League beyond 2019-20|website=Goal.com|date=19 December 2019|accessdate=11 June 2020}}</ref> During a council meeting in October 2019 before the season began, the number of relegated teams was reduced from five to three.<ref name="leaguestructure" />

The GFA announced that the league was suspended on March 15, 2020, in the middle of match-week 15, because of the global [[COVID-19 pandemic]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.goal.com/en-gh/news/coronavirus-ghana-football-association-suspends-premier/lj6rk3p2k21u1x0twtmmfz3vz|title=Coronavirus: Ghana Football Association suspends Premier League after presidential directive|website=Goal.com|date=15 March 2020|accessdate=11 June 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.goal.com/en-gh/news/coronavirus-aduana-stars-coach-fabin-afraid-to-restart-ghana/155ez1glmsbfc16t6v5qr4drwx|title=Coronavirus: Aduana Stars coach Fabin afraid to restart Ghana Premier League|website=Goal|date=6 June 2020|accessdate=9 June 2020}}</ref> On 31 May, the league was further suspended until at least 31 June.

On 30 June, the FA had a meeting and cancelled the league due to the COVID-19 pandemic. A meeting was held on 27 August 2020 to determine the start and logistics of the 2020–21 season.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.goal.com/en-us/news/covid-19-more-blow-for-premier-league-as-ghana-extends-ban/8usari43jmby14fnn1o04qmgq|title=Covid-19: Blow for Premier League as Ghana extends ban on contact sports |website=Goal|date=31 May 2020|accessdate=8 June 2020}}</ref>
