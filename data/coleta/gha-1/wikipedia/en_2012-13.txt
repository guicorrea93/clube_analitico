The '''2012–13 [[Ghana Premier League]]''', known as the '''[[Glo (company)|Glo]] Premier League''' for sponsorship purposes,<ref name="sponsor">{{cite web|title=FA signs on Glo as premiership sponsor |url=http://blog.vibeghana.com/2008/12/23/fa-signs-on-glo-as-premiership-sponsor.aspx |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090527134056/http://blog.vibeghana.com/2008/12/23/fa-signs-on-glo-as-premiership-sponsor.aspx |url-status=dead |archive-date=27 May 2009 |publisher=VibeGhana |date=27 May 2012 }}</ref><ref name="sponsor 2">{{cite web|title=National Teams Sponsorships|url=http://www.gloworld.com/sponsor_nationalteam.asp|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101123103427/http://gloworld.com/sponsor_nationalteam.asp|archive-date=23 November 2010|website=[[Glo (company)|Glo]]|access-date=22 December 2010|quote= The partnership signed in 2008 to the sponsorship of [...] the Ghana Premier League in five years.}}</ref> season was the 54th season of the top-tier football league in Ghana that began on 6 October 2012.
