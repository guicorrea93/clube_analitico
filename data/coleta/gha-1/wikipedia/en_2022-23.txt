The '''2022–23 [[Ghana Premier League]]''', known as the '''betPawa Premier League''' for sponsorship reasons,<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |date=2022-08-03 |title=GFA announces betPawa as new headline sponsor of the Ghana Premier League |url=https://www.ghanafa.org/gfa-announces-betpawa-as-new-headline-sponsor-of-the-ghana-premier-league |access-date= |website=Ghana Fa |language=en}}</ref> was the 67th season of the top [[Professional sports|professional]] [[association football]] league in Ghana.<ref>{{cite news |title=PREMIER LEAGUE HISTORY |website=[[Ghana Football Association]] |publisher=The [[Ghana Football Association]] |url=https://www.ghanafa.org/premier-league/premier-league-history |access-date=11 June 2021}}</ref> The season started in September 2022 and is scheduled to end in June 2023.

[[Asante Kotoko S.C.|Asante Kotoko]] are the defending champions.

== Teams ==
Eighteen teams will compete in the league; the 15 from [[2020–21 Ghana Premier League|the previous season]] and the 3 winners of the Division One zones. The promoted teams are [[Samartex]] and [[Kotoku Royals F.C.|Kotoku Royals]] who both achieved promotion for the first time.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2021-07-18 |title=Division One League: Accra Lions earn historic promotion to the GPL after victory over Danbort |url=https://ghanasoccernet.com/division-one-league-accra-lions-earn-historic-promotion-to-the-gpl-after-victory-over-danbort |access-date=2021-07-19 |website=GhanaSoccernet |language=en}}</ref> They replaced [[West African Football Academy|WAFA]], [[Techiman Eleven Wonders FC|Techiman Eleven Wonders]] and [[Elmina Sharks F.C.|Elmina Sharks]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2022-06-18 |title=2021/22 Ghana Premier League: Elmina Sharks go into final matchday already relegated |url=https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/SportsArchive/2021-22-Ghana-Premier-League-Elmina-Sharks-go-into-final-matchday-already-relegated-1563824 |access-date=2022-06-19 |website=GhanaWeb |language=en |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230410223421/https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/SportsArchive/2021-22-Ghana-Premier-League-Elmina-Sharks-go-into-final-matchday-already-relegated-1563824 |archive-date=10 April 2023 |url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2022-05-29 |title=2021/22 Ghana Premier League: Elmina Sharks relegated after defeat to Berekum Chelsea |url=https://ghanasoccernet.com/2021-22-ghana-premier-league-elmina-sharks-relegated-after-defeat-to-berekum-chelsea |access-date=2022-06-19 |website=GhanaSoccernet |language=en}}</ref>
