The '''2018 [[Ghanaian Premier League]]''' was the 62nd season of top professional [[association football]] in [[Ghana]]. The season was initially scheduled to begin on 11 February 2018, but was postponed to 4 March 2018,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://ghanafa.org/index/league_news_details/3/12651|title=2017/2018 Premier League season to kick off on March 3|publisher=Ghana Football Association|date=21 February 2018}}</ref> and later postponed indefinitely.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.espn.com/soccer/ghana/story/3401373/ghana-premier-league-postponed-indefinitely|title=Ghana Premier League postponed indefinitely|publisher=ESPNFC|date=1 March 2018}}</ref> It was then scheduled to begin on 17 March 2018.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ghanafa.org/news/full_news/16/12679|title=2017/18 Division One League to kick off next weekend|publisher=Ghana Football Association|date=15 March 2018}}</ref><ref>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablesg/gha2018.html Ghana 2018], rsssf.org</ref> The league kicked off on 17 March with a match between [[International Allies F.C.|Inter-Allies]] vs [[Bechem United F.C.|Bechem United]] in Tema.

The Premier League was later cancelled in June 2018 by government directive following the Number 12 Expose corruption scandal within [[Ghana Football Association]].
