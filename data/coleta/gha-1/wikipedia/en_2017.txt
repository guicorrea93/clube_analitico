The '''2017 [[Ghanaian Premier League]]''' is the 61st season of top professional [[association football]] in [[Ghana]]. The season began on 12 February.<ref>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablesg/gha2017.html Ghana 2017], RSSSF.com</ref> [[All Stars F.C.|Wa All Stars]] are the defending champions coming off their first league title. The eventual winners Aduana Stars were crowned champions during a special coronation match with Berekum Chelsea. They have automatically qualified for the CAF Champions League next year. 

==Teams==

The [[Ghanaian Premier League]] comprises 16 sides, of which the bottom three will be relegated to the [[Ghana Football Leagues|Division One]].
