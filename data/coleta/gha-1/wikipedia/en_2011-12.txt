The '''2011–12 [[Ghana Premier League|Ghanaian Premier League]]''' (''known as the [[Globacom|Glo]] Premier League for sponsorship reasons'')<ref name="sponsor">{{cite news |title=FA signs on Glo as premiership sponsor |url=http://blog.vibeghana.com/2008/12/23/fa-signs-on-glo-as-premiership-sponsor.aspx |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090527134056/http://blog.vibeghana.com/2008/12/23/fa-signs-on-glo-as-premiership-sponsor.aspx |url-status=dead |archive-date=27 May 2009 |work=VibeGhana |date=8 September 2011 }}</ref><ref name="sponsor 2">{{cite web |url=http://www.gloworld.com/sponsor_nationalteam.asp |title=National Teams Sponsorships |website=[[Globacom]] |accessdate=22 December 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101123103427/http://gloworld.com/sponsor_nationalteam.asp |archive-date=23 November 2010 |url-status=dead |df=dmy-all }}</ref> season was the 53rd season of top-tier football in [[Ghana]]. The competition began on 5 September 2011, and ended on 27 May 2012.
