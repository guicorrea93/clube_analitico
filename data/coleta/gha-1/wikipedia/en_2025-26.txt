The '''2025–26 Ghana Premier League''' was the 70th season of the [[Ghana Premier League]], the top-tier [[association football|football]] league in [[Ghana]] that began on 12 September 2025 and ended on 24 May 2026.<ref name="Schedule">{{cite web |date=14 July 2025 |title= FA confirms Match Officials for 2025/26 Premier League Season |url=https://www.ghanafa.org/fa-confirms-match-officials-for-2025-26-premier-league-season/|website=[[Ghana Football Association]]|access-date=15 August 2025}}</ref>

[[Medeama S.C.|Medeama]] were crowned league winners for a second time after a 5–2 win over [[Heart of Lions F.C.|Heart of Lions]] on 17 May 2026 with a game to spare<ref>{{cite web|date=17 May 2026|title=Medeama win 2025–26 Ghana Premier League|url=https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/SportsArchive/Medeama-win-2025-26-Ghana-Premier-League-2034942|website=[[GhanaWeb]]|access-date=}}</ref> and thus represented Ghana in the following season's [[2026–27 CAF Champions League|CAF Champions League]]. After finishing the season with winning the Ghana FA Cup and a point below Young Apostles, Nations got relegated to the [[Ghana Division One]] after 3 years in the top flight, with [[Eleven Wonders]] and Hohoe United following suit after only a year of promotion.

With an average attendance of 20,163, [[Asante Kotoko]] recorded the highest average home league attendance.

==Teams==
This season's league consisted of 18 teams – the top 15 teams from the previous season, and three teams promoted from the previous season's [[Division One League]].

=== Changes from the previous season ===
Hohoe United, [[Techiman Eleven Wonders FC|Eleven Wonders]] and [[Swedru All Blacks F.C.|Swedru All Blacks]] were promoted from the 2024–25 [[Division One League]], replacing the [[2024–25 Ghana Premier League|2024–25]] bottom three teams; [[Nsoatreman F.C.|Nsoatreman]], [[Legon Cities FC|Legon Cities]] and [[Accra Lions FC|Accra Lions]].<ref>{{cite web|date=8 June 2025|title= Accra Lions Relegated join Legon Cities and Nsoatreman FC in Ghana Premier League drop|url=https://ghanasoccernet.com/accra-lions-relegated-join-legon-cities-and-nsoatreman-fc-in-ghana-premier-league-drop/|website=Ghana Soccernet|access-date=18 June 2025}}</ref>

== Controversies ==
After a 2-0 loss against Hearts of Oak on 29 March 2026, Hohoe United decided to withdraw from the league with accusations of alleged match officiating irregularities and bias.<ref name="Hearts">{{cite web|date=24 May 2026|title=Heart of Lions relegated on head-to-head rule|url=https://ghanasportsonline.com/bashir-hayfords-heart-of-lions-relegated-on-head-to-head-rule/|website=Ghana Sports Online|access-date=23 June 2026}}</ref><ref name=Hohoe>{{Cite news|date=7 April 2026|title=Why Hohoe United quit Ghana Premier League: Alleged bias, questionable officiating|url=https://www.graphic.com.gh/sports/sports-news/why-hohoe-united-quit-ghana-premier-league-o-allege-bias-questionable-officiating.html|newspaper=[[Daily Graphic (Ghana)|Daily Graphic]]|location=[[Accra]], Ghana|access-date=13 April 2026}}</ref> On 1 May that year, the [[Ghana Football Association]] through its disciplinary committee in response banned Hohoe United from the league system for 3 seasons and declared their players and coaches as [[free agent]]s, thus ending their brief stay in the top-flight.<ref>{{cite press release|date=1 May 2026|title=Disciplinary committee slaps Hohoe United with 3-season ban over Ghana Premier League withdrawal|url=https://www.ghanafa.org/disciplinary-committee-slaps-hohoe-united-fc-with-3-season-ban-over-ghana-premier-league-withdrawal|publisher=[[Ghana Football Association]]|access-date=4 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|date=2 May 2026|title=Hohoe United handed three-season ban for GPL withdrawal|url=https://www.myjoyonline.com/hohoe-united-handed-three-season-ban-for-gpl-withdrawal/|website=[[Joy FM (Ghana)|MyJoyOnline.com]]|access-date=4 September 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last=Vincent Ashitey|first=Vincent|date=2 May 2026|title=Hohoe United banned until 2029/30 season after GPL withdrawal|url=https://www.primenewsghana.com/sports/hohoe-united-banned-until-2029-30-season-after-gpl-withdrawal.html|website=Prime News Ghana|access-date=4 September 2026}}</ref>
