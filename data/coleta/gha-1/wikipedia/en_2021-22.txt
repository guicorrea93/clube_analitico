The '''2021–22 [[Ghana Premier League]]''' was the 66th season of the top [[professional sports|professional]] [[association football]] league in Ghana<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.ghanafa.org/premier-league/premier-league-history|title= PREMIER LEAGUE HISTORY|website=[[Ghana Football Association]]|access-date= 11 June 2021}}</ref> which began on 29 October 2021 and concluded on 19 June 2022.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.ghanafa.org/ghana-premier-league-2021-22-season-kicks-off-october-29-2021|title=GHANA PREMIER LEAGUE 2021/22 SEASON KICKS OFF OCTOBER 29, 2021|website=[[Ghana Football Association]]|date=10 August 2021|access-date=13 December 2021}}</ref> [[Accra Hearts of Oak S.C.|Hearts of Oak]] were the defending champions.<ref>{{Cite web|date=11 July 2021|title=GPL: Hearts of Oak win league title after 12 years|work=[[Joy FM (Ghana)|Joy Sports]]|publisher=[[Multimedia Group Limited]]|url=https://www.myjoyonline.com/gpl-hearts-of-oak-win-league-title-after-12-years/|access-date=19 July 2021}}</ref>

[[Asante Kotoko S.C.|Asante Kotoko]] won the league for a record-extending 25th time and the first since 2014 after a 1–1 draw against [[Ashanti Gold S.C.|Ashanti Gold]]<ref>{{cite web|date=4 June 2022|title=Asante Kotoko win record-extending 25th Ghana Premier League title after AshantiGold draw|website=GHANAsoccernet|url=https://ghanasoccernet.com/asante-kotoko-win-record-extending-25th-ghana-premier-league-title-after-ashantigold-draw|access-date=17 June 2022}}</ref> and were handed the trophy on the season's final day after a 3-0 triumph over relegated [[Elmina Sharks F.C.|Elmina Sharks]].<ref>{{cite web|date=19 June 2022|url=https://www.ghanafa.org/asante-kotoko-outclass-elmina-sharks-as-champions-are-crowned-in-style-in-kumasi|title=Asante Kotoko outclass Elmina Sharks as Champions are crowned in style in Kumasi|website=[[Ghana Football Association]]|access-date=20 June 2022}}</ref> As champions, Kotoko will participate in the qualification procedures for [[2022–23 CAF Champions League|the following season's CAF Champions League]].

== Teams ==
Eighteen teams competed in the league; the 15 from [[2020–21 Ghana Premier League|the previous season]] and the 3 winners of the Division One League zones. The promoted teams were [[Bibiani Gold Stars F.C.|Bibiani Gold Stars]] and [[Accra Lions FC|Accra Lions]], who both achieved promotion for the first time.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2021-07-18|title=Division One League: Accra Lions earn historic promotion to the GPL after victory over Danbort|url=https://ghanasoccernet.com/division-one-league-accra-lions-earn-historic-promotion-to-the-gpl-after-victory-over-danbort|access-date=2021-07-19|website=GhanaSoccernet.com|language=en}}</ref> [[Real Tamale United|RTU]] returned to the league for the first time since their relegation in 2013.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2021-07-25|title=Breaking News: Real Tamale United seal Ghana Premier League return on the final day in Division One
|url=https://ghanasoccernet.com/breaking-news-real-tamale-united-seal-ghana-premier-league-return-on-final-day-in-division-one|access-date=2021-07-25|website=GhanaSoccernet.com|language=en}}</ref>
They replaced [[Cape Coast Ebusua Dwarfs|Ebusua Dwarfs]], [[Liberty Professionals F.C.|Liberty Professionals]] and [[International Allies F.C.|Inter Allies]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=GPL wrap: Liberty and Ebusua Dwarfs relegated, Aduana end season unbeaten at home - MyJoyOnline.com|url=https://www.myjoyonline.com/gpl-wrap-liberty-and-ebusua-dwarfs-relegated-aduana-end-season-unbeaten-at-home/|access-date=2021-07-19|website=MyJoyOnline.com|language=en-US}}</ref>

==Attendances==

Even though COVID-19 limitations were in place, [[Asante Kotoko]] drew an average home attendance of 4,585 during the 2021–22 Ghana Premier League season.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-01-03 |title=Nhyira Power Sports embarks on campaign to solve poor stadium attendance in Ghana - MyJoyOnline |url=https://www.myjoyonline.com/nhyira-power-sports-embarks-on-campaign-to-solve-poor-stadium-attendance-in-ghana/ |access-date=2026-10-21 |language=en-US}}</ref> One of the games with a high attendance was the game between the [[Accra Hearts of Oak]] and [[Asante Kotoko]], which took place in [[Accra]] in front of a crowd of 20,000.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2022-02-21 |title=Super Clash: Thousands watch Hearts-Asante Kotoko game at Accra Sports Stadium amid COVID-19 pandemic - Ghana Latest Football News, Live Scores, Results |url=https://ghanasoccernet.com/super-clash-thousands-watch-hearts-asante-kotoko-game-at-accra-sports-stadium-amid-covid-19-pandemic |access-date=2026-10-21 |website=GhanaSoccernet |language=en}}</ref>
