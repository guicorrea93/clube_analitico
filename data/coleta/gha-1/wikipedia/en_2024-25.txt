The '''2024–25 [[Ghana Premier League]]''' was the 69th season of the top [[professional sports|professional]] [[association football]] league in Ghana. At the end of the season the winners qualified for the [[CAF Champions League]] next season while the bottom three teams were relegated to the [[Division One League]].

[[Samartex]] were the defending champions.<ref>{{cite web|date=16 June 2024|title=Samartex 1996 crowned 2023–24 Ghana Premier League champions|url=https://www.ghanafa.org/fc-samartex-1996-crowned-2023-24-ghana-premier-league-champions|website=[[Ghana Football Association]]|access-date=23 June 2024}}</ref> On 8 June 2025, [[Bibiani Gold Stars]] won their maiden league title in dramatic style at the final round of matches after longtime leaders, [[Nations F.C.|Nations FC]], failed to win their last match of the season.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.myjoyonline.com/2024-2025-gpl-bibiani-gold-stars-claim-ghana-premier-league-title-after-win-over-accra-lions/|title=2024–25 GPL: Bibiani Gold Stars claim Ghana Premier League title after win over Accra Lions|work=myjoyonline|date=8 June 2025}}</ref> With an average of 20,381, [[Asante Kotoko]] recorded by far the highest average home league attendance in the 2024–25 Ghana Premier League.

==Teams==
The following 18 clubs competed in the Ghana Premier League this season; 15 clubs remaining from the previous season's [[2023–24 Ghana Premier League|Premier League]] and [[Division One League]].
