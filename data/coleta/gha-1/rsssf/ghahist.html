1966

Championship started in March, just after the coup d'état on Feb 24;
due to that coup, Real Republicans (dubbed Osagyefo's Own Club or OOC)
were dissolved, as were Sekondi Independence (the club of Ambrose Yankey,
the personal aide of Kwame Nkrumah who was toppled on Feb 24); Defence
Stars (the army team) withdrew as some of their players were soldiers
in active service and involved in the coup.

cancelled in December after 22 rounds.
