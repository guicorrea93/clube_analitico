Division 1

NB: this season the league is restricted to Tahiti, so Mira from Mooréa, who
    had finished third last season after leading the standings until late in
    the season, do not enter; instead, all five clubs playing in Division 2
    last season were admitted, together with Mataiea and the reserve side of
    Central Sport; predictably, this led to numerous silly results (Dragon
    won their first two matches with an aggregate score of 43-0)
