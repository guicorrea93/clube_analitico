Relegation Playoff
Central beat Fei-Pi for a place in the top level (DH) 2002/03

Promoted: Jeunes Tahitiens (D1 champions)
Relegated: PTT and Matavai; PTT have several protests running involving
           matches against Dragon, Arué, Piraé and Manu Ura which may yet
           lead to the annulment of the entire championship.

In June the federation decide to play with 10 clubs next season; due to this,
PTT remain at the first level and Matavai (last at top level) play off against
Fei-Pi (2nd level runners-up) for the last spot.

Playoff
Fei-Pi   6-0 Matavai

Fei-Pi promoted along with Jeunes Tahitiens; Matavai relegated
