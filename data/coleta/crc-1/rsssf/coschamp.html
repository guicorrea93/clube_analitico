Costa Rica - List of Champions and Runners-Up

NB: a substantial number of the seasons indicated as a calendar year tournament below
    in fact finished in the first few months of the next calendar year, but at least
    all were started with the intention of finishing before the end of the year; this
    pretension was given up de iure in 1990 (a season cancelled, as 1989, which had
    finished in October 1990, was followed by 1991, which started in November 1990),
    but de facto already by the 1986 season, which started in July and finished in 
    July 1987); below the actual fall-spring rhythm is reflected in the denomination
    of the seasons from 1986/87 onwards.
    [ArL 02] refers to Olympic Club as "national champions" in March 1918, but it is
    not clear on what this is based.
