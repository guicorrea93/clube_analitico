Costa Rica 1976

NB: tournament played as two stages, one triple round robin 
    (27 matches each for all clubs), from which the top-5
    qualified for the championship playoff (a double round 
    robin, 8  matches for all clubs involved) while the 
    remaining 5 clubs entered a relegation playoff (also a 
    double round robin, 8 matches for all clubs).
