Costa Rica 1986/87

NB: season officially known as 1986, but it lasted from July 1986
    to May 1987;
    after a quadruple round robin between 10 clubs (36 matches each
    for all clubs), the top-5 qualified for the championship playoff
    (a double round robin, 8 matches for all clubs involved);
    the bottom club after the regular phase was relegated.
