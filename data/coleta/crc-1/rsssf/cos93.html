Championship Playoff

1st leg Herediano       2-0     Cartaginés

NB: Herediano, winners of the regular championship, and Cartaginés, who
    won the final round, were due to decide the title in Costa Rica, but
    the first leg went to the law courts and the second was suspended just
    before the half-time. Herediano won the first leg 2-0 but Cartaginés
    claimed that the penalty with which they scored one of the goal was not
    valid. The foul was outside the area, and they had a TV-film to prove
    it. They handed the film to their lawyers to go to court, and sent a
    copy and fax to FIFA to get their opinion. Everything would have been
    settled if Herediano had got at least a draw in the second leg, but
    after 44 minutes a fan broke on the field and was manhandled by police,
    who where then stoned by spectators.
 
Herediano is champion 1992/93 after FA-decisions.
