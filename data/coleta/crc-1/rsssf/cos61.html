Costa Rica 1961

Prior to the championship, five clubs (Alajuelense, Herediano,
Saprissa, Cartaginés and Orión) withdrew from the Federación
de Fútbol and formed the Asociación de Fútbol (Asofútbol).

Because of this, two leagues were organised, that of the
Federación only including Carmen FC, SG Española (Gimnástica)
and promoted CS Uruguay.

Federación Championship 1961

Final Table:

 1.Carmen FC              4  3  1  0   9- 3   7  Champions

 3.SG Española            4  0  1  3   3-12   1

NB: after the championship, the two rival organisations were
    unified.  Whereas all 5 clubs from the rebel organisation
    were admitted to the 1962 championship, the three clubs 
    which had remained within the Federación had to play off 
    with the top 3 teams from the second division for just one
    place in the top level.  This playoff was won by Uruguay,
    so that championship winners Carmen were relegated to the
    second level.  Their 1961 championship was only recognised
    in 1999 by the Unión Nacional de Fútbol (Unafut) upon its
    creation.
