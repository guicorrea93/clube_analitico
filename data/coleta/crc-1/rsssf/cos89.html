Costa Rica 1989/90

NB: season officially known as 1989, but it lasted from September 1989
    to October 1990;
    after a triple round robin between 10 clubs (327 matches each
    for all clubs), the top-6 qualified for the championship playoff
    (a double round robin, 10 matches for all clubs involved);
    the bottom club after the regular phase was relegated.
