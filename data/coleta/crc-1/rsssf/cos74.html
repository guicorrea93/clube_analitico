Costa Rica 1974

NB: tournament played as two stages, both double round robins 
    (14 matches each); both stages qualified 2 clubs for the
    championship playoff (3 matches for all clubs involved);
    as Saprissa won both stages, they were declared champions
    and the playoff was annulled; relegation decided by
    aggregate season record.
