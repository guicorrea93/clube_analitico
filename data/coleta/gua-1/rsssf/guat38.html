Guatemala 1938

Liga Mayor de Guatemala 1938

NB: point system was changed to 2-1-0; 6 participants, including two newly
    promoted clubs, IRCA (second level champions twice in succession) and
    Ayuntamiento (renamed Municipal, founded 1936);
    defending champions Quetzal, who had been stripped of their republican
    championship of 1936, withdrew;
    Magisterio were renamed Minerva
