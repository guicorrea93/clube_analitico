Campeonato de la República 1935

NB: second edition, between 10 departmental champions, of which only 6 showed;
    capital champions Cibeles renounced and were replaced by Quetzal

Participants (6):
   América (Quetzaltenango)
   Imperial (Cobán)
   Izabal
   Nacional (Suchitepéquez)
   Pensativo (Antigua)
   Quetzal (Ciudad de Guatemala)

NB: the Pensativo squad included 8 players from Herculés (including the scorer in
    the final, Héctor Mansilla) and Tipografia Nacional and only 3 from Antigua
