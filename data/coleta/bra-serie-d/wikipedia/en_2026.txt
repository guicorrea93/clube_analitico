The '''2026 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]]''' (officially the '''Brasileirão Série D ZeroUm 2026''' for sponsorship reasons) was a football competition to be held in Brazil, equivalent to the fourth division. It began on 4 April and ended on 13 September.<ref name=GE/>

Ninety-six teams competed in the tournament.

==Format changes==
On 1 October 2025, [[Brazilian Football Confederation|CBF]] announced a format change to the Série D, expanding the competition from 64 teams to 96. Due to this expansion, the 32 teams qualified to the second stage of the [[2025 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D|2025 edition]] were automatically qualified, and as four of those teams were promoted to the [[2026 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|2026 Série C]], the confederation added one further spot to four regional federations based on the [[CBF ranking|CBF State Ranking]].<ref name=GE>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pb/futebol/brasileirao-serie-d/noticia/2025/10/01/serie-d-tera-96-clubes-a-partir-de-2026-entenda-novo-formato.ghtml|title=Série D terá 96 clubes a partir de 2026; entenda novo formato|trans-title=Série D will have 96 clubs starting from 2026; understand new format|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=1 October 2025|accessdate=6 October 2025}}</ref>

CBF also established four new criteria of qualification:

* '''Criteria 1''': Teams relegated from the [[2025 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|2025 Série C]];
* '''Criteria 2''': Teams qualified through the state leagues and state cups;
* '''Criteria 3''': Teams qualified to the second stage of the [[2025 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D|2025 Série D]];
* '''Criteria 4''': Best-placed teams in the [[CBF ranking|CBF Club Ranking]] not qualified to any national division.

All of those criteria overlap each other, with criteria 1 being the most important one. If a team qualify to the 2026 Série D through criteria 2 and 3, their spot will be filled by the criteria 4, independent of their state.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pb/futebol/brasileirao-serie-d/noticia/2025/10/02/serie-d-com-96-clubes-entenda-os-criterios-adotados-pela-cbf.ghtml|title=Série D com 96 clubes: entenda os critérios adotados pela CBF|trans-title=Série D with 96 clubs; understand the criteria adopted by CBF|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=2 October 2025|accessdate=6 October 2025}}</ref>

Six teams are promoted instead of the previous four, with the competition beginning in the first stage with 16 groups of six teams each. The four best teams qualify to the second stage in a knockout format, which prevails until the finals. The 32 teams qualified from the second stage are also qualified to the [[2027 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D|2027 Série D]] (excluding the six promoted teams), and the four teams qualified to the semifinals are promoted to the [[2027 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|2027 Série C]], with the four teams eliminated in the quarterfinals playing a play-off stage to define the other two teams promoted to the third division.<ref name=GE/>

The winners of the competition qualify directly to the third stage of the [[2027 Copa do Brasil]].<ref name=GE/>

==Format==
During the group stage, the 96 teams were divided into 16 groups of six, organised regionally. The top four teams from each group qualified for the round of 64. From this point onwards, the competition was played as a knockout tournament, with each round contested over two legs.<ref name=CBFRules>{{cite web|url=https://stcbfsiteprdimgbrs.blob.core.windows.net/img-site/cdn/REC_Brasileiro_Serie_D_2026_df281f18b7.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição Brasileiro Série D 2026 |language=pt |publisher=CBF |date=24 February 2026}}</ref>

==Group stage==
In the group stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie was only between two teams); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 16).<ref name=CBFRules/> The top four teams qualified for the round of 64.

CBF published the 16 groups of six teams each on 6 March 2026.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/al/futebol/brasileirao-serie-d/noticia/2026/03/06/serie-d-2026-cbf-divulga-os-16-grupos-da-competicao.ghtml|title=Série D 2026: CBF divulga os 16 grupos da competição|trans-title=2026 Série D: CBF publish the 16 groups of the competition|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=6 March 2026|accessdate=9 March 2026}}</ref>

==Final stages==
The final stages were played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis. For the round of 32, round of 16, semi-finals, and finals, the team with the best overall performance hosted the second leg. For the quarter-finals and the promotion play-offs, teams were seeded based on the table of results of all matches in the competition. The top seeded teams hosted the second leg.

Except for the promotion play-offs, if teams were tied on aggregate at any stage, the [[away goals rule]] would not be used and [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played. Instead, a [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] would be used to determine the winners (Regulations Article 17). In the promotion play-offs, if teams were tied on aggregate, the winners would be declared based on their overall performance (Regulations Article 21).

The four semifinalists and the two promotion play-offs winners were promoted to 2027 Série C.<ref name=CBFRules />
