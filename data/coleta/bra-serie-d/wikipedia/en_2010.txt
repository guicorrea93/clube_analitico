The '''[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]] 2010 fue la segunda edición del Campeonato Brasileño de Serie D'''. The competition has 40 clubs, four of which will eventually ascendieron to the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]] de [[2011 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|2011]].

==Competition format==
The 40 teams are divided in ten groups of 4, playing within them in a double round-robin format. The two best ranked in each group at the end of 6 rounds will qualify to the Second Stage, which will be played in home-and-away system. Winners advance to Third Stage along with the three losers with best record in previous stages. The Quarter-Final winners will be promoted to the [[2011 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C 2011]]. As there is no Série E, or Fifth Division, technically there will be no relegation. However, teams who were not promoted will have to re-qualify for [[2011 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D|Série D 2011]] through their respective state leagues.

[[América Futebol Clube (AM)|América (AM)]] was judged not guilty on November 5 by the [[:pt:STJD|STJD]], accused of fielding an out-of-contract player in the Quarterfinals against [[Joinville Esporte Clube|Joinville]]. If found guilty, the club would have been disqualified from the Série D, losing its promotion, which would then by awarded to Joinville. The Semifinals should be then replayed, this time facing [[Madureira Esporte Clube|Madureira]] and Joinville. The club will now be judged again, accused of fielding a suspended player in the Second Leg of the Semifinals against Madureira.<br />On November 12, América (AM) was declared guilty of fielding a suspender player. However, later on the same day, the club achieved to suspend the decision.<br />
Finally, on December 9, the STJD decided to punish América (AM) with the loss of 6 points due to fielding an out-of-contract player.
