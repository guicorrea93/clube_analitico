The '''2024 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the fourth division. The competition began on 27 April and ended on 29 September 2024.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/index/campeonato-brasileiro-2024-tera-pela-primeira-vez-jogo-de-abertura |title=Campeonato Brasileiro 2024 terá pela primeira vez jogo de abertura |publisher=CBF |language=pt|date=31 October 2023}}</ref>

Sixty-four teams competed in the tournament. Sixty teams qualified from their state leagues and cups, and four relegated from the [[2023 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]] ([[Associação Atlética de Altos|Altos]], [[América Futebol Clube (RN)|América de Natal]], [[Manaus Futebol Clube|Manaus]] and [[Pouso Alegre Futebol Clube|Pouso Alegre]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pe/futebol/brasileirao-serie-c/noticia/2023/08/26/serie-c-veja-classificados-aos-quadrangulares-do-acesso-e-rebaixados.ghtml|title=Série C: veja classificados aos quadrangulares do acesso e rebaixados|trans-title=Série C: check out the qualified to the promotion groups and relegated teams|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=26 August 2023|accessdate=31 August 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pi/futebol/brasileirao-serie-d/noticia/2023/10/22/serie-d-do-campeonato-brasileiro-de-2024-tem-todos-participantes-definidos-veja-lista.ghtml|title=Série D do Campeonato Brasileiro de 2024 tem todos participantes definidos; veja lista|trans-title=Série D of the 2024 Campeonato Brasileiro has all the participants defined; see the list|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=22 October 2023|accessdate=22 November 2023}}</ref>

The four semi-finalists, [[Anápolis Futebol Clube|Anápolis]], [[Associação Olímpica de Itabaiana|Itabaiana]], [[Maringá Futebol Clube|Maringá]] and [[Retrô Futebol Clube Brasil|Retrô]], were promoted to the [[2025 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/detalhes/competicoes-campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/na-semi-da-serie-d-retro-itabaiana-anapolis-e-maringa-garantem-acesso-a-serie-c |title=Na semi da Série D, Retrô, Itabaiana, Anápolis e Maringá garantem acesso à Série C |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese |date=2 September 2024}}</ref>

In the finals, Retrô won their first title after defeating [[Anápolis Futebol Clube|Anápolis]] 4–3 on aggregate.<ref>{{cite web|title=Retrô é campeão da Série D com festa em Pernambuco |url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/detalhes/competicoes-campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/retro-e-campeao-da-serie-d-com-festa-em-pernambuco |date=29 September 2024 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

==Format==
In the group stage, the 64 teams were divided into eight groups of eight organized regionally. Top four teams qualified for the round of 32. From the round of 32 on the competition was played as a knock-out tournament with each round contested over two legs.<ref name=CBFRules>{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/202403/20240315192241_815.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição Brasileiro Série D 2024 |language=Portuguese |publisher=CBF |date=14 March 2024}}</ref>

==Group stage==
In the group stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie was only between two teams); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 15).<ref name=CBFRules/>

The top four teams qualified for the round of 32.

==Final stages==
The final stages were played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis. For the round of 16, semi-finals and finals, the best-overall-performance team hosted the second leg. If tied on aggregate, the [[away goals rule]] would not be used, [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played, and the [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] would be used to determine the winners (Regulations Article 20).

For the quarter-finals, teams were seeded based on the table of results of all matches in the competition. The top four seeded teams hosted the second leg.

The four quarter-finals winners were promoted to 2025 Série C.<ref name=CBFRules />

===Round of 32===
The round of 32 was a two-legged knockout tie, with the draw regionalised. ===Quarter-finals===
The draw for the quarter-finals was seeded based on the table of results of all matches in the competition for the qualifying teams. The teams were ranked according to points. If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Fewest red cards; 5. Fewest yellow cards; 6. Draw in the headquarters of the Brazilian Football Confederation (Regulations Article 17).<ref name=CBFRules/>
