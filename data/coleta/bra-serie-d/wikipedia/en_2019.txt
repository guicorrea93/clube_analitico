The '''2019 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the fourth division. The competition started on 4 May and ended on 18 August 2019.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/201903/20190301185455_71.pdf |title=CAMPEONATO BRASILEIRO SÉRIE D - TABELA BÁSICA / EDIÇÃO 2019 |date=1 March 2019 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

Sixty-eight teams competed in the tournament. Sixty-four teams qualified from their state leagues and cups, and four relegated from the [[2018 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]] ([[Joinville Esporte Clube|Joinville]], [[Sociedade Desportiva Juazeirense|Juazeirense]], [[Salgueiro Atlético Clube|Salgueiro]] and [[Tupi Football Club|Tupi]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/pb/futebol/brasileirao-serie-c/noticia/santa-cruz-e-bota-pb-ficam-com-as-ultimas-vagas-no-mata-mata-confira-os-duelos.ghtml |title=Santa Cruz e Bota-PB ficam com as últimas vagas no mata-mata; confira os duelos |date=11 August 2018 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

[[Brusque Futebol Clube|Brusque]], [[Ituano FC|Ituano]], [[Esporte Clube Jacuipense|Jacuipense]] and [[Manaus Futebol Clube|Manaus]] qualified for the semi-finals and were promoted to the [[2020 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/manaus-ituano-brusque-e-jacuipense-conquistam-vaga-na-serie-c |title=Manaus, Ituano, Brusque e Jacuipense conquistam vaga na Série C |date=22 July 2019 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

The finals between [[Brusque Futebol Clube|Brusque]] and [[Manaus Futebol Clube|Manaus]] finished in a 4–4 draw on aggregate, but Brusque clinched their first title by winning 6–5 on penalties.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/am/futebol/brasileirao-serie-d/jogo/18-08-2019/manaus-fc-brusque.ghtml |title=Após empate com público recorde, Brusque converte todos os pênaltis e é campeão |date=18 August 2019 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

==Competition format==
In the first stage, 68 teams were divided into seventeen groups of four, organized regionally. 32 teams (17 winners and 15 runners-up) qualified for the second stage. From the second stage on the competition was played as a knock-out tournament with each round contested over two legs.<ref name=CBFRules>{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/201903/20190301185313_180.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição - Campeonato Brasileiro da Série D 2019 |language=Portuguese |publisher=CBF |date=1 March 2019}}</ref>

==First stage==
In the first stage, each group played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The winners of each group and the best 15 runners-up qualified for the second stage. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie was only between two teams); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 12).<ref name=CBFRules/>

==Second stage==
The Second stage was a two-legged knockout tie, with the draw regionalised. 

===Qualification and draw===
The 32 qualifiers (17 group winners and 15 best performing group runners-up) were divided into two pots. Pot 1 contained the 16 best performing group winners. Pot 2 contained the worst performing group winner and the 15 qualifying group runners-up. In pot 1 the teams were numbered 1 to 16 in numerical order of the group they qualified from. In pot 2 the teams were numbered 17 to 32 in numerical order of the group they qualified from. In the case that one of the qualifying runners-up was from the same group as the worst performing group winner, both teams would be in pot 2 and the group winners would be numbered lower in sequence than the group runners-up.

The teams were ranked according to points. If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. Goal difference; 3. Goals scored; 4. Draw in the headquarters of the Brazilian Football Confederation (Regulations Article 14).<ref name=CBFRules />

To keep the draw regionalised Team 1 played Team 18, Team 2 played Team 17 and this pattern was repeated throughout the draw. The higher numbered team played at home in the first leg.

==Third stage==
The third stage was a two-legged knockout tie, with the draw regionalised. The ties were predetermined from the second stage, with the winners of second stage tie 1 playing the winners of second stage tie 2, etc. The teams were seeded according to their performance in the tournament with the higher-seeded team hosting the second leg.<ref name=CBFRules />

==Final stages==
The final stages were a two leg knockout competition with quarter-finals, semi-finals and finals rounds. The draw for the quarter-finals was seeded based on the table of results of all matches in the competition for the qualifying teams. First played eighth, second played seventh, etc. The top four seeded teams played the second leg at home. The four quarter-final winners were promoted to Série C for 2020. 

The draw for the semi-finals was seeded based on the table of results of all matches in the competition for the qualifying teams. First played fourth, second played third. The top two seeded teams played the second leg at home.

In the finals, the team with the best record in the competition played the second leg at home.<ref name=CBFRules />
