The '''2023 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the fourth division. The competition began on 6 May and ended on 16 September 2023.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/index/brasileirao-sera-paralisado-em-datas-fifa-e-copa-do-brasil-tera-finais |title=Calendário 2023: Brasileirão será paralisado em Datas FIFA pela primeira vez|trans-title=2023 calendar: ''Brasileirão'' will stop on FIFA Dates for the first time|publisher=[[Brazilian Football Confederation|CBF]]|language=pt-BR|date=21 September 2022}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pe/caruaru-regiao/futebol/brasileirao-serie-d/noticia/2023/02/20/com-entrada-do-patrocinense-serie-d-2023-esta-completa-veja-times.ghtml|title=Com entrada do Patrocinense, Série D 2023 está completa; veja times|trans-title=With the entrance of Patrocinense, 2023 Série D is complete; see the teams|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=20 February 2023|accessdate=21 February 2023}}</ref>

Sixty-four teams competed in the tournament. Sixty teams qualified from their state leagues and cups, and four relegated from the [[2022 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]] ([[FC Atlético Cearense|Atlético Cearense]], [[Grêmio Esportivo Brasil|Brasil de Pelotas]], [[Campinense Clube|Campinense]] and [[Ferroviário Atlético Clube (CE)|Ferroviário]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/conheca-os-grupos-da-segunda-fase-da-serie-c |title=Série C: conheça os classificados, os rebaixados e os grupos da segunda fase |publisher=CBF |language=pt |date=13 August 2022}}</ref>

The four semi-finalists, [[Athletic Club (MG)|Athletic]], [[Sociedade Esportiva e Recreativa Caxias do Sul|Caxias]], [[Associação Ferroviária de Esportes|Ferroviária]] and Ferroviário, were promoted to the [[2024 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/serie-d-conhece-os-quatro-semifinalistas-que-conseguiram-acesso-a-seri |title=Série D conhece os quatro semifinalistas que conseguiram acesso à Série C do Brasileirão |publisher=CBF |language=pt |date=3 September 2023}}</ref>

In the finals, Ferroviário won their second title after defeating Ferroviária 2–1 on aggregate.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/ferroviario-ce-e-bicampeao-invicto-da-serie-d |title=Ferroviário-CE é bicampeão invicto da Série D |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese |date=16 September 2023}}</ref> Ferroviário finished the tournament without a single defeat.

==Format==
In the group stage, the 64 teams were divided into eight groups of eight organized regionally. Top four teams qualified for the round of 32. From the round of 32 on the competition was played as a knock-out tournament with each round contested over two legs.<ref name=CBFRules>{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/202303/20230303222329_898.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição Brasileiro Série D 2023 |language=Portuguese |publisher=CBF |date=3 March 2023}}</ref>

==Group stage==
In the group stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie was only between two teams); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 14).<ref name=CBFRules/>

The top four teams qualified for the round of 32.

==Final stages==
The final stages were played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis. For the round of 16, semi-finals and finals, the best-overall-performance team hosted the second leg. If tied on aggregate, the [[away goals rule]] would not be used, [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played, and the [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] would be used to determine the winners (Regulations Article 19).

For the quarter-finals, teams were seeded based on the table of results of all matches in the competition. The top four seeded teams hosted the second leg.

The four quarter-finals winners were promoted to 2024 Série C.<ref name=CBFRules />

===Round of 32===
The round of 32 was a two-legged knockout tie, with the draw regionalised. The matches were played from 29 July to 6 August.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/serie-d-confira-a-tabela-detalhada-do-mata-mata?csrt=411950531250904193 |title=Série D: confira a tabela detalhada do mata-mata |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese |date=25 July 2023}}</ref>

===Quarter-finals===
The draw for the quarter-finals was seeded based on the table of results of all matches in the competition for the qualifying teams. The teams were ranked according to points. If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Fewest red cards; 5. Fewest yellow cards; 6. Draw in the headquarters of the Brazilian Football Confederation (Regulations Article 16).<ref name=CBFRules/>
