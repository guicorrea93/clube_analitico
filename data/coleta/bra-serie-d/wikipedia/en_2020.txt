The '''2020 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the fourth division. The competition was originally scheduled to begin on 3 May and end on 22 November,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro/cbf-publica-calendario-de-2020-com-datas-fifas-livres-e-16-datas-para |title=CBF publica calendário de 2020 com Datas FIFA livres |date=3 October 2019 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref> however due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic in Brazil|COVID-19 pandemic]] the tournament was rescheduled for 6 September 2020 – 6 February 2021.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/index/cbf-publica-calendario-revisado-da-temporada-2020 |title=CBF publica calendário revisado da temporada 2020 |date=9 July 2020 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

Sixty-eight teams competed in the tournament. Sixty-four teams qualified from their state leagues and cups, and four relegated from the [[2019 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]] ([[ABC Futebol Clube|ABC]], [[Atlético Acreano]], [[Globo Futebol Clube|Globo]] and [[Luverdense Esporte Clube|Luverdense]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/pe/futebol/brasileirao-serie-c/noticia/paysandu-e-ypiranga-dentro-remo-fora-serie-c-define-confrontos-do-mata-mata-e-os-rebaixados-confira.ghtml |title=Paysandu e Ypiranga dentro, Remo fora: Série C define jogos do mata-mata e os rebaixados; Confira |date=25 August 2019 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref> Luverdense declined to participate being replaced by [[Sinop Futebol Clube|Sinop]].<ref name=Luverdense>{{cite web|url=http://www.olharesportivo.com.br/noticia.php?codigo=12487 |title=Sinop confirma participação na Série D do Brasileiro |date=17 August 2020 |publisher=Olhar Esportivo |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

The match Guarany de Sobral v Salgueiro, scheduled for 20 September 2020 (Group A3 1st round), was postponed after 12 Guarany de Sobral players tested positive for COVID-19.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/nota-oficial-adiamento-de-partida-da-serie-d-do-campeonato-brasileir |title=Nota oficial - adiamento de partida da Série D do Campeonato Brasileiro |date=20 September 2020 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

In the Group A8 12th round, (14 November 2020), [[Associação Desportiva São Caetano|São Caetano]] players refused to play their away match against [[Clube Náutico Marcílio Dias|Marcílio Dias]] in protest over unpaid wages. Marcílio Dias was awarded a 3–0 win by [[Forfeit (sport)|forfeit]].<ref name="MARSCA">{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/sc/futebol/brasileirao-serie-d/noticia/jogadores-do-sao-caetano-nao-entram-em-campo-e-marcilio-dias-vence-por-wo-na-serie-d-do-brasileiro.ghtml |title=Jogadores do São Caetano não entram em campo, e Marcílio Dias vence por W.O. na Série D do Brasileiro |date=14 November 2019 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

[[Associação Atlética de Altos|Altos]], [[Floresta Esporte Clube|Floresta]], [[Mirassol Futebol Clube|Mirassol]], and [[Grêmio Novorizontino|Novorizontino]] qualified for the semi-finals and were promoted to the [[2021 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/altos-pi-floresta-ce-e-novorizontino-sp-sobem-para-a-serie-c |title=Altos-PI, Floresta-CE, Mirassol-SP e Novorizontino-SP sobem para a Série C do Brasileiro |date=10 January 2021 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

In the finals, Mirassol defeated Floresta 2–0 on aggregate to win their first title.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/competicoes/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/2020/518 |title=Mirassol-SP volta a vencer o Floresta-CE e conquista a Série D 2020 |date=6 February 2021 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

==Format changes==
A preliminary stage was played during this season. In the preliminary stage, eight teams were drawn into four ties, with the winners of each tie advancing to the group stage. The group stage had 64 teams, 60 teams qualified directly and four teams decided in the preliminary stage. The 64 teams were drawn into eight groups of eight teams each. The top four teams of each group qualified for the round of 32.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/pi/futebol/brasileirao-serie-d/noticia/cbf-muda-serie-d-em-2020-amplia-campeonato-ate-novembro-e-altera-formato-da-fase-de-grupos.ghtml |title=CBF muda Série D em 2020, amplia campeonato até novembro e altera formato da fase de grupos |publisher=Globo |date=3 October 2019 |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

==Competition format==
In the preliminary stage, eight teams from the worst ranked federations in the CBF ranking were drawn into four ties, with the winners of each tie advancing to the group stage. In the group stage, the remaining 60 teams and the 4 four teams qualified from the preliminary stage were divided into eight groups of eight organized regionally. Top four teams qualified for the round of 32. From the round of 32 on the competition was played as a knock-out tournament with each round contested over two legs.<ref name=CBFRules>{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/202012/20201210170709_414.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição - Campeonato Brasileiro Série D de 2020 |language=Portuguese |publisher=CBF |date=3 March 2020}}</ref>

==Preliminary stage==
It was played from 6 to 13 September 2020.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/brasileiro-serie-d-datas-definidas-para-a-fase-preliminar |title=Brasileiro Série D 2020: datas definidas para a Fase Preliminar |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese |date=18 August 2020}}</ref> The lowest-seeded teams from the eight worst ranked federations in the 2020 CBF ranking (Amazonas, Distrito Federal, Mato Grosso do Sul, Espírito Santo, Tocantins, Rondônia, Amapá, and Roraima) competed to decide four places in the group stage.

Each tie was played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis. If tied on aggregate, the [[away goals rule]] would not be used, [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played, and the [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] would be used to determine the winners (Regulations Article 18).<ref name=CBFRules/>

==Group stage==
In the group stage, each group played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie was only between two teams); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 13).<ref name=CBFRules/>

The top four teams qualified for the round of 32.

==Final stages==
The final stages were played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis. For the round of 16, semi-finals and finals, the best-overall-performance team hosted the second leg. If tied on aggregate, the [[away goals rule]] would not be used, [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played, and the [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] would be used to determine the winners (Regulations Article 18).

For the quarter-finals, teams were seeded based on the table of results of all matches in the competition. The top four seeded teams played the second leg at home.

The four quarter-final winners were promoted to 2021 Série C.<ref name=CBFRules />
