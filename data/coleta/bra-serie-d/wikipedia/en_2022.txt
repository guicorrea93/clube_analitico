The '''2022 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the fourth division. The competition began on 17 April and ended on 25 September 2022.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/index/cbf-apresenta-calendario-do-futebol-brasileiro-em-2022|title=CBF apresenta calendário do futebol brasileiro em 2022|trans-title=CBF presents the calendar of the Brazilian football in 2022|publisher=[[Brazilian Football Confederation]]|language=pt-BR|date=4 November 2021|accessdate=17 November 2021}}</ref>

Sixty-four teams competed in the tournament. Sixty teams qualified from their state leagues and cups, and four relegated from the [[2021 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]] ([[Esporte Clube Jacuipense|Jacuipense]], [[Oeste Futebol Clube|Oeste]], [[Paraná Clube|Paraná]] and [[Santa Cruz Futebol Clube|Santa Cruz]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pe/futebol/times/santa-cruz/noticia/serie-d-de-2022-tem-55-clubes-garantidos-veja-possiveis-rivais-do-santa-cruz.ghtml|title=Série D de 2022 tem 55 clubes garantidos; veja possíveis rivais do Santa Cruz|trans-title=Série D have 55 clubs assured; see possible rivals of Santa Cruz|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=22 September 2021|accessdate=19 October 2021}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.fgf.esp.br/418113-noticia-jacuipense-ba-completa-o-quarteto-de-rebaixados-da-serie-c2021-para-a-serie-d2022|title=Jacuipense (BA) completa o quarteto de rebaixados da Série C/2021 para a Série D/2022|trans-title=Jacuipense complete the quartet of relegated from the 2021 Série C to the 2022 Série D|publisher=[[Federação Goiana de Futebol]]|language=pt-BR|date=25 September 2021|accessdate=19 October 2021}}</ref>

The four semi-finalists, [[Amazonas Futebol Clube|Amazonas]], [[América Futebol Clube (RN)|América de Natal]], [[Pouso Alegre Futebol Clube|Pouso Alegre]] and [[São Bernardo Futebol Clube|São Bernardo]], were promoted to the [[2023 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/america-rn-amazonas-pouso-alegre-e-sao-bernardo-sobem-para-a-serie-c |title=América-RN, Amazonas, Pouso Alegre e Sâo Bernardo sobem para a Série C |publisher=CBF |language=pt |date=29 August 2022}}</ref>

In the finals, América de Natal won their first title after defeating Pouso Alegre 2–1 on aggregate.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-d/fora-de-casa-america-rn-e-superado-mas-conquista-titulo-inedito-no-b |title=Fora de casa, América-RN é superado, mas conquista título inédito no Brasileirão da Série D |publisher=CBF |language=pt |date=25 September 2022}}</ref>

==Competition format==
In the group stage, the 64 teams were divided into eight groups of eight organized regionally. Top four teams qualified for the round of 32. From the round of 32 on the competition was played as a knock-out tournament with each round contested over two legs.<ref name=CBFRules>{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/202202/20220216223106_921.pdf |title=Regulamento Específico da Competição Campeonato Brasileiro Série D – 2022 |language=Portuguese |publisher=CBF |date=16 February 2022}}</ref>

==Group stage==
In the group stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis. The teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Head-to-head (if the tie was only between two teams); 5. Fewest red cards; 6. Fewest yellow cards; 7. Draw in the headquarters of the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] (Regulations Article 13).<ref name=CBFRules/>

The top four teams qualified for the round of 32.

==Final stages==
The final stages were played on a home-and-away [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] basis. For the round of 16, semi-finals and finals, the best-overall-performance team hosted the second leg. If tied on aggregate, the [[away goals rule]] would not be used, [[extra time (association football)|extra time]] would not be played, and the [[penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]] would be used to determine the winners (Regulations Article 18).

For the quarter-finals, teams were seeded based on the table of results of all matches in the competition. The top four seeded teams hosted the second leg.

The four quarter-finals winners were promoted to 2023 Série C.<ref name=CBFRules />

===Round of 32===
The round of 32 was a two-legged knockout tie, with the draw regionalised. ===Quarter-finals===
The draw for the quarter-finals was seeded based on the table of results of all matches in the competition for the qualifying teams. The teams were ranked according to points. If tied on points, the following criteria would be used to determine the ranking: 1. Wins; 2. [[Goal difference]]; 3. Goals scored; 4. Fewest red cards; 5. Fewest yellow cards; 6. Draw in the headquarters of the Brazilian Football Confederation (Regulations Article 15).<ref name=CBFRules/>
