About this document

Source for league information: El Universal, Venezolan newspaper with Venezolan journalist Hermán “Chiquitín” Ettedgui who wrote the notes as Baby Chiqui. He had a section on football named “Taquitos futboleros”). This journalist and outstanding football player too was champion scorer of tournament with 12 goals with Unión Sport Club.

Source for cup tournament: Foro Vinotinto
