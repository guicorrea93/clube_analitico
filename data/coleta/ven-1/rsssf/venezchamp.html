Venezuela - List of Champions and Runners Up

The first report of a football match in Venezuela dates from 1876, when on the occasion of the celebration of the Virgen del Carmen (on 16 July) a match was organised by a Welsh teacher, A.W. Simpson, in El Caratal (near El Callao); participants included British and Trinidadian players.

NB: Unión Atlético Táchira was a merger of Atlético San Cristóbal and Deportivo Táchira at the
    end of the 1985 season.  In 1999 the team reverted to the name Deportivo Táchira.

    Universidad de Los Andes (Mérida) were relocated and renamed Atlético Zulia (Maracaibo)
    during the 1996/97 and 1997/98 seasons.

    Trujillanos (Valera) were relocated and renamed Internacional de Lara (Barquisimeto) after
    the 1997/98 season.

    ItalChacao was a temporary merger of Deportivo Italia and Deportivo Chacao.
