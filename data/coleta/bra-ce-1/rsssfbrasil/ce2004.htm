Championship Finals

[Aug 21 and Sep 19]
Fortaleza    n/p   n/p  Ceará

Alleging that they would play 4 times in 8 days, Fortaleza successfully convinced the Ceará state FA to postpone the
finals.  However, Ceará SC disagreed with the FA decision and refused their decision to end the tournament without a 
champion.  Ceará SC filed a protest in the TJD (Sports Justice Tribunal) to win the tournament and was unsuccessful, 
since TJD ordered the clubs to play the finals.  Again a new agreement was made by the FA to end the tournament, and 
again Ceará SC rejected it.  This time, the club went to the STJD (Supreme Sports Justice Tribunal) who maintained the
decision for the clubs to play the finals.  Ceará SC tried, in vain, to contest that decision in a common justice court 
by using third parties and did not show to play Fortaleza.  STJD declared Fortaleza the Ceará state champions, but were 
convinced by the FA and even Fortaleza not to punish Ceará SC with a harsh suspension.
