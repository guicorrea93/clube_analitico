Ceará State Championship 1915

Organized by Liga Metropolitana Cearense de Futebol.
 
Participating Clubs

Ceará Sporting Club (Fortaleza)
Maranguape Foot-ball Club (Maranguape)
Rio Negro Foot-ball Club (Fortaleza)
Stela Foot-ball Club (Fortaleza)

Final
 
[Nov 7]
Ceará		2-1	Stela
Humberto Ribeiro	Pedro Riquet
Pacatuba
Venue: Fortaleza (Ceará)
Stadium: Campo do Prado
Referee: Lúcio Bauerfeldt
Ceará: Aldo - Meton, Garcia - Ninito, Silveira, Rola - Abreu, Pacatuba, Humberto Ribeiro, Gotardo, Guilherme.
Stela: Gilberto - Oscar Cabral, Oscar Loureiro - Carlos Alberto, João Gentil, Clóvis Holanda - Pedro Riquet, Clodoveu, J. Bruno, Walter Barroso e Walter Olsen.
 
*******Ceará was 1915 Ceará State champions********
