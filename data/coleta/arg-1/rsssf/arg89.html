. In this season were give 3 points to winner, and 1 when a match ended tied.
  But, in this case, the match involved was defined by shootouts; winner team
  obtained one extra point. For example:
  Racing Club won 10 matches and 3 on P.K.
  It lost 3 matches and 3 on P.K.
  Then, 3x10 + 2x3 + 3 = 39 points.
. This tournament is part of the Primera División 1988/1989 championship.
