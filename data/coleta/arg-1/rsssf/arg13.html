[Oct 8, Wed]
Council resolutions:
The championship had to be played in a double round-robin format.
But, as lack of time the championship was reorganized as follow:
. To cancel match Comercio v. Banfield, played on October 5.
. To distribute teams in three groups.
. To retain points won by each team.
. To play return matches only with teams in the same group.
. To include the last four teams in Group C to play for avoiding relegation.
. To make the standing of top 11 teams at October 8 ordered as follow:
.. Top 7 teams by lost point and goal average.
.. Four remaining teams by won points and goal average.
. Group A included teams in odd positions, except the 11th.
. Group B included teams in even positions and Comercio, the number 11.
