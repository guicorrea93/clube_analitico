CHAMPIONSHIP PLAYOFF: 


[Aug 22 Sun, 1926]


Huracán	                1-1     Nueva Chicago             [at Sportivo Barracas]


  [G.Stábile 65; E.Maure 4]


 


Huracán:		A.Ceresetto; C.Nóbile, J.Pratto; L.Molinari, A.Chiesa, C.Federico;


			A.Loizo, E.Gaztelú, G.Stábile, A.Chiesa, C.Onzari.


Nueva Chicago:		C.García; J.Locatti, J.Voltura; V.Capano, E.Semino, E.Solari;


			J.Villagra, E.Maure, F.Minalla, S.Varela, R.Corvetto.


 


Referee:		L.Celleri.


Sent off:		S.Varela 67.


Note:			Suspended at 83, Nueva Chicago abbandoned the field.


 


    


Nueva Chicago did not play the extra time and the match was awarded to Huracán. 


On Aug 24 Nueva Chicago abbandoned the Asociación Argentina de Football. 


On Aug 25 was inscribed in the Asociación Amateurs de Football.



 


 


 


Notes:
  


. Del Plata-Temperley, Universal-Palermo and Alvear-Argentino de Quilmes
  were never played.
. Boca Juniors travelled to Europe and only played 7 matches.


. Then, 18 matches were no disputed.
. Boca Alumni 0-0 Sportivo Barracas was abandoned after 65 minutes and was never
  finished, the result stood.


. Sportivo del Norte changed name to Colegiales.   


. Platense changed name to Retiro and later to Universal.
. Villa Urquiza changed name to General San Martín.
