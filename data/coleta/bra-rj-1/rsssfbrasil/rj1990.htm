Final Phase

Obs.: The championship was interrupted until the end of July due to the
participation of players from Botafogo, Fluminense and Vasco in the World
Cup.
First Round:

22/Jul Vasco 1-0 Fluminense
       Botafogo bye
Second Round:

29/Jul Botafogo 1-0 Vasco

Botafogo considered itself the champions, while Vasco asked for an extra
time, arguing that both teams had won two points. Botafogo refused to play
the extra-time and, due to this, Vasco considered itself the champions.

In 09/Aug, Botafogo was legally confirmed as champions of 1990.
