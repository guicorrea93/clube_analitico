NB: the 2005/06 season starts August 20 and is divided into
    Apertura, Clausura, and play-offs;

    at the end of the Apertura, the 3 worst teams of the Torneo
    Uruguay Especial and the Apertura are relegated while 2 teams
    from the 2nd level are promoted;

    at the end of the Clausura (start Feb 2006, played with 
    17 clubs), the 2 worst teams over 2005/06 (average points) 
    are relegated while 1 club is promoted from 2nd level;

    the winners of Apertura and Clausura play a 'semifinal'
    over 2 or 3 matches; the winners of that match play the 
    best team in the aggregate table for the 2005/06 championship;

    finally, the 'Copa Artigas 2006' will be played between the
    top-6 teams in the aggregate table of Apertura and Clausura
    as a single round robin to determine the remaining places in
    the Copa Libertadores 2007 and Copa Sudamericana 2006;

    in 2006/07 there will be 16 clubs in the top level.
