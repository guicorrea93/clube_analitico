Uruguay 1932 Championship

The General Assembly of the AUF (Asocación Uruguaya de Fútbol) formed the Uruguayan
Professional League on 29th April 1932, composed of the 11 teams that remained in
First Level after 1931.

It would later descend to 10 teams, as on 11th May 1932 CA Capurro and Olimpia FC merged
into CA River Plate.
