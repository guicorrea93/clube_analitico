The '''1966–67 Egyptian Premier League''', was the 17th season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 30 September 1966 and concluded on 7 May 1967.
[[Ismaily SC|Ismaily]] won the league for the first time in the club's history.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1966–67]]</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry6667.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201122151440/http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry6667.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=November 22, 2020|title= 1966–67 season}}</ref>
