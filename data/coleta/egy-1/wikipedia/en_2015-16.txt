The '''2015–16 Egyptian Premier League''' was the 57th season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The fixtures were announced on 20 September 2015.<ref>{{cite news |url=http://efa.com.eg/article4159.htm |title=إعلان موعد قرعة الدوري الممتاز |website=efa.com.eg |date=16 September 2015 |language=ar |access-date=16 September 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150918002026/http://www.efa.com.eg/article4159.htm |archive-date=18 September 2015 |url-status=dead |df=dmy-all }}</ref> The season began on 20 October 2015 and concluded on 9 July 2016.<ref>{{cite news |url=http://www.kooora.com/?n=432281&cz=10805&o=n10805 |title=تحديد الموعد الرسمي لقرعة الدوري الممتاز 2015/16 والموعد الرسمي لبدايته|website=kooora.com |date=15 September 2015 |language=ar |access-date=16 September 2015}}</ref>

[[Zamalek SC|Zamalek]] began the season as defending champions of the [[2014–15 Egyptian Premier League|2014–15 season]]. [[Aswan SC|Aswan]], [[El-Entag El-Harby SC|El Entag El Harby]] and [[Ghazl El Mahalla SC|Ghazl El Mahalla]] entered as the three promoted teams from the [[2014–15 Egyptian Second Division]].

On 24 June 2016, [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] won the title with two games to spare after a 2–1 away win over [[Ismaily SC|Ismaily]] to secure their 38th [[Egyptian Premier League]] title.
