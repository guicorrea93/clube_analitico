The '''1961–62 Egyptian Premier League''', was the 12th season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 6 October 1961 and concluded on 22 April 1962.
[[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] won the league for the eleventh time in the club's history.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1961–62]]</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry6162.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201122151338/http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry6162.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=November 22, 2020|title= 1961–62 season}}</ref>
