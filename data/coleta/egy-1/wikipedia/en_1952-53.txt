The '''1952–53 Egyptian Premier League''' started in November 1952. [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] were crowned champions for the fourth time in the club's history.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1952–53]]{{Circular reference|date=July 2020}}</ref>
