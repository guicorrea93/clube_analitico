The '''1964–65 Egyptian Premier League''', was the 15th season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 6 November 1964 and concluded on 11 June 1965.
[[Zamalek SC|Zamalek]] won the league for the third time in the club's history.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1964–65]]</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry6465.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201122151400/http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry6465.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=November 22, 2020|title= 1964–65 season}}</ref>
