The '''1958–59 Egyptian Premier League''', was the ninth season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 3 October 1958 and concluded on 17 April 1959.
Defending champions [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] won their 9th consecutive and 9th overall Egyptian Premier League title in the club history.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1959–58]]{{Circular reference|date=March 2021}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry5960.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201026035139/http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry5960.html |url-status=usurped |archive-date=October 26, 2020 |title= 1958–59 season}}</ref>
