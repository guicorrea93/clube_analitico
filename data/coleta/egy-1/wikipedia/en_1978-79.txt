The '''1978–79 Egyptian Premier League''', was the 22nd season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 13 October 1978 and concluded on 6 April 1979.
[[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] won the league for the 15th time in the club's history.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1978–79]]</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry7879.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210114201707/http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry7879.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=January 14, 2021|title= 1978–79 season}}</ref>
