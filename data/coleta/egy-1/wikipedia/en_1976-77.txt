The '''1976–77 Egyptian Premier League''', was the 20th season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 3 September 1976 and concluded on 6 June 1977.
[[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] won the league for the 14th time in the club's history.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1976–77]]</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry7677.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201126103912/http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry7677.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=November 26, 2020|title= 1976–77 season}}</ref>
