The '''2018–19 Egyptian Premier League''', also known as '''The [[Telecom Egypt|WE]] League''' for sponsorship purposes, was the 60th season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 31 July 2018 and concluded on 28 July 2019. Fixtures for the 2018–19 season were announced on 8 July 2018.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2018/07/08/egyptian-league-fixtures-ahly-zamalek/ |title=Egyptian Premier League 2018/19 fixtures: Ahly clash with Ismaily, Zamalek face Petrojet in opener |work=[[KingFut]] |date=8 July 2018 |access-date=17 July 2018}}</ref>

[[El Gouna FC|El Gouna]], [[Nogoom FC|Nogoom]] and [[Haras El Hodoud SC|Haras El Hodoud]] entered as the promoted teams from the [[2017–18 Egyptian Second Division]]. They replaced [[El Raja SC|El Raja]], [[Tanta SC|Tanta]] and [[Al Nasr SC (Egypt)|Al Nasr]] who were relegated to the [[2018–19 Egyptian Second Division]].

Defending champions [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] won their 4th consecutive and 41st overall Egyptian Premier League title on 24 July 2019, following their 3–1 away win against [[Al Mokawloon Al Arab SC|Al Mokawloon Al Arab]].

===Al Masry return to Port Said===
From this season on, the [[Egyptian Football Association]] (EFA) was supposed to allow Al Masry to play all local home matches at [[Al Masry Club Stadium]] (previously known as Port Said Stadium) in [[Port Said]], after the club successfully acquired the stadium from the [[Port Said Governorate|Governorate of Port Said]] on 5 January 2016.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://almasryclub.com/?p=4816 |title=محطات مهمة في تاريخ ستاد الدوري المصري |trans-title=History of Al Masry Club Stadium |language=ar |work=[[Al Masry SC|Al Masry]] Online |date=10 February 2018 |access-date=24 March 2018 |archive-date=15 July 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180715131134/http://almasryclub.com/?p=4816 |url-status=dead }}</ref> However the local security authorities refused this decision, and the club ended up playing their home matches at various stadiums in different cities. The EFA banned Al Masry Club Stadium from hosting all football or sport activities after the [[Port Said Stadium riot]] occurred in 2012, and Al Masry played all of their local matches, both home and away, at [[Borg El Arab Stadium]] in [[Alexandria]] and at [[Ismailia Stadium]] in [[Ismailia]] for [[2017 CAF Confederation Cup|CAF matches]] since then.

===Al Assiouty Sport takeover===
On 18 June 2018, Mahmoud Al Assiouty, president of [[Al Assiouty Sport]], announced that the club was officially sold to two investors from [[Saudi Arabia]]. The club name was changed to [[Pyramids FC|Pyramids Football Club]], and the deal was later confirmed on 27 June 2018.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youm7.com/story/2018/6/18/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B3%D9%8A%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%89-%D9%8A%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%86-%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%89-%D9%88%D8%AA%D8%BA%D9%8A%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D9%85%D9%87-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%8B/3838324 |title=الأسيوطى يعلن بيع النادى وتغيير اسمه للأهرام رسمياً |language=ar |trans-title=Mahmoud Al Assiouty, president of Al Assiouty Sport, announces the selling of the club and confirms renaming it to Pyramids FC |work=[[Youm7]] |date=18 June 2018 |access-date=8 July 2018}}</ref>

===Nogoom El Mostakbal rebranding===
On 29 July 2018, just two days before the start of the season, [[Nogoom El Mostakbal FC|Nogoom El Mostakbal]] announced on their official [[Facebook]] page that the club name has been changed to [[Nogoom FC|Nogoom Football Club]]. Club president Mohamed El Tawila stated that the decision to rename the club came after they reached their goal by promoting to the [[Egyptian Premier League]] for the first time. The old name, Nogoom El Mostakbal, in [[Arabic]], means "Future Stars", and the new name, Nogoom, means "Stars".<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.facebook.com/Nogoomfc/photos/a.624023187715753.1073741829.356617501122991/1749125341872193/ |title=بيان من النادي |language=ar |trans-title=Club statement |work=[[Nogoom FC|Nogoom]] on [[Facebook]] |date=29 July 2018 |access-date=31 July 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2018/07/30/nogoom-el-mostakbal-changes-name-and-logo-after-promotion-to-egyptian-premier-league/ |title=Nogoom El Mostakbal changes name and logo after promotion |work=[[KingFut]] |date=30 July 2018 |access-date=31 July 2018}}</ref>

Eighteen teams competed in the league - the top fifteen teams from the previous season, and three teams promoted from the [[2017–18 Egyptian Second Division|Second Division]].

'''Teams promoted to the Egyptian Premier League'''<br />
The first team to be promoted was [[Haras El Hodoud SC|Haras El Hodoud]] from Group C, following their 1–0 away win against [[Al Hammam SC|Al Hammam]] on 10 April 2018. The [[Alexandria]]n side returned to the Premier League after staying only two season in the Second Division, and participated in the top flight for the fifteenth time in their history.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2018/04/10/haras-el-hodood-promotion-premier-league/ |title=Haras El Hodoud clinch promotion back to Egyptian Premier League |work=[[KingFut]] |date=10 April 2018 |access-date=10 April 2018}}</ref>

The second team to be promoted was [[El Gouna FC|El Gouna]] from Group A, following their 1–0 away win against [[Al Salam SC (Esna)|Al Salam]] on 11 April 2018. El Gouna earned a spot in the Premier League for the fifth time in the club's history, having played four seasons in the top flight before with the [[2014–15 Egyptian Premier League|2014–15 season]] being the most recent.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2018/04/11/el-gouna-seal-promotion-premier-league/ |title=El Gouna seal Egyptian Premier League promotion |work=[[KingFut]] |date=11 April 2018 |access-date=12 April 2018}}</ref>

The third team to be promoted was [[Nogoom FC|Nogoom]] (which was known as [[Nogoom El Mostakbal FC|Nogoom El Mostakbal]] during that time) from Group B, following their 1–0 home win against [[Suez SC|Montakhab Suez]] on 18 April 2018. Nogoom managed to secure the promotion spot after a fierce contest that lasted until the last day of the league with [[Tersana SC|Tersana]], who lost 1–0 to [[Gomhoriat Shebin SC|Gomhoriat Shebin]] on the same day to confirm the promotion of Nogoom El Mostakbal to the Premier League for the first time in the club's history.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2018/04/18/nogoom-el-mostakbal-promotion-pl/ |title=Nogoom El Mostakbal clinch promotion to Premier League for first time in history |work=[[KingFut]] |date=18 April 2018 |access-date=18 April 2018}}</ref>

'''Teams relegated to the Egyptian Second Division'''<br />
The first club to be relegated was [[Al Nasr SC (Egypt)|Al Nasr]], who suffered an immediate return to the Second Division following a 2–1 away defeat to [[Al Masry SC|Al Masry]] on 12 April 2018.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2018/04/12/al-nasr-mathematically-relegated-from-the-egyptian-premier-league/ |title=Al Nasr mathematically relegated from the Egyptian Premier League |work=[[KingFut]] |date=12 April 2018 |access-date=12 April 2018}}</ref>

The second club to be relegated was [[Tanta SC|Tanta]], their stay in the Premier League came to an end after spending only two season in the top flight following a 1–2 home defeat to [[Al Masry SC|Al Masry]] on 21 April 2018.<ref name="relegation">{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2018/04/21/al-ragaa-tanta-relegated-as-wadi-degla-clinches-safety/ |title=El Raja, Tanta relegated as Wadi Degla clinches safety |work=[[KingFut]] |date=21 April 2018 |access-date=22 April 2018}}</ref>

The third club to be relegated was [[El Raja SC|El Raja]], who also suffered an immediate return to the Second Division after [[Wadi Degla SC|Wadi Degla]] secured the last safe spot in the Premier League following a 2–1 home win against [[El Entag El Harby SC|El Entag El Harby]] on 21 April 2018.<ref name="relegation"/>

===Foreign players===
Clubs could have a maximum of four foreign players registered during the season. Clubs could not sign foreign players unless these players had played in the first or second tier in their countries. Clubs also could not sign any foreign goalkeepers. In addition, each club could register a total of two players from [[Palestine]] or [[Syria]]; those players were treated as [[Egypt]]ians, and did not count as foreign players.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://twitter.com/EFA/status/998937490877698048/ |title=أعلنت لجنة شئون اللاعبين بالاتحاد المصري لكرة القدم تعليمات القيد وانتقالات اللاعبين للموسم الجديد 2018 / 2019 على النحو التالي |trans-title=Players registration rules for the 2018–19 season |language=ar |work=[[Egyptian Football Association]] on [[Twitter]] |date=22 May 2018 |access-date=14 August 2018}}</ref>

*Players name in '''bold''' indicates the player is registered during the mid-season transfer window.
*Players name in ''ITALICS'' indicates the player have left the club during the mid-season transfer window.
