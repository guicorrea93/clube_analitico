The '''2009–10 Egyptian Premier League''' is the fifty-third season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]] since its establishment in 1948. A total of 16 teams are contesting the league, with [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] the defending champions for the fifth year in a row and for the thirty-fourth time in total. The Egyptian season began on 6 August 2009 and ended on 17 May 2010.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20090714115437/http://www.efa.com.eg/TournamentMatchTable.aspx?TournamentID=51&TournamentNameID=47&TournamentName=%C7%E1%CF%E6%D1%EC+%C7%E1%DA%C7%E3+%C7%E1%E3%D5%D1%EC+2009/++2010&TournamentTypeID=1 Egyptian Premier League fixture list] www.[[Egyptian Football Association|efa]].com.eg (Arabic)</ref>

The defending champions, [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]], won its sixth league title in a row after a 3–0 win against [[El Mansoura SC|Mansoura]].<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20100501203454/http://www.fifa.com/worldfootball/clubfootball/news/newsid=1202643.html#al+ahly+again+crowned+egyptian+kings "Al Ahly again crowned Egyptian kings"] FIFA, 2010-4-30. Retrieved on 17 May 2010.</ref>

===Teams and stadiums===
{{further|:Category:Football venues in Egypt|List of football clubs in Egypt}}
The recently opened [[Borg El Arab Stadium]] in [[Alexandria]] its capacity 80,000 is currently mainly used for international competitions for [[Egypt national football team|Egyptian National Team]], it was constructed as part of Egypt's failed bid to land the [[2010 FIFA World Cup]].  However, there is speculation that League sides from Alexandria will eventually call it their home grounds. [[Cairo International Stadium]] is home to Egypt giants [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] and [[Zamalek SC|Al-Zamalek]] and [[Ismailia Stadium]] is home to [[Ismaily|Al-Ismaily]].

==Broadcasting==
When [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] announced that their satellite channel will have the exclusive satellite rights of airing the team's domestic games but they will be broadcast terrestrially as usual the problem began.<ref>[http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=30543 Ahli to launch satellite channel in June] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120223150927/http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=30543 |date=23 February 2012 }} [[filgoal]].com</ref><ref>[http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=45365 Ahli TV to broadcast team games exclusively] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120223150934/http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=45365 |date=23 February 2012 }} [[filgoal]].com</ref> After that [[Egyptian Football Association|EFA]] have earlier announced their rejection of Al-Ahly's proposal that club's channel and ESC would be the only satellite broadcaster due to the Association's running contract with other satellite networks to air all league games. After that Al-Ahly president [[Hassan Hamdy]] confirmed that the club is still keen on maintaining their rights of exclusive satellite broadcasting to the club's league football games.<ref>[http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=46871 Ahli still coy on broadcasting rights] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120223150949/http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=46871 |date=23 February 2012 }} [[filgoal]].com</ref> In October 2008 A Bahraini corporation has made a bid of EGP 200&nbsp;million to acquire the Egyptian League broadcasting exclusive rights, The company is also interested in televising and promoting the Egyptian cup games as well as Egypt's international matches, It is worth noting that the EFA's current EGP 18-million deal with several satellite channels will run out by the end of the ongoing season.<ref>[http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=47918 Bahrain Corporation interested in Egyptian league] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120223151000/http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=47918 |date=23 February 2012 }} [[filgoal]].com</ref> After that several TV networks have decided to sue the Egyptian FA (EFA) and the public Radio and TV Union (ERTU) after being prevented from broadcasting a league game for Ahly.<ref>[http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=48250 Satellite channels to sue EFA over Ahli row] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120223151023/http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=48250 |date=23 February 2012 }} [[filgoal]].com</ref> [[Zamalek SC|Zamalek]] chairman Mohamed Amer said his club might follow in the footsteps of arch-rivals [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] in demanding the exclusive rights to broadcast their home league games.<ref>[http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=49331 Broadcasting rights row intensifies] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120223151033/http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=49331 |date=23 February 2012 }} [[filgoal]].com</ref> January, 2009 [[Egyptian Football Association]] (EFA) president Samir Zaher said that the league games' broadcasting row will be resolved before the upcoming Cairo derby.<ref>[http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=50998 Zaher vows to resolve broadcasting conflict] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120223151040/http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=50998 |date=23 February 2012 }} [[filgoal]].com</ref> In April 2009 the Egyptian FA reached an agreement with IMG Media Company to sell the rights of broadcasting the Premier League next season, The company made an amazing offer, guaranteeing at least a EGP 140million revenue. They will also cost us nothing if they failed to reach that target.<ref>[http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=54129 EFA agree match-broadcasting deal with British company] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120223151045/http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=54129 |date=23 February 2012 }} [[filgoal]].com</ref> Before the [[Egyptian Super Cup]], Egyptian FA president Samir Zaher said the local [[Egyptian Super Cup|Super Cup]] between [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] and [[Haras El Hodood]], which is scheduled for 21 July, would not be televised as the row over broadcasting rights intensified, TV viewers will not be able to watch the season curtain-raiser after a seven-member committee failed to reach an agreement with the Egyptian Radio and Television Union (ERTU) over airing domestic games for next season, As the committee, which includes several clubs like [[Cairo]] duo [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] and [[El Zamalek]] in addition to the FA, did not accept the [[Egyptian Radio and Television Union]] (ERTU)'s offer to broadcast league and cup matches for EGP90&nbsp;million.<ref>[http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=57732 Egyptian FA decide not to air Super Cup] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120223151057/http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=57732 |date=23 February 2012 }} [[filgoal]].com</ref> After 4 days the [[ERTU]] has acquired the broadcasting rights of the Super Cup match between [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] and [[Haras El Hodood]] for EGP1.25&nbsp;million,<ref>[http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=57847 ERTU acquires Super Cup broadcasting rights] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120223151111/http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=57847 |date=23 February 2012 }} [[filgoal]].com</ref> The first week of the Egyptian premier league have not been broadcasting on the cable channels but only on the state TV channels. On 19 August 2009 (the second week of the league) Egyptian premier league clubs did not allow the state TV to air Wednesday's Egyptian Premier League matches after both parties failed to reach an agreement over the ongoing broadcasting saga, Only the first half of [[Haras El Hodood]] and [[El Geish]] match was broadcast while usual TV viewers had to listen to radio to follow game between title holders Al-Ahly and [[Ittihad El-Shorta]]. Zamalek will follow in the footsteps of those teams after deciding not to broadcast Thursday's match against [[Petrojet SC|Petrojet]] as Zamalek's spokesman Sabri Serag said on the club's official website.<ref>[http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=58718 Egyptian clubs refuse to broadcast league games] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120223151442/http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=58718 |date=23 February 2012 }} [[filgoal]].com</ref> Finally after nearly two years and half the Egyptian Premier League broadcasting saga had been resolved, on Thursday 20 August 2009 this means that Thursday's league games will be broadcast live.  Local Channel 2 and the Egyptian Satellite Channel are entitled to broadcast games for free, while private cable networks will be charged EGP 8million each per season.<ref>[http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=58743 Broadcasting saga resolved] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120223151542/http://new.filgoal.com/English/News.aspx?NewsID=58743 |date=23 February 2012 }} [[filgoal]].com</ref>
