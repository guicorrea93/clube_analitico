The '''2025–26 Egyptian Premier League''' season was the 67th edition of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top professional league for [[association football]] clubs in the [[Egyptian football league system]], since its establishment in 1948.

The championship phase concluded on 20 May 2026, with [[Zamalek SC]] securing their 15th league title following a 1–0 victory over [[Ceramica Cleopatra FC|Ceramica Cleopatra]] in the final round.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.masrawy.com/sports/sports_news/details/2026/5/20/2991376/ |title=الزمالك بطلاً للدوري الممتاز للموسم 2025-2026 بعد فوزه على سيراميكا |publisher=Masrawy |date=20 May 2026 |access-date=21 May 2026 |language=ar}}</ref> Zamalek clinched the championship after a fierce race with [[Pyramids FC]], finishing at the top of the championship group with 56 points, a two-point margin ahead of Pyramids who finished as runners-up with 54 points.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.kooora.com/%D9%83%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D9%82%D8%AF%D9%85/%D8%A3%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1/bltd988afe84cbae59c |title=الزمالك يتوج بالدوري المصري للمرة 15 في تاريخه متفوقاً على بيراميدز بفارق نقطتين |publisher=Kooora |date=20 May 2026 |access-date=21 May 2026 |language=ar}}</ref> Meanwhile, the relegation phase continues until 27 May to determine the remaining two teams to be relegated alongside Ismaily and Pharco.

==Format==

On 18 May 2025, the Egyptian Pro League Association announced the cancellation of relegation for the 2024–25 season.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.kingfut.com/2025/05/18/major-egyptian-premier-league-reforms-no-relegation-expanded-league-for-next-season/ |title=Major Egyptian Premier League Reforms: No Relegation, Expanded League for Next Season |publisher=KingFut |date=18 May 2025 }}</ref> Three teams were promoted to the Premier League, increasing the size of the league from 18 to 21 teams.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.youm7.com/story/2025/8/8/قبل-انطلاق-الإيجبشيان-ليج-تعرف-على-نظام-الدوري-المصري-بالنسخة/7082293 |title=قبل انطلاق الإيجبشيان ليج.. تعرف على نظام الدوري المصري بالنسخة الجديدة |publisher=youm7.com |language=ar |date=8 August 2025 }}</ref>

The league consists of 21 teams in a single group and is played in two phases. In the first phase, teams face each other once in a single-leg tie, after which the league splits into two groups. Phase two features a championship group for the teams finishing first to seventh and a relegation group for teams placed eighth to twenty-first. The first-place team in the championship group is crowned league champion and qualifies for the [[CAF Champions League]] alongside the runner-up, while the third-place team qualifies for the [[CAF Confederation Cup]]. Four of the teams in the relegation group get relegated to the [[Egyptian Second Division A]] at the end of the season.<ref>{{cite web |title=Egyptian Premier League 2025/26: Draw Date and New Format Revealed |url=https://sports.yahoo.com/article/egyptian-premier-league-2025-26-171300165.html |website=sports.yahoo.com |publisher=yahoo!sports |access-date=9 August 2025}}</ref>

A total of three teams were promoted to the league from the [[2024–25 Egyptian Second Division A]]. The first team was champions [[Al Mokawloon Al Arab SC]], who secured promotion back to the Egyptian Premier League who reached an immediate return to the league. They were joined by [[Wadi Degla SC]], who returned to the league after a five-year absence by clinching the second promotion spot. [[Kahraba Ismailia SC]] were the last team to earn promotion, who got promoted to the league for the first time in their history, after coming third.<ref>{{cite web |title=تأسس في الثمانينات.. أبرز المعلومات عن نادي كهرباء الإسماعيلية بعد صعوده للدوري المصري |url=https://www.masrawy.com/sports/sports_news/details/2025/5/31/2796834/%D8%AA%D8%A3%D8%B3%D8%B3-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AB%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A3%D8%A8%D8%B1%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%8A-%D9%83%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%B3%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D8%B5%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%87-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%8A |website=مصراوي |access-date=9 August 2025}}</ref>
