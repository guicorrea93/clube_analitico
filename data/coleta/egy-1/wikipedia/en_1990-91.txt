18 teams participated in the '''1990–91 Egyptian Premier League season''' as the previous season was cancelled so there was no relegation. The first team in the league was the champion, and qualified to the [[1992 African Cup of Champions Clubs|African Cup of Champions Clubs]].
[[Ismaily SC|Ismaily]] won the league for the 2nd time in the club's history.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1990–91]]</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry9091.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210131183402/http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry9091.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=January 31, 2021|title=Egyptian Soccer League 1990/91|website=drtareksaid.net}}</ref>
