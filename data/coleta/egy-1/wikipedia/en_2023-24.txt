The '''2023–24 Egyptian Premier League''' was the 65th season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top professional league for [[association football]] clubs in the [[Egyptian football league system]], since its establishment in 1948. Fixtures for the 2023–24 season were announced on 11 September 2023.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://see.news/egyptian-premier-league-2023-2024-draw-scheduled-for-september-11 |title=Egyptian Premier League 2023-2024 Draw Scheduled for September 11 |website=[[Sada El-Balad|Sada El Balad]] |date=7 September 2023 |access-date=10 September 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/6/51/508193/Sports/Egyptian-Football/Ahly-to-host-Masry,-Zamalek-clash-with-Pyramids-FC.aspx |title=Ahly to host Masry, Zamalek clash with Pyramids FC in EPL opener |website=[[Al-Ahram|Al Ahram Online]] |date=11 September 2023 |access-date=12 April 2024}}</ref> [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] were the defending champions.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/6/51/504479/Sports/Egyptian-Football/Ahly-crowned--Egyptian-Premier-League-champions.aspx |title=Ahly crowned 2022-23 Egyptian Premier League champions |website=[[Al-Ahram|Al Ahram Online]] |date=10 July 2023 |access-date=6 May 2024}}</ref>

The season started on 18 September 2023, and concluded on 18 August 2024.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.filgoal.com/articles/485140 |title=رابطة الأندية تعلن مواعيد مؤجلات الدوري.. والنهاية 18 أغسطس |trans-title=EPL announce new date for league's postponed fixture, season to conclude on 18 August |website=[[FilGoal]] |language=Arabic |date=25 June 2024 |access-date=25 June 2024}}</ref>

Al Ahly successfully defended their title and won the league on 8 August 2024 following a 1–0 away win against [[Smouha SC|Smouha]], winning a record-extending 44th league title.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.kingfut.com/2024/08/08/al-ahly-egyptian-champions-44th-time/ |title=Al Ahly crowned Egyptian Premier League champions for 44th time |website=[[KingFut]] |date=8 August 2024 |access-date=9 August 2024}}</ref>

==Format==
The league consist of 18 teams in one group. All teams play each other twice, home and away, for a total of thirty-four matches. The first-placed team is crowned champions of the league, and qualify for the [[CAF Champions League]] alongside the runners-up. The third-placed team qualify for the [[CAF Confederation Cup]], while the bottom three teams face relegation to the [[Egyptian Second Division A]].

A total of 18 teams participate in the 2023–24 edition of the Egyptian Premier League.

The first team to earn promotion to the league was [[ZED FC|ZED]], who were promoted on 9 June 2023 after securing first place in [[2022–23 Egyptian Second Division|Second Division]] Group B following a 3–1 win against [[Wadi Degla SC|Wadi Degla]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/6/51/502692/Sports/Egyptian-Football/ZED-FC-make-history-with-promotion-to-Egyptian-Pro.aspx |title=ZED FC make history with promotion to Egyptian Pro League |website=[[Al-Ahram|Al Ahram Online]] |date=9 June 2023 |access-date=10 June 2023}}</ref> This was the second time in the club's history to reach the Premier League, with the first time being in 2019 when the club was known as FC Masr.

The second team to promote to the Premier League was [[Baladiyat El Mahalla SC|Baladiyat El Mahalla]], who were promoted to the league as Group C winners following a 4–0 away win against promotion-rivals Dikernis on 14 June 2023; ending the club's absence from the top tier that lasted 15 years.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.kingfut.com/2023/06/14/baladeyet-el-mahalla-egyptian-premier-league/ |title=Baladeyet El Mahalla returns to Egyptian Premier League after 15 years |website=[[KingFut]] |date=14 June 2023 |access-date=14 June 2023}}</ref>

The third and last team to earn promotion was [[El Gouna FC|El Gouna]], who returned to the top flight immediately after getting relegated in 2022. Their promotion was secured on 15 June 2023, following a 2–1 away win against [[Muslim Youths SC (Qena)|Muslim Youths (Qena)]] that secured Group A first place with just one point above the runners-up [[La Viena FC|La Viena]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/503121.aspx |title=Gouna FC promoted to Egypt top-flight after final round 'hard-fought' win |website=[[Al-Ahram|Al Ahram Online]] |date=15 June 2023 |access-date=15 June 2023}}</ref>

The first team to be relegated from the league was [[Haras El Hodoud SC|Haras El Hodoud]], who suffered an immediate return to the second tier following a 3–0 defeat against [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] on 22 June 2023 that confirmed the relegation of the [[Alexandria]]-based side.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentP/6/503599/Sports/Ahly-regain-league-top-spot,-Haras-ElHodoud-relega.aspx |title=Ahly regain league top spot; Haras El Hodoud relegated to second division |website=[[Al-Ahram|Al Ahram Online]] |date=23 June 2023 |access-date=28 June 2023}}</ref>

The second team to be relegated was [[Ghazl El Mahalla SC|Ghazl El Mahalla]], whose stay in the top flight came to an end after three seasons, following a 2–1 defeat against already-relegated side Haras El Hodoud on 14 July 2023.<ref name=GMHASW>{{cite web |url=https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/6/51/504780/Sports/Egyptian-Football/Dakhleya-and-ElGeish-survive-Premier-League;-Aswan.aspx |title=Dakhleya and El-Geish survive Premier League; Aswan and Ghazl El Mahalla suffer relegation |website=[[Al-Ahram|Al Ahram Online]] |date=14 July 2023 |access-date=14 July 2023}}</ref>

The third and last team to be relegated was [[Aswan SC|Aswan]], another team that suffered an immediate return to the second tier, after losing 4–2 on home ground against [[El Dakhleya SC|El Dakhleya]] on 14 July 2023. El Dakhleya survived relegation thanks to this win.<ref name=GMHASW/>
