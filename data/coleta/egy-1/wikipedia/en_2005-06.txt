In the '''2005–06 Egyptian Premier League''', [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] were crowned champions for the second year in a row,<ref>{{Cite news |title=Al-Ahly secures title of Egyptian Premier League |url=https://www.proquest.com/docview/452534560 |access-date=2024-05-17 |work=[[Xinhua News Agency]] |id={{ProQuest|452534560}} }}</ref> and for the thirty-first time in total since the league started in 1948. As in the previous campaign, they went undefeated throughout their 26 matches.<ref>{{Cite web |date= |title=Summary – Premier League – Egypt – Results, fixtures, tables and news |url=https://us.soccerway.com/national/egypt/premier-league/2005-2006/regular-season/r2281/ |access-date=26 May 2025 |website=Soccerway}}</ref>
