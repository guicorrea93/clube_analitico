The '''1953–54 Egyptian Premier League''', was the fifth season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 27 September 1953 and concluded on 23 May 1954.
[[El Qanah FC|El Qanah]] entered as the promoted team from the [[Egyptian Second Division]]. They replaced [[Port Fuad]], who was relegated.

Defending champions [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] won their 5th consecutive and 5th overall Egyptian Premier League title in the club's history.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1953–54]]{{Circular reference|date=July 2020}}</ref>
