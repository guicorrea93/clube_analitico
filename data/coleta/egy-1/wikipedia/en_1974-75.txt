The '''1974–75 Egyptian Premier League''', was the 19th season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 6 September 1974 and concluded on 6 April 1975.
[[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] won the league for the 12th time in the club's history.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1974–75]]</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry7475.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201125135246/http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry7475.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=November 25, 2020|title= 1974–75 season}}</ref>
