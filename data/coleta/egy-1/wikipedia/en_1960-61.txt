The '''1960–61 Egyptian Premier League''', was the 11th season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 7 October 1960 and concluded on 16 April 1961. [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] won the league for the tenth time in the club's history.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1960–61]]{{Circular reference|date=May 2021}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry6061.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201122151247/http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry6061.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=November 22, 2020|title= 1960–61 season}}</ref>
