The '''1977–78 Egyptian Premier League''', was the 21st season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 17 September 1977 and concluded on 2 April 1978.
[[Zamalek SC|Zamalek]] won the league for the 4th time in the club's history.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1977–78]]</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry7778.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201130000816/http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry7778.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=November 30, 2020|title= 1977–78 season}}</ref>
