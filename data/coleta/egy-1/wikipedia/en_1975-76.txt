The '''1975–76 Egyptian Premier League''', was the 19th season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 26 September 1975 and concluded on 21 May 1976.
[[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] won the league for the 13th time in the club's history.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1975–76]]</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry7576.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201125142710/http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry7576.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=November 25, 2020|title= 1975–76 season}}</ref>
