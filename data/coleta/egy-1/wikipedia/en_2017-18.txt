The '''2017–18 Egyptian Premier League''' (also known as the '''[[Telecom Egypt]] Premier League''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 59th season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 8 September 2017 and concluded on 20 May 2018. Fixtures for the 2017–18 season were announced on 30 August 2017.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.youm7.com/story/2017/8/30/%D9%86%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%B1-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85-%D9%85%D9%88%D8%B3%D9%85-2017-2018/3392973 |title=جدول مباريات الدورى العام موسم 2017-2018 |trans-title=EFA announce fixtures for the 2017–18 Egyptian Premier League |work=[[Youm7]] |date=30 August 2017 |access-date=11 September 2017}}</ref>

Al Ahly won and secured their record-extending fortieth Egyptian Premier League title with six games to spare, following [[Al-Masry SC|Al Masry]]'s 0–0 draw with [[El Entag El Harby SC|El Entag El Harby]] on 12 March 2018.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.kingfut.com/2018/03/12/official-alahly-league-champions/ |title=Al Ahly clinch their 40th Egyptian Premier League title |work=[[KingFut]] |date=12 March 2018 |access-date=12 March 2018}}</ref> The team broke numerous league records over the course of the season, including: most points (88), most wins (28) and most goals scored (75).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.filgoal.com/articles/333921 |title=ثنائية حمراء.. الأهلي ينتصر على المصري ويكسر رقم الزمالك التاريخي |trans-title=Al Ahly defeats Al Masry and breaks many records, including Zamalek historical most points record |work=[[FilGoal]] |date=21 May 2018 |access-date=21 May 2018}}</ref> [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] were the defending champions and successfully defended it, while [[Al Assiouty Sport]], [[Al Nasr SC (Egypt)|Al Nasr]] and [[El Raja SC|El Raja]] have entered as the promoted teams from the [[2016–17 Egyptian Second Division]].
