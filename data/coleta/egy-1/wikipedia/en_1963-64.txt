The '''1963–64 Egyptian Premier League''', was the 14th season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948.The league consisted of 2 groups each of 12 clubs. The season started on 4 October 1963 and concluded on 16 June 1964.
[[Zamalek SC|Zamalek]] won the league for the second time in the club's history.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1963–64]]</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry6364.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201117161642/http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry6364.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=November 17, 2020|title= 1963–64 season}}</ref>
