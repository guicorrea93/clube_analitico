The '''1972–73 Egyptian Premier League''', was the 18th season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 6 October 1972 and concluded on 22 April 1973.
[[Ghazl El Mahalla SC|Ghazl El Mahalla]] won the league for the first time in the club's history.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1972–73]]</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry7273.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201122160509/http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry7273.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=November 22, 2020|title= 1972–73 season}}</ref>
