The '''2019–20 Egyptian Premier League''', also known as '''The [[Telecom Egypt|WE]] League''' for sponsorship purposes, is the 61st season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season began on 21 September 2019 and will conclude on 31 October 2020. Fixtures for the 2019–20 season were announced on 12 September 2019.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://vetogate.com/3556655 |title=اتحاد الكرة يحدد موعد إجراء قرعة الدوري الممتاز |trans-title=EFA announce draw date for the Premier League |work=Veto Gate |language=ar |date=8 September 2019 |access-date=11 September 2019}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2019/09/12/cairo-derby-fourth-round-draw/ |title=Cairo Derby in fourth round as 2019/20 Egyptian Premier League draw conducted |work=[[KingFut]] |date=12 September 2019 |access-date=13 September 2019}}</ref>

Defending champions [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] won their 5th consecutive and 42nd overall Egyptian Premier League title on 18 September 2020, following [[Zamalek SC|Zamalek]]'s 1–0 defeat against [[Aswan SC|Aswan]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2020/09/18/aswan-beat-zamalek-al-ahly-champions/ |title=Al Ahly crowned League champions as Zamalek lose to Aswan |work=[[KingFut]] |date=18 September 2020 |access-date=19 September 2020}}</ref>

On 14 March 2020, a decision was made by the [[Egyptian Football Association]] to postpone all football activities in Egypt due to the spread of the [[COVID-19 pandemic]] in the country.<ref name=covid19>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2020/03/14/efa-suspend-football-acitvities/ |title=Egypt FA suspend all football activities amid COVID-19 concerns |work=[[KingFut]] |date=14 March 2020 |access-date=15 March 2020}}</ref> The initial suspension, until 29 March, was then extended multiple times until at least mid-July. On 2 July 2020, the EFA announced that the league would recommence on 6 August, and confirmed that all clubs' players and staff will be tested for COVID-19 before resuming training.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2020/07/02/egyptian-premier-league-to-resume-on-august-7th/ |title=Egyptian Premier League to resume on August 7th |work=[[KingFut]] |date=2 July 2020 |access-date=26 July 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2020/07/07/efa-change-resumption-date/ |title=Egyptian FA change date for league resumption |work=[[KingFut]] |date=7 July 2020 |access-date=26 July 2020}}</ref>

==Overview==
===VAR introduction===
In August 2019, [[Egyptian Football Association]] acting-president [[Amr El Ganainy]] revealed the intentions to implement the [[video assistant referee]] (VAR) technology in the [[Egyptian Premier League]] starting from the second half of the 2019–20 season; stating that this would be a huge step forward for Egyptian football. Three months later, the EFA started a four-month training programme headed by EFA head of referees committee [[Gamal Al-Ghandour|Gamal El Ghandour]] and Egyptian international referee [[Gehad Grisha]] for Egyptian referees to prepare them for the VAR introduction.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.masrawy.com/sports/sports_news/details/2019/11/11/1668455/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%BA%D9%86%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B1-%D9%8A%D9%83%D8%B4%D9%81-%D8%A2%D8%AE%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%88%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%80-%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1 |title=الغندور يكشف آخر تطورات تطبيق الـ"فار" في مصر |trans-title=Gamal El Ghandour reveal news ahead of the VAR introduction in Egypt |work=Masrawy |language=ar |date=11 November 2019 |access-date=15 March 2020}}</ref>

In late January 2020, the EFA received final [[International Football Association Board|IFAB]] approval for VAR usage in the domestic competitions after fulfilling all the necessary requirements to introduce VAR technology into Egyptian football. By the end of the training programmes held by the EFA, El Ghandour announced that 52 new Egyptian referee have received a VAR license; describing it as a "miracle" to have from 4 to 56 licensed referee in just four months.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kooora.com/?n=901868 |title=الغندور: 56 حكما مصريا حصلوا على رخصة الفار |trans-title=El Ghandour: 56 Egyptian referees now own a VAR license |work=Kooora |language=ar |date=5 March 2020 |access-date=15 March 2020}}</ref>

The VAR technology was officially introduced in the Egyptian Premier League on 5 March 2020 in a match between [[Tala'ea El Gaish SC|Tala'ea El Gaish]] and [[ENPPI SC|ENPPI]] at [[Gehaz El Reyada Stadium]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentP/6/364764/Sports/Enppi,-Talae-ElGaish-game-saw-introduction-of-VAR-.aspx |title=ENPPI, Tala'ea El Gaish game saw introduction of VAR in Egypt |work=[[Al-Ahram|Al Ahram Online]] |date=5 March 2020 |access-date=15 March 2020}}</ref>

=== Effects of the COVID-19 pandemic ===
After the first case of [[COVID-19]] was identified [[COVID-19 pandemic in Egypt|in Egypt]] on 14 February 2020, the [[Egyptian Football Association]] revealed that there is no intentions to postpone football activities in Egypt and instead they announced a series of precautionary measures to be used by all clubs' players and staff.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/81641/Egypt-announces-first-Coronavirus-infection |title=Egypt announces first Coronavirus infection |work=[[Egypt Today]] |date=14 February 2020 |access-date=15 March 2020}}</ref> By the arrival of the first COVID-19 case in Egypt, the league was paused for the mid-season break.

On 10 March 2020, the EFA decided to continue the league [[Behind closed doors (sport)|behind closed doors]] until further notice to avoid the spreading of the disease.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2020/03/10/egypt-fa-suspends-attendance-coronavirus/ |title=Egypt FA suspends fans' attendance indefinitely amid COVID-19 concerns |work=[[KingFut]] |date=10 March 2020 |access-date=15 March 2020}}</ref> However, after the number of infected people in Egypt reached 110 cases just four days later of the initial announcement, the EFA decided to suspend all football activities in Egypt until at least 29 March 2020.<ref name=covid19/>

====Resumption====
On 1 July 2020, [[Zamalek SC|Zamalek]] refused to resume training or return to complete the season due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic in Egypt|COVID-19 pandemic in the country]] unless a "cure" for coronavirus is found,<ref>{{cite news |title= Zamalek will not play unless "Coronavirus is gone"  |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/africa/53253060 |work= BBC Sport Africa |access-date= 1 July 2020}}</ref> this came after the club's [[goalkeeper (association football)|goalkeeper]] [[Mohamed Awad (Egyptian footballer)|Mohammed Awad]] revealed he had tested positive for COVID-19 on 26 June.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.kingfut.com/2020/06/26/zamalek-mohamed-awad-covid-19/ |title= Zamalek's Mohammed Awad reveals he tested positive for COVID-19 |work= Kingfut |date= 26 June 2020}}</ref> Football activities were due to return on 25 July after the government allowed teams to resume training in June<ref>{{cite news |title= Egyptian teams likely to resume league on 25 July |url=http://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/6/51/372203/Sports/Egyptian-Football/UPDATED-Egypts-clubs-to-resume-training-on--June,-.aspx |work= Ahramonline |date= 15 June 2020}}</ref>

Nevertheless, the EFA announced on 3 July 2020 that the season would commence on 6 August 2020 after almost five months of being suspended due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic in Egypt|COVID-19 pandemic]], the season would commence its schedule in two phases starting with the postponed games according to the head of Competitions Committee Hossam El Zanati.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/africa/53279552 |title= August restart for Egyptian Premier League |work= BBC SportAfrica |access-date= 3 July 2020}}</ref>

The first phase will include the games scheduled to be played from 6 August until the end of the month, while the second phase of the games are scheduled to be played starting from 1 September until the end of the season. These will be revealed when the EFA reaches a final agreement concerning the [[Aswan Stadium]] as the venue for matches after clubs rejected [[Aswan Stadium]] located south of Cairo, as the proposed location for matches to be played citing the distance and long journey for the players and after [[Confederation of African Football|CAF]] decides the final dates for the [[2019–20 CAF Champions League#Semi-finals|Champions League]] and [[2019–20 CAF Confederation Cup#Semi-finals|CAF Confederation Cup]] semi-finals and finals.

The EFA in June had implemented safety guidelines and testing procedures for players mandating two tests with five days separating each test, since then 19 tests have returned positive with individuals in isolation.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/8/88636/Egypt-announces-new-measures-for-the-football-activity-Egyptian-League |title=Egypt announces new measures for football activities |work= Egypttoday |access-date= 15 June 2020}}</ref>

Eighteen teams will compete in the league - the top fifteen teams from the previous season, and three teams promoted from the [[2018–19 Egyptian Second Division|Egyptian Second Division]].

'''Teams promoted to the Egyptian Premier League'''<br />
The first team to be promoted was [[Aswan SC|Aswan]] from Group A, as they secured the promotion to the Egyptian Premier League following their 2–1 away win against [[Al Nasr Lel Taa'den SC|Al Nasr Lel Taa'den]] on 4 April 2019. The club returned to the Egyptian Premier League after staying two seasons in the Egyptian Second Division, and will play in the top flight for the tenth time in their history.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.filgoal.com/articles/360833/%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%88-%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%AD%D8%A8%D8%A7-%D8%A8%D9%80-%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%AD-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A3%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%B2 |title=مرحبا بـ تماسيح النيل.. أسوان أول العائدين إلى الدوري الممتاز |trans-title=Welcome to the Nile Crocodiles.. Aswan secures the promotion to the Egyptian Premier League |work=[[FilGoal]] |date=4 April 2019 |access-date=4 April 2019}}</ref>

The second team to be promoted was [[Tanta SC|Tanta]] from Group C, as they sealed the promotion to the Egyptian Premier League following their 2–0 away win against [[Sidi Salem SC|Sidi Salem]] on 11 April 2019. The club returned to the Egyptian Premier League after spending only one season in the Egyptian Second Division, as they were relegated from the Egyptian Premier League during the [[2017–18 Egyptian Premier League|2017–18 season]]. This season will mark the fifteenth appearance for Tanta in the Egyptian Premier League.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.filgoal.com/articles/361285/%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%88-%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%AD%D8%A8%D8%A7-%D8%A8%D8%AB%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%B7%D8%A7-%D9%8A%D8%B5%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%B2-%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A7 |title=مرحبا بثاني العائدين إلى الكبار.. طنطا يصعد للدوري الممتاز رسميا |trans-title=Tanta seals promotion to the Egyptian Premier League |work=[[FilGoal]] |date=11 April 2019 |access-date=11 April 2019}}</ref>

The third team to be promoted was [[FC Masr]] from Group B, as they earned the promotion to the Egyptian Premier League following their 0–0 away draw with Al Merreikh on 18 April 2019. The club earned the promotion after an intense contents with 4 other clubs for the only promotion spot from their group. FC Masr will play in the Egyptian Premier League for the first time in the club's history.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.filgoal.com/articles/361836/%D8%AB%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AB-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%A3%D9%87%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%8A-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%B2-%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A7 |title=ثالث المتأهلين.. نادي مصر إلى الدوري الممتاز رسميا |trans-title=The third promoted team... FC Masr earns promotion to the Egyptian Premier League |work=[[FilGoal]] |date=18 April 2019 |access-date=18 April 2019}}</ref>

'''Teams relegated to the Egyptian Second Division'''<br />
The first club to be relegated was [[Nogoom FC|Nogoom]], who suffered an immediate return to the Egyptian Second Division following a 2–1 away defeat to [[Misr Lel Makkasa SC|Misr Lel Makkasa]] on 10 May 2019. Despite defeating [[Zamalek SC|Zamalek]] 1–0 earlier in the previous season and having a great start, the club started slowly to collapse which eventually made them finish in the last place.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2019/05/11/nogoom-fc-relegation/ |title=Nogoom FC suffer relegation from Egyptian Premier League |work=[[KingFut]] |date=11 May 2019 |access-date=11 May 2019}}</ref>

The second club to be relegated was [[El Dakhleya SC|El Dakhleya]], who were relegated from the Egyptian Premier League for the first time in their history following a 2–1 home defeat to [[Zamalek SC|Zamalek]] on 12 May 2019; ending their stay in the league that lasted for eight years. El Dakhleya were one of eight clubs that are not founding members of the Egyptian Premier League and have never been relegated since their debut in the top flight.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2019/05/12/el-dakhelya-relegated-egyptian-league/ |title=El Dakhleya relegated from Egyptian Premier League |work=[[KingFut]] |date=12 May 2019 |access-date=18 May 2019}}</ref>

The third club to be relegated was [[Suez]]-based side [[Petrojet SC|Petrojet]], who were also relegated from the Egyptian Premier League for the first time in their history following a 0–0 draw with [[Al Mokawloon Al Arab SC|Al Mokawloon Al Arab]] on 3 June 2019; ending their top flight spell of thirteen years. Similar to El Dakhleya, Petrojet were one of eight clubs that are not founding members of the Egyptian Premier League and have never been relegated since their debut in the top flight.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2019/06/04/petrojet-first-ever-relegation-arab-contractors/ |title=Petrojet suffer first-ever relegation following Al Mokawloon Al Arab draw |work=[[KingFut]] |date=4 June 2019 |access-date=6 June 2019}}</ref>

===Foreign players===
Clubs can have a maximum of four foreign players registered during the season. Clubs cannot sign foreign players unless these players have played in the first or second tier in their countries. Clubs also cannot sign any foreign goalkeepers. In addition, each club can register a player from [[Palestine]], [[Syria]], or the [[Union of North African Football Federations|UNAF region]]; those players are not counted as foreign players. Also, any foreign player who holds Egyptian nationality is not considered a foreign player and will be registered as a local player. For example, Al Masry's player [[Mahmoud Wadi]] of Palestine holds both Palestinian and Egyptian nationalities, and as a result he is not registered as a foreign player.

*Players name followed with <sup>{{dagger}}</sup> indicates the player is playing out on loan.
*Players name followed with <sup>§</sup> indicates the player is playing for the club on loan.
*Players name in '''bold''' indicates the player is registered during the mid-season transfer window.
*Players name in ''ITALICS'' indicates the player has left the club during the mid-season transfer window.
