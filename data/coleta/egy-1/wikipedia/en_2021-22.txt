The '''2021–22 Egyptian Premier League''', also known as '''The [[Telecom Egypt|WE]] Egyptian Premier League''' for sponsorship purposes, was the 63rd season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 25 October 2021 and is concluded on 30 August 2022. Fixtures for the 2021–22 season were announced on 12 October 2021.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.kingfut.com/2021/10/12/al-ahly-zamalek-egyptian-premier-league-draw/ |title=Egyptian PL draw: Al Ahly handed tough start, early Cairo Derby set |work=[[KingFut]] |date=12 October 2021 |accessdate=26 October 2021}}</ref>

[[Zamalek SC|Zamalek]] are the defending champions, having won their 13th league title in the previous season.

Eighteen teams are competing in the league - the top fifteen teams from the previous season, and three teams promoted from the [[2020–21 Egyptian Second Division|Egyptian Second Division]].

===Venues===
A total of 12 stadiums in 5 governorates were selected by clubs to host their 2021–22 home fixtures. Each club has the option to submit a reserve home venue in case of unavailability for their main home stadium.

Due to security concerns, selected matches are played on neutral ground due to high intensity. For example, fixtures between [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] and [[Ismaily SC|Ismaily]] are played in [[Alexandria]]. All matches involving [[Al Masry SC|Al Masry]] are also played in the same city.
