The '''2006–07 Egyptian Premier League''' started on 3 August 2006, and the season ended on 25 May 2007. [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] were crowned champions for the third year in a row and for the thirty-second time in total since the league start in 1948.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Summary - Premier League - Egypt - Results, fixtures, tables and news - Soccerway |url=https://us.soccerway.com/national/egypt/premier-league/2006-2007/regular-season/r3762/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231123133251/https://us.soccerway.com/national/egypt/premier-league/2006-2007/regular-season/r3762/ |archive-date=2023-11-23 |access-date=2025-05-26 |website=us.soccerway.com |language=en}}</ref>

== Stadiums ==

[[Borg El Arab Stadium]] in Alexandria opened recently and it will be the home of majority of the Alexandria clubs. The stadium has a capacity of 80,000. In 2007, [[Damanhour Stadium]] in the city of Damanhour will go into construction. Its capacity will be 60,000. In 2009, [[Mubarak International Stadium]] will go into construction in Six October City & its capacity will be 65,000. In 2009 the [[Cairo Military Academy Stadium]] will be rebuilt and its capacity will be 65,000. As well Ismailia has future plans for expansion or a new venue with a capacity of a minimum of 45,000.
