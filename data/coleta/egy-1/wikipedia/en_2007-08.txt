The '''2007–08 Egyptian Premier League''' started on 13 August 2007, and finished on 22 May 2008. [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] were crowned champions for the fourth year in a row and for the thirty-third time in total since the league started in 1948.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Summary - Premier League - Egypt - Results, fixtures, tables and news - Soccerway |url=https://us.soccerway.com/national/egypt/premier-league/2009-2010/regular-season/r8995/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221211052922/https://us.soccerway.com/national/egypt/premier-league/2009-2010/regular-season/r8995/ |archive-date=2022-12-11 |access-date=2025-05-26 |website=us.soccerway.com |language=en}}</ref>
