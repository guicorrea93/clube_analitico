The '''2016–17 Egyptian Premier League''' (also known as the '''Obour Land Premier League''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 58th season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season began on 15 September 2016 and concluded on 17 July 2017. Fixtures for the 2016–17 season were announced on 22 August 2016.

On 29 May 2017, [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] won a record thirty-ninth title and successfully defended their title with four games in hand following their 2–2 draw with [[Misr Lel-Makkasa SC|Misr Lel Makkasa]].<ref>{{cite news |url=http://www.kingfut.com/2017/05/30/al-ahly-crowned-league-champions/ |title=Al Ahly crowned Egyptian Premier League Champions despite slip up against Misr Lel Makkasa |work=[[KingFut]] |date=30 May 2017 |access-date=31 May 2017}}</ref>

[[Al Nasr Lel Taa'den SC|Al Nasr Lel Taa'den]], [[El Sharkia SC|El Sharkia]] and [[Tanta SC|Tanta]] have entered as the three promoted teams from the [[2015–16 Egyptian Second Division]].
