The '''1959–60 Egyptian Premier League''', was the tenth season of the [[Egyptian Premier League]], the top Egyptian professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1948. The season started on 11 September 1959 and concluded on 17 June 1960.
[[Zamalek SC|Zamalek]] won the league for the first time.<ref>[[:ar:الدوري المصري الممتاز 1959–60]]{{Circular reference|date=May 2021}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry5960.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201026035139/http://drtareksaid.net/SocDawry5960.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=October 26, 2020|title= 1959–60 season}}</ref>
