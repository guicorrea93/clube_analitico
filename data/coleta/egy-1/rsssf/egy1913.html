Egypt 1913

Challenge Bolanachi 1912/13
Challenge Bolanachi 1913/14
The Challenge Bolanachi was organised by the Union Internationale des Sociétés Sportives d'Egypte (known as the Union of Egyptian Sports Associations in English), a sports federation presided by Angelo Bolanachi, an Egyptian sports pioneer of Greek descent who represented the Egyptian Olympic Committee in the I.O.C. until 1932 and the Greek from 1933; he changed the transcription of his name to Bolanaki late in his life but the standard modern transcription of his name into the Latin alphabet would be Ángelos Volanákis. However, note that both the French and Italian newspapers used as sources for this page often rendered his family name as Bolonachi. (The use of "ch" rather than "k" to transcribe the Greek κ would suggest some Italian or Romanian influence.)
The tournament consisted of separate elimination tournaments in Alexandria and Egypt, with the two winners meeting in a national final for the "Championnat d'Egypte".

The Challenge Bolanachi probably was the first organised football championship in Egypt, although the issue of L'Imparziale of 5 Jan 1913 identified Alexandria side Devons (surely a British military team; the 2nd Battalion, Devonshire Regiment had arrived in Alexandria in January 1912) as "vincitori del campionato 1912".
However, note that the current Egyptian Football Association was founded in 1921 and does not recognise these tournaments. The Union of Egyptian Sports Associations was also responsible for the participation of the Egyptian football team at the 1920 Olympic Games (likewise, the Greek football team there was selected by a general sports federation, SEGAS, and not by the current Greek FA, founded in 1926).

Alexandria Tournament

NB: the start of the tournament was announced (on Nov 21, 1912) for Feb 13, 1913;
    P.O.E.A. (aka Hellenic FC) did not enter after several players had left to join Red Star;
    the following three matches were played prior to the end of February 1913:

First Category

Red Star FBC            4-2 Saint Andrews FC
  [played at the end of Nov 1912 and thus probably not part of the tournament]
Red Star FBC            4-0 Saint François Xavier
  [reported on May 2 as part of the competition]

Second Category

Savoia FBC              2-0 Union SC

NB: Savoia Foot-Ball Club were proclaimed second level champions at the occasion
    of the Egyptian final on May 4.

It is not known whether more matches were played, but on May 2 another result
between Red Star and Saint Andrews was reported as part of the competition:

Red Star FBC            2-0 Saint Andrews FC

NB: Red Star FBC qualified for Egyptian final.

Cairo Tournament

NB: no matches were played prior to March 1913 (but on Dec 1, 1912, Deutscher SV
    lost 0-1 to Ex-Allievi in a friendly); the following three results are known:
