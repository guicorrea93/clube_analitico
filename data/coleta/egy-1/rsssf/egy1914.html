Egyptian Championship (U.I.S.S.E.) 1914

NB: organised by the Union Internationale des Sociétés Sportives d'Egypte
    (known as the Union of Egyptian Sports Associations in English).

Participants

Alexandria
G.O.A.C.                           [Government Officials' Athletic Club]
P.O.E.A.                           [Podosferikós Ómilos Ellínon Alexándrias; aka Greek or Hellenic FC]
Savoia Foot-Ball Club   
Red Star Foot-Ball Club            [second category]
U.S.A.                             [second category; Unione Sportive Alessandria]
U.S.C.                             [second category; Union Sports Club]
Itala                              [third category]

Cairo
A.E.A.S.I.                         [Associazione fra gli Ex-Allievi delle Scuole Italiane]
C.I.S.C.                           [Cairo International Sports Club]                        [*1]
C.R.C.                             [Cairo Recreation Club]
E.S.R.I.                           [Egyptian State Railways Institute]                      [*2]

[*1] known as Mokhtalat in Arab, later King Farouk Club; called Zamalek since 1952.
[*2] now called El-Sekka El-Hadid.

NB: at this point, with the winners already decided, A.E.A.S.I. declared its general forfeit
    because of the disqualification of Ibraim Osman (who had inscribed for both C.I.S.C. and
    A.E.A.S.I. and had originally been admitted to play for A.E.A.S.I. but then barred from
    playing further following a protest by C.I.S.C.) – cf. "L'Imparziale", 7 Feb 1914, p. 2:
    "La squadra dell'A.E.A.S.I. in segno di protesta contro la squalifica del giuocatore
     Osman inflitta dall'U.I.S.S.E. ha dichiarato forfait non solo per il match di domani,
     ma per tutti gli altri incontri di campionato già sostenuti.".

 -.A.E.A.S.I.              withdrew

The final fixture was played anyway, but as a friendly training match only
("L'Imparziale" described it as "incontro amichevole" in the match preview
on 7 February and as "match di allenamento" in the match report in its issue
of 9-10 February; it was refereed by a member of A.E.A.S.I.):

[Feb 8, friendly training match]
C.I.S.C.                4-1 A.E.A.S.I.
