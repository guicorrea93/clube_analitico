Other Cup Tournaments

The Union Internationale des Sociétés Sportives d'Egypte organised Egyptian championships already before the first World War; the first two, both played in 1913, were also known as the Challenge Bolanachi.
Another competition was that for the Sultan Hussein Cup, held on 22 occasions between 1917 and 1938. The first four were won by different British army teams.
