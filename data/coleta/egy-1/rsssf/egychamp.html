Egypt - List of Champions

U.I.S.S.E. Egyptian Championship

The Union Internationale des Sociétés Sportives d'Egypte (known as the Union of Egyptian Sports Associations in English) was the first national sports federation, established before the current Egyptian Football Association (founded in 1921), and responsible for the participation of the Egyptian football team at the 1920 Olympic Games.
The first two editions were also known as "Challenge Bolanachi"; for all three editions listed below, separate tournaments were held in Alexandria and Cairo with the winners meeting in the Egyptian final.

Cairo League

A league for Cairo was first organised in 1922/23 by the Cairo Football Board, under the name Cairo Zone Competition. In 1938, the Egypt FA took over the organisation, together with three other regional leagues (Alexandria (organised between 1926 and 1953), Bahary (for Upper Egypt) and Canal (organised between 1927 and 1948, won by Al-Masry in its final season 1947/48)). From 1948 to 1953 the Cairo League was held in parallel with the new nationwide league championship (i.e., results in the nationwide league between Cairo clubs also counted for the city league) before being discontinued (it was held once more in 1957/58 but then stopped again).

Other Regional Leagues

Alexandria League
Won by Al-Ittihad on 27 occasions (apparently all editions 1926/27 to 1952/53);
usually played by 5 clubs, including (apart from Ittihad) Olympi and Teram

Bahary League
No information available

Canal League
The Canal (Zone) League included the teams of the three cities on the Suez Canal:
Port Said, Ismailia, and Suez; the two most popular participants were Al-Masry
(Port Said), reportedly record champions with 17 titles, and Ismaily (Ismailia),
who won the league once.
