Honduras 1960/61

Liga Amateur de Honduras

NB: thirteenth edition, played Feb 14, 1960-Mar 6, 1961;
    Feb 14 refers to start date regional leagues

Participants:
CD Vida (La Ceiba)            [champions Atlántida]
CD España (San Pedro Sula)    [champions Cortés]
CD Olimpia (Tegucigalpa)      [champions Francisco Morazán]
Honduras (El Progreso)        [champions Yoro]
Tigre                         [champions Zona Sur]

NB: was to be played as double round robin, but eventually only one
    match was played:

Honduras            2-0 España              

NB: Honduras did not show for the return fixture in San Pedro Sula;
    Vida withdrew for financial problems caused by the delay of the
    pentagonal due to World Cup qualifiers;
    eventually, the Federación Deportiva Extraescolar de Honduras
    decided to abandon the tournament on Mar 6, 1961, following the
    withdrawal of Honduras and Vida, and declared Olimpia champions.
