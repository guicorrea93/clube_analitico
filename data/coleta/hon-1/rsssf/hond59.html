Honduras 1959

Liga Amateur de Honduras

NB: twelfth edition, played Feb 1, 1959-Nov 16, 1959;
    Feb 1 refers to start date regional leagues

Participants:
CD Marathón (San Pedro Sula)  [champions Cortés]
CD Olimpia (Tegucigalpa)      [champions Francisco Morazán-Zona Centro/Sur]
Honduras (El Progreso)        [champions Yoro]

NB: played as double round robin.

Known results:

Olimpia             3-0 Honduras            
Honduras            1-1 Marathón            
Olimpia             1-0 Marathón            
Honduras            1-2 Olimpia             
Motagua             n/p Olimpia             

NB: it is not clear whether the second match between Marathón and Honduras
    was played; the last match between Motagua and Olimpia was not played
    due to irrelevance.
