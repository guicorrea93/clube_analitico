Honduras 1954/55

Liga Amateur de Honduras

NB: eighth edition, played Feb 28, 1954-Mar 30, 1955;
    Feb 28 refers to start date regional leagues

Participants:
Club Aduana Deportivo (Tela)  [champions Atlántida]
CD Sula (La Lima)             [champions Cortés]
CD Motagua (Tegucigalpa)      [champions Francisco Morazán]
CD Abacá (Yoro)               [champions Yoro]

NB: played as double round robin; delayed because of strike by workers 
    of the Tela Railroad Company and floodings on the north coast.
