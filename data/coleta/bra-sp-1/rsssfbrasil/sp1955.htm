O Santos conquistou o Campeonato Paulista de 1955

No team was relegated to 2nd Level. Jabaquara would be relegated but recurred successfully to
the Sportive Court alleging that, due to be one of the FPF founder clubs, the federation rules
allowed the club to never be relegated.
