Belgium - Final Tables 1895-2008

  
NB: 

The title of  ""Royale/Koninklijke"" is given when a club celebrated their:
        25 Years existence before 1958
        35 Years existence between 1958 and 1968
        50 Years existence after 1958
The royal house can give the title if the club appeals for it, and is accepted by the FA when
they change their name. For example SC Anderlechtois founded in 1909 received the title Royale in
1933 and changes their name in 1933 to become RSC Anderlechtois.

        At the end of the season 1951-1952 there was major change in the structure
        of the national competion. Since 1931 the structure had been as follows:

        Division d'honneur      i.e. first level with 16 teams
        Division I Serie A & B  i.e. second level with 32 teams divided in 2 divisions of each 16 teams
        Promotion A-B-C-D       i.e. third level with 64 teams divided in 4 divisions of each 16 teams

        From 1952-1953 on this is the new organisation:

        Division I              i.e. first level with 16 teams 
        Division II             i.e. second level with 16 teams
        Division III A & B      i.e. third level with 32 teams divided in 2 divisions of each 16 teams
