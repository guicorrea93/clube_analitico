[1] all matches of championship playoff annulled after protest Karlsruher FV
    against the fact their quarterfinal against TuFC Britannia Berlin had
    been played in Berlin, contrary to regulations requiring a neutral venue.
[2] Hamburger SV and 1.FC Nürnberg had qualified for the final and played
    two matches, which finished 2-2 and 1-1, both aet; the second match was
    abandoned after 1.FC Nürnberg had only 7 players left (8 were at the
    time required); initially the title was awarded to HSV (since Nürnberg
    had caused the abandonment), but this was revoked after an appeal by
    Nürnberg; another appeal by HSV followed, and at an extraordinary
    general meeting of the German FA they were awarded the title again, only
    to decline it - the German championship was not awarded for 1921/22.
