The '''2024–25 Elite One''' is a season of the [[Elite One]], the top-tier football league in [[Cameroon]]. It commenced on 7 December 2024 and is scheduled to conclude on 28 May 2025.<ref>{{cite news |title=Calendrier du Championnat MTN Elite One 2024-2025 |url=https://fecafoot-officiel.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/CALENDRIER-MTN-ELITE-ONE-SAISON-2024-2025.pdf |access-date=18 December 2024 |publisher=Cameroonian Football Federation |date=25 November 2024 |format=PDF}}</ref>
