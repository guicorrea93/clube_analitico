The '''2015 [[Elite One]]''' was the 55th season of the Cameroon Top League. The season began on 8 February 2015. [[Coton Sport FC de Garoua|Cotonsport]] won their third consecutive title and 14th overall, all in the last 19 years.

[[Coton Sport FC de Garoua|Cotonsport]] clinched the title in the final round of the season, holding onto their 1-point lead over [[Union Douala]] when both teams closed the season with a draw. Union Douala picked up a road point with a 1-1 score at [[New Star de Douala|New Star]], while Cotonsport played a scoreless home tie against [[Botafogo FC (Douala)|Botafogo FC]]. With their respective top-two finishes, Cotonsport and Union Douala qualified for the [[2016 CAF Champions League]]. New Star and Botafogo finished in a third-place tie and will play a play-off to determine the [[2016 CAF Confederation Cup]] qualifier.

[[Canon Yaoundé|Canon]], [[Njala Quan Sports Academy|Njala Quan]] and [[Tonnerre Yaoundé|Tonnerre]] finished 16th, 17th and 18th, respectively and will be relegated to [[Elite Two]] for the 2016 season. While there was no doubt about the fates of Njala Quan and Tonnerre, Canon could have escaped relegation with a win in their final game, but played [[Cosmos de Bafia]] to a 1-1 draw on the road to seal their fate.

[[Elite One]] was reduced from 19 to 18 teams for the 2015 season with four clubs relegated to [[Elite Two]] and only three promoted. [[Renaissance FC de Ngoumou|Renaissance]], [[Scorpion de Bey|Scorpion de Bé]], [[Sable FC (Cameroon)|Sable Batié]] and [[Douala Athletic Club|Douala AC]] were all relegated to [[Elite Two]] after finishing in the last four spot of the [[2014 Elite One|2014 season]]. [[Dragon de Yaoundé]], [[Botafogo FC de Douala|Botafogo FC]] and [[Lion Blessé de Fotouni|Lion Blessé]] were each promoted from [[Elite Two]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Summary - Elite ONE - Cameroon - Results, fixtures, tables and news - Soccerway|url=https://www.soccerway.com/cameroon/elite-one-2015/results/|access-date=25 March 2015}}</ref>
