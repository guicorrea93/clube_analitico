The '''2016 [[Elite One]]''' was the 56th season of the Cameroon Top League. The season began on 30 January 2016. [[UMS de Loum]] dominated the league, winning their first title by a 10-point margin over runner-up [[Coton Sport FC de Garoua|Cotonsport]]. It was a significant turnaround for UMS de Loum, who finished tied for 11th in 2015, just four points away from relegation.

[[Elite One]] consists of 18 teams for the 2016 season with three clubs relegated to [[Elite Two]] and three promoted. [[Fovu Baham|Fovu Club]], [[Njala Quan Sports Academy|Njala Quan]] and [[Tonnerre Yaoundé|Tonnerre]] were all relegated to [[Elite Two]] after finishing in the last three spots of the [[2015 Elite One|2015 season]]. [[Aigle Royal Menoua|Aigle Royal]], [[Eding Sport FC]] and [[RC Bafoussam|Racing Club Bafoussam]] were each promoted from [[Elite Two]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Summary - Elite ONE - Cameroon - Results, fixtures, tables and news - Soccerway|url=https://www.soccerway.com/cameroon/elite-one-2016/results/|access-date=11 February 2016}}</ref>
