The '''Championnat du Cameroun de football''' (also known as '''MTN Elite One''' since 2007, as it is sponsored by [[MTN Group]]) is the highest division of professional [[association football]] league system in [[Cameroon]], governed by the [[Cameroonian Football Federation]] since its establishment in 1961.
