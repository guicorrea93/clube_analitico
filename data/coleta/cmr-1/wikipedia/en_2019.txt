The '''2019 Elite One''' was the 59th season of [[Elite One]], the top-tier [[association football|football]] league in [[Cameroon]]. The season started on 2 February 2019.<ref>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablesk/kam2019.html Cameroon 2019], RSSSF.com</ref> [[UMS de Loum]] won the 2019 Elite One.
