The '''2019–20 Elite One''' was the 60th season of the [[Elite One]], the top-tier [[association football|football]] league in [[Cameroon]]. This was the first season in the [[Elite One]] to use an October–May schedule since the 2011–12 season, it was changed so as to conform with the new [[Confederation of African Football|CAF]] calendar.<ref>{{cite news |title=Local clubs to be affected by CAF calendar changes |url=https://www.soccer24.co.zw/2018/07/24/local-clubs-affected-caf-calendar-changes/ |work=Soccer24
|access-date=29 June 2020}}</ref>

The season started on 18 October 2019 and was scheduled to end on 26 April 2020, but due to the effects of the [[COVID-19 pandemic in Cameroon|COVID-19 pandemic]], the season was cancelled on 12 May 2020 by [[Cameroonian Football Federation|FECAFOOT]].<ref>{{cite news | title= PWD Bamenda crowned champions as FECAFOOT cancels season |url=https://www.goal.com/en-ug/news/coronavirus-pwd-bamenda-crowned-cameroonian-champions-after/1uz329qmqptax11xzokvuf20wg |work= Goal.com |access-date= 18 June 2020}}</ref>
[[PWD Bamenda]] won their first [[Elite One]] silverware as they were crowned champions, alongside [[Coton Sport FC de Garoua|Coton Sport]], they will represent [[Cameroonian Football Federation|Cameroon]] at the 2020–21 [[2020–21 CAF Champions League|CAF Champions League]] and [[2020–21 CAF Confederation Cup|CAF Confederation Cup]] respectively as the [[Cameroonian Cup]] won't hold this year. Only [[Dragon Club (Yaoundé)|Dragon Club]] were relegated to the [[Elite Two]] as the teams would be increased to twenty teams during the next season.
There were no Championship and Relegation group this season.

==Teams==
Eighteen teams competed in the league, the top 15 teams from the [[2019 Elite One|previous season]] and the three promoted teams from the [[Elite Two]] ([[Bamboutos FC]], [[Canon Yaoundé|Canon Sportif]] and [[Panthère du Ndé]]) while ([[PWD Bamenda]], [[New Star de Douala]] and [[Unisport FC de Bafang]]) relegated from the previous season after finishing last at the playoffs.<ref>{{cite news |title=What future relegated for New Stars of Douala, PWD Bamenda and Unisport of Bafang |url=https://www.kick442.com/what-future-relegated-for-new-stars-of-douala-pwd-of-bamenda-and-unisport-of-bafang/ |work=[[Kick442.com]] |access-date=18 June 2019 }}{{Dead link|date=September 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}</ref> [[PWD Bamenda]] later replaced [[Les Astres FC]].

[[Coton Sport FC de Garoua|Coton Sport FC]] were the defending champions.
