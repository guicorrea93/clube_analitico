The '''2022–23 Elite One''' was a season of the [[Elite One]], the top-tier football league in [[Cameroon]].

[[Coton Sport FC]] won their third consecutive title and 18th overall after winning all three games in the championship stage, including a 2–0 win over [[Canon Yaoundé]] on the final match day to clinch the title.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://kick442.com/mtn-elite-one-playoffs-coton-are-2023-champions-of-cameroon-as-it-happened/|title=MTN Elite One playoffs: Coton are 2023 Champions of Cameroon – As it happened|date=21 May 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://sportnewsafrica.com/en/breve/football-cameroon-coton-sport-crowned-champion-2023/|title=Football - Cameroon: Coton Sport crowned champion 2023|publisher=Sport News Africa|date=22 May 2023}}</ref>

Avion Academy escaped relegation after a ruling saw them awarded three points in their match against [[UMS de Loum]] after UMS fielded an ineligible player, relegating [[Djiko FC de Bandjoun|Djiko]] in Group A.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://kick442.com/djiko-relegated-as-avion-recovers-three-points-that-changes-everything/|title=Djiko relegated as Avion recovers three points that changes everything|date=13 May 2023|publisher=Kick 442}}</ref>

[[Yong Sports Academy|YOSA de Bamenda]] defeated [[Eding Sport FC|Eding Sport]] 2–0 in the relegation playoff to remain in Elite One.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://kick442.com/relegation-playoff%f0%9f%87%a8%f0%9f%87%b2-yosa-ends-edings-7-year-stay-at-the-top/|title=Relegation Playoff🇨🇲: YOSA ends Eding’s 7-year stay at the top|date=19 May 2023|publisher=Kick 442}}</ref>
