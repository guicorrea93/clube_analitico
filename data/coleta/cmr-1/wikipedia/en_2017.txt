The '''2017 [[Elite One]]''' was the 57th season of the [[Cameroon]] top-tier [[association football|football]] league. The season began on 25 February 2017.<ref>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablesk/kam2017.html Cameroon 2017], RSSSF.com</ref> [[UMS de Loum]] were the defending champions coming off their first league title.

[[Elite One]] consisted of 18 teams for the 2017 season with three clubs relegated to [[Elite Two]] and three promoted. [[Botafogo FC (Douala)|Botafogo]], [[Cosmos de Bafia]] and [[Panthère du Ndé|Panthère]] were all relegated to [[Elite Two]] after finishing in the last three spots of the [[2015 Elite One|2015 season]]. Colombe, Feutcheu and Stade Renard were each promoted from [[Elite Two]].
