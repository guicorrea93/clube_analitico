Italian Champion: Torino.
Later FIGC revoked the title because of a corruption case for the match 
Torino -  Juventus 2-1 (5 giu 1927). Some executives of Torino gave 25.000
lire to the Juventus player Allemandi who was disqualified for life but
readmitted afterwards.
