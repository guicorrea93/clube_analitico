2018/19 Cypriot First Division

Each team played against every other team twice, once at home and once away, for a total of 22 matches. 
After these matches, the twelve teams were divided into two groups: 1st-6th, 7th-12th. 

The teams ranked first through sixth played out the champion and the participants for the European 
competitions. Teams ranked seventh through 12th determined the relegated clubs. Every team played 
twice against its group opponents. Regular season records are carried over without any modifications.

2018/19 Cyprus Cup 

(1) = 2018/19 Cypriot First Division 
(2) = 2018/19 Cypriot Second Division

Only teams from the First and Second division could participated (28 teams total). Four of them did't 
want to participate (AEZ Zakakiou, Anagennisi Deryneia, Othellos Athienou, MEAP Pera Choriou Nisou). 
Teams from the lower divisions (Third and Fourth) competed in a separate cup competition (2018/19 Cypriot 
Cup for Third and STOK Elite Division).

First round
-----------------------------
In the first round participated 16 teams. The following eight teams advanced directly to second round, 
meeting the winners of first round ties: 

*AEK Larnaca (2017/18 Cyprus Cup winner)
*Apollon Limassol (2017/18 Cyprus Cup finalist)
*ASIL Lysi (wild card, via draw)
*Doxa Katokopias (wild card, via draw)
*E.N. TH.O.I. Lakatamias (wild card, via draw)
*Ermis Aradippou (wild card, via draw)
*Ethnikos Achna (wild card, via draw)
*Karmiotissa Pano Polemidion (wild card, via draw)

The winner was qualifying to the next round. If a match was drawn, extra time was following. If extra 
time was drawn, the winner was decided by penalty shootout.

The next 3 rounds were played in a two-legged format, each team playing a home and an away match 
against their opponent. The team which scored more goals on aggregate, was qualifying to the next 
round. If the two teams scored the same number of goals on aggregate, then the team which scored 
more goals away from home was advancing to the next round.

If both teams had scored the same number of home and away goals, then extra time was following 
after the end of the second leg match. If during the extra thirty minutes both teams had managed 
to score, but they had scored the same number of goals, then the team who scored the away goals 
was advancing to the next round (i.e. the team which was playing away). If there weren’t scored 
any goals during extra time, the qualifying team was determined by penalty shootout.

The next round was played in a two-legged format, each team playing a home and an away match against 
their opponent. The team which scored more goals on aggregate, was qualifying to the next round. If 
the two teams scored the same number of goals on aggregate, then the team which scored more goals away 
from home was advancing to the next round.

If both teams had scored the same number of home and away goals, then extra time was following after 
the end of the second leg match. If during the extra thirty minutes both teams had managed to score, 
but they had scored the same number of goals, then the team who scored the away goals was advancing 
to the next round (i.e. the team which was playing away). If there weren’t scored any goals during 
extra time, the qualifying team was determined by penalty shootout.

2019 STOK Integration Championship (5th level promotion playoff)


Twenty-nine teams from local federations members of the Confederation of local federations of Cyprus 
(STOK) participated in the 2019 STOK Integration Championship.
The competition consisted of five knock-out rounds. In all rounds each tie was played as a single leg 
and was held at a neutral stadium. Each tie winner qualified to the next round. If a match was drawn, 
extra time was following. If extra time was also drawn, the winner was decided by penalty shoot-out.
The four teams that qualified to semiifinals were promoted to the 2019/20 STOK Elite Division.
