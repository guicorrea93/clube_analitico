1960/61 Cypriot Second Division

The 1960/61 Cypriot Second Division was split to two geographical groups, depending from Districts of Cyprus each participated team came from. 
The winners of each group were playing against each other in the final phase of the competition and the winner were the champions of the Second Division.


Limassol-Larnaca-Paphos Group
--------------------------------
Participated teams: Amathus Limassol, Anagennisi Larnacas, APOP Paphos, Ethnikos Asteras Limassol, Panellinios Limassol

Champions: Panellinios Limassol [Qualification to final phase]


Nicosia-Famagusta-Keryneia Group
--------------------------------
Participated teams: AEK Ammochostos, Enosis Agion Omologiton, Othellos Famagusta, PAEK Kyrenia

Champions: Enosis Agion Omologiton [Qualification to final phase]
