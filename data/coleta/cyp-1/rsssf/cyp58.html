1957/58 Cypriot Second Division

The 1957/58 Cypriot Second Division was split to two geographical groups, depending from Districts of Cyprus each participated team came from. 
The winners of each group were playing against each other in the final phase of the competition and the winner were the champions of the Second Division.


Limassol-Larnaca-Paphos Group
--------------------------------
Participated teams: Alki Larnaca, Amathus Limassol, APOP Paphos, Panellinios Limassol

Winner: Panellinios Limassol [Qualification to final phase]


Nicosia-Famagusta-Keryneia Group
--------------------------------
Participated teams: AYMA, Enosis Agion Omologiton, Othellos Famagusta, Orfeas Nicosia, PAEK Kyrenia

Winner: Orfeas Nicosia [Qualification to final phase]


Final Phase
----------------------
Orfeas Nicosia        2 - 0   Panellinios Limassol
Panellinios Limassol  1 - 2   Orfeas Nicosia

Champions: Orfeas Nicosia (Promoted to 1959/60 First Division; the 1958/59 First Division was cancelled)
