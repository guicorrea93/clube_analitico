1939/40 Cyprus Cup 

(1) = 1939/40 Cypriot First Division 

In all rounds, each tie was played as a single leg and was held at the home ground of the one of the two teams, according to the draw results. Ôhe winner was 
qualifying to the next round. If a match was drawn, extra time was following. If extra time was drawn, there was a replay at the same ground.
