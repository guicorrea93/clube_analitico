2014/15 Cypriot First Division

Each team played against every other team twice, once at home and once away, for a total of 22 matches. After these matches, the twelve teams were divided into two groups: 1st-6th, 7th-12th. 

The teams ranked first through sixth played out the champion and the participants for the European competitions. Teams ranked seventh through 12th determined the relegated club. Every team played twice against its group opponents. Regular season records are carried over without any modifications.

2014/15 Cyprus Cup 

(1) = 2014/15 Cypriot First Division 
(2) = 2014/15 Cypriot Second Division

Only teams from the First and Second division participated (26 teams total). Teams from the lower divisions (Third and Fourth) competed in a separate cup competition (2014/15 Cypriot Cup for Third and Fourth Division).

First round
-----------------------------
In the first round participated 20 teams. The following six teams advanced directly to second round, meeting the winners of first round ties: 

*APOEL (2013/14 Cyprus Cup winner and 2013/14 Cypriot First Division winner)
*Ermis Aradippou (2013/14 Cyprus Cup finalist and 2013/14 Cypriot First Division 4th placed team)
*AEL Limassol (2013/14 Cypriot First Division runners-up)
*Apollon Limassol (2013/14 Cypriot First Division 3rd placed team)
*Omonia (2013/14 Cypriot First Division 5th placed team)
*AEK Larnaca (2013/14 Cypriot First Division Fair Play winner)

The Cypriot First Division teams were not drawn together.

The winner was qualifying to the next round. If a match was drawn, extra time was following. If extra time was drawn, the winner was decided by penalty shootout.

The next 3 rounds were played in a two-legged format, each team playing a home and an away match against their opponent. The team which scored more goals on aggregate, was qualifying to the next round. If the two teams scored the same number of goals on aggregate, then the team which scored more goals away from home was advancing to the next round.

If both teams had scored the same number of home and away goals, then extra time was following after the end of the second leg match. If during the extra thirty minutes both teams had managed to score, but they had scored the same number of goals, then the team who scored the away goals was advancing to the next round (i.e. the team which was playing away). If there weren’t scored any goals during extra time, the qualifying team was determined by penalty shootout.

2014/15 Cypriot Cup for Third and Fourth Division

(3) = 2014/15 Cypriot Third Division 
(4) = 2014/15 Cypriot Fourth Division

Only teams from the Third and Fourth division could participate. Participation was not compulsory. 12 of 28 participated that season. 

First round 
------------
4 out of the 12 teams were drawn to qualify directly to quarterfinals, without needing to play any match.

The winner was qualifying to the next round. If a match was drawn, extra time was following. If extra time was drawn, the winner was decided by penalty shootout.

[5 November 2014]

The next two rounds were played in a two-legged format, each team playing a home and an away match against their opponent. The team which scored more goals on aggregate, was qualifying to the next round. If the two teams scored the same number of goals on aggregate, then the team which scored more goals away from home was advancing to the next round.

If both teams had scored the same number of home and away goals, then extra time was following after the end of the second leg match. If during the extra thirty minutes both teams had managed to score, but they had scored the same number of goals, then the team who scored the away goals was advancing to the next round (i.e. the team which was playing away). If there weren’t scored any goals during extra time, the qualifying team was determined by penalty shootout.
