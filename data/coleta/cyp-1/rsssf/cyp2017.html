2016/17 Cypriot First Division

Each team played against every other team twice, once at home and once away, for a total of 26 matches. 
After these matches, the two teams with the worst records were relegated to the Second Division. 
The remaining twelve teams were divided into two groups: 1st-6th, 7th-12th. 

The teams ranked first through sixth played out the champion and the participants for the European competitions. 
Teams ranked seventh through 12th determined the third and fourth relegated clubs. Every team played twice 
against its group opponents. Regular season records are carried over without any modifications.

2016/17 Cyprus Cup 

(1) = 2016/17 Cypriot First Division 
(2) = 2016/17 Cypriot Second Division

Only teams from the First and Second division could participated (28 teams total). Three of 
them did't want to participate (Enosis Neon Parekklisia, E.N. TH.O.I. Lakatamias, 
ENAD Polis Chrysochous). Teams from the lower divisions (Third and Fourth) competed in a 
separate cup competition (2016/17 Cypriot Cup for Third and STOK Elite Division).

First round
-----------------------------
In the first round participated 18 teams. The following seven teams advanced directly to second round, 
meeting the winners of first round ties: 

*Apollon Limassol (2015/16 Cyprus Cup winner)
*Omonia Nicosia (2015/16 Cyprus Cup finalist)
*APOEL Nicosia (2015/16 Cypriot First Division Fair Play winner)
*Olympiakos Nicosia (wild card, via draw)
*ASIL Lysi (wild card, via draw)
*Ethnikos Achna (wild card, via draw)
*Ethnikos Assia (wild card, via draw)

The winner was qualifying to the next round. If a match was drawn, extra time was following. If extra time 
was drawn, the winner was decided by penalty shootout.

The next 3 rounds were played in a two-legged format, each team playing a home and an away match against their 
opponent. The team which scored more goals on aggregate, was qualifying to the next round. If the two teams 
scored the same number of goals on aggregate, then the team which scored more goals away from home was advancing 
to the next round.

If both teams had scored the same number of home and away goals, then extra time was following after the end of 
the second leg match. If during the extra thirty minutes both teams had managed to score, but they had scored 
the same number of goals, then the team who scored the away goals was advancing to the next round (i.e. the 
team which was playing away). If there weren’t scored any goals during extra time, the qualifying team was 
determined by penalty shootout.

The next round waw played in a two-legged format, each team playing a home and an away match against their 
opponent. The team which scored more goals on aggregate, was qualifying to the next round. If the two teams 
scored the same number of goals on aggregate, then the team which scored more goals away from home was 
advancing to the next round.

If both teams had scored the same number of home and away goals, then extra time was following after the end 
of the second leg match. If during the extra thirty minutes both teams had managed to score, but they had 
scored the same number of goals, then the team who scored the away goals was advancing to the next round 
(i.e. the team which was playing away). If there weren’t scored any goals during extra time, the qualifying 
team was determined by penalty shootout.

2017 STOK Integration Championship (5th level promotion playoff)


Twenty-eight teams from local federations members of the Confederation of local federations of Cyprus (STOK) 
participated in the 2017 STOK Integration Championship.
The competition consisted of five knock-out rounds. In all rounds each tie was played as a single leg and was 
held at a neutral stadium. Each tie winner qualified to the next round. If a match was drawn, extra time was 
following. If extra time was also drawn, the winner was decided by penalty shoot-out.
The four teams that qualified to semiifinals and the team that won the fifth place were promoted to the 2017/18 
STOK Elite Division.

First round
-----------------------------
In the first round participated 24 teams. Teams from the same federation and teams that were champions at 
their federation were not drawn together.

4 teams (Anagennisi/Elpida Mouttalou, ES Thiela Agiou Thedorou, AEK Korakou, Omonia Psevda) advanced directly 
(via draw) to the second round, without playing any match.
