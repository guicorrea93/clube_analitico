1973/74 Cyprus Cup 

(1) = 1973/74 Cypriot First Division 
(2) = 1973/74 Cypriot Second Division
(3) = 1973/74 Cypriot Third Division

All rounds were played in a two-legged format, each team playing a home and an away match against their opponent. The team which scored more goals on aggregate, 
was qualifying to the next round. If the two teams scored the same number of goals on aggregate, then extra time was following after the end of the second leg match. 
If during the extra thirty minutes both teams scored the same number of goals or scored no goals, the qualifying team was determined by penalty shootout.
