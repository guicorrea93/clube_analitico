1955/56 Cypriot Second Division

The 1955/56 Cypriot Second Division was split to two geographical groups, depending from Districts of Cyprus each participated team came from. 
The winners of each group were playing against each other in the play-offs of the competition and the winner were the champions of the Second Division.

Play-offs
----------------------

Aris Limassol  - Orfeas Nicosia 3-0  (3 June 1956 at GSO Stadium)
