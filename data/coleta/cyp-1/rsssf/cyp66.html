1965/66 Cyprus Cup 

(1) = 1965/66 Cypriot First Division 
(2) = 1965/66 Cypriot Second Division 

In all rounds, each tie was played as a single leg and was held at the home ground of the one of the two teams, according to the draw results. Ôhe winner was 
qualifying to the next round. If a match was drawn, extra time was following. If extra time was drawn, there was a replay at the ground of the team who were 
away for the first game.

1965/66 Cypriot Second Division

The 1965/66 Cypriot Second Division was split to two geographical groups, depending from Districts of Cyprus each participated team came from. 
The winners of each group were playing against each other in the final phase of the competition and the winner were the champions of the Second Division.

Nicosia-Larnaca-Famagusta Group
----------------------
Participated teams: AEK Ammochostos, Anagennisi Larnacas, ENAD Ayiou Dometiou, Enosis Neon Paralimniou, Keravnos Strovolou and Orfeas Nicosia. 
Winner: Keravnos Strovolou [Qualification to final phase]


Limassol-Paphos Group
----------------------
Participated teams: APOP Paphos, Arion Lemesou, Evagoras Paphos, Ethnikos Asteras Limassol and Enosis Panelliniou-Antaeus Limassol.
Winner: APOP Paphos [Qualification to final phase]
