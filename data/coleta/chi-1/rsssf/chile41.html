NB: The two missing matches were never played and nobody officially
    knows why.  It is said that Colo Colo argued that they were about 
    to start some international matches and they didn't have enough 
    time. Secretly, they might have been afraid of losing their last
    match to Badminton and thereby their unbeaten status, as they
    considered Badminton their "bête noire". In 1937, they had almost
    lost to Badminton in the final round that season, which also then
    would have been their first loss in the championship that year.
