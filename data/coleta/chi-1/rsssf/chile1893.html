Chile 1893

Two friendly matches were played between clubs from Santiago and Valparaíso;
the first of the two was the first ever club match in Chile.

[Aug 5, Parque Cousino (now Parque O'Higgins), Santiago]
Santiago Cricket and Athletic Club    7-2 Valparaíso Football Club 
 
[Sep ?, Viña del Mar]
Valparaíso Football Club              0-5 Santiago Cricket and Athletic Club
