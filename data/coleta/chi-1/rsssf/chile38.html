Chile 1938

Campeonato de Apertura

NB: for the División de Honor Amateur teams Universidad Católica
    and Universidad de Chile, the first round also decided on their
    possible promotion.  That of Universidad Católica was rejected
    after their 2-6 loss, but Universidad de Chile were accepted
    after reaching a 1-1 draw against Audax Italiano in regulation
    time (they lost in extra time).

Final
COLO COLO		3-1 Audax Italiano

Colo Colo are winners of the Campeonato de Apertura
