On August 3, the Minister of Sports, Juma Kapuya, ordered the FA to admit 
both Bandari and 977 KJ to the playoff, which the FA agreed to at a meeting 
in Dodoma convened by the Minister.  

On August 7, Tanzania Breweries Limited, sponsors to the FA, summoned the
FA to reverse the decision to extend the playoff to eight team within one
week, as the extension is in breach with the sponsor agreement signed earlier
in the year, and TBL had not been invited to the Dodoma meeting on August 3
where the decision was taken, and which took place one day after the TBL had
paid the 6 qualified teams.

The FAT left Bandari and 977 KJ in the tournament, and the TBL terminated
the 5-year contract.

security at the venue.  Bandari are playing all home matches in
Mtwara (Umoja Stadium).
