Second Level 2006/07

NB: former second, third and fourth divisions disbanded;

    instead there will be district leagues, with each district
    qualifying 2 teams to the regional league (except for
    Dar es Salaam, whose districts each qualify 4 teams);

    there will apparently be 21 regional leagues, which all
    qualifying one team for the national second level league
    (except for Dar es Salaam whose regional league will
    qualify three teams);

    the national league will comprise 23 clubs, the top-3 of
    which are to be promoted to the top level.

District Leagues: Jul 16-Oct 15
Regional Leagues: Oct 16-Jan 15
National League:  Apr 14-Jun 15, 2007
