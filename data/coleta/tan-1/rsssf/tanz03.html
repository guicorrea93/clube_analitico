   protest to the format change and being granted a temporary 
   injunction by Ilala District magistrate Agatha Kabuta pending 
   hearing of a suit the club filed against the Football Association 
   of Tanzania (FAT) (final decision due in February 2004)]

NB: following the injunction against FAT continuing the Union League,
    the FAT and ZFA decided to inscribe Mainland champions Simba for 
    the CAF Champions Cup and Zanzibar champions Jamhuri for the new
    Confederation Cup; as Jamhuri declined the registration by FAT
    and ZFA, stating they were not ready for the challenge, FAT and
    ZFA decided to inscribe Mtibwa Sugar for the Confederation Cup 
    instead, provided they will play their home matches at Amaan
    stadium in Zanzibar.  Meanwhile Yanga have petitioned the same
    Ilala Disctrict court to dissolve FAT or jail its leaders...

NB: FAT also decided to simply scrap the remainder of the Union
