NB: on November 19, the National Sports Council ruled to award three
    matches (Kahama vs Yanga of round 5, Prisons vs Simba of round 8
    and Kahama vs Mtibwa of round 11) as 0-2 losses against Simba
    and Kahama; whil this did not change the order of the top-4 places,
    a later ruling in December by the Minister for Labour, Youth
    Development and Sports, Juma Kapuya, which presumably entailed
    overturning the decision on the Prisons vs Simba match, saw
    Young Africans finish in third place; as Mtibwa Sugar are not 
    allowed to enter the CAF Confederations Cup due to their running 
    2-year CAF ban, the TFF wanted to give their place, which had 
    been awarded to Prisons, to Young Africans; however, CAF refused
    to acknowledge this (as the deadline for registering participants
    had been sometime in November) and insisted on Prisons as entrants.
    Prisons have meanwhile filed a civil suit against the TFF for
    declaring Young Africans third winners of the league.
    Mtibwa Sugar will be allowed to enter the CECAFA Club Cup along
    with Simba.

NB: on November 19, the National Sports Council ruled to award three

    and Kahama vs Mtibwa of round 11) as 0-2 losses against Simba
    and Kahama; whil this did not change the order of the top-4 places,
    a later ruling in December by the Minister for Labour, Youth
    Development and Sports, Juma Kapuya, which presumably entailed
    overturning the decision on the Prisons vs Simba match, saw
    Young Africans finish in third place; as Mtibwa Sugar are not 
    allowed to enter the CAF Confederations Cup due to their running 
    2-year CAF ban, the TFF wanted to give their place, which had 
    been awarded to Prisons, to Young Africans; however, CAF refused
    to acknowledge this (as the deadline for registering participants
    had been sometime in November) and insisted on Prisons as entrants.
    Prisons have meanwhile filed a civil suit against the TFF for
    declaring Young Africans third winners of the league.  Due to the
    controversy, no prize for third place was given on Feb 16, 2005 
    by sponsors Vodacom, when Simba and Mtibwa obtained their cash
    prizes.

    Mtibwa Sugar will be allowed to enter the CECAFA Club Cup along
    with Simba.
