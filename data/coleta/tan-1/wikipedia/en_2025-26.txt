The '''2025–26 Tanzanian Premier League''' (known as the '''NBC Premier League''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 61st season of the [[Tanzanian Premier League]], the top-tier [[Association football|football]] league in [[Tanzania]] (mainland only), since its establishment in 1965. The season started on 17 September 2025 and ended on 30 June 2026.<ref name="Schedule">{{cite web |date=29 August 2025 |title=RATIBA YA LIGI KUU YA NBC MSIMU WA 2025/2026|url=https://ligikuu.co.tz/ratiba-ya-ligi-kuu-ya-nbc-msimu-wa-2025-2026/ |access-date=2025-08-29}}</ref>

==Teams==
The league consisted of 16 teams; the top 14 teams from the previous season, and two teams promoted from the [[Tanzanian Championship League]]. [[Young Africans S.C.|Young Africans]] entered the season as defending champions (for the fourth consecutive season).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cecafaonline.com/young-africans-sc-retain-nbc-tanzania-premier-league-title/|title=Young Africans retain Premier League title|publisher=Cecafaonline.com}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://dailynews.co.tz/yanga-claim-fourth-straight-title/|title=Yanga claim fourth straight title|publisher=dailynews.co.tz}}</ref>

== Tanzanian Premier League play-off ==
The 13th and 14th-placed teams ([[Tanzania Prisons S.C|Prisons]] and [[Mbeya City F.C.|Mbeya City]]) qualified for the Tanzanian Premier League play-off (held over two legs), alongside the 3rd and 4th-placed teams from the [[2025–26 Tanzanian Championship League]] ([[Polisi Tanzania]] and Mbeya Kwanza). The Championship teams qualified for the quarter-finals, and the Premier League teams qualified for the semi-finals; the quarter-final winners then face the semi-final losers for the final place in the 2026–27 Tanzanian Premier League.
