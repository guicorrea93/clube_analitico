The '''2019–20 Tanzanian Premier League''' (known as the '''Vodacom Premier League''' for sponsorship reasons) is the 55th season of the [[Tanzanian Premier League]], the top-tier [[association football|football]] league in [[Tanzania]] (mainland only), since its establishment in 1965. The season started on 24 August 2019.<ref>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablest/tanz2020.html Tanzania (Mainland) 2019/20], RSSSF.com</ref>

The match between the [[Young Africans]] and [[Simba SC]] drew an attendance of 58,400, the highest in the league.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Nnyanzi|first=Herman|date=2020-01-07|title=[Uganda] USD: 234, 463 collected in Tanzania’s Dar Derby|url=https://zonefoot.net/2020/01/07/uganda-usd-234-463-collected-in-tanzanias-dar-derby/|access-date=2020-05-13|website=ZoneFoot|language=en-US}}</ref>
