The '''2017–18 [[Tanzanian Premier League]]''' is the 53rd season of top-tier [[Association football|football]] in [[Tanzania]]. The season started on 26 August 2017 and ended on 28 May 2018.<ref>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablest/tanz2018.html Tanzania (Mainland) 2017/18], RSSSF.com</ref>
