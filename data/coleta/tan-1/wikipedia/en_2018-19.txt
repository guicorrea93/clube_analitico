The '''2018–19 Tanzanian Premier League''' is the 54th season of the [[Tanzanian Premier League]], the top-tier [[association football|football]] league in [[Tanzania]] (mainland only), since its establishment in 1965. The season started on 22 August 2018.<ref>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablest/tanz2019.html Tanzania (Mainland) 2018/19], RSSSF.com</ref>
