The '''2021–22 Tanzanian Premier League''' (known as the '''NBC Premier League''' for sponsorship reasons)<ref>{{Cite news |title=Tanzania Premier League gets new title sponsor |work=IPP Media (The Guardian) |url=https://www.ippmedia.com/en/sport/tanzania-premier-league-gets-new-title-sponsor-%C2%A0-%C2%A0}}</ref> was the 57th season of the [[Tanzanian Premier League]], the top-tier [[Association football|football]] league in [[Tanzania]] (mainland only), since its establishment in 1965. The season started on 27 September 2021. The season ended with [[Young Africans S.C.]] clinching their 28th premier league title, winning the entire season undefeated.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Football |first=CAF-Confedération Africaine du |title=Young Africans SC win Tanzania Premier League after four years {{!}} Total CAF Champions League 2021/22 |url=https://www.cafonline.com/total-caf-champions-league/news/young-africans-sc-win-tanzania-premier-league-after-four-years |access-date=2022-08-14 |website=CAFOnline.com |language=en}}</ref>
