The '''2023–24 Tanzanian Premier League''' (known as the '''NBC Premier League''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 59th season of the [[Tanzanian Premier League]], the top-tier [[Association football|football]] league in [[Tanzania]] (mainland only), since its establishment in 1965. The season started on 15 August 2023 and ended on 28 May 2024.

The winners ([[Young Africans S.C.|Young Africans]], their third consecutive title win and twenty-seventh overall) and runners-up ([[Azam F.C.|Azam]]) qualified for the [[2024–25 CAF Champions League|Champions League]]. The third and fourth-placed teams ([[Simba S.C.|Simba]] and [[Coastal Union F.C.|Coastal Union]]) qualified for the [[2024–25 CAF Confederation Cup|Confederation Cup]]. The thirteenth and fourteenth-placed teams (JKT Tanzania and [[Kitayosce|Tabora United]]) qualified for the [[#Tanzanian Premier League play-off|Tanzanian Premier League play-off]], both winning and retaining their places in the league. The bottom two teams (Geita Gold and [[Mtibwa Sugar F.C.|Mtibwa Sugar]]) were relegated to the [[2024–25 Tanzanian Championship League]].

==Teams==
The league consisted of 16 teams; the top 13 teams from the previous season, and three teams promoted from the Tanzanian Championship League. [[Young Africans S.C.|Young Africans]] entered the season as defending champions (for the second consecutive season), clinching their 26th Premier League title during the previous season.<ref>{{cite news |title=Yanga SC beat Dodoma Jiji FC to win 29th Premiership title |url=https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/sports/yanga-sc-beat-dodoma-jiji-fc-to-win-29th-premiership-title-4233884 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230513182744/https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/sports/yanga-sc-beat-dodoma-jiji-fc-to-win-29th-premiership-title-4233884 |url-status=dead |archive-date=May 13, 2023 |access-date=10 June 2023 |agency=The Citizen |publisher=The Citizen |date=13 May 2023 |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-06-09 |title=YANGA YAONGEZA NAMBA KATIKA HISTORIA – TPLB |url=https://ligikuu.co.tz/2023/06/09/yanga-yaongeza-namba-katika-historia/ |access-date=2023-06-10 |language=en-US |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230610221445/https://ligikuu.co.tz/2023/06/09/yanga-yaongeza-namba-katika-historia/ |archive-date=2023-06-10 |url-status=dead}}</ref> 

JKT Tanzania and [[Kitayosce|Tabora United]] were promoted as champions and runners-up of the 2022–23 Tanzanian Championship League, replacing the [[2022–23 Tanzanian Premier League]] bottom two teams ([[Polisi Morogoro]] and [[Ruvu Shooting F.C.|Ruvu Shooting]]). Fourth-placed Championship side Mashujaa were also promoted after defeating fourteenth-placed Premier League side [[Mbeya City F.C.|Mbeya City]] in the [[2022–23 Tanzanian Premier League#Tanzanian Premier League play-off|Tanzanian Premier League play-off]] to take their place in the league.

== Tanzanian Premier League play-off ==
The 13th and 14th-placed teams (JKT Tanzania and [[Kitayosce|Tabora United]]) qualified for the Tanzanian Premier League play-off, alongside the 3rd and 4th-placed teams from the 2023–24 Tanzanian Championship League (Mbeya Kwanza and [[Biashara United Mara|Biashara United]]). The Championship teams faced each other over two legs; the Premier League teams then also faced each other over two legs, with the winners remaining in the Tanzanian Premier League and the losers competing against the Championship play-off winners for the final place in the following season's Tanzanian Premier League.
