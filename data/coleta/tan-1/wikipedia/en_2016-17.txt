The '''2016–17 [[Tanzanian Premier League]]''' is the 52nd season of top-tier [[Association football|football]] in [[Tanzania]]. The season started on 20 August 2016 and concluded on 20 May 2017.<ref>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablest/tanz2017.html Tanzania (Mainland) 2016/17], RSSSF.com</ref>
