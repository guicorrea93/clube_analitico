The '''2021–22 Tanzanian Premier League''' (known as the '''NBC Premier League''' for sponsorship reasons)<ref>{{Cite news |title=Tanzania Premier League gets new title sponsor |work=IPP Media (The Guardian) |url=https://www.ippmedia.com/en/sport/tanzania-premier-league-gets-new-title-sponsor-%C2%A0-%C2%A0}}</ref> is the 56th season of the [[Tanzanian Premier League]], the top-tier [[association football|football]] league in [[Tanzania]] (mainland only), since its establishment in 1965. The season started on 6 August 2020. The season ended with [[Simba S.C.]] clinching their 21st premier league title.<ref>{{Cite web |title=‘Simba SC won Tanzania title in May’ – Da Rosa delighted after winning fourth straight crown {{!}} Goal.com |url=https://www.goal.com/en-cm/news/simba-sc-won-tanzania-title-in-may-da-rosa-delighted-after/1af3ssfxg5uel18dzh5canrc37 |access-date=2022-03-13 |website=www.goal.com}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Ligi Kuu Bara 2020/2021 Results, Standings, Table - (Football/Tanzania) - Flashscore.co.uk |url=https://www.flashscore.co.uk/football/tanzania/ligi-kuu-bara-2020-2021/results/ |access-date=2022-03-13 |website=www.flashscore.co.uk |language=en-GB}}</ref>
