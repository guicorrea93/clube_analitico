First Division

NB: The First Division is divided into two different geographical regions,
    the Coastal Stream and the Inland Stream. The League was streamlined
    in 1998 when a 30-team structure was divided into 16 teams in the Inland 
    Stream and 14 in the Coastal Stream, with the winners of each stream 
    gaining promotion to the Premiership. 
    Teams relegated are joining National second division divided into nine 
    provinces.
