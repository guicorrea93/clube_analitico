The '''1985 National Soccer League''' was the ninth season of the [[National Soccer League]]. The season was again played with two Conferences as separate leagues, followed by a conference playoff final. The Grand Final was won by [[Brunswick Zebras Football Club|Brunswick Juventus]] 2–0 on aggregate over [[Hakoah Sydney City East FC|Sydney City]]. The season began on 9 March 1985, and concluded on 8 September 1985. The league was known as the '''Olympic Airways Soccer League''' under a sponsorship arrangement with Greek airline [[Olympic Airways]].<ref>{{Cite news |date=1984-10-31 |title=NSL's $200,000 sponsor for 1985 |url=https://www.newspapers.com/article/the-sydney-morning-herald-nsls-200000/170049390/ |access-date=2025-04-11 |work=The Sydney Morning Herald |pages=43}}</ref>

==Teams==
Twenty-four teams competed in the league (twelve in the Northern Conference and twelve in the Southern Conference) – the sixteen teams from the previous season and eight teams accepted from their respective state leagues.
