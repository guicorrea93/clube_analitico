The '''2012&ndash;13 South African Premiership''' season (known as the '''ABSA Premiership''' for sponsorship reasons) was the seventeenth season of the [[South African Premiership|Premiership]] since its establishment in 1996. The season began in the second week of August 2012.

[[Orlando Pirates F.C.|Orlando Pirates]] were the defending champions, having won the previous [[2011–12 Premier Soccer League|2011–12]] season, but lost their title to [[Kaizer Chiefs]]. The season featured 14 teams from the 2011-12 season and two new teams promoted from the [[2011–12 National First Division]]: [[University of Pretoria F.C.|Tuks FC]] and [[Chippa United F.C.|Chippa United]] who replacer relegated [[Santos Football Club (South Africa)|Santos]] and [[Jomo Cosmos]].

==Format changes==

The 2012–13 season saw the introduction of the [[PSL Q-innovation|Q-innovation]] system. The league schedule was split into four fixture-blocks referred to as quarters, the first and third blocks had eight fixtures, the second and fourth blocks had seven fixtures.<ref name="q-innovation">{{cite web|title=PSL launch exciting Q-Innovation for the Absa Premiership|url=http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=1525961|publisher=PSL|access-date=10 August 2012|date=10 August 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120901074724/http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=1525961|archive-date=1 September 2012|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Prize money was given to the teams who finish top of the table after each block of fixtures.<ref name="q-innovation"/>

==Teams==
A total of 16 teams contested the league, including 14 sides from the [[2011–12 Premier Soccer League|2011–12 season]] and two promoted from the [[2011–12 National First Division]].

Relegation for [[Jomo Cosmos]] to the [[2012–13 National First Division]] was confirmed on 16 May 2012, as ''Jomo Cosmos'' maintained their reputation as a [[yo-yo club]], suffering their third relegation from the Premiership in five seasons.<ref>{{cite web|title=Jomo Cosmos relegated from the PSL after draw with Black Leopards|url=http://www.kickoff.com/league/psl/match-report/7060/jomo-cosmos-relegated-for-the-psl-after-draw-with-black-leopards.php|publisher=Kickoff.com|access-date=16 July 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120521204121/http://www.kickoff.com/league/psl/match-report/7060/jomo-cosmos-relegated-for-the-psl-after-draw-with-black-leopards.php|archive-date=21 May 2012|url-status=dead}}</ref> [[Santos Football Club (South Africa)|Santos]] had finished 15th and thus had to go through the [[2011–12 National First Division#PSL playoff tournament|2011-12 PSL Playoff Tournament]] alongside [[Chippa United]] and [[Thanda Royal Zulu F.C.|Thanda Royal Zulu]] of the [[National First Division]]. ''Santos'' were unable to retain their place in the Premiership following a 4–3 defeat in the final game of the Playoff Tournament on 30 June 2012 and were subsequently relegated to the National First Division for the [[2012-13 National First Division|2012-13 season]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Santos relegated from PSL|url=http://www.sport24.co.za/Soccer/PSL/Santos-relegated-from-PSL-20120630|publisher=Sport24|access-date=16 July 2012}}</ref>

[[University of Pretoria F.C.|Tuks FC]] earned promotion to the Premiership as 2011-12 National First Division Champions.<ref>{{cite web|title=Celebrations as Tuks savour Premier Division promotion|url=http://psl.co.za/article.asp?id=1380421|archive-url=https://archive.today/20130422002500/http://psl.co.za/article.asp?id=1380421|url-status=dead|archive-date=22 April 2013|publisher=PSL|access-date=17 July 2012}}</ref> [[Chippa United F.C.|Chippa United]], who had finished second in the 2011-12 National First Division season, earned their place in the PSL after defeating [[Santos Football Club (South Africa)|Santos]] and [[Thanda Royal Zulu F.C.|Thanda Royal Zulu]] in the 3-team PSL promotion tournament. Both teams made their first appearances in the PSL.

==PSL playoff tournament==
The teams that finished second and third during the ''2012-13 National First Division'' season were joined by the team that finished 15th in the Premiership in a 3-team promotion and relegation playoff called the '''PSL Playoff Tournament'''.

=== Format ===
The 3 teams participated in a mini-league in which they played one another twice (home and away) with log points being awarded for winning a match (3 points) and drawing a match (1 point). At the conclusion of the mini-league phase the team that was in first place would either earn or maintain their place in the [[South African Premiership|Premiership]] for the [[2013–14 South African Premiership|2013-14 season]]. If the team that finished 15th in the league was unable to win the mini-league, they were relegated to the National First Division for the 2013–14 season.
