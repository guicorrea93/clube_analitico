The '''2006–07 South African Premiership''', known as the '''Castle Premiership''' for sponsorship purposes, and also commonly referred to as the [[Premier Soccer League|PSL]] after the governing body, was the eleventh season of the [[South African Premiership|Premiership]] since its establishment in 1996.

The league was won by [[Mamelodi Sundowns F.C.|Mamelodi Sundowns]], who defended their title from the previous season.<ref>{{Cite web |title=South Africa 2005/06 |url=https://www.rsssf.org/tablesz/zaf06.html |access-date=2024-12-24 |website=www.rsssf.org}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=South Africa 2006/07 |url=https://www.rsssf.org/tablesz/zaf07.html |access-date=2024-12-24 |website=www.rsssf.org}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |author=Staff Reporter |date=2007-09-27 |title=Sundowns reign supreme in PSL |url=https://mg.co.za/article/2007-09-27-sundowns-reign-supreme-in-psl/ |access-date=2025-01-01 |website=The Mail & Guardian |language=en-ZA}}</ref>

== Premiership awards ==
* Player of the Season: [[Godfrey Sapula]] ([[Mamelodi Sundowns]])
* Players' Player of the Season: [[Godfrey Sapula]]
* Coach of the Season: [[Muhsin Ertugral]] ([[Ajax Cape Town]])
* Lesley Manyathela Golden Boot Award: [[Christopher Katongo]] ([[Jomo Cosmos]])
* Young Player Award: [[Clifford Ngobeni]] ([[Jomo Cosmos]])
* Goalkeeper of the Season: [[Calvin Marlin]] ([[Mamelodi Sundowns]])
* Finest Moment of the Season: [[Mamelodi Sundowns]] vs [[Kaizer Chiefs]] (3 February 2007)
* Perfectly Balanced Team of the Season: [[Mamelodi Sundowns]]
* Referee of the Season: [[Abdul Ebrahim]]
* Assistant Referee of the Season: [[Toko Malebo]]
* Supporters through Commitment Award: [[Bloemfontein Celtic]]
