The '''1971 National Professional Soccer League season''' was the first season of the [[National Professional Soccer League (South Africa)|National Professional Soccer League]]. [[Orlando Pirates]] won the inaugural title.

At the time, due to the country's [[apartheid]] policies, the competition was only open to black South African teams, and it ran in parallel with the [[Federation Professional League|FPL]] and the [[National Football League (South Africa)|NFL]].
