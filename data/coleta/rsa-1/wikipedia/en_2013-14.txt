The '''2013&ndash;14 South African Premiership''' season (known as the '''[[ABSA Premiership]]''' for sponsorship reasons, and also commonly referred to as the [[Premier Soccer League|PSL]] after the governing body) was the eighteenth season of the [[South African Premiership|Premiership]] since its establishment in 1996. The draw for the fixtures was held on 20 June 2013.<ref>{{cite web|title=PSL Fixtures Announced|url=http://www.soccerladuma.co.za/news/article/south-africa/psl-fixtures-announced|publisher=Soccer Laduma|access-date=2 August 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130625081359/http://www.soccerladuma.co.za/news/article/south-africa/psl-fixtures-announced|archive-date=25 June 2013|url-status=dead|df=dmy-all}}</ref> The season was scheduled to begin on 2 August 2013 and end on 10 May 2014.

[[Kaizer Chiefs F.C.|Kaizer Chiefs]] were the defending champions, having won the previous [[2012–13 Premier Soccer League]] (PSL) season. The season featured 14 teams from the 2012-13 season and two new teams promoted from the [[2012–13 National First Division]]: [[Polokwane City F.C.|Polokwane City]] and [[Mpumalanga Black Aces F.C.|Mpumalanga Black Aces]] who replace relegated [[Black Leopards F.C.|Black Leopards]] and [[Chippa United F.C.|Chippa United]].

==Teams==
A total of 16 teams contested the league, including 14 sides from the [[2012–13 Premier Soccer League|2012–13 season]] and two promoted from the [[2012–13 National First Division]].
