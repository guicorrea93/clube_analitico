The '''1995 NSL First Division''' was the 11th edition of the [[National Soccer League (South Africa)|National Soccer League]] First Division. The competition was won by [[Cape Town Spurs]], coached by [[Mich d'Avray]],<ref name=bc>{{Cite web|url=http://www.bloemfonteincelticfc.co.za/profiles/player/mich.htm |title=Player profiles - Mich D'Avray |date=31 July 2008 |accessdate=7 October 2008 |publisher=[[Bloemfontein Celtic]] |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20081225012019/http://www.bloemfonteincelticfc.co.za/profiles/player/mich.htm |archivedate=25 December 2008 }}</ref> who won the double by also winning the [[1995 Bob Save Superbowl]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=1995-11-03 |title=Spurs dribble for the double |url=https://mg.co.za/article/1995-11-03-spurs-dribble-for-the-double/ |access-date=2023-06-15 |website=The Mail & Guardian |language=en-ZA}}</ref>
