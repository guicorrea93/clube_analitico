The '''2016–17 South African Premiership''' season (known as the '''[[ABSA Premiership]]''' for sponsorship reasons) is the 21st season of the Premiership since its establishment in 1996.

[[Mamelodi Sundowns F.C.|Mamelodi Sundowns]] were the defending champions, having won the previous [[2015–16 South African Premier Division]] (PSL) season. The season featured 14 teams from the 2015–16 season and two new teams promoted from the [[2015–16 National First Division]]: [[Baroka F.C.|Baroka]] and [[Highlands Park F.C.|Highlands Park]] who replace relegated [[Jomo Cosmos F.C.|Jomo Cosmos]] and [[University of Pretoria F.C.|University of Pretoria]]. Highlands Park promotion was won via the [[2015–16 National First Division#Play-offs|PSL Playoff Tournament]]. [[Cape Town City F.C.
