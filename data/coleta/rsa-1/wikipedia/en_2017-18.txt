The '''2017–18 South African Premiership''' season (known as the '''ABSA Premiership''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 22nd season of the Premiership since its conception in 1996.

[[Bidvest Wits]] were the defending champions, having won the previous [[2016–17 South African Premier Division|2016–17]] [[South African Premier Division]] season. The season featured 15 teams from the 2016–17 season and one new team from the [[2016–17 National First Division]]: [[AmaZulu F.C.|AmaZulu]] replaced relegated [[Highlands Park F.C.|Highlands Park]]. AmaZulu, although finishing only fifth in the National First Division (NFD) purchased winner's [[Thanda Royal Zulu F.C.|Thanda Royal Zulu's]] league status upon completion of the NFD season.<ref>{{cite web |title=PSL approve AmaZulu-Thanda sale |url=http://www.kickoff.com/mobile/news/76891/the-psl-have-approved-amazulus-purchase-of-thanda-royal-zulus-top-flight-status |publisher=Kick-Off |access-date=6 July 2017 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180917080747/http://www.kickoff.com/mobile/news/76891/the-psl-have-approved-amazulus-purchase-of-thanda-royal-zulus-top-flight-status |archive-date=17 September 2018 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=South Africa's AmaZulu FC win promotion by buying league champions |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/football/40563709 |publisher=BBC Sport |access-date=12 July 2017}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=PSL APPROVES AMAZULU-THANDA ROYAL ZULU SALE |url=http://www.goal.com/en-za/news/psl-approves-amazulu-thanda-royal-zulu-sale/cadc616zei4g1ac6urycbd8l9 |publisher=Goal |access-date=6 July 2017}}</ref>
