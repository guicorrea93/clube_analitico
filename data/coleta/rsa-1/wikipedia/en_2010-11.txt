The '''2010–11 South African Premiership''', known as the '''ABSA Premiership''' for sponsorship purposes, and also commonly referred to as the [[Premier Soccer League|PSL]] after the governing body, was the fifteenth season of the [[South African Premiership|Premiership]] since its establishment in 1996.

[[Supersport United]] were the defending champions, having won their third Premiership title the previous season. The campaign began on 27 August 2010 and ended on 21 May 2011.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=361245 |title=A bumper start to the Absa Premiership |access-date=14 August 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120229134109/http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=361245 |archive-date=29 February 2012 |url-status=dead |df=dmy-all }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.psl.co.za/fixtures.asp?id=794 |title=2010–11 Fixture List |access-date=14 August 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100815004336/http://www.psl.co.za/fixtures.asp?id=794 |archive-date=15 August 2010 |url-status=dead |df=dmy-all }}</ref> A total of 16 teams contested the league, 15 of which already contested in the [[2009–10 South African Premiership|2009–10 season]] and one of which was promoted from the [[2009-10 National First Division|National First Division]].

[[Orlando Pirates]] secured the title with a 2–1 win against [[Golden Arrows]] on 21 May 2011.<ref name="Pirates win Absa Premiership title">{{cite web|title=Pirates win Absa Premiership title|url=http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=418316|publisher=[[Premier Soccer League|PSL]]|date=21 May 2011|access-date=21 May 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110804024136/http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=418316|archive-date=4 August 2011|url-status=dead|df=dmy-all}}</ref> This was their third Premiership title. At the bottom, [[Mpumalanga Black Aces]] was relegated to the First Division<ref name="Aces looking for companions in their woes">{{cite web|title=Aces looking for companions in their woes|url=http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=415516|publisher=[[Premier Soccer League|PSL]]|date=29 April 2011|access-date=29 April 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120314015935/http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=415516|archive-date=14 March 2012|url-status=dead|df=dmy-all}}</ref> while [[Vasco da Gama (South Africa)|Vasco da Gama]] contested the relegation playoffs and failed to secure its premier league status.<ref name="Vasco to contest playoffs">{{cite web|title=Vasco to contest playoffs|url=http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=418336|publisher=[[Premier Soccer League|PSL]]|date=21 May 2011|access-date=21 May 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110803062725/http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=418336|archive-date=3 August 2011|url-status=dead|df=dmy-all}}</ref><ref name="Vasco secure a draw with Leopards">{{cite web|title=Vasco secure a draw with Leopards|url=http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=420587|publisher=[[Premier Soccer League|PSL]]|date=8 June 2011|access-date=8 June 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120314020522/http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=420587|archive-date=14 March 2012|url-status=dead|df=dmy-all}}</ref><ref name="Leopards relegate Vasco">{{cite web|title=Leopards relegate Vasco|url=http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=420953|publisher=[[Premier Soccer League|PSL]]|date=11 June 2011|access-date=11 June 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120314020537/http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=420953|archive-date=14 March 2012|url-status=dead|df=dmy-all}}</ref>

The [[Soweto Derby]] on 26 February 2011 attracted the highest crowd with 92,515. The second-highest crowd was the [[Soweto Derby]] on 13 November 2011 with 74,875.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Soweto Derby Attendance Record For The Last Five Years {{!}} Soccer Laduma | url=https://www.soccerladuma.co.za/fan-park/soweto-derby-attendance-record-for-the-last-five-years-20150303 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251231133714/https://www.soccerladuma.co.za/fan-park/soweto-derby-attendance-record-for-the-last-five-years-20150303 | access-date=2026-09-15 | archive-date=2025-12-31}}</ref>
