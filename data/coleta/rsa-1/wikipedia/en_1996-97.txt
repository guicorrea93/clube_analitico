The '''1996–97 South African Premiership''', known as the '''1996–97 Castle Premiership''' for sponsorship purposes, and also commonly referred to as the [[Premier Soccer League|PSL]] after the governing body, was the first season of the newly established top-flight professional [[Football (soccer)|football]] league in South Africa. The league was an 18-team competition established in 1996 by [[Irvin Khoza]], [[Kaizer Motaung]], Raymond Hack and [[Jomo Sono]] in conjunction with the [[South African Football Association]] and it would run parallel to the European football calendar.

==Season summary==
[[Manning Rangers]] comfortably finished top of the log to win the first Premiership title ahead of several more fancied opponents.<ref>{{Cite web |author=Staff Reporter |date=1997-06-20 |title=Unknowns who made Rangers famous |url=https://mg.co.za/article/1997-06-20-unknowns-who-made-rangers-famous/ |access-date=2024-11-28 |website=The Mail & Guardian |language=en-ZA}}</ref> Rangers, known as the ''Mighty Maulers'', dominated to such an extent that they finished eight points clear of second-placed [[Kaizer Chiefs]] and a further two ahead of [[Orlando Pirates]].

The Chiefs, known as the ''Amakhosi'', had a more potent attack and a better defensive record than Rangers, and they lost fewer games, but their propensity to draw matches that they should have won cost them the trophy. It handed [[Gordon Igesund]] the first of four Premiership titles that he would win in the competition and got South Africa's new top-flight league off to a noteworthy start.

[[Bush Bucks F.C. (1957)|Umtata Bush Bucks]] and [[Hellenic FC|Hellenic]] completed the top five, with [[Mamelodi Sundowns F.C.|Mamelodi Sundowns]] in sixth, [[Jomo Cosmos]] in seventh and [[Ajax Cape Town F.C.|Cape Town Spurs]] in eighth.

Popular clubs [[Bloemfontein Celtic F.C.|Bloemfontein Celtic]] and [[Moroka Swallows F.C.|Moroka Swallows]] came in 10th and 11th respectively, while KwaZulu-Natal giants [[AmaZulu F.C.|AmaZulu]] finished 14th on the 18-team log.

The first two teams to be relegated to the second tier from the Premiership were [[Mpumalanga Black Aces F.C.|Witbank Aces]] – who won just four games all season and finished with 19 points – and [[Michau Warriors]]. Real Rovers and [[Vaal Professionals]] (36 points each) finished a single point ahead of Warriors and avoided the demotion.

There was an abandoned game between Bloemfontein Celtic and Kaizer Chiefs on Sunday 16 February 1997. The game in Bloemfontein was called off in the 40th minute, with the home side trailing Chiefs 2-0, due to crowd trouble. Kaizer Chiefs were given the result along with 3 log points due to the trouble with the crowd being instigated by the Celtic fans. This was a rather surprising turn of events as the Celtic fans, while having a reputation for being extremely vocal and passionate, are not known to be unruly.<ref>{{Cite web |author=Staff Reporter |title=Five Kaizer Chiefs matches that were abandoned over the years |url=https://www.snl24.com/kickoff/sanews/premiership/five-kaizer-chiefs-matches-that-were-abandoned-over-the-years-20211206 |access-date=2024-11-28 |website=Kickoff |language=en-US}}</ref>
