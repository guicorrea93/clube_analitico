The '''1983 National Soccer League''' was the seventh season of the [[National Soccer League]], the former top-flight league in [[Australia]]. It began on 13 March 1983, and ended on 9 October 1983. [[St George FC|St George-Budapest]] won the championship for the first time.

==Changes from 1982==
For this season, the league shifted wins to be worth [[Three points for a win|three points]] instead of two. No post-season finals series were played during this season for the first time since the [[1977 National Soccer League|1977]] season.

==Teams==
Sixteen teams competed in the league.
