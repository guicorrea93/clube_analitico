The '''2011–12 South African Premiership''', known as the '''ABSA Premiership''' for sponsorship purposes, and also commonly referred to as the [[Premier Soccer League|PSL]] after the governing body, was the sixteenth season of the [[South African Premiership|Premiership]] since its establishment in 1996.

[[Orlando Pirates F.C.|Orlando Pirates]] were the defending champions, having won their third Premiership title, and in the process ended [[SuperSport United F.C.|SuperSport United]]'s 3 year grip on the championship. The season kicked off on 12 August 2011 and ended on 19 May 2012.<ref>{{cite web|title=2010–11 Fixtures released|url=http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=359595|publisher=PSL.com|date=16 July 2011|access-date=30 August 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110827041321/http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=359595|archive-date=27 August 2011|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=ABSA Premiership fixture list|url=http://www.psl.co.za/fixtures.asp?id=794|publisher=PSL.com|access-date=30 August 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100815004336/http://www.psl.co.za/fixtures.asp?id=794|archive-date=15 August 2010|url-status=dead|df=dmy-all}}</ref>

16 teams contested the season, including two newly promoted teams – yo-yo club [[Jomo Cosmos]]<ref>{{cite web|title=Cosmos beat Bay United 5–4|url=http://www.sabcnews.co.za/portal/site/SABCNews/menuitem.5c4f8fe7ee929f602ea12ea1674daeb9/?vgnextoid=7d52f63461810310VgnVCM10000077d4ea9bRCRD&vgnextfmt=default|archive-url=https://archive.today/20120908201151/http://www.sabcnews.co.za/portal/site/SABCNews/menuitem.5c4f8fe7ee929f602ea12ea1674daeb9/?vgnextoid=7d52f63461810310VgnVCM10000077d4ea9bRCRD&vgnextfmt=default|url-status=dead|archive-date=8 September 2012|publisher=SABCNews|date=22 May 2011|access-date=30 August 2011}}</ref> – who were promoted for the second time in 3 seasons after winning the [[2010–11 National First Division]] champions' playoff 5–4 on penalties against [[Bay United F.C.|Bay United]], and [[Black Leopards F.C.|Black Leopards]]<ref>{{cite web|title=Leopards return to the top-flight|url=http://www.kickoff.com/news/22416/black-leopards-win-promotional-play-off-final-vs-bay-united.php|publisher=Kickoff.com|date=18 June 2011|access-date=30 August 2011}}</ref> who came through a four-way promotion playoff, defeating [[Bay United F.C.|Bay United]] 2–0 over a two-legged final. [[Orlando Pirates F.C.|Orlando Pirates]] secured the title with a 4–2 win against [[Golden Arrows]] on 19 May 2012.<ref name="Benni shines as Bucs conquer the Absa Premiership">{{cite web|title=Benni shines as Bucs conquer the Absa Premiership|url=http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=1388881|publisher=[[Premier Soccer League|PSL]]|date=19 May 2012|access-date=19 May 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120521202303/http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=1388881|archive-date=21 May 2012|url-status=dead}}</ref> This was their fourth PSL title overall and the second in succession. At the bottom, [[Jomo Cosmos]] was automatically relegated to the First Division<ref name="Heartbreak as Cosmos is relegated to the NFD">{{cite web|title=Heartbreak as Cosmos is relegated to the NFD|url=http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=1384711|publisher=[[Premier Soccer League|PSL]]|date=16 May 2012|access-date=16 May 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131024170943/http://psl.co.za/article.asp?id=1384711|archive-date=24 October 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref> while [[Santos Football Club (South Africa)|Santos]] was relegated via the [[2011–12 National First Division#PSL playoff tournament|PSL Playoff Tournament]].<ref name="Nooij: only ourselves to blame for predicament">{{cite web|title=Nooij: only ourselves to blame for predicament|url=http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=1390771|publisher=[[Premier Soccer League|PSL]]|date=21 May 2012|access-date=21 May 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303234206/http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=1390771|archive-date=3 March 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name="Leopards secure PSL status">{{cite web|title=Leopards secure PSL status|url=http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=1389051|publisher=[[Premier Soccer League|PSL]]|date=19 May 2012|access-date=19 May 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120619155904/http://psl.co.za/article.asp?id=1389051|archive-date=19 June 2012|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name="Santos relegated from PSL">{{cite web|title=Santos relegated from PSL|url=http://www.sport24.co.za/Soccer/PSL/Santos-relegated-from-PSL-20120630|publisher=Sport24.com|date=30 June 2012 |access-date=18 July 2012}}</ref>

==TV rights==
The PSL announced that current digital broadcaster [[SuperSport (South African broadcaster)|SuperSport]] had won the rights to continue broadcasting the PSL after their current contract expired at the end of the 2011–12 season. The new broadcasting deal will commence at the start of the 2012–13 season and is said to be worth R2 billion over five years. As with their outgoing contract, SuperSport will continue to lease a select number of matches to free-to-air public broadcaster [[South African Broadcasting Corporation|SABC]].<ref>{{cite web|title=PSL and SuperSport continue partnership|url=http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=430192|publisher=PSL.com|date=19 August 2011|access-date=30 August 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304002258/http://www.psl.co.za/article.asp?id=430192|archive-date=4 March 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Teams==
The team finishing 16th and last during the [[2010–11 South African Premiership|2010–11 season]] – [[Mpumalanga Black Aces]] – was automatically relegated from the Premiership while the team finishing 15th – [[Vasco da Gama (South Africa)|Vasco da Gama]] – was entered into a four-way relegation/promotion playoff with 3 teams from the 2010–11 National First Division season, where they were comfortably beaten 4–2 on aggregate by Black Leopards in the semi-finals and thus relegated from the Premiership. As it was, both Black Aces and Vasco had been promoted in the previous season, so they each spent a single season in the top flight before going straight back down.{{citation needed|date=August 2023}}

Jomo Cosmos and Black Leopards had finished first and second in the Inland Stream of the 2010–11 National First Division season. Cosmos, as champions of the Inland Stream, faced off against the champions of the Coastal Stream – Bay United – and beat them 5–4 on penalties after two goalless draws, thus securing promotion to the PSL. In the next phase of the relegation/promotion playoffs Black Leopards had taken on and defeated Vasco da Gama of the PSL over two legs and proceeded to reach the final of the playoffs, where they met Bay United. Leopards continued Bay United's heartbreaking end to their season by defeating them over two legs to earn a return to the PSL after two seasons out of the top flight.{{citation needed|date=August 2023}}

The ''PSL Footballer of the Year'' was awarded to [[Siyabonga Nomvethe]].<ref name="PSLAwards">{{cite news |author=Kickoff.com |title=All the PSL Award winners |url=http://www.kickoff.com/news/28630/all-the-psl-award-winners.php |publisher=kickoff.com |date=27 May 2012 |access-date=17 July 2012}}</ref>

Palesa Shabalala
The ''PSL Player of the Season'' was also awarded to Siyabonga Nomvethe.<ref name="PSLAwards"/>

===PSL Players' Player of the Season===
The ''PSL Players' Player of the Season'' was also awarded to Siyabonga Nomvethe.<ref name="PSLAwards"/>

===PSL Red Hot Young Player of the Season===
The ''PSL Red Hot Young Player of the Season'' was awarded to [[Ronwen Williams]].<ref name="PSLAwards"/>

===PSL Coach of the Season===
The ''PSL Coach of the Season'' was awarded to [[Gordon Igesund]].<ref name="PSLAwards"/>

===PSL Top Goalscorer===
The ''PSL Top Goalscorer'' award went to Siyabonga Nomvethe.<ref name="PSLAwards"/>

===PSL Goalkeeper of the Season===
The ''PSL Goalkeeper of the Season'' award went to [[Wayne Sandilands]].<ref name="PSLAwards"/>

===PSL Absa-lutely Awesome Goal of the Season===
The ''PSL Absa-lutely Awesome Goal of the Season'' award went to [[Benni McCarthy]].<ref name="PSLAwards"/>
