The '''2018–19 South African Premiership''' season (known as the '''[[South African Premiership|ABSA Premiership]]''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 23rd season of the Premiership since its establishment in [[1996–97 South African Premiership|1996]]. The season began in August 2018 and concluded in May 2019, with [[Mamelodi Sundowns F.C.|Mamelodi Sundowns]] winning their second consecutive title.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Mamelodi Sundowns crowned PSL champions |url=https://www.news24.com/Sport/mamelodi-sundowns-crowned-psl-champions-20190511 |access-date=2024-12-26 |website=Sport |language=en-US}}</ref>
