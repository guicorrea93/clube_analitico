The '''2020–21 South African Premiership''' season (known as the '''DSTV Premiership''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 25th season of the [[South African Premiership|Premiership]] since its establishment in [[1996–97 Premier Soccer League|1996]]. [[Mamelodi Sundowns F.C.|Mamelodi Sundowns]] won the title for the fourth consecutive year, qualifying for the [[2021–22 CAF Champions League]] along with second placed [[AmaZulu F.C.|AmaZulu]]. Third placed [[Orlando Pirates F.C.|Orlando Pirates]] and [[2020–21 Nedbank Cup|Nedbank Cup]] winners [[Tshakhuma Tsha Madzivhandila F.C.|Tshakhuma Tsha Madzivhandila]] qualified for the [[2021–22 CAF Confederation Cup]].
