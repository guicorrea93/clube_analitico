The '''1976 National Professional Soccer League''' was the sixth season of the [[National Professional Soccer League (South Africa)|National Professional Soccer League]], a [[Soccer in South Africa|South African soccer]] league. It was won by [[Orlando Pirates]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Pirates can move ahead of Downs on all-time titles list |url=https://www.sowetanlive.co.za/sport/2012-05-11-pirates-can-move-ahead-of-downs-on-all-time-titles-list/ |access-date=2023-12-31 |website=SowetanLIVE |language=en-ZA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Koketso |title=The Dark Age of Orlando Pirates – Pro Philani Media |url=https://prophilanimedia.co.za/the-dark-age-of-orlando-pirates/ |access-date=2024-11-21 |language=en-ZA}}</ref>

At the time, due to the country's [[apartheid]] policies, the competition was only open to black South African teams, and it ran in parallel with the [[Federation Professional League|FPL]] and the [[National Football League (South Africa)|NFL]].
