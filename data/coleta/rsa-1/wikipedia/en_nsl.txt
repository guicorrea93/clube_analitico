The '''National Soccer League''' ('''NSL''') was a [[Soccer in South Africa|South African]] [[South African Football Association|football league]] established in 1985 as a response to boardroom disagreements in the non-racial [[National Professional Soccer League (South Africa)|NPSL]].<ref>{{Cite web |author=Staff Reporter |date=2000-02-18 |title=A diary of disarray |url=https://mg.co.za/article/2000-02-18-a-diary-of-disarray/ |access-date=2025-01-23 |website=The Mail & Guardian |language=en-ZA}}</ref> The NPSL had been established in 1978 after a merger between the white [[National Football League (South Africa)|NFL]] and the previous racial black NPSL. A portion of the NPSL continued under its own name, and later merged with the NSL to form today's [[Premier Soccer League]] (PSL).

The NSL faced accusations of endemic bribery and corruption in its ranks.<ref>{{Cite web |author=Staff Reporter |date=1994-07-29 |title=Blowing The Whistle On Corruption |url=https://mg.co.za/article/1994-07-29-blowing-the-whistle-on-corruption/ |access-date=2025-03-15 |website=The Mail & Guardian |language=en-ZA}}</ref>
