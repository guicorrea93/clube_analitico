The '''1982 National Soccer League''' was the sixth season of the [[National Soccer League]], the former top-flight league in Australia. [[Hakoah Sydney City East FC|Sydney City]] won the championship for the third consecutive season.

==Teams==
Sixteen teams competed in the league.
