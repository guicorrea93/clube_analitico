The '''1989 National Soccer League''' season, was the 13th season of the [[National Soccer League]] (NSL) in [[Australia]].

==Background==
The [[Football Federation Australia|Australian Soccer Federation]] began the year by announcing that the NSL would be televised on [[ABC (Australian TV channel)|ABC]] rather than on [[SBS (Australian TV channel)|SBS]] who had shown the league since 1979.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Blake |first=Greg |date=18 January 1989 |title=Television rumpus |page=1 |work=Australian Soccer Weekly |url=https://drive.google.com/file/d/1uALfJuZazydTUvTX_VucrJT-1HOe5r7P/view?usp=drive_open&usp=embed_facebook |access-date=2020-06-08 |via=Melbourne Soccer}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |last=Schwab |first=Laurie |date=14 January 1989 |title=Soccer prepares for new season with the ABC |language=en |page=41 |work=The Age |url=http://www.newspapers.com/image/120577735/ |url-access=subscription |access-date=2020-06-08 |via=Newspapers.com}}</ref> SBS challenged the decision in the [[New South Wales Supreme Court]] and were allowed to show matches in rounds 1 and 2 before the court found in favour of the ABC.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Gautier |first=Lou |date=1 February 1989 |title=ABC–SBS Show Hits The Road |page=2 |work=Australian Soccer Weekly |url=https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ljn3p5dqckT9BiVefOM-klBlEeiSg8ji/view |access-date=2020-06-08 |via=Melbourne Soccer}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |last=Cockerill |first=Michael |date=7 February 1989 |title=Match crowds decide summer game debate |language=en |page=45 |work=The Sydney Morning Herald |url=http://www.newspapers.com/image/122767841/ |url-access=subscription |access-date=2020-06-08 |via=Newspapers.com}}</ref>
