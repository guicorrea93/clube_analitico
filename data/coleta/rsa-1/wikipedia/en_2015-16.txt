The '''2015&ndash;16 South African Premiership''' season (known as the '''ABSA Premiership''' for sponsorship reasons) was the twentieth season of the [[South African Premiership|Premiership]] since its establishment in 1996.

[[Mamelodi Sundowns F.C.|Mamelodi Sundowns]] won their seventh league title in dominant fashion, finishing 14 points ahead of second-place [[Bidvest Wits F.C.|Bidvest Wits]]. League newcomers [[Jomo Cosmos F.C.|Jomo Cosmos]] dropped into last place for the first time all season in their final game, and were relegated to the [[National First Division]] for the 2016-17 season.
