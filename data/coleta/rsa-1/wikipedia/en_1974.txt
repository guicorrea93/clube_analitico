The '''1974 National Professional Soccer League season''' was the fourth season of the [[National Professional Soccer League (South Africa)|National Professional Soccer League]], a [[Soccer in South Africa|South African soccer]] league. It was won by [[Kaizer Chiefs F.C.|Kaizer Chiefs]].

At the time, due to the country's [[apartheid]] policies, the competition was only open to black South African teams, and it ran in parallel with the [[Federation Professional League|FPL]] and the [[National Football League (South Africa)|NFL]]
