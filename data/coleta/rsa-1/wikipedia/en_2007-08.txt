The '''2007–08 South African Premiership''', known as the '''ABSA Premiership''' for sponsorship purposes, and also commonly referred to as the [[Premier Soccer League|PSL]] after the governing body, was the twelfth season of the [[South African Premiership|Premiership]] since its establishment in 1996.

It was won by [[SuperSport United F.C.|Supersport United]], their first league title.<ref name="rssf08">{{Cite web |title=South Africa 2007/08 |url=https://www.rsssf.org/tablesz/zaf08.html |access-date=2025-01-01 |website=www.rsssf.org}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |author=Staff Reporter |date=2008-05-18 |title=Ajax ‘robbed’ of PSL top spot |url=https://mg.co.za/article/2008-05-18-ajax-robbed-of-psl-top-spot/ |access-date=2025-01-01 |website=The Mail & Guardian |language=en-ZA}}</ref>

== Team changes ==
Silver Stars, runners-up in the previous season, were sponsored by the [[Royal Bafokeng Nation]], and were renamed [[Platinum Stars F.C.|Platinum Stars]], moving to the [[Royal Bafokeng Stadium]] near [[Rustenburg]], while [[Benoni United F.C.|Benoni United]] sold their franchise to [[Thanda Royal Zulu F.C.|Thanda Zulu Royal]], located in Durban.<ref name="rssf08" />

[[Maritzburg United F.C.|Maritzburg United]] were relegated at the end of the [[2006–07 South African Premiership|2006–07 season]], and replaced by [[2006–07 National First Division]] champions [[Free State Stars F.C.|Free State Stars]].

== Sponsorship ==
The league was renamed the ABSA Premiership after a R500-million sponsorship deal with banking group ABSA.<ref>{{Cite web |author=Staff Reporter |date=2007-09-26 |title=Absa becomes main sponsor of PSL |url=https://mg.co.za/article/2007-09-26-absa-becomes-main-sponsor-of-psl/ |access-date=2025-01-01 |website=The Mail & Guardian |language=en-ZA}}</ref>
