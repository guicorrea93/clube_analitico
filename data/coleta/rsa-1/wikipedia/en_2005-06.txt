The '''2005–06 South African Premiership''', known as the '''Castle Premiership''' for sponsorship purposes, and also commonly referred to as the [[Premier Soccer League|PSL]] after the governing body, was the tenth season of the [[South African Premiership|Premiership]] since its establishment in 1996.

It was won by [[Mamelodi Sundowns F.C.|Mamelodi Sundowns]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=South Africa 2005/06 |url=https://www.rsssf.org/tablesz/zaf06.html |access-date=2025-01-01 |website=www.rsssf.org}}</ref>
