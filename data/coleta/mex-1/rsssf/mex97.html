Mexico 1996/97

On April 24, 1996 the Mexican FA announced that the 1996-97 Mexican Football League  
(Primera Division & Primera División A) is split into two different tournaments 
(Invierno (Winter) 1996 and Verano (Summer) 1997).  

In each tournament, 
a 17-game regular season will be followed by a playoff to determine the champion.

Playoff qualification for each tournament will be determined by the following criteria:

(a) The winner of every group (4 in total) goes right to the quarter-finals, that is,
    has a 1st-round bye.

(b) The runner-up of every group goes to the playoffs as well. The top two runner-ups
    get a 1st-round bye. The third and fourth runner up (RU.3 & RU.4) might play in
    the 1st-round playoff against two wild card teams.

(c) The top two teams of the remaining ten not yet considered will go to the 1st-round 
    playoff ONLY if they have a better overall standing than that of the lowest 
    qualified team according to (a) and (b) above. They are tagged WC.1 and WC.2
    (wild cards) according to their final overall standing.
 
(d) Every match-up is played in a home-to-home series with the following tie-breaker
     criteria:

    (i) Aggregate score.
   (ii) Away goals.
  (iii) Final overall standing.

Therefore, every series (except the final) will be decided after 180 minutes of action 
(no extra time).
Tie-breaker (ii) and (iii) are removed for the final, allowing for the typical extra-time period 
plus penalty shots should it be necessary.
