International Match

For the first time the League decided to play a match between an English team and a Spanish team.
White Team composed of players from Rovers and Reforma against the Red Team with players from España and Club Mexico.

[Jan 19, 1913]
Grounds of Reforma Athletic Club
White Team  1-1 Red Team
  [Robert Pryor (p); Delio Bonet]
