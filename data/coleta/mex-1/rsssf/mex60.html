NB: *On June 24, 1959 the owner of Toluca, Luis Gutierrez Dosal, passed away, Toluca 
    requested to postpone its match of round 1, but when its request was denied they 
    decided don't show up, match awarded to Atlas 1-0.

NB: + On June 25, 1959 the city government closed the Estadio de la Ciudad de los Deportes 
      due to the inclusion of old demands for the improvement of the property.
      On July 2, 1959, the three teams of Mexico City agreed to play the rest of the 
      season in Estadio de la Ciudad Universitaria.
