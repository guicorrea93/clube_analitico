Mexico 1931/32

After the cancelation of 1930/31 season, eight teams took part in 1931/32 season.

Leones OP and Sporting replaced Real Club España and Club México. 

Venues:
Parque Asturias I, Paseo de la Reforma
Parque Necaxa, Pueblo de La Piedad

All the teams from Mexico City
