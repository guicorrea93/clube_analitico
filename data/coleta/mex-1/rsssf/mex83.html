1982-83 Segunda División B (Third Level)

Inaugural Season

The Second División B was created in Mexico since the season 1982-83 as intermediate league between the Segunda División and the Tercera División, except the inaugural season, the winners and the runners-up were promoted to Segunda División A and the last two teams were relegated to Tercera División; after the season 1993-94 it was dissolved.

Teams relegated from Segunda División after 1981-82 season: Irapuato, Bachilleres, Lobos Tlaxcala and Tuberos

Teams promoted from Tercera División after 1981-82 season: Celaya (runners-up), Martínez de la Torre, Nezahualcoyotl, U.A.B.J., Uruapán, U.V. Xalapa, U.A.Tamaulipas, Iguala, U.P.A.E.P., Orizaba, Industriales Tepatitlán, Tabasco, Estudiantes Chiapas, Átomos, Salmantino, Durango.
