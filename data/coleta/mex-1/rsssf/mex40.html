Round 17
[Dec 24]
Parque Necaxa
Marte    1-3 Necaxa
  [Pablo González 1t; Donato Alonso 50, 76, José Pizano 1t]
América   - Asturias*
  [Octavio Vial 1, Jorge Sota 75, Luis García Cortina 78, Pedro Andrade Pradillo 84; 
   Enrique Larrinaga 19, 31, Tomás Ordóñez 26]
*Originally 4-3, match annulled due to the intervention of the non-football authority that decreed 
the ejection of Asturias' player José Soto before the start of the second half; on January 2 and 9, 
the annulment of the result and the repetition of the match was determined, which did not happen because 
there were no available dates and because it no longer affected the outcome of the tournament.

Tournament interrumpted due to visit of Vélez Sarsfield (Argentina) from Jan. 1 to Feb. 11, 1940.
