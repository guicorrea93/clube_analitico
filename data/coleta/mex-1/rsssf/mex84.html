Mexico 1983/84

Copa Federación Mexicana de Futbol 1983 (Extraordinary Cup Tournament)

Cup tournament played between some teams of first, second and third level.

First level teams: Atlas, Atlético Morelia, Atlético Potosino, Cruz Azul, Deportivo Neza, 
   León, Oaxtepec, Tampico-Madero, Toluca, U.A.G., U.A.N.L. and U.N.A.M. 
 
Second Level teams (Segunda División A): Azucareros Córdoba, Irapuato, Pachuca, 
   Salamanca, S.U.O.O., Tepic, Texcoco, Tulancingo, U.A.B.J., U.A.Tamaulipas, Veracruz and 
   Zamora.

Third level teams (Segunda División B): Iguala, Martínez de la Torre, Tabasco and Uruapan.

Only matches between teams of first and second or third level, the winners of each group 
  qualified to final, runners-up play for third place.
