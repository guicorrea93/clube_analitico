Necaxa       –  RC España*
*According to the fixture published before the start of the season, Necaxa was supposed to face España, however, 
  the league decided that the match of the 11th round between Asturias and Germania would be played, the latter, 
  announced that they would not appear as indeed happened, Asturias for its part carried out the protocol that gave 
  them the game won by forfeit 1-0, days later, the league determined that the match would be held on January 17.
