Mexico 1985/86


Due to celebration in Mexico of FIFA world Cup 1986, the Mexican FA cancelled the season 1985-86, 
which was replaced by two short tournaments called "PRODE 85" and "MÉXICO 86".

In the first one there were created 4 groups of 5 teams each one, only were played matches
between the teams of the same group.

There was no relegation.

NB: Originally both tournaments (Prode 85 and México 86) were not considered as official 
    league championships.
    In October 1986, after obtaining FIFA's endorsement, the Mexican FA included them as 
    official league championships.
