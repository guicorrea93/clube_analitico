Solomon Islands - List of Foundation Dates

1946        Melanesian Team (Honiara)            [first local Honiara soccer team]
1969        Real Kakamora FC (Honiara)           [merger of KAMP FC and Flying Fox FC]
1981        Solomon Warriors FC (Honiara)        [founded as Uncles FC]
1987        Laugu United FC (Honiara)
1992?       Kolei United FC (Honiara)
1997        Waneagu United FC (Honiara)
1998        Koloale FC (Honiara)
1999        Makuru FC (Honiara)
200?        Hana FC (Honiara)
            Marist FC (Honiara)
2004        KOSSA FC (Honiara)                   [founded as Fairwest FC]
2009        Sokamora FC (Honiara)                [since 2011 reserve side of Real Kakamora FC]
2010        Henderson Eels FC (Honiara)
            Western United FC (Honiara)
2017        FC Guadalcanal (Honiara)
2020        Southern United FC (Honiara)
