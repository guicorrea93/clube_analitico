The '''1977–78 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 16th season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] after its establishment in 1962. A total of 14 teams contested the league, with [[JS Kabylie|JS Kawkabi]] as the defending champions. The Championnat started on September 30, 1977, and ended on May 5, 1978.
