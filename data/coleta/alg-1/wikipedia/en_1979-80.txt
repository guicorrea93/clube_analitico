The '''1979–80 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 18th season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 14 teams contested the league, with [[MC Alger|MP Alger]] as the defending champions, The Championnat started on [[october 5]], [[1979 in association football|1979]]. and ended on [[June 13]], [[1980 in association football|1980]].
