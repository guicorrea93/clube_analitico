The '''1981–82 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 20th season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league, with [[RC Kouba|RS Kouba]] as the defending champions, The Championnat started on September 4, 1981. and ended on May 14, 1982.
