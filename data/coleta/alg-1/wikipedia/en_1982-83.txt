The '''1982–83 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 21st season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league, with [[JS Kabylie|JE Tizi-Ouzou]] as the defending champions, The Championnat started on September 17, 1982. and ended on June 17, 1983.
