The '''1970–71 Algerian Championnat National''' was the ninth season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league, with [[CR Belouizdad|CR Belcourt]] as the defending champions, The Championnat started on [[September 20]], [[1970 in association football|1970]]. and ended on [[April 25]], [[1971 in association football|1971]].
