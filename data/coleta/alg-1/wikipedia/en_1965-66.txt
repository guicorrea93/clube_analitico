The '''1965–66 Algerian Championnat National''' was the fourth season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league,<ref name="Algeria1965_66">{{cite web|title=Algeria 1965/66|url=https://www.rsssf.org/tablesa/alg66.html|publisher=[[Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation]] (RSSSF)|accessdate=18 October 2017}}</ref> with [[CR Belouizdad|CR Belcourt]] as the defending champions.
