The '''1987–88 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 26th season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 18 teams contested the league, with [[ES Sétif|EP Sétif]] as the defending champions, The Championnat started on [[September 11]], [[1987 in association football|1987]] and ended on [[July 5]], [[1988 in association football|1988]].
