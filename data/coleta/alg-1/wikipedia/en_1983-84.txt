The '''1983–84 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 22nd season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league with [[JS Kabylie|JE Tizi-Ouzou]] as the defending champions. The Championnat started on September 2, 1983 and ended on June 8, 1984.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Menard |first=Michel |date=2022-07-09 |title=Algeria 1983/84 |url=https://www.rsssf.org/tablesa/alg84.html#premiere |access-date=2026-08-16 |website=[[RSSSF]]}}</ref>
