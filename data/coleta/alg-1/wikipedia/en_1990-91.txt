The '''1990–91 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 29th season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league, with [[JS Kabylie]] as the defending champions, The Championnat started on [[August 30]], [[1990 in association football|1990]]. and ended on [[october 1]], [[1991 in association football|1991]].

=== Promotion and relegation ===

* [[ES Guelma]]

'''Teams relegated to Algerian Division 2 1991-1992'''
* [[CS Constantine]]
* [[RC Kouba]]
