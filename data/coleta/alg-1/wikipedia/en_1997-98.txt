The '''1997–98 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 36th season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league, with [[CS Constantine]] as the defending champions, The Championnat started on January 1, [[1997 in association football|1997]]. and ended on June 29, [[1998 in association football|1998]].
