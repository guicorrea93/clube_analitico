The '''1964–65 Algerian Championnat National''' was the third season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league, with [[Hamra Annaba|USM Annaba]] as the defending champions.

==Team summaries==

=== Promotion and relegation ===
'''Teams promoted from Algerian Division 2 1964-1965''' 
* [[SCM Oran]]
* [[RC Kouba]]
* [[JSM Skikda]]

'''Teams relegated to Algerian Division 2 1965-1966'''

* [[MC Alger]]
* [[JSM Tiaret]]
* [[USM Alger]]
