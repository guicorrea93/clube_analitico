The '''2021–22 Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1''' was the 60th season of the [[Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 18 teams contested the league. It began on 8 October 2021 and concluded on 17 June 2022.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.competition.dz/actualites/89-ligue-1/78840-la-saison-2021-2022-debutera-le-23-octobre.html|title=La saison 2021-2022 débutera le 23 octobre}}</ref>

==Teams==
18 teams contest the league. HB Chelghoum Laïd and RC Arbâa were promoted from the 2020–21 Ligue 2.
