The '''1968–69 Algerian Championnat National''' was the seventh season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league, with [[ES Sétif]] as the defending champions, The Championnat started on [[September 15]], [[1968 in association football|1968]]. and ended on [[June 22]], [[1969 in association football|1969]].

==Team summaries==
=== Promotion and relegation ===
'''Teams promoted from Algerian Division 2 1968-1969''' 
* [[USM Alger]]
* [[JS Kabylie]]

'''Teams relegated to Algerian Division 2 1969-1970'''
* [[ASM Oran]]
* [[MC Saïda]]
