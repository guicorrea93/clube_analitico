The '''1985–86 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 24th season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 20 teams contested the league, with [[JS Kabylie|JE Tizi-Ouzou]] as the defending champions.

==Team summaries==
=== Promotion and relegation ===
'''Teams promoted from Algerian Division 2 1985-1986''' 
* [[MC Alger|MP Alger]]
* [[CS Constantine|CM Constantine]]
* [[MC Saïda]]

'''Teams relegated to Algerian Division 2 1986-1987'''
* [[NA Hussein Dey|MA Hussein Dey]]
* [[USM Bel-Abbès|ESM Bel-Abbès]]
* [[DNC Alger|JH Djazaïr]]
