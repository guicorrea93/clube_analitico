The '''1995–96 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 34th season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league, with [[JS Kabylie]] as the defending champions, The Championnat started on September 14, [[1995 in association football|1995]]. and ended in August [[1996 in association football|1996]].
