The '''1984–85 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 23rd season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 20 teams contested the league, with [[GC Mascara|GCR Mascara]] as the defending champions, The Championnat started on September 31, [[1984 in association football|1984]] and ended on May 26, [[1985 in association football|1985]].
