The '''1974–75 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 13th season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league, with [[JS Kabylie|JS Kawkabi]] as the defending champions.

==Team summaries==
=== Promotion and relegation ===
'''Teams promoted from Algerian Division 2 1974-1975''' 
* [[USM El Harrach|USM Maison-Carrée]]
* [[CA Batna]]
* [[ASM Oran]]

'''Teams relegated to Algerian Division 2 1975-1976'''
* [[MC Saïda]]
* [[USM Sétif]]
* [[USM Blida]]
