The '''1996–97 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 35th season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams competed, with [[USM Alger]] as the defending champions, The season began on [[October 10]], [[1996 in association football|1996]]. and ended on [[June 26]], [[1997 in association football|1997]].
