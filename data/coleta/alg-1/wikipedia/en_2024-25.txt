The '''2024–25 Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1''' is the 63rd season of the [[Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contest the league. On June 27, 2024 The federal office approved the calendar for the 2024–25 Ligue 1 season with the aim of ending on May 31, 2025. The first round is scheduled for September 14, this delay is motivated both by an extended end of the 2023–24 season but also by the holding of early [[2024 Algerian presidential election|presidential elections]] which will take place on September 7, 2024.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.horizons.dz/?p=120146|title=Ligue 1 Mobilis : Coup d'envoi de la saison 2024-2025 le 12 septembre prochain|language=fr|work=Horizons|publisher=M. F.|date=13 May 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.dzfoot.com/football-en-algerie/ligue-1-le-calendrier-2024-2025-presente-261563.html |title=Ligue 1 : Le calendrier 2024/2025 présenté |website=DZfoot |date=28 June 2024 |access-date=29 June 2024}}</ref> However, the [[Ligue de Football Professionnel (Algeria)|Ligue de Football Professionnel]] decided to postpone the start of the Ligue 1 by a week, on September 21.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.dzfoot.com/football-en-algerie/ligue-1-le-debut-de-championnat-encore-decale-263172.html |title=Ligue 1 : Le début de championnat encore décalé... |website=DZfoot |date=12 August 2024 |access-date=14 August 2024}}</ref> However, the first match was actually played on September 19.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.dzfoot.com/football-en-algerie/ligue-1-la-saison-2024-2025-lancee-264845.html |title=Ligue 1 : La saison 2024/2025 lancée |website=DZfoot |date=19 September 2024 }}</ref>

The [[Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1]] will see the introduction of [[Video assistant referee]] this season. VAR will be used from the first round of the Ligue 1. The VAR will not be able to be used for all matches because several stadiums do not meet standards, making it impossible to install the necessary equipment. The [[Algerian Football Federation|FAF]] received the first fully equipped stations, in accordance with an agreement duly signed with the Portuguese company Medialuso, following a call for tenders for a rental. The rest of the equipment should be received. In addition to the equipment, the Portuguese company has also sent five technicians who will be responsible for the operation and maintenance of the devices.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.dzfoot.com/football-en-algerie/ligue-1-la-var-utilisee-des-ce-week-end-264739.html |title=Ligue 1 : La VAR utilisée dès ce week-end |website=DZfoot |date=17 September 2024 |access-date=17 September 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/sports/ligue-1-mobilis-technologie-de-la-var-coup-d-envoi-ce-jeudi-a-setif-223759 |title=Ligue 1 Mobilis  Technologie de la VAR : Coup d'envoi ce jeudi à Sétif |website=[[El Moudjahid]] |date=16 September 2024 |access-date=17 September 2024}} {{Dead link|date=October 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}</ref>

==Teams==
A total of sixteen teams are participating in the 2024–25 edition of the Ligue Professionnelle 1. [[ES Mostaganem]] return to the top flight after 25 year's absence<ref>{{cite news|url=https://inter-lignes.com/25-ans-apres-les-mostaganem-revient-en-ligue-1-de-football/|title=25 ans après : l'ES Mostaganem revient en ligue 1 de football|language=fr|work=inter-lignes.com|publisher=Melissa Nait Ali|date=29 May 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250209215602/https://inter-lignes.com/25-ans-apres-les-mostaganem-revient-en-ligue-1-de-football/|archive-date=9 February 2025|access-date=15 June 2024|url-status=dead}}</ref> and [[Olympique Akbou]] is promoted for the first time.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.tsa-algerie.com/football-algerien-lexploit-spectaculaire-et-inedit-de-lolympique-akbou/|title=Football algérien : l'exploit spectaculaire et inédit de l'Olympique Akbou|language=fr|work=tsa-algerie.com|publisher=Karim Kebir|date=14 May 2024}}</ref> Both teams after finishing first on their groups in the [[2023–24 Algerian Ligue 2]]. [[ES Ben Aknoun]] and [[US Souf]] were relegated to 2024–25 Ligue 2 after one year in the top flight for both.

===Foreign players===
Following urgent requests from certain clubs, the [[Algerian Football Federation]] has decided to increase the number of foreign players in the [[Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1|Ligue 1]].<ref name="ligue-1-augmentation-du-nombre-detranger-par-club">{{cite web |url=https://www.dzfoot.com/football-en-algerie/ligue-1-augmentation-du-nombre-detranger-par-club-262467.html |title=Ligue 1 : Augmentation du nombre d'étranger par club |website=DZfoot |date=25 July 2024 |access-date=27 July 2024}}</ref> After having formally passed through the technical college chaired by [[Rabah Saâdane]], the [[Algerian Football Federation|FAF]] ratified the increase in the number of foreigners per club from 3 to 5. However, that there is a provision intended to serve as a safeguard for the license of a foreign player or coach to be validated, the club must pay the federation a deposit equivalent to 12 months salary.<ref name="ligue-1-augmentation-du-nombre-detranger-par-club" /> This is to protect against any financial dispute.<ref name="ligue-1-augmentation-du-nombre-detranger-par-club" />
