The '''2011–12 Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1''' was the 50th season of the [[Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league, with [[ASO Chlef]] as the defending champions. The league started on 6 September 2011, and ended on 22 May 2012.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.dzfoot.com/news-9489/ligue-1-le-calendrier-complet-de-la-saison-prochaine/ |title=Ligue 1 : Le calendrier complet de la saison prochaine |access-date=13 July 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110716222116/http://www.dzfoot.com/news-9489/ligue-1-le-calendrier-complet-de-la-saison-prochaine/ |archive-date=16 July 2011 |url-status=dead |df=dmy-all }}</ref>

While the league was originally scheduled to start on 10 September 2011, the official start date will be 6 September 2011, with a match between [[JS Kabylie]] and [[MC Alger]]. The tie was moved up due to the two club's participation in African competition.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.lnf.dz/article.php?titre=1601&lang=0 |title=Le programme des matches du mois de septembre |access-date=27 August 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120320213046/http://www.lnf.dz/article.php?titre=1601&lang=0 |archive-date=20 March 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
