The '''1988–89 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 27th season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league, with [[MC Oran|Mouloudia d'Oran]] as the defending champions, The Championnat started on September 22, 1988. and ended on June 1, 1989.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Algeria 1988/89 |url=https://www.rsssf.org/tablesa/alg89.html |access-date=2026-02-05 |website=www.rsssf.org}}</ref>
