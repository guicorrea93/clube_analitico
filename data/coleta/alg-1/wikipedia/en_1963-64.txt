The '''1963–64 Algerian Championnat National''' was the second season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 49 teams contested the league, with [[USM Alger]] as the defending champions. [[Hamra Annaba|USM Annaba]] was the winner beating in the final [[NA Hussein Dey]].
