The '''2020–21 Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1''' was the 59th season of the [[Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 20 teams contested the league. The Ligue Professionnelle 1 kicked off on 28 November 2020.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.dzfoot.com/2020/08/26/ligue-1-les-dates-de-reprise-en-algerie-187045.php |title=Ligue 1 : Les dates de reprise en Algérie |website=dzfoot.com |date=26 August 2020 |access-date=28 August 2020 |archive-date=25 January 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250125115032/https://www.dzfoot.com/2020/08/26/ligue-1-les-dates-de-reprise-en-algerie-187045.php |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Teams==
20 teams contested the league. RC Relizane, Olympique de Médéa, JSM Skikda and WA Tlemcen were promoted from the 2019–20 Ligue 2.
