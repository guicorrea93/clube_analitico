The '''2019–20 Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1''' was the 58th season of the [[Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.dzfoot.com/2019/05/24/ligue-1-le-championnat-debutera-le-15-aout-152170.php |title=Ligue 1 : Le championnat débutera le 15 août |website=dzfoot.com |date=24 May 2019 |access-date=7 June 2019}}</ref> The Ligue Professionnelle 1 kicked off on August 15, 2019, this time without an official sponsorship for the first time since 2008–09, [[Mobilis (Algeria)|Mobilis ATM]] did nothing to renew the contract.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.dzfoot.com/2019/08/15/ligue-1-mobilis-nest-plus-le-sponsor-officiel-160948.php |title=Ligue 1 : Mobilis n'est plus le sponsor officiel |website=dzfoot.com |date=15 August 2019 |access-date=15 August 2019}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.dzfoot.com/2019/08/15/ligue-1-debut-du-championnat-cet-apres-midi-160939.php |title=Ligue 1 : Début du championnat cet après-midi |website=dzfoot.com |date=15 August 2019 |access-date=15 August 2019}}</ref> On September 17, at the Extraordinary General Assembly of [[Algerian Football Federation]], it unanimously endorses the change of the competition system by increasing the number of clubs from 16 to 18, as for the second division to 32 clubs from two groups Central East and Central West from 16 clubs also became the number of professional clubs 18 instead of 32 starting from the season 2020–21.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.dzfoot.com/2019/09/17/officiel-une-ligue-1-a-18-clubs-a-partir-de-la-saison-prochaine-163892.php |title=Officiel : Une Ligue 1 à 18 clubs à partir de la saison prochaine |website=dzfoot.com |date=17 September 2019 |access-date=17 September 2019}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.dzfoot.com/2019/09/17/faf-comprendre-le-changement-de-systeme-de-competition-163924.php |title=FAF : Comprendre le changement de système de compétition |website=dzfoot.com |date=17 September 2019 |access-date=17 September 2019}}</ref> On September 30, at the monthly statutory meeting held in Ouargla. After debate and exchanges between the members, the Federal Office opted for the variant favoring the relegation of two clubs of the Ligue Professionnelle 1 and the promotion of four clubs of the League 2 to the upper tier.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.dzfoot.com/2019/09/30/faf-deux-clubs-qui-descendent-et-quatre-qui-montent-165107.php |title=FAF : Deux clubs qui descendent et quatre qui montent |website=dzfoot.com |date=30 September 2019 |access-date=1 October 2019}}</ref>

On March 15, 2020, the [[Ligue de Football Professionnel (Algeria)|Ligue de Football Professionnel]] (LFP) decided to halt the season due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic in Algeria]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://al-ain.com/article/frensh-scenario-algerian |title=بسبب كورونا.. الدوري الجزائري مهدد بالسيناريو الفرنسي |website=al-ain.com |language=ar |date=12 July 2020 }}</ref> On July 29, 2020, the LFP declared that season is over and [[CR Belouizdad]] to be the champion, the promotion of four teams from the League 2, and scraping the relegation for the current season.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.goal.com/en/news/coronavirus-algerian-ligue-1-season-cancelled-without/iouqsd4ianvw1k86qf9nk9chc |title=Coronavirus: Algerian Ligue 1 season cancelled without relegation |website=Goal.com |date=29 July 2020 }}</ref>

==Teams==
16 teams contest the league. US Biskra, NC Magra and ASO Chlef were promoted from the 2018–19 Ligue 2.
