The '''2000–01 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 39th season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league, with [[CR Belouizdad]] as the defending champions, The Championnat started on [[September 7]], [[2000 in association football|2000]]. and ended on [[June 25]], [[2001 in association football|2001]].
