The '''2018–19 Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1''' was the 57th season of the [[Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested in the league.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://footalgerien.com/ligue-1-2-coup-denvoi-de-saison-2018-2019-10-11-aout/ |title=Ligue 1 et 2 : Coup d’envoi de la saison 2018-2019 les 10 et 11 août |website=footalgerien.com |access-date=1 June 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=2018–19 Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1 |website=[[Soccerway]] |access-date=17 July 2021 |url=https://www.soccerway.com/algeria/ligue-1-2018-2019/results/}}</ref>

==Teams==
16 teams contest the league. MO Bejaia, Ain M'lila and CABB Arreridj were promoted from the 2017-18 Ligue 2.

== Presidential elections Protests 2019 == 
Immediately after the announcement of several parties in the social networking sites on the events of Friday, February 22 against the fifth term of the current President [[Abdelaziz Bouteflika]], [[Ligue de Football Professionnel (Algeria)|LFP]] announced the suspension of a match between [[MC Alger]] vs [[MC Oran]] for round 22 and [[JSM Bejaia]] vs [[Paradou AC]] in the [[2018–19 Algerian Cup|Algerian Cup]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.dzfoot.com/2019/02/23/ligue-1-j22-mc-alger-mc-oran-reporte-aussi-145732.php |title=Ligue 1 (J22) : MC Alger - MC Oran reporté aussi |website=dzfoot.com |access-date=26 February 2019}}</ref> On 24 February 2019, The match between the [[CR Belouizdad]] vs [[DRB Tadjenanet]] were suspended, Which was to play on Tuesday, also has been delayed 3 games In addition, the matches [[USM Alger]] vs [[Paradou AC]] of Ligue 1 23rd day, [[US Biskra]] vs [[JSM Skikda]] and [[ASM Oran]] vs [[RC Relizane]] of Ligue 2 24th day, initially fixed for Friday 1 March are staggered to Saturday 2 March.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.dzfoot.com/2019/02/24/lfp-un-troisieme-match-reporte-dautres-decales-145873.php |title=LFP : Un troisième match reporté, d'autres décalés |website=dzfoot.com |access-date=26 February 2019}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.tsa-algerie.com/manifestations-contre-le-5e-mandat-le-calendrier-footballistique-fortement-perturbe/ |title=Manifestations contre le 5e mandat : le calendrier footballistique fortement perturbé |website=tsa-algerie.com |access-date=26 February 2019}}</ref> A day later, another match was suspended in the Ligue 1 between [[USM Bel Abbès]] and [[JS Saoura]], in Ligue 2 Also postponed another match [[ASO Chlef]] vs [[JSM Bejaia]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.dzfoot.com/2019/02/25/lfp-troisieme-vague-de-reports-de-matchs-145899.php |title=LFP : Troisième vague de reports de matchs |website=dzfoot.com |access-date=26 February 2019}}</ref> To be the sixth match postponed since the beginning of the demonstrations.
