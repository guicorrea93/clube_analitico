The '''1966–67 Algerian Championnat National''' was the fifth season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league, with [[CR Belouizdad|CR Belcourt]] as the defending champions.

==Team summaries==

=== Promotion and relegation ===
'''Teams promoted from Algerian Division 2 1966-1967''' 
* [[USM Bel-Abbès]]
* [[JSM Skikda]]

'''Teams relegated to Algerian Division 2 1967-1968'''
* [[USM Blida]]
* [[SCM Oran]]
