The '''1989–90 Algerian Championnat National''' was the 28th season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league with [[JS Kabylie]] as the defending champions. The Championnat started on August 31, 1989. and ended on June 14, 1990.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Menard |first=Michel |date=2020-09-17 |title=Algeria 1989/90 |url=https://www.rsssf.org/tablesa/alg90.html |access-date=2026-08-18 |website=[[RSSSF]]}}</ref>
