The '''2023–24 Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1''' is the 62nd season of the [[Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contest the league. It began on 15 September 2023 and concluded on 14 June 2024.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.dzfoot.com/football-en-algerie/lfp-la-date-de-reprise-du-championnat-annonce-245070.html |title=LFP : La date de reprise du championnat annoncé |website=dzfoot.com |date=17 July 2023 |access-date=17 July 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.lescore.dz/2023/07/ligue-1-le-coup-denvoi-du-championnat-fixe-pour-le-15-septembre/ |title=Ligue 1: le coup d’envoi du championnat fixé pour le 15 septembre |website=lescore.dz |date=17 July 2023 |access-date=17 July 2023}}</ref> The league was suspended on 18 October for three weeks in solidarity with the Palestinian people in Gaza during the [[Gaza war]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.sabqpress.dz/sports/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%81-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%AC%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A/|title=الفاف: تعليق جميع المنافسات الكروية في الجزائر تضامنا مع فلسطين|first=وليد قبلي &#124; سبق|last=برس|date=October 18, 2023}}</ref>

The highest league attendance was 80,000 during a game between [[MC Alger]] and [[USM Alger]].<ref>https://africasoccer.com/algerian-derby-breaks-attendance-records-with-remarkable-fan-turnout/</ref>

==Teams==
16 teams contest the league. [[US Souf]] and [[ES Ben Aknoun]] were promoted from the [[2022–23 Algerian Ligue 2]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://lnfa.dz/2023/05/16/championnat-de-ligue-2-lus-souf-assure-son-accession-en-ligue-2/ |title=Championnat de Ligue 2 : L’US Souf assure son accession en Ligue 1 |website=lnfa.dz |date=16 May 2023 |accessdate=16 May 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230531001008/https://lnfa.dz/2023/05/16/championnat-de-ligue-2-lus-souf-assure-son-accession-en-ligue-2/ |archive-date=31 May 2023 |url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://lnfa.dz/2023/06/02/championnat-de-ligue2-les-ben-aknoun-en-ligue-1-et-la-mc-saida-3eme-relegable/ |title=Championnat de Ligue2 : L’ES Ben Aknoun en Ligue 1 et le MC Saida 3ème relégué |website=lnfa.dz |date=2 June 2023 |accessdate=2 June 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230602223733/https://lnfa.dz/2023/06/02/championnat-de-ligue2-les-ben-aknoun-en-ligue-1-et-la-mc-saida-3eme-relegable/ |archive-date=2 June 2023 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
