The '''2025–26 Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1''' was the 64th season of the [[Algerian Ligue Professionnelle 1]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contest the league. It began on 21 August 2025 and concluded on 6 June 2026.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.dzfoot.com/football-en-algerie/ligue-1-la-lfp-detaille-un-calendrier-25-26-complet-281470.html|title=Ligue 1 : La LFP détaille un calendrier 25/26 complet !|language=fr|work=Dzfoot|publisher=Hocine S.|date=10 July 2025}}</ref>

==Teams==
16 teams contest the league. [[ES Ben Aknoun]] and [[MB Rouissat]] were promoted from the [[2024–25 Algerian Ligue 2]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.aps.dz/sport/187195-ligue-2-l-es-ben-aknoun-retrouve-l-elite-apres-l-avoir-quittee-il-y-a-une-annee-seulement#:~:text=ALGER%20%2D%20L'Etoile%20Sportive%20de,surtout%20de%20tr%C3%A8s%20fortes%20pressions.|title=Ligue 2: l'ES Ben Aknoun retrouve l'élite, après l'avoir quittée il y a une année|language=fr|work=aps.dz|publisher=Redaction team|date=13 May 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/64826|title=Accession historique du MB Rouissat en Ligue 1 Mobilis|language=fr|work=Radio Algérie|publisher=Radio Algérie Multimédia|date=17 May 2025}}</ref>
