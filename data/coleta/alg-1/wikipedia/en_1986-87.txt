The '''1986–87 Algerian {{Lang|fr|Championnat National|italic=no}}''' was the 25th season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 20 teams contested the league, with [[JS Kabylie|JE Tizi-Ouzou]] as the defending champions, The Championnat started on 29 August, 1986 and ended on 5 June, 1987.
