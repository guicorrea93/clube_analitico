The '''1994–95 Algerian {{Lang|fr|Championnat National|italic=no}}''' was the 33rd season of the [[Algerian Championnat National]] since its establishment in 1962. A total of 16 teams contested the league, with [[US Chaouia]] as the defending champions, The Championnat started on November 11, 1994. and ended on June 22, 1995.
