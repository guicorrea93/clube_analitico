NB: at the end of the season no relegation took place as the first level
    was reorganized in 2 groups of 14 teams each for 1998/99; all 16 teams
    remained at the top level and the top-4 of all three second division
    groups earned promotion.  The background behind this decision is that
    for the 1999/2000 season the Algerian FA will start a new, professional
    division, and they wanted a wide range of clubs to have the chance to
    become members of it.
