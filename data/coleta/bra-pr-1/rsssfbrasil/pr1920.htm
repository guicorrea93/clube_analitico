Savóia-agua Verde, were the fusing of two clubs of one same quarter

division, came back to participate of the main championship of the ASP, in 1920, 
with its proper team
(3) Esperança EC, ones of the forces of the varzeano soccer, decided to venture 
itself enters the teamses strongest of the state
(4)Britânia SC received, in definitive, to the end of the season the "Taça 
Paraná" instituted in 1918, for the club that reached the heading in three time 
consecutive or alternated
