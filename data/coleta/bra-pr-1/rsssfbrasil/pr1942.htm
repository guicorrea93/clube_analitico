 (1)  Palestra Itália and Savóia is obliged to change of name being 
obeyed a determination of the CBD. The Palestra Itália passes if to call 
Paranaense and the Savóia passes if to call Esporte Clube Brasil. The measure 
aimed at to disentail the name of these clubs of Italy, during the Second World 
War I
