Londrina                            União Bandeirante (Bandeirantes)

Full Details

Note: Apucarana withdrew from the tournament.  Initially the local FA appointed Grêmio Maringá as
the replacement due to political reasons.  Prudentópolis, 3rd place in the 1999 2nd level, decided
to go to sports justice to obtain the berth, and the latter did award the berth to Prudentópolis.
