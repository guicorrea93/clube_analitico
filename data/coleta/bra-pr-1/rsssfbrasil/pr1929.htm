Coritiba           4-4 Atlético 

Note: Near the end of the match, Coritiba scored the 5th goal, but it was
disallowed by the referee. Coritiba players then walked away. The FA later
anulled the match. Atlético entered with an appeal on the Brazilian FA, and
the result was maintained.
Obs: Quase ao final do jogo, o Coritiba fez o 5º gol, mas o árbitro
anula. O Coritiba se retirou de campo em protesto. A Federação depois anulou
o jogo. O Atlético entrou com um recurso na CBD e conseguiu a manutenção do
resultado.
