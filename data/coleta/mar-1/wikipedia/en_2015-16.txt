The '''2015–16 Botola''', also known as '''Botola Maroc Telecom''' for sponsorship reasons, is the 59th season of the Premier League, the top Moroccan professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1915. The fixtures were announced on 22 July 2015. The season started on 5 September 2015 and is scheduled to conclude on 15 April 2016.

[[Wydad Casablanca|Wydad]] came into the season as defending champions of the [[2014–15 Botola|2014–15 season]]. [[IR Tanger]] and [[MC Oujda]] entered as the two promoted teams from the [[2014–15 GNF 2]].

On June 5, 2016, FUS Rabat won the football championship after a 4–2 victory against Mouloudia D'Oujda. The FUS Rabat club won the championship for the first time after being runner-up to the title for 5 previous times.<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://www.africatopsports.com/2016/06/05/300990/|title=LE FUS DE RABAT CHAMPION POUR LA PREMIÈRE FOIS}}</ref>

}}A total of 16 teams compete in the league, including 14 sides from the 2014–15 season and two promoted from the [[2014–15 GNF 2]]. On 12 April 2015, [[IR Tanger|Tanger]] became the first Championship side to be promoted following their 0–0 draw over [[Youssoufia Berrechid]] in week 27. On 4 May 2015 (in week 30), MC Oujda got promoted to the top flight after defeating US Temara 1–0 in their home match.

The two promoted clubs replaced Chabab Atlas Khenifra and Ittihad Khemisset.
