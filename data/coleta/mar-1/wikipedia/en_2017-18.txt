The '''2017–18 Botola''', also known as '''Botola Maroc Telecom''' for sponsorship reasons, is the 61st season of the Premier League and the 7th under its new format of Moroccan Pro League, the top Moroccan professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1956. The season started on 8 September 2017 and ended on 21 May 2018.

[[Wydad Athletic Club (Football)|Wydad Athletic Club]] came into the season as defending champions of the [[2016–17 Botola|2016–17 season]]. [[Rapide Oued Zem]] and [[Racing de Casablanca]] entered as the two promoted teams from the [[2016–17 GNF 2]].

On May 12, 2018, [[IR Tanger]] won the Botola Pro after a 2-1 victory against Moghreb Tétouan. The [[IR Tanger]] club won the championship for the first time after being runner-up to the title in 1989-90 season.<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://www.frmf.ma/fr/article/la-frmf-félicite-lirt-champion-de-botola-maroc-télécom-d1-2017-2018|title=La FRMF félicite l'IRT champion de Botola Maroc Télécom D1 (2017-2018)}}</ref>
