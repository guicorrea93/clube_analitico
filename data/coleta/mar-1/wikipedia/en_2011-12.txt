The '''2011–12''' [[Botola Pro]] is the 55th season of the [[Morocco|Moroccan]] Top League, but the 1st under its new format of Moroccan Pro League. It began on Friday 19 August 2011 with [[Maghreb Fez|MAS Fes]] beating [[Hassania Agadir|HUS Agadir]] 2–0. [[Raja CA]] are the holders of the title. [[MA Tétouan]] won the '''2011–12''' [[Botola Pro]], after defeating the runner-up [[FUS Rabat]] in the last game of the season at [[Stade Moulay Abdellah]], [[Rabat]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/18249904 |title=BBC Sport – Moghreb Tetouan win Moroccan title for the first time |publisher=BBC Sport |date=2012-05-29 |access-date=2015-09-18}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.fifa.com/worldfootball/clubfootball/news/newsid=1640907.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120601173847/http://www.fifa.com/worldfootball/clubfootball/news/newsid=1640907.html |url-status=dead |archive-date=1 June 2012 |title=Tetouan top champions list |publisher=FIFA |date=2015-07-15 |access-date=2015-09-18}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.marocfootball.info/botola/9934-le-moghreb-de-tetouan-champion-du-maroc-2012.html|title=Le Moghreb de Tétouan champion du Maroc 2012|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120531002140/http://www.marocfootball.info/botola/9934-le-moghreb-de-tetouan-champion-du-maroc-2012.html|archive-date=31 May 2012|access-date=29 May 2012}}</ref> Both teams were pushing for the title until the last game. It was the first time in history of the [[Botola Pro]] that a team from the north of the country wins it.
