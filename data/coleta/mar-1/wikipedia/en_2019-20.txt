The '''2019–20 Botola''', is the 63rd season of the Premier League and the 9th under its new format of Moroccan Pro League, [[Botola|the top Moroccan professional league]] for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1956.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.frmf.ma/fr/content/histoire-du-football-au-maroc|title=Histoire du Football au Maroc {{!}} الموقع الرسمي للجامعة الملكية المغربية لكرة القدم|website=www.frmf.ma|access-date=2019-08-22}}</ref>

[[Wydad Casablanca]] came into the season as defending champions of the [[2018–19 Botola|2018–19 season]]. [[Nahdat Zemamra]] and [[Raja Beni Mellal]] entered as the two promoted teams from the [[2018–19 Botola 2]].

The season started on 14 September 2019, and was scheduled to end on 1 July 2020.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://botola.snrt.ma/resultats|title=Botola - Société Nationale de Radiodiffusion et de Télévision|website=Botola - Société Nationale de Radiodiffusion et de Télévision|language=en|access-date=2019-08-22}}</ref> However, the season was suspended in March 2020, due to [[COVID-19 pandemic in Morocco]], then resumed in July and ended in 11 October 2020.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.cafonline.com/news-center/news/morocco-s-botola-resumed-after-four-months-of-stoppage |title=Morocco's "Botola" resumes after 4-month COVID-19 break |website=cafonline.com |date=28 July 2020 }}</ref>
