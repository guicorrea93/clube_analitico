The '''2025–26 Botola Pro''', also known as '''Botola Pro Inwi''' for sponsorship reasons, was the 69th season of the [[Botola Pro]], the highest tier [[Association football|football]] league of [[Morocco]]. Competition started on the 25th of September 2025.

[[RS Berkane]] were the defending champions, having won their maiden title in the previous season.<ref>{{cite news |last1=El Aalaoui |first1=Abdellah |title=RS Berkane are celebrating their league title |url=https://www.hespress.com/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%87%D8%B6%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%AA%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D8%B7%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A9-1598115.html|language=ar |work=Hesspress |date=24 July 2025}}</ref>

This season was marked by organizational issues regarding the match calendar, causing the end of the regular season to only happen on the 5th of July 2026, even with the [[2026 FIFA World Cup]] happening at the same time.

[[Maghreb Fez]] were crowned champions on the 5th of July 2026, clinching their 5th league title by defeating [[Olympic Dcheira]] 2-0. This triumph ends a 41-year title drought, with their previous title dating back to the 1984-85 season.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Kabbour |first1=Abderrahim |title=MAS Fez end 41 Year wait for Botola Pro Title|url=https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/07/327434/mas-fes-end-41-year-wait-to-win-botola-pro-title/|work=Morocco World News |date=5 June 2026}}</ref>

==Teams==
A total of 16 teams contest the league, including 13 sides from the [[2024–25 Botola Pro|2024–25 season]] and 3 promoted teams from [[2024–25 Botola Pro 2]].
