The '''2012–13''' [[Botola Pro]] was the 56th season of the top-flight [[Morocco|Moroccan]] professional football league, but only the 2nd under the new format of Moroccan Pro League. It began on 14 September 2012 with [[Kénitra AC]] beating [[COD Meknès]] 2–1 in their away match and ended on 2 June 2013 when Wydad Fes beating Raja Beni Mellal 4-1 in their away match. [[Raja CA]] finished the season as champions, qualifying for the [[2013 FIFA Club World Cup]] as host representatives.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://int.soccerway.com/national/morocco/gnf-1/2011-2012/regular-season/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160401130445/http://int.soccerway.com/national/morocco/gnf-1/2011-2012/regular-season/r15767/|url-status=dead|archive-date=April 1, 2016|title = Summary - Botola Pro - Morocco - Results, fixtures, tables and news - Soccerway}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://int.soccerway.com/national/morocco/gnf-1/20122013/regular-season/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130207123940/http://int.soccerway.com/national/morocco/gnf-1/20122013/regular-season/|url-status=dead|archive-date=February 7, 2013|title = Summary - Botola Pro - Morocco - Results, fixtures, tables and news - Soccerway}}</ref> Defending champions [[MA Tétouan]] finished in 6th place, failing to qualify for regional CAF club competitions for the following season.
