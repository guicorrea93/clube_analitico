The '''2022–23 Botola Pro''', also known as '''Botola Pro Inwi''' for sponsorship reasons, was the 66th season of the Premier League and the 12th under its current format of Moroccan Pro League, [[Botola|the top Moroccan professional league]] for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1956.

[[Wydad Casablanca]] came into the season as defending champions of the [[2021–22 Botola|2021–22 season]]. [[Moghreb Tétouan]] and [[Union de Touarga]] entered as the two promoted teams from the [[2021–22 Botola 2]].

On June 23, 2023, [[AS FAR (football)|AS FAR]] won the Botola Pro after a 2–3 victory against [[IR Tangier|Ittihad Tanger]] in the last round. The [[AS FAR (football)|AS FAR]] club won its 13th title after fifteen years since the last time in 2007–08.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://site.frmf.ma/%d8%aa%d9%87%d9%86%d8%a6%d8%a9-29/|title=تهنئة |trans-title=A felicitation}}</ref>
