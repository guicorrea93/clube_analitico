The '''2021–22 Botola Pro''', also known as '''Botola Pro Inwi''' for sponsorship reasons, is the 65th season of the Premier League and the 11th under its new format of Moroccan Pro League, [[Botola|the top Moroccan professional league]] for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1956.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.frmf.ma/fr/content/histoire-du-football-au-maroc|title=Histoire du Football au Maroc {{!}} الموقع الرسمي للجامعة الملكية المغربية لكرة القدم|website=www.frmf.ma|access-date=2019-08-22}}</ref>

[[Wydad Casablanca]] came into the season as defending champions of the [[2020–21 Botola|2020–21 season]]. [[Olympique Khouribga]] and [[Jeunesse Sportive Soualem|Jeunesse Soualem]] entered as the two promoted teams from the [[2020–21 Botola 2]].

The season began on September 10th, 2021, and ended on July 5th, 2022.

[[Wydad AC]] are crowned champion of this edition with a record of 22 Botola. [[Wydad AC]] and [[Raja CA]] drew the highest average home attendances in the 2021–22 Botola, with 45,000 and 40,087, respectively.

The UMFP (Union Marocaine des Footballeurs Professionnels), in partnership with the [[Royal Moroccan Football Federation]], organized the Night of Stars Award in its 8th edition, which celebrated the brilliants of the Botola Pro for the 2021/22 season.<ref>{{cite news |date=8 July 2022 |title="La Nuit des Stars" : le Wydad Casablanca (hommes) et l'AS FAR (dames) raflent la mise |trans-title="The Night of the Stars": Wydad Casablanca (men) and AS FAR (women) win the day |url=https://lematin.ma/express/2022/nuit-stars-wydad-casablanca-hommes-las-far-dames-raflent-mise/378264.html |publisher=lematin.ma |access-date=8 August 2022}}]</ref><ref>{{cite news |date=9 July 2022 |title=ÉVÉNEMENT : TOUT SUR " LA NUIT DES STARS " QUI CÉLÈBRE LA FAMILLE SPORTIVE |trans-title=EVENT: ALL ABOUT « THE NIGHT OF STARS » WHICH CELEBRATES THE SPORTS FAMILY |url=https://fr.le7tv.ma/2022/07/09/evenement-la-nuit-des-stars-pour-celebrer-la-famille-sportive-videos/ |publisher=fr.le7tv.ma/ |access-date=8 August 2022}}]</ref>
