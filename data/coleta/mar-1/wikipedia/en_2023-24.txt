The '''2023–24 Botola Pro''', also known as '''Botola Pro Inwi''' for sponsorship reasons, is the 67th season of the Premier League and the 13th under its current format of Moroccan Pro League, [[Botola|the top Moroccan professional league]] for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1956. [[AS FAR (football club)|AS FAR]] came into the season as defending champions of the [[2022–23 Botola|2022–23 season]]. [[RCA Zemamra]] and [[Youssoufia Berrechid]] entered as the two promoted teams from the 2022–23 Botola 2.

On 14 June 2024, Raja CA won the last match 0–3 against [[MC Oujda]] and secured their thirteenth Botola title. They won their last 9 league games, finishing on 72 points without a single defeat, both all-time records.
