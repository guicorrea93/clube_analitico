National Club Championship 2011

RC Manibhai/Southern Forest National Club Championship 

NB: the majority of the clubs, including those from the districts of Ba, Labasa, Nadroga,
    Navua and Tavua, failed to meet requirements and thus their nominations were rejected;
    only seven clubs from the southern districts and four from the western districts (two
    each from Lautoka and Nadi) were accepted; however, the clubs from Lautoka and Nadi
    withdrew because of financial reasons, meaning only seven clubs from the southern
    districts of Lami, Rewa, Suva and Tailevu/Naitasiri entered the tournament, so that
    no zonal playoffs were required.
