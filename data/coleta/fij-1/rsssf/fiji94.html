BATTLE OF THE GIANTS 1994
  
Final [Nadi (neutral ground)] 
Ba      0-0     Rewa 
  
Final Replay (scheduled for Rewa]
Rewa    n/p     Ba    
  
Rewa awarded a walkover
  
NB: The Fiji Football Association announced that the replay would be
    played on Rewa's home ground. Enraged Ba officials immediately
    announced their team would boycott it.
    They were true to their heated words and Rewa had to go through the
    motions of lining up on the pitch in front of 1500 fans and wait for
    the referee to award them the game through Ba's non-appearance. It was
    a most unsatisfactory way for Rewa to win their first major honour
    since 1955.
