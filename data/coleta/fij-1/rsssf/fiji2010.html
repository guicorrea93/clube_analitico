NB: Nadi, who had originally qualified for the semifinals as runners-up of
    pool 1 behind Labasa, requested arbitration on the award of the Navua 
    vs Nadroga match on Oct 11 but the Fiji FA refused to hear this before
    the semifinals, in spite of the fact those had already been postponed
    by a week due to bad weather on Oct 7 and 8.  The Navua vs Nadroga match
    had been awarded to Navua for a supposedly ineligible player of Nadroga,
    Koshmick Kumar, who had played for a club in California (Sacramento
    Combined Brothers Soccer Club) and Nadroga did not have an international
    transfer certificate; however, this US club is a non-profit organisation 
    not affiliated to any regional or national football organisation (in
    particular not to the USSF, let alone to FIFA), so it is more than
    questionable whether there was any need for such a certificate.  Note
    that Fiji is the country which managed to lose its own independence
    declaration.
