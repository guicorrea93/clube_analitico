Burns Philp Homecentre National Club Championship 1992

NB: seventh edition; at the first edition in 1986 26 teams entered, and in 1987 28; after
    that, participation was restricted to one club per district in 1988, with 12 teams
    divided over 4 pools; in 1989, 15 teams entered in the tournament at the National
    Stadium in Suva and in 1990 it was played at Govind Park, Ba.  In 1991, district
    runners-up again could enter, and 20 teams participated.  The 1992 edition saw the
    introduction of a preliminary, zonal phase, in which 26 teams entered of which 8
    qualified for the final tournament.
