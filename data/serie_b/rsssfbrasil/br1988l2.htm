NOTE: At first, Ponte Preta  was declared promoted.  However, as usual, the
tiebreaking criteria of the tournament was unclear.  Náutico contested Ponte
Preta's promotion, alleging that the first tiebreaking criterion was the number of wins in the whole tournament and not the number of wins in the final phase. CBF decided to award the promotion to Náutico.
