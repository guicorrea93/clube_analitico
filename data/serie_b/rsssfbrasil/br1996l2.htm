Note: 
1.Before the start, América-SP, Ferroviária, Novorizontino and Bangu withdrew citing financial trouble; 
Barra do Garças were excluded for having debts with the federation.  Their replacements were Ponte Preta, 
Atlético-GO, Gama, ABC and Joinville.
2.América-MG were re-admitted in the league after a 2 years ban after the club fielded a protes in a common
justice court against their relegation in the 1993 championship.
