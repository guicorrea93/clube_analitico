The '''1984 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B''', officially, the '''Taça CBF''', was the 7th edition of the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]]. The championship was disputed by 32 clubs in a knockout tournament form. the champion would be promoted to the Third phase of the [[1984 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Copa Brasil]] of that year, and the champions, along with the runner up, would have berths in the [[1985 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|First level championship of the next year.]] [[Uberlândia Esporte Clube|Uberlândia]] won the title, beating [[Clube do Remo|Remo]] in the finals.
