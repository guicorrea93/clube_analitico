In 2011, the '''[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]]''', the second level of the Brazilian League, will be contested by 20 clubs from May 20 until November 26, 2011.<ref name="CBFs">{{cite news |title=Calendário 2011 |trans-title=2011 Schedule |url=http://www.cbf.com.br/cal/calendario2011.pdf |publisher=[[Brazilian Football Confederation|CBF]] |access-date=October 23, 2010 |language=pt |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101011091944/http://www.cbf.com.br/cal/calendario2011.pdf |archive-date=October 11, 2010 |url-status=dead |df=mdy-all }}</ref> Top four teams in the table will qualify to the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]] to be contested in [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A 2012|2012]], meanwhile the bottom four will be relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C 2012|Série C next season]].

==Format==
For the sixth consecutive season, the tournament will be played in a double round-robin system. The team with the most points will be declared champions. Top four clubs will ascend to [[2012 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]], meanwhile the bottom four will be relegated to [[2012 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]].
