The '''2021 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the second division. The competition began on 28 May and ended on 28 November.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/cbf-divulga-tabela-da-serie-b-2021 |title=CBF divulga tabela da Série B 2021 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese |date=25 March 2021}}</ref>

Twenty teams competed in the tournament, twelve returning from the [[2020 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|2020]] season, four promoted from the [[2020 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]] ([[Brusque Futebol Clube|Brusque]], [[Londrina Esporte Clube|Londrina]], [[Clube do Remo|Remo]] and [[Vila Nova Futebol Clube|Vila Nova]]),<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/pe/futebol/brasileirao-serie-c/noticia/serie-c-vila-nova-se-junta-a-brusque-remo-e-londrina-entre-os-classificados-para-a-2adivisao.ghtml |title=Série C: Vila Nova se junta a Brusque, Remo e Londrina entre os classificados para a 2ª divisão |date=17 January 2021 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref> and four relegated from the [[2020 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]] ([[Botafogo de Futebol e Regatas|Botafogo]], [[Coritiba Foot Ball Club|Coritiba]], [[Goiás Esporte Clube|Goiás]] and [[CR Vasco da Gama|Vasco da Gama]]).<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.goal.com/br/not%C3%ADcias/quem-sao-os-rebaixados-do-brasileirao-2020/16kj1pq9zcka1woq0t19zm7ur |title=Quem são os rebaixados do Brasileirão 2020? |publisher=Goal (Brasil) |date=26 February 2021 |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

Brusque were deducted three points after they were punished by Superior Tribunal de Justiça Desportiva (STJD) for racial abuse aimed toward Londrina's player [[Celsinho (footballer, born August 1988)|Celsinho]] during the match Brusque v Londrina, played on 28 August (21st round). Brusque also were sanctioned with a fine of [[Brazilian real|R$]]60,000.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://ge.globo.com/sc/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/noticia/caso-de-raciscmo-celsinho-brusque-e-multado-e-perde-3-pontos-na-serie-b.ghtml |title=Caso Celsinho: Brusque é multado e perde três pontos na Série B por injúria racial ao meia do Londrina |publisher=Globo |date=24 September 2021 |language=Portuguese}}</ref> On 18 November 2021, STJD overturned its decision and the three points were returned to Brusque.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://ge.globo.com/sc/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/noticia/stjd-julga-recurso-do-brusque-por-caso-de-racismo-e-determina-devolucao-de-tres-pontos-na-serie-b.ghtml |title=STJD aceita recurso do Brusque em caso de racismo e determina devolução de três pontos na Série B |publisher=Globo |date=18 November 2021 |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

The top four teams were promoted to the [[2022 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]]. [[Botafogo de Futebol e Regatas|Botafogo]] and [[Coritiba Foot Ball Club|Coritiba]] became the first two clubs to be promoted on 15 November 2021 after a 2–1 win against Operário Ferroviário and a 1–0 Brusque win against CRB, respectively.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/rj/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/jogo/15-11-2021/botafogo-operario-pr.ghtml |title=O Bota já subiu: Botafogo vira sobre o Operário e garante acesso à Série A |date=15 November 2021 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pr/futebol/times/coritiba/noticia/vice-lider-coritiba-se-beneficia-de-tropeco-do-crb-e-garante-acesso-a-serie-a.ghtml |title=Coritiba se beneficia de tropeço do CRB e garante acesso antecipado à Série A |date=15 November 2021 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref> [[Goiás Esporte Clube|Goiás]] were promoted on 22 November 2021<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/sp/campinas-e-regiao/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/jogo/22-11-2021/guarani-goias.ghtml |title=Goiás vence em Campinas, garante acesso à Série A e complica sonho do Guarani |date=22 November 2021 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref> and [[Avaí FC|Avaí]] on 28 November 2021.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/sc/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/jogo/28-11-2021/avai-sampaio-correia.ghtml |title=Festa azurra! Avaí vira contra o Sampaio Corrêa e conquista o acesso à elite de 2022 |date=28 November 2021 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref>
