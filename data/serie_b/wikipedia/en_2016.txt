The '''[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Serie B of the Brazilian Championship 2016]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the second division. It was contested by 20 clubs. The top four teams will were promoted to [[2017 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A in 2017]] and the bottom four were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]] in 2017.The games had a break during the [[2016 Olympics]], which was held between July and August in Brazil. The competition had nineteen rounds played before the stoppage.

[[Atlético Clube Goianiense|Atlético Goianiense]] were the champions.

==Teams==

20 teams participated in the 2016 edition: the 12 teams from 5th to 16th of the previous (2015) edition of Série B, 4 relegated from 2015 Série A and 4 promoted from 2015 Série C.
Teams in red left the competition this season (either due to a promotion or a relegation). Teams in green joined the competition this season (either due to a promotion or a relegation).
