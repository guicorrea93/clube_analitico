The '''1996 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B''', the second level of Brazilian National League, was played from August 8 to December 8, 1996. The competition had 25 clubs and two of them were promoted to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]] and three were to be relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]]. The competition was won by [[União São João Esporte Clube|União São João]].

[[União São João Esporte Clube|União São João]] finished the final phase group with the most points, and was declared 1996 [[Brazilian Série B]] champions, claiming the promotion to the [[1997 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|1997 Série A]] along with [[América Futebol Clube (RN)|América-RN]], the runners-up. The three worst ranked teams ([[Goiatuba Esporte Clube|Goiatuba]], [[Club Sportivo Sergipe|Sergipe]] and [[Central Sport Club|Central]]) were originally relegated to play [[1997 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]] in 1997. However, with the cancellation of the relegations of Fluminense and Bragantino in the [[1996 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]], the three teams were kept in the Série B for 1997.
