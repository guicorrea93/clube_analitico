The '''Campeonato Brasileiro Série B 2005''', the second level of the Brazilian National League, was played from April 23 to November 26, 2005. The competition had 22 clubs, of which two were promoted to the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]] and six were relegated to the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]].

In the first round, every team played against each other, much like what happens in the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]]. Differently from the Série A, each team played against the other only once. Thus, each team played 21 games, 11 home and 10 away (or the opposite), for a total of 231 games. The eight best ranked teams advanced to the second round, where they were divided into two groups of four. Teams in each group played against each other home and away. The two best ranked teams in each group advanced to the final round. Those four teams were put into a single group, and played against each other home and away.

The [[Grêmio Foot-Ball Porto Alegrense|Grêmio]] finished with the most points, advancing to the Série A along with [[Santa Cruz Futebol Clube|Santa Cruz]]. The six last ranked teams in the first round ([[Associação Atlética Anapolina|Anapolina]], [[Esporte Clube Bahia|Bahia]], [[Sociedade Esportiva e Recreativa Caxias do Sul|Caxias]], [[Criciúma Esporte Clube|Criciúma]], [[União Agrícola Barbarense Futebol Clube|União Barbarense]] and [[Esporte Clube Vitória|Vitória]]) were relegated to play the [[2006 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]] in 2006.

==Trivia==
* 2005 was a particularly dreadful year for the clubs from the state of [[Bahia]]. Both the [[Esporte Clube Vitória|Vitória]] (runners-up in 1993) and the [[Esporte Clube Bahia|Bahia]] (champions in 1988), accustomed to first-division football, found themselves in the unlikely position of being jointly relegated from the second to the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]].
* [[Grêmio Foot-Ball Porto Alegrense|Grêmio]] may have won the title, but it was not without drama. In the last match against [[Clube Náutico Capibaribe|Náutico]], the club conceded two penalties. The first, in first half, Náutico's [[Bruno Carvalho (footballer, born 1974)|Bruno Carvalho]] hit the post. The second, in the 80th minute, cost [[Grêmio Foot-Ball Porto Alegrense|Grêmio]] three players for arguing with the referee (the [[Grêmio Foot-Ball Porto Alegrense|Grêmio]] already had a man down for a second yellow card). The shot was saved by [[Rodrigo José Galatto|Galatto]]. Forward [[Anderson Luís de Abreu Oliveira|Anderson]] ended up scoring the win.  The match was named The [[Batalha dos Aflitos (2005)|"A Batalha dos Aflitos"]] (''The Battle of the Aflitos''), referring to the game as a [[battle]] in Náutico's stadium, [[Estádio dos Aflitos]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=GloboEsporte.com > Futebol 2007 > Brasileirão - NOTÍCIAS - Batalha dos Aflitos completa dois anos |url=https://ge.globo.com/ESP/Noticia/Futebol/Campeonatos/0,,MUL192265-4276,00.html |access-date=2024-05-22 |website=ge.globo.com}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Dramático, Grêmio vence e conquista a Série B - 26/11/2005 - UOL Esporte - Futebol |url=https://www.uol.com.br/esporte/futebol/ultimas/2005/11/26/ult59u98053.jhtm |access-date=2024-05-22 |website=www.uol.com.br}}</ref>
