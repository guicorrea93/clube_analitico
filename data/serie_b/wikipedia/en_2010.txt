In 2010, the '''[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]]''', the second level of the Brazilian League, was contested by 20 clubs from May 7 to November 27, 2010. The top four teams in the table, former [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Brazilian Série A]] champions [[Coritiba Foot Ball Club|Coritiba]] and [[Esporte Clube Bahia|Bahia]] alongside [[Figueirense Futebol Clube|Figueirense]] and [[América Futebol Clube (MG)|América (MG)]], were promoted to the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]] to be contested in [[2011 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|2011]], meanwhile the bottom four were relegated to [[2011 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C next season]]. Three former Brazilian champion played in this edition: [[Esporte Clube Bahia|Bahia]] and [[2009 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|2009]] relegated [[Coritiba Foot Ball Club|Coritiba]] and [[Sport Club do Recife|Sport Recife]]. Playing for the first time were [[Guaratinguetá Futebol|Guaratinguetá]], which was promoted along with [[Agremiação Sportiva Arapiraquense|ASA de Arapiraca]], [[Associação Desportiva Recreativa Cultural Icasa|Icasa]] and [[América Futebol Clube (MG)|América Mineiro]] from the [[2009 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]]. After spending a single season in [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]], [[Esporte Clube Santo André|Santo André]] returned to Série B along with [[Clube Náutico Capibaribe|Náutico]].

==Format==
For the fifth consecutive season, the tournament was played in a double round-robin system. The team with most points has been declared champions. The top four clubs ascended to [[2011 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]], meanwhile the bottom four were relegated to [[2011 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]].
