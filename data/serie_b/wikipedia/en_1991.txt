The football (soccer) '''Campeonato Brasileiro Série B 1991''', the second level of Brazilian National League, was played from February to May 1991. The competition had 64 clubs and two of them were promoted to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]]. The competition was won by [[Paysandu Sport Club|Paysandu]].
