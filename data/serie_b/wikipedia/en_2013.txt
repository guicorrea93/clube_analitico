The '''[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Serie B of the Brazilian Championship 2013]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the second division. It is contested by 20 clubs, between May 24 and November 30. The top four teams will have access to [[2014 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A in 2014]] and the last four will be relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]] in 2014.

The games had a break during the [[2013 FIFA Confederations Cup]], which was held between June and July in Brazil. The competition was played for six rounds before the stoppage.
