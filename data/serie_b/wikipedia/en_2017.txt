The '''[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Serie B of the Brazilian Championship 2017]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the second division. It was contested by 20 clubs, between 12 May and 25 November.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://globoesporte.globo.com/futebol/noticia/2016/07/cbf-lanca-calendario-2017-com-pausas-em-jogos-da-selecao-nas-eliminatorias.html|title=CBF lança calendário 2017 com pausas em jogos da Seleção nas eliminatórias|publisher=GloboEsporte.com|date=2016-07-06|access-date=2016-10-20}}</ref> The top four teams were promoted to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]] in 2018 and the bottom four were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]] in 2018.

[[América Futebol Clube (MG)|América Mineiro]] were crowned champions.
