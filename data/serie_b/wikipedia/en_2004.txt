The football (soccer) '''Campeonato Brasileiro Série B 2004''', the second level of Brazilian National League, was played from April 23 to December 11, 2004. The competition had 24 clubs and two of them were promoted to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]] and six were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]].

In the first round, each team played against each other, much like what happens in [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]]. However, in contrast to Série A, each team played against the other only once. Therefore, each team played 23 games, 12 home and 11 away (or the opposite). The eight best ranked teams advanced to the second round, where they were divided in two groups of four. Teams in each group played against each other home and away. The two best ranked teams in each group advanced to the final round. Those four teams were put in a single group, and played against each other home and away.

[[Brasiliense FC|Brasiliense]] finished the final phase group with most points and was declared 2004 Brazilian Série B champions, claiming the promotion to the [[2005 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|2005 Série A]] along with [[Fortaleza Esporte Clube|Fortaleza]], the runners-up. The six worst ranked teams in the first round ([[América Futebol Clube (RN)|América-RN]], [[Clube do Remo|Remo]], [[América Futebol Clube (MG)|América Mineiro]], [[Joinville Esporte Clube|Joinville]], [[Mogi Mirim Esporte Clube|Mogi Mirim]] and [[Londrina Esporte Clube|Londrina]]) were relegated to play [[2005 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]] in 2005.

==Trivia==
* [[Esporte Clube Santo André|Santo André]] was punished by losing 12 points for fielding 2 ineligible players (Osmar and Dirceu) in games 1 and 2 of the first round. That eliminated Santo André from a second round spot and allowed [[Santa Cruz Futebol Clube|Santa Cruz]] to move ahead and clinch the final spot for the second round.
* [[Fortaleza Esporte Clube|Fortaleza]] had to beat [[Avaí Futebol Clube|Avaí]] by two goals in the last game of the season in order to be promoted to the Brazilian elite. So on December 11, 2004, they were able to beat Avaí 2–0 in front of their home crowd and clinch the spot.
