The '''[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]]''', namely the second level of [[Brazilian football league system]], was contested by 20 teams in 2008. Giants [[Sport Club Corinthians Paulista|Corinthians]] played Série B for the first time after its poor season in Série A in 2007.<ref>{{Cite web |last=lance |title=Ponto mais baixo, mas que impulsionou reconstrução: rebaixamento do Corinthians completa 14 anos |url=https://www.terra.com.br/esportes/corinthians/ponto-mais-baixo-mas-que-impulsionou-reconstrucao-rebaixamento-do-corinthians-completa-14-anos,cac713499e8b355a8ad77dd5ff152d4b2iz5oe0q.html |access-date=2025-08-03 |website=Terra |language=pt-BR}}</ref> Also, former Série A champions [[Esporte Clube Bahia|Bahia]] returned from Série C.

The tournament started on May 9 and [[Sport Club Corinthians Paulista|Corinthians]] begun defeating [[Clube de Regatas Brasil|CRB]] 3-2 in [[São Paulo]]. Due to its tradition and huge number of supporters, [[Sport Club Corinthians Paulista|Corinthians]] attracted most of the attention from the media. The club won the first six matches and never left the top of the table. Promotion came on round 32 - six matches before the end of season - after defeating [[Ceará Sporting Club|Ceará]] 2-0 in [[São Paulo]]. They were crowned champions two rounds later, beating [[Criciúma Esporte Clube|Criciúma]] 2-0 as visitors.<ref>{{Cite web |last=admin |date=2020-05-25 |title=Você sabia? Até hoje, Corinthians tem melhor campanha da história da Série B |url=https://www.gazetaesportiva.com/times/corinthians/voce-sabia-ate-hoje-corinthians-tem-melhor-campanha-da-historia-da-serie-b/ |access-date=2025-08-03 |website=Gazeta Esportiva |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

[[Avaí Futebol Clube|Avaí]] was the second team to reach Série A as they defeated [[Brasiliense Futebol Clube|Brasiliense]] 1-0 on Round 35. One week later, [[Esporte Clube Santo André|Santo André]] also reached promotion after their 3-2 win as visitors against [[Ceará Sporting Club|Ceará]]. Finally, [[Grêmio Recreativo Barueri|Barueri]] completed the G4 (as the group of  promoted teams are called) on Round 37 after beating [[América Futebol Clube (RN)|América de Natal]] 3-0 at home.

On the other side of the table, [[Clube de Regatas Brasil|CRB]]'s poor record caused the club relegation on Round 33. [[Sociedade Esportiva do Gama|Gama]] saw their hope comes to an end three weeks later. In the last round, on November 29, [[Criciúma Esporte Clube|Criciúma]] and [[Marília Atlético Clube|Marília]] could not escape as [[Fortaleza Esporte Clube|Fortaleza]] and [[América Futebol Clube (RN)|América de Natal]] won their last matches and managed to stay out of the bottom four.

[[Vila Nova Futebol Clube|Vila Nova]] and former [[Brazilian national football team|Brazilian international]] [[Túlio Costa|Túlio]] was the top scorer of the competition with 24 goals in the age of 39 years.
