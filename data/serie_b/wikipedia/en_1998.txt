The football (soccer) '''Campeonato Brasileiro Série B 1998''', the second level of Brazilian National League, was played from August 2 to December 20, 1998. The competition had 24 clubs and two of them were promoted to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]] and six were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]]. The competition was won by [[Sociedade Esportiva do Gama|Gama]].

[[Sociedade Esportiva do Gama|Gama]] finished the final phase group with the most points, and was declared 1999 [[Brazilian Série B]] champions, claiming the promotion to the [[1999 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|1999 Série A]] along with [[Botafogo Futebol Clube (SP)|Botafogo-SP]], the runners-up. The six worst ranked teams in the first round ([[Fluminense Football Club|Fluminense]], [[Atlético Clube Goianiense|Atlético-GO]], [[Clube Náutico Capibaribe|Náutico]], [[Clube Atlético Juventus|Juventus]], [[Volta Redonda Futebol Clube|Volta Redonda]] and [[Americano Futebol Clube|Americano]]) were relegated to play [[1999 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]] in 1999.
