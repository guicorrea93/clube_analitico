The football (soccer) '''Campeonato Brasileiro Série B 1999''', the second level of Brazilian National League, was played from August 1 to December 12, 1999. The competition had 22 clubs and two of them were promoted to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]] and six were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]]. The competition was won by [[Goiás Esporte Clube|Goiás]].

[[Goiás Esporte Clube|Goiás]] finished the final phase group with the most points, and was declared 1999 [[Brazilian Série B]] champions, claiming the promotion to the 2000 Série A along with [[Santa Cruz Futebol Clube|Santa Cruz]], the runners-up. The six worst ranked teams in the first round ([[União São João Esporte Clube|União São João]], [[Criciúma Esporte Clube|Criciúma]], [[Paysandu Sport Club|Paysandu]], [[América Futebol Clube (RN)|América-RN]], [[Tuna Luso Brasileira|Tuna Luso]] and [[Associação Desportiva Ferroviária Vale do Rio Doce|Desportiva]]) would be relegated to play Série C in 2000. However, with the debacle that resulted in the creation of the [[Copa João Havelange]] in 2000, Bahia (third-placed) and América-MG (sixth-placed) were promoted to [[Copa João Havelange Group Blue|Série A]], and from the originally-relegated teams, only Tuna Luso actually played the [[Copa João Havelange Group Green and White|Green and White Group]], the equivalent to the Série C, with the other five relegated teams playing the [[Copa João Havelange Group Yellow|Group Yellow]]. For 2001, all of the participating teams in 1999's Série B, with the exception of Goiás, São Caetano, América-MG, Bahia and Santa Cruz were included in that year's Série B.
