The '''[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] 2007''', namely the [[Brazilian football league system|second division]] of the Brazilian League, was contested by 20 teams. The tournament started in May, and reached its end on November 24. The 20 teams played [[home and away]] matches among each other and, by the end of the year, the four best-ranked ([[Coritiba Foot Ball Club|Coritiba]], [[Ipatinga Futebol Clube|Ipatinga]], [[Associação Portuguesa de Desportos|Portuguesa]] and [[Esporte Clube Vitória|Vitória]]) were promoted to the first division and the four worst-ranked ([[Paulista Futebol Clube|Paulista]], [[Santa Cruz Futebol Clube|Santa Cruz]], [[Clube do Remo|Remo]], [[Ituano Futebol Clube|Ituano]]) were relegated to the third division.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Coritiba joga como campeão, vence o Ipatinga e se aproxima da Série A |url=https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/esportes/coritiba-joga-como-campeao-vence-o-ipatinga-e-se-aproxima-da-serie-a-anweess5d170234hfiwajpf0u/ |access-date=2025-08-03 |website=www.gazetadopovo.com.br |language=pt-BR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2006-11-29 |title=Definidos os times que disputam a Série B em 2007 |url=https://oglobo.globo.com/esportes/definidos-os-times-que-disputam-serie-em-2007-4543588 |access-date=2025-08-03 |website=O Globo |language=pt-BR}}</ref>
