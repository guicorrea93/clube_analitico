The '''[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Serie B of the Brazilian Championship]] 2014''' is a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the second division. It is contested by 20 clubs, between April 18 and November 28. The top four teams will be promoted to [[2015 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A in 2015]] and the last four will be relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]] in 2015.

The games had a break during the [[2014 FIFA World Cup]], which was held between June and July in Brazil.
