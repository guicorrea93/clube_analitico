The football (soccer) '''Campeonato Brasileiro Série B 1989''', the second level of Brazilian National League, was played from 9 September to 20 December 1989. The competition had 96 clubs and two of them were promoted to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]]. The competition was won by [[Clube Atlético Bragantino|Bragantino]].<ref name="rsssfbrasil">{{cite web|url=http://www.rsssfbrasil.com/tablesae/rj1989l2.htm|title=RSSSF – Brazilian Série B 1989|accessdate=8 August 2019}}</ref><ref name="futeboldegoyaz">{{cite web|url=http://futeboldegoyaz.com.br/campeonatos/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/1989|title=Futebol de Goyaz – Brazilian Série B 1989|accessdate=8 August 2019}}</ref>
