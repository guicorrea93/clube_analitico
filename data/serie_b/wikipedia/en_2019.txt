The '''2019 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]]''' was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the second division. The competition started on 26 April and ended on 30 November 2019.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/201902/20190226113559_403.pdf |title=CAMPEONATO BRASILEIRO SÉRIE B - TABELA BÁSICA / EDIÇÃO 2019 |date=20 February 2019 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

Twenty teams competed in the tournament, twelve returning from the [[2018 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|2018]] season, four promoted from the [[2018 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]] ([[Botafogo Futebol Clube (SP)|Botafogo-SP]], [[Clube Atlético Bragantino|Bragantino]], [[Cuiabá Esporte Clube|Cuiabá]] and [[Operário Ferroviário Esporte Clube|Operário Ferroviário]]),<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-c/os-quatro-times-que-subiram-para-a-serie-b-de-2019 |title=Série B de 2019: os quatro times que subiram por meio da Série C |date=28 August 2018 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref> and four relegated from the [[2018 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]] ([[América Futebol Clube (MG)|América Mineiro]], [[Paraná Clube|Paraná]], [[Sport Club do Recife|Sport]] and [[Esporte Clube Vitória|Vitória]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/noticia/veja-como-sera-a-serie-b-do-campeonato-brasileiro-2019.ghtml |title=Veja como será a Série B do Campeonato Brasileiro 2019 |date=2 December 2018 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

[[Red Bull GmbH]] signed a deal with the promoted team [[Clube Atlético Bragantino|Bragantino]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://sponsorship.sportbusiness.com/news/red-bull-expands-global-football-empire-takes-over-at-bragantino/ |title=Red Bull expands global football empire, takes over at Bragantino |date=28 March 2019 |publisher=SportBusiness SPONSORSHIP}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/sp/vale-do-paraiba-regiao/futebol/noticia/bragantino-anuncia-acordo-com-o-rb-brasil-para-gestao-do-time-na-serie-b.ghtml |title=Bragantino anuncia acordo com o RB Brasil para gestão do time na Série B |date=26 March 2019 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref> Team was merged with [[Red Bull Brasil]] and renamed as [[Red Bull Bragantino|RB Bragantino]], although in the 2019 Série B their name was simply Bragantino with Red Bull as main sponsor.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.espn.com.br/futebol/artigo/_/id/5535855/red-bull-bragantino-entenda-como-o-novo-time-paulista-ira-funcionar |title=Red Bull Bragantino: Entenda como o novo time paulista irá funcionar |date=23 April 2019 |publisher=ESPN Brasil |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

In the 17th round (20 August), [[Figueirense FC|Figueirense]] players refused to play their away match against [[Cuiabá Esporte Clube|Cuiabá]] in protest over unpaid wages. Cuiabá was awarded a 3–0 win by [[Forfeit (sport)|forfeit]].<ref name="CUIFIG">{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/sc/futebol/times/figueirense/noticia/jogadores-do-figueirense-nao-entram-em-campo-e-cuiaba-vence-por-wo-na-serie-b-do-brasileiro.ghtml |title=Jogadores do Figueirense não entram em campo, e Cuiabá vence por W.O. na Série B do Brasileiro |date=20 August 2019 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref> On 4 October, Figueirense was sanctioned by the Superior Tribunal de Justiça Desportiva (STJD) with loss of three points and a fine of [[Brazilian real|R$]]3,000.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.stjd.org.br/noticias/figueirense-punido-por-w-o |title=Figueirense punido por w.o |date=October 4, 2019 |publisher=STJD |language=Portuguese}}</ref> Few days later the STJD corrected its decision and gave back the three points to Figueirense.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/sc/futebol/times/figueirense/noticia/decisao-do-stjd-desfaz-equivoco-sobre-julgamento-do-wo-e-indica-devolucao-pontos-ao-figueirense.ghtml |title=Decisão do STJD desfaz equívoco sobre julgamento do W.O. e indica devolução de pontos ao Figueirense |date=8 October 2019 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

The top four teams were promoted to the [[2020 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]]. [[Clube Atlético Bragantino#Red Bull agreement|Bragantino]] became the first club to be promoted after a 3–1 win against Guarani on 5 November 2019.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/em-noite-de-serie-b-bragantino-carimba-acesso-a-elite-do-brasileirao |title=Em noite de Série B, Bragantino carimba acesso à elite do Brasileirão |date=5 November 2019 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref> [[Sport Club do Recife|Sport]] was promoted on 20 November,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/sport-vence-a-ponte-preta-e-confirma-acesso-a-serie-a-do-brasileirao |title=Sport vence a Ponte Preta e confirma acesso à Série A do Brasileirão |date=20 November 2019 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref> and [[Coritiba Foot Ball Club|Coritiba]] and [[Atlético Clube Goianiense|Atlético Goianiense]] on 30 November.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/coritiba-e-atletico-go-garantem-ultimas-vagas-de-acesso-na-serie-b |title=Coritiba e Atlético-GO garantem últimas vagas de acesso na Série B |date=30 November 2019 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>
