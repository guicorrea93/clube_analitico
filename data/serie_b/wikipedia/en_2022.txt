The '''2022 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]]''' (officially the '''Brasileirão [[Sportingbet|SportingBet]] - Série B 2022''' for sponsorship reasons)<ref>{{cite web|title=CBF e SportingBet fecham acordo por naming rights do Brasileirão SportingBet - Série B |url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/cbf-e-sportingbet-acertam-naming-rights-da-serie-b-sportingbet |date=8 April 2022 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref> was a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the second division. The competition began on 8 April and ended on 6 November.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/index/cbf-apresenta-calendario-do-futebol-brasileiro-em-2022 |title=CBF apresenta calendário do futebol brasileiro em 2022 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese |date=4 November 2021}}</ref>

Twenty teams competed in the tournament, twelve returning from the [[2021 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|2021]] season, four promoted from the [[2021 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]] ([[Criciúma Esporte Clube|Criciúma]], [[Ituano FC|Ituano]], [[Grêmio Novorizontino|Novorizontino]] and [[Tombense Futebol Clube|Tombense]]),<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/am/futebol/brasileirao-serie-c/noticia/serie-c-novorizontino-se-junta-a-tombense-ituano-e-criciuma-entre-os-classificados-para-a-serie-b.ghtml |title=Série C: Novorizontino se junta a Tombense, Ituano e Criciúma entre os classificados para a Série B |date=7 November 2021 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref> and four relegated from the [[2021 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]] ([[Esporte Clube Bahia|Bahia]], [[Associação Chapecoense de Futebol|Chapecoense]], [[Grêmio Foot-Ball Porto Alegrense|Grêmio]] and [[Sport Club do Recife|Sport]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.uol.com.br/esporte/futebol/ultimas-noticias/2021/12/09/quem-sao-os-clubes-rebaixados-para-a-serie-b-do-brasileirao.htm |title=Grêmio cai para a Série B; quem são os rebaixados do Brasileirão |date=9 December 2021 |publisher=UOL |language=Portuguese}}</ref> 

The top four teams were promoted to the [[2023 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]]. [[Cruzeiro Esporte Clube|Cruzeiro]] became the first club to be promoted on 21 September 2022 after a 3–0 win against [[CR Vasco da Gama|Vasco da Gama]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/mg/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/jogo/21-09-2022/cruzeiro-vasco.ghtml |title=O Cabuloso voltou! Cruzeiro domina, vence o Vasco e garante acesso para a série A |date=21 September 2022 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref> [[Grêmio Foot-Ball Porto Alegrense|Grêmio]] were promoted on 23 October 2022,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/gremio-derrota-o-nautico-e-confirma-retorno-a-serie-a-do-brasileirao |title=Grêmio derrota o Náutico e confirma retorno à Série A do Brasileirão |date=23 October 2022 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref> and [[Esporte Clube Bahia|Bahia]] and [[CR Vasco da Gama|Vasco da Gama]] were promoted on 6 November 2022.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/bahia-e-vasco-da-gama-garantem-vaga-no-brasileirao-assai-2023 |title=Bahia e Vasco da Gama garantem vaga no Brasileirão Assaí 2023 |date=6 November 2022 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

[[Brusque Futebol Clube|Brusque]], [[Centro Sportivo Alagoano|CSA]], [[Clube Náutico Capibaribe|Náutico]] and [[Operário Ferroviário Esporte Clube|Operário Ferroviário]] were relegated to the [[2023 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.gazetaweb.com/noticias/esportes/alagoano/com-rebaixamento-decretado-csa-conhece-seus-adversarios-na-serie-c-de-2023/ |title=Com rebaixamento decretado, CSA conhece seus adversários na Série C de 2023 |date=7 November 2022 |publisher=Gazetaweb |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

The match [[Sport Club do Recife|Sport]] v [[CR Vasco da Gama|Vasco da Gama]], played on 16 October 2022 (1–1, 35th round), ended in the 49th minute of the second half, 8 minutes before the end of injury time. Before the suspension of the match, [[Raniel]] (Vasco da Gama) scored a penalty goal and during his celebration, close to the Sport fans, objects were thrown onto the field of play towards the players. After this, an invasion of many fans began and the match was suspended. The referee Raphael Claus waited 50 minutes before decide to end of the match.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pe/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/noticia/2022/10/17/sumula-de-sport-x-vasco-e-divulgada.ghtml |title=Súmula de Sport x Vasco explica encerramento: "Ambiente totalmente impossibilitado" |date=17 October 2022 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

On 9 November 2022, Sport were sanctioned by the Superior Tribunal de Justiça Desportiva (STJD) with a fine of [[Brazilian real|R$]]180,000, eight host matches behind closed doors and the loss of one point, while Vasco da Gama received two additional points.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://conteudo.cbf.com.br/cdn/estjd/resultado/202211/20221110134903_71.pdf |title=4ª Comissão Disciplinar DO S.T.J.D. ATA DE RESULTADO - 099/2022 |date=9 November 2022 |publisher=STJD |language=Portuguese}}</ref> The STJD partially overruled their decision, on 31 January 2023, and the sanction was reduced to R$150,000 and six host matches behind closed doors.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.stjd.org.br/noticias/pleno-reforma-penas-de-sport-e-vasco-por-infracoes-na-serie-b-de-2022 |title=Sport x Vasco: Pleno reforma parcialmente penas de infrações na Série B |date=31 January 2023 |publisher=STJD |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

==Teams==
Twenty teams competed in the league – twelve teams from the previous season, as well as four teams promoted from the [[2021 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]], and four teams relegated from the [[2021 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]].

[[Criciúma Esporte Clube|Criciúma]], [[Ituano FC|Ituano]], [[Grêmio Novorizontino|Novorizontino]] and [[Tombense Futebol Clube|Tombense]] achieved promotion on 7 November 2021, after knocking out Botafogo-PB and Paysandu from the Group A, and Manaus and Ypiranga from the Group B.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://placar.abril.com.br/placar/criciuma-ituano-novorizontino-e-tombense-garantem-acesso-na-serie-c/|title=Criciúma, Ituano, Novorizontino e Tombense garantem acesso na Série C|trans-title=Criciúma, Ituano, Novorizontino and Tombense assure promotion in the Série C|publisher=[[Placar]]|language=pt-BR|date=7 November 2021|accessdate=8 August 2022}}</ref>

[[Associação Chapecoense de Futebol|Chapecoense]] became the first team relegated on 10 November 2021, after Santos defeated Bragantino.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/sc/futebol/times/chapecoense/noticia/com-vitoria-do-santos-chapecoense-e-a-primeira-rebaixada-para-a-serie-b-2022.ghtml|title=Com vitória do Santos, Chapecoense é a primeira rebaixada para a Série B 2022|trans-title=With the victory of Santos, Chapecoense is the first team relegated to the 2022 Série B|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=10 November 2021|accessdate=8 August 2022}}</ref> [[Sport Club do Recife|Sport]] suffered relegation on 30 November, after Juventude also defeated Bragantino,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pe/futebol/times/sport/noticia/sport-e-o-segundo-clube-rebaixado-para-a-serie-b-2022.ghtml|title=Sport é o segundo clube rebaixado para a Série B 2022|trans-title=Sport is the second team relegated to the 2022 Série B|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=30 November 2021|accessdate=8 August 2022}}</ref> with [[Grêmio Foot-Ball Porto Alegrense|Grêmio]] and [[Esporte Clube Bahia|Bahia]] being relegated in the last round on 9 December, despite the former's win over champions Atlético Mineiro.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://esportes.r7.com/futebol/gremio-e-bahia-caem-para-a-serie-b-do-campeonato-brasileiro-29062022#/foto/3|title=Grêmio e Bahia caem para a Série B do Campeonato Brasileiro|trans-title=Grêmio and Bahia are relegated to the Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|publisher=[[R7.com]]|language=pt-BR|date=9 December 2021|accessdate=8 August 2022}}</ref>
