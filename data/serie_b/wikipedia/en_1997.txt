The football (soccer) '''Campeonato Brasileiro Série B 1997''', the second level of Brazilian National League, was played from August 9 to December 7, 1997. The competition had 25 clubs and two of them were promoted to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]] and five were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]]. The competition was won by [[América Futebol Clube (MG)|América-MG]].

[[América Futebol Clube (MG)|América-MG]] finished the final phase group with the most points, and was declared 1997 [[Brazilian Série B]] champions, claiming the promotion to the [[1998 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|1998 Série A]] along with [[Associação Atlética Ponte Preta|Ponte Preta]], the runners-up. The five worst ranked teams in each group ([[Mogi Mirim Esporte Clube|Mogi Mirim]], [[Club Sportivo Sergipe|Sergipe]], [[Moto Club de São Luís|Moto Club]], [[Central Sport Club|Central]] and [[Goiatuba Esporte Clube|Goiatuba]]) were relegated to play [[1998 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]] in 1998.
