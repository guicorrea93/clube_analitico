The '''[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Serie B of the Brazilian Championship 2015]]''' is a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the second division. It is contested by 20 clubs, between May 9 and November 28. The top four teams will have access to [[2016 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A in 2016]] and the last four will be relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]] in 2016. recently promoted [[Mogi Mirim Esporte Clube|Mogi Mirim]] was the first team to have its relegation confirmed, after losing to [[Ceará Sporting Club|Ceará]] in October 31.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://ge.globo.com/sp/campinas-e-regiao/noticia/2015/11/magal-lamenta-rebaixamento-e-culpa-falta-de-planejamento-do-mogi-mirim.html|title=Magal lamenta rebaixamento e culpa falta de planejamento do Mogi Mirim|work=[[Grupo Globo|Globo]]|date=1 November 2015|access-date=12 February 2024|language=pt}}</ref> After a defeat to Luverdense in November 6, [[Ituiutaba Esporte Clube|Boa Esporte]] also was relegated. [[ABC Futebol Clube|ABC]] also was relegated, after a tie against Bahia in November 10. [[Botafogo de Futebol e Regatas|Botafogo]] was promoted that same day, after a victory against [[Luverdense Esporte Clube|Luverdense]]. In November 14, [[Esporte Clube Vitória|Vitória]], [[Santa Cruz Futebol Clube|Santa Cruz]] and [[América Futebol Clube (MG)|América-MG]] confirmed their promotions. [[Ceará Sporting Club|Ceará]], that had spent most of the championship in the relegation zone, managed to escape relegation after a victory against Macaé in the last round, with this result, combined with Oeste's draw against Paysandu, relegating the Fluminense team.
