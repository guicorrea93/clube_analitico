The football (soccer) '''Campeonato Brasileiro Série B 1994''', the second level of Brazilian National League, was played from August 7 to December 4, 1994. The competition had 24 clubs and two of them were promoted to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]] and two were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]]. The competition was won by [[Esporte Clube Juventude|Juventude]].

The championship was reestablished after the [[1993 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|1993 Campeonato Brasileiro]], and its participants included the eight teams relegated from the 1993 Série A, along with 16 teams chosen through qualificatory tournaments. However, with [[América Futebol Clube (MG)|América-MG]]'s suspension, [[Esporte Clube Democrata|Democrata]], that had been eliminated in the qualification, was chosen to take América's place. [[Esporte Clube Juventude|Juventude]] beat [[Goiás Esporte Clube|Goiás]] in the final match, and was declared 1994 [[Brazilian Série B]] champions, and both Goiás and Juventude achieved promotion to the [[1995 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|1995 Série A]]. The two worst ranked teams ([[Fortaleza Esporte Clube|Fortaleza]] and [[Grêmio Esportivo Tiradentes|Tiradentes-DF]]) were relegated to play [[1995 Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Série C]] in 1995.

As punishment, the result of its final match was counted as a 1–0 victory for Sampaio Corrêa, and the point earned against in the draw against Sampaio Corrêa in the first match was given to Sampaio Corrêa, tying it and Moto Club in number of points, forcing an extra match, won by Moto Club by 2–1.
