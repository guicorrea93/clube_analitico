The '''2004 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A''' was the 48th edition of the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]]. The competition was won by [[Santos Futebol Clube (São Paulo)|Santos]], coached by [[Vanderlei Luxemburgo]].
Runners-up were [[Clube Atlético Paranaense|Atlético Paranaense]], which led the competition for 11 weeks and lost the title in the penultimate round. The other teams qualified for the [[Copa Libertadores]] were from the state of São Paulo, [[São Paulo Futebol Clube|São Paulo]] and [[Sociedade Esportiva Palmeiras|Palmeiras]].
The highest goal scorer was [[Washington Stecanela Cerqueira|Washington]] (Atlético Paranaense), who scored 34 goals and broke the tournament's record. Beginning two seasons were four teams would be relegated and only two promoted so the tournament would have 20 teams by [[2006 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|2006]], the four teams relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|the second division]] were [[Criciúma Esporte Clube|Criciúma]], [[Guarani Futebol Clube|Guarani]], [[Esporte Clube Vitória|Vitória]] and  [[Grêmio Foot-Ball Porto Alegrense|Grêmio]].

== Format ==
{{commons|2004 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A kits}}
For the second consecutive season, the tournament was played in a [[Round-robin tournament|double round-robin]] system. The team with most points at the end of the season will be declared champion. The bottom four teams will be relegated and will play in the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] in the 2005 season.

=== International qualification ===
The Série A will serve as a qualifier to [[CONMEBOL]]'s [[2005 Copa Libertadores]]. The top-three teams in the standings will qualify to the Second Stage of the competition, while the fourth place in the standings will qualify to the First Stage.
