The '''2019 {{Lang|pt|Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|italic=no}}''' (officially the '''Brasileirão Assaí 2019''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 63rd season of the {{Lang|pt|[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]]|italic=no}}, the top level of professional football in Brazil, and the 17th edition in a double round-robin since its establishment in 2003. The competition started on 27 April and ended on 8 December 2019.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/a-cbf/informes/index/cbf-divulga-calendario-do-futebol-brasileiro-para-2019 |title=CBF divulga calendário do futebol brasileiro para 2019 |date=3 October 2018 |publisher=CBF |language=pt}}</ref>

The top six teams as well as the [[2019 Copa do Brasil]] champions qualified for the [[2020 Copa Libertadores|Copa Libertadores]]. The next six best-placed teams not qualified for Copa Libertadores qualified for the [[2020 Copa Sudamericana|Copa Sudamericana]] and the last four were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]] for 2020. [[Sociedade Esportiva Palmeiras|Palmeiras]] were the defending champions. During the 2019 season, the official match ball was the [[Nike, Inc.|Nike]] Merlin CBF.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.cbf.com.br/a-cbf/informes/index/cbf-e-nike-apresentam-bola-oficial-do-brasileirao-e-copa-do-brasil |title=CBF e Nike apresentam bola oficial do Brasileirão e Copa do Brasil |date=2019-01-11 |publisher=CBF |access-date=2019-10-16 |language=pt}}</ref> [[File:2019 Nike Merlin CBF.jpg|thumb|2019 Nike Merlin CBF]]

[[Clube de Regatas do Flamengo|Flamengo]] secured their sixth league title with four matches to spare on 24 November and just one day after winning the [[2019 Copa Libertadores]], following Palmeiras' 2–1 loss to [[Grêmio Foot-Ball Porto Alegrense|Grêmio]] at home.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/futebol/times/flamengo/noticia/flamengo-e-campeao-brasileiro-e-se-torna-primeiro-time-desde-1963-a-ganhar-nacional-e-libertadores-no-mesmo-ano.ghtml |title=Flamengo é campeão brasileiro e se torna primeiro time desde 1963 a ganhar Nacional e Libertadores no mesmo ano |date=24 November 2019 |access-date=8 December 2019 |publisher=globoesporte.com |language=pt}}</ref>

Twenty teams competed in the league – the top sixteen teams from the previous season, as well as four teams promoted from the [[2018 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].
