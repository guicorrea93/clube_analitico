The '''2024 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A''' (officially the '''Brasileirão Betano 2024''' for sponsorship reasons)<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/negocios-do-esporte/noticia/2024/04/05/casa-de-aposta-chega-a-acordo-com-a-cbf-para-compra-de-naming-rights-do-brasileirao-2024.ghtml |title=Casa de aposta chega a acordo com a CBF para compra de naming rights do Brasileirão 2024 |publisher=Globo |language=pt |date=5 April 2024}}</ref> was the 68th season of the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]], the top level of professional football in Brazil, and the 22nd edition in a double round-robin since its establishment in 2003. The competition began on 13 April and ended on 8 December 2024.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/index/campeonato-brasileiro-2024-tera-pela-primeira-vez-jogo-de-abertura |title=Campeonato Brasileiro 2024 terá pela primeira vez jogo de abertura |publisher=CBF |language=pt |date=31 October 2023}}</ref>

The top six teams as well as the [[2024 Copa do Brasil]] champions qualified for the [[2025 Copa Libertadores|Copa Libertadores]]. Meanwhile, the next six best-placed teams not qualified for Copa Libertadores qualified for the [[2025 Copa Sudamericana|Copa Sudamericana]], and the last four were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]] for 2025.

In this tournament, [[Botafogo de Futebol e Regatas|Botafogo]] won their third Brazilian championship and first since [[1995 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|1995]], clinching the title after defeating [[São Paulo FC|São Paulo]] 2–1 on the final day of the season.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.uol.com.br/esporte/futebol/ultimas-noticias/2024/12/08/botafogo-campeao-brasileirao-2024.htm |title=É tri! Botafogo vence o São Paulo e conquista o Brasileirão após 29 anos |trans-title=It's three-time! Botafogo beat São Paulo and claim the ''Brasileirão'' after 29 years |publisher=[[Universo Online|UOL]] |language=pt-br |date=8 December 2024 |access-date=8 December 2024}}</ref> [[SE Palmeiras|Palmeiras]] were the defending champions.

==Teams==
Twenty teams competed in the league: the top 16 teams from the previous season and four teams promoted from the [[2023 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].

[[Esporte Clube Vitória|Vitória]] became the first club to be promoted on 12 November 2023 after a 2–1 win against [[Novorizontino]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/sp/tem-esporte/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/jogo/12-11-2023/novorizontino-vitoria.ghtml |title=Vitória marca nos acréscimos, ganha e sobe para a Série A do Brasileiro |publisher=Globo |language=pt |date=12 November 2023}}</ref> [[Criciúma Esporte Clube|Criciúma]] were promoted on 18 November 2023,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/sc/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/jogo/18-11-2023/criciuma-botafogo-sp.ghtml |title=Mais um na Série A! Criciúma vence o Botafogo-SP e carimba o acesso |publisher=Globo |language=pt |date=18 November 2023}}</ref> and [[Esporte Clube Juventude|Juventude]] and [[Atlético Clube Goianiense|Atlético Goianiense]] were promoted on 25 November 2023.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/juventude-garante-acesso-a-serie-a-de-2024 |title=Juventude garante acesso à Série A |publisher=CBF |language=pt |date=25 November 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/atletico-go-esta-de-volta-a-serie-a-do-brasileirao |title=Atlético-GO vai disputar a Série A do Brasileirão de 2024 |publisher=CBF |language=pt |date=25 November 2023}}</ref>
