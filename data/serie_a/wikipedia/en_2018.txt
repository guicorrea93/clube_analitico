The '''2018 {{Lang|pt|Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|italic=no}}''' (officially the '''Brasileirão Assaí 2018''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 62nd season of the {{Lang|pt|[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]]|italic=no}}, the top level of professional football in Brazil, and the 16th edition in a double round-robin since its establishment in 2003. The season began on 14 April 2018 and ended on 2 December 2018. The top six teams as well as the [[2018 Copa do Brasil]] champions qualified to the [[2019 Copa Libertadores|Copa Libertadores]]. The next six best-placed teams not qualified for Copa Libertadores qualified for the [[2019 Copa Sudamericana|Copa Sudamericana]] and the last four were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]] in 2019.

[[Sociedade Esportiva Palmeiras|Palmeiras]] won their 10th title, which is the most titles in the tournament.

Twenty teams competed in the league – the top sixteen teams from the previous season, as well as four teams promoted from the [[2017 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].

[[América Futebol Clube (MG)|América Mineiro]] became the first club to be promoted after a 1–2 win against [[Figueirense FC|Figueirense]] on 11 November 2017.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/sc/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/noticia/america-mg-vence-o-figueirense-e-garante-o-acesso-antecipado-a-serie-a.ghtml |title=América-MG vence o Figueirense e garante o acesso antecipado à Série A |date=11 November 2017 |language=pt |publisher=Globo}}</ref> [[Sport Club Internacional|Internacional]] was promoted on 14 November 2017,<ref>{{cite web |url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/sp/tem-esporte/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/noticia/em-jogo-fraco-inter-empata-com-o-oeste-em-barueri-e-garante-retorno-a-serie-a.ghtml |title=Em jogo fraco, Inter empata com o Oeste em Barueri e garante retorno a Série A |date=14 November 2017 |language=pt |publisher=Globo}}</ref> and [[Paraná Clube|Paraná]] and [[Ceará Sporting Club|Ceará]] were promoted on 18 November 2017.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/al/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/noticia/parana-vence-o-crb-resultados-ajudam-e-time-garante-o-acesso-antecipado-a-serie-a.ghtml |title=Paraná vence o CRB, resultados ajudam, e time garante o acesso antecipado à Série A |date=18 November 2017 |language=pt |publisher=Globo}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/ce/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/noticia/com-a-serie-a-garantida-ceara-empata-com-o-criciuma-no-heriberto-hulse.ghtml |title=Com a Série A garantida, Ceará empata com o Criciúma no Heriberto Hülse |date=18 November 2017 |language=pt |publisher=Globo}}</ref>
