The '''2014 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A''' was the 58th edition of the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]], the [[Brazilian football league system|top-level of professional football in Brazil]]. [[Cruzeiro Esporte Clube|Cruzeiro]], the defending champions, won the title for the second time in a row and the fourth time overall. The championship had a break between the ninth and tenth rounds due to the [[2014 FIFA World Cup]], which was held between June and July in Brazil.<ref>{{cite web|title=Com Estaduais reduzidos e pausa da Copa, CBF divulga calendário de 2014|url=http://esportes.terra.com.br/futebol/com-estaduais-reduzidos-e-pausa-da-copa-cbf-divulga-calendario-de-2014,9d83ff83fbc31410VgnVCM3000009acceb0aRCRD.html|access-date=23 April 2014|language=pt|archive-date=7 April 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230407053122/https://esportes.terra.com.br/futebol/com-estaduais-reduzidos-e-pausa-da-copa-cbf-divulga-calendario-de-2014,9d83ff83fbc31410VgnVCM3000009acceb0aRCRD.html|url-status=dead}}</ref> [[Cruzeiro Esporte Clube|Cruzeiro]] took the lead in round 6 and from then on, stayed in that position, winning the title after a 2–1 victory over [[Goiás Esporte Clube|Goiás]] in the [[Mineirão]] stadium, in round 36.

On the other side of the table, [[Criciúma Esporte Clube|Criciúma]], that had been in the relegation zone since round 17, and held the last place since round 30, was relegated after a 1–1 draw against [[Clube de Regatas Flamengo|Flamengo]], also in round 36. In the following round, [[Botafogo de Futebol e Regatas|Botafogo]] was relegated after losing to [[Santos Futebol Clube|Santos]] by 2–0. In the last round, [[Sociedade Esportiva Palmeiras|Palmeiras]], despite only obtaining a draw in its last match, against [[Clube Atlético Paranaense|Atlético Paranaense]], guaranteed its survival in the first division after defeats of its competitors, [[Esporte Clube Vitória|Vitória]] and [[Esporte Clube Bahia|Bahia]].

==Format==
Twenty teams competed in the league – for the twelfth consecutive season, the tournament was played in a [[Round-robin tournament|double round-robin]] system. The team with the most points at the end of the season is declared champion, while the bottom four teams are relegated and play in the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] in the 2015 season.

===International qualification===
The Série A serves as a qualifier to [[CONMEBOL]]'s [[2015 Copa Libertadores]]. The top-three teams in the standings qualify to the Second Stage of the competition, while the fourth place in the standings qualify to the First Stage.
