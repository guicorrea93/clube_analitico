The '''2025 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A''' (officially the '''Brasileirão Betano 2025''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 69th season of the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]], the top level of professional football in Brazil, and the 23rd edition in a double round-robin since its establishment in 2003. The competition began on 29 March and ended on 7 December 2025.<ref name=GE>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/futebol/noticia/2024/11/12/calendario-do-futebol-brasileiro-em-2025-veja-as-datas.ghtml|title=Calendário do futebol brasileiro em 2025: veja as datas|trans-title=2025 Brazilian football calendar: see dates|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=12 November 2024|accessdate=14 November 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/futebol/noticia/2025/08/23/cbf-muda-calendario-de-2025-antecipa-fim-do-brasileirao-e-adia-final-da-copa-do-brasil.ghtml|title=CBF muda calendário de 2025, antecipa fim do Brasileirão e adia final da Copa do Brasil|trans-title=CBF changes the 2025 calendar, brings forward the end of the Brasileirão and postpones the Copa do Brasil final|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=23 August 2025|accessdate=22 September 2025}}</ref>

[[CR Flamengo|Flamengo]] were the champions, clinching their ninth league title on the penultimate matchday of the season after defeating [[Ceará Sporting Club|Ceará]] 1–0 on 3 December 2025.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/rj/futebol/brasileirao-serie-a/jogo/03-12-2025/flamengo-ceara.ghtml|title=É campeão! Flamengo vence o Ceará e conquista o título do Brasileirão|trans-title=They're champions! Flamengo beat Ceará and claim the ''Brasileirão'' title|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=3 December 2025|access-date=3 December 2025}}</ref> The top six teams as well as the [[2025 Copa do Brasil]] champions qualified for the [[2026 Copa Libertadores|Copa Libertadores]]. Meanwhile, the next six best-placed teams not qualified for Copa Libertadores qualified for the [[2026 Copa Sudamericana|Copa Sudamericana]], and the last four were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]] for 2026.

[[Botafogo de Futebol e Regatas|Botafogo]] were the defending champions, having won their third title in the previous season.

==Teams==
Twenty teams competed in the league: the top 16 teams from the previous season and four teams promoted from the [[2024 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].

[[Santos FC|Santos]] became the first club to be promoted on 11 November 2024 after a 2–0 win over [[Coritiba Foot Ball Club|Coritiba]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.uol.com.br/esporte/futebol/ultimas-noticias/2024/11/11/coritiba-x-santos-como-foi-o-jogo-brasileirao-serie-b.htm|title=Santos vence o Coritiba com golaço, põe fim ao pesadelo e volta à Série A|trans-title=Santos defeat Coritiba with a great goal, put the nightmare to an end and return to the Série A|publisher=[[Universo Online|UOL Esporte]]|language=pt-BR|date=11 November 2024|accessdate=14 November 2024}}</ref> [[Mirassol Futebol Clube|Mirassol]], [[Sport Club do Recife|Sport]] and [[Ceará Sporting Club|Ceará]] all achieved promotion in the last round, on 24 November.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.terra.com.br/esportes/futebol/brasileiro-serie-b/sobem-mirassol-sport-e-ceara-veja-a-ultima-rodada-da-serie-b,dbdb58c25aec3869ae8178a9959af6a1yqfba2sv.html|title=Sobem Mirassol, Sport e Ceará.
