The '''2007 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A''' was the 51st edition of the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]]. It began on May 12, 2007, and ended on December 2, 2007.

==Format==
{{commons|2007 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A kits}}
The format was identical to the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A 2006|2006]] edition.  20 teams competed, each team playing the other 19 twice in a double [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] format (one home game, one away). At the season finale, [[São Paulo FC|São Paulo]] were the champions.

==Calendar==
Several teams had their attentions divided between other tournaments over the same duration:
* [[Copa do Brasil 2007]] - Fluminense defeated Figueirense in the finals. 2-1 Aggregate Score.
* [[Copa Libertadores 2007]] - Grêmio defeated by [[Boca Juniors]]-ARG in the finals. 5-0 Aggregate Score.
* [[Copa Sudamericana 2007]] - São Paulo, Vasco da Gama; Botafogo; Goiás, Figueirense, Atlético Paranaense, Cruzeiro and Corinthians were eliminated.

==Doping case==
On 8 July, [[Botafogo de Futebol e Regatas|Botafogo]]'s player [[Ricardo Lucas|Dodô]] was caught on a [[Doping (sport)|doping]] exam. On 24 July, it was confirmed that he was banned for 120 days. In a new case, on 2 August, the player was acquitted.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nsctotal.com.br/noticias/dodo-e-absolvido-da-acusacao-de-doping|title=Dodô é absolvido da acusação de doping |author=<!--Not stated--> |date=2007-08-02 |website=nsctotal.com.br |access-date=2019-10-17}}</ref>
