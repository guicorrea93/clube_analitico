The '''2009 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A''' was the 53rd edition of the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]], the [[Brazilian football league system|top-level of professional football in Brazil]]. It was contested by 20 clubs starting on 9 May and ending on 6 December. The 2009 edition was won by [[Clube de Regatas do Flamengo|Flamengo]].<ref>GlobeEsporte.com Tabela de classificação. https://globoesporte.globo.com/Esportes/Futebol/Classificacao/0,,ESP0-9827,00.html Retrieved 7 December 2009.</ref>

The first goal of the tournament was scored by [[Igor Nascimento Soares|Igor]] on the 13th minute of the match between his team, [[Sport Club do Recife|Sport]] and [[Grêmio Recreativo Barueri|Grêmio Barueri]], which ended in a 1–1 draw. Holders [[São Paulo Futebol Clube|São Paulo]] had a bad start losing to [[Fluminense Football Club|Fluminense]] 1–0.

[[Sport Club Internacional|Internacional]] led the tournament from round 2 to 6, when [[Clube Atlético Mineiro|Atlético Mineiro]] took the lead. In the 9th round, [[Sport Club Internacional|Internacional]] regained the lead but one round later [[Clube Atlético Mineiro|Atlético Mineiro]] managed to come back to the top of the table after defeating their city rivals [[Cruzeiro Esporte Clube|Cruzeiro]] 3–0. The club from [[Belo Horizonte]] held the lead until round 15, when [[Sociedade Esportiva Palmeiras|Palmeiras]] reached the top. In the middle of the championship, [[Clube de Regatas Flamengo|Flamengo]] was only on the 10th position.

[[Sociedade Esportiva Palmeiras|Palmeiras]] managed to stay in the top until round 34, when they lost 1–0 against [[Fluminense Football Club|Fluminense]], which was struggling to avoid relegation. Four days earlier, [[São Paulo Futebol Clube|São Paulo]] had tied 1–1 with [[Grêmio FBPA|Grêmio]] after having three players sent off.

Round 37 saw several changes in the standings. [[São Paulo Futebol Clube|São Paulo]] could have won their seventh title, and fourth in a row, if they had defeated [[Goiás Esporte Clube|Goiás]], exactly as it had happened the season prior. However, this time [[Goiás Esporte Clube|Goiás]] won 4–2, sending [[São Paulo Futebol Clube|São Paulo]] from the top of the table to fourth place. [[Clube de Regatas Flamengo|Flamengo]] defeated [[Sport Club Corinthians Paulista|Corinthians]] and took the lead for the first time in the tournament. [[Sport Club Internacional|Internacional]] advanced to second place and in the last round, needed both a win against [[Esporte Clube Santo André|Santo André]] and for their city rivals [[Grêmio FBPA|Grêmio]] to tie or defeat [[Clube de Regatas Flamengo|Flamengo]] in the last match. Despite rumors that [[Grêmio FBPA|Grêmio]] would not play as hard as they could, [[Clube de Regatas Flamengo|Flamengo]] had to come back from a 0–1 score to win the tournament. [[Sport Club Internacional|Internacional]]'s 4–1 win was not enough.

[[Sociedade Esportiva Palmeiras|Palmeiras]] also came to the last round of the championship with chances to end in the top position. For that, they had to beat [[Botafogo de Futebol e Regatas|Botafogo]], which would be relegated if did not win the last match. The result was tragic for [[Sociedade Esportiva Palmeiras|Palmeiras]]: 0–2 defeat that combined with [[Cruzeiro Esporte Clube|Cruzeiro]] 2–1 over [[Santos Futebol Clube|Santos]] left the team outside the top four and, therefore, out of [[2010 Copa Libertadores]]. [[Botafogo de Futebol e Regatas|Botafogo]], together with city rivals [[Fluminense Football Club|Fluminense]], managed to avoid relegation. After spending 37 rounds in relegation zone, [[Fluminense Football Club|Fluminense]], which managed to leave the bottom four positions one match earlier, held a 1–1 draw away against [[Coritiba Foot Ball Club|Coritiba]] and sent the team from [[Curitiba]] to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]] exactly when the club was celebrating 100 years of foundation.

The 2009 edition of the Brasileirão marked the professional debut of players such as [[Neymar]].

==Format==
For the seventh consecutive season, the tournament will be played in a double round-robin system. The team with most points will be declared the champion. The bottom-four teams will be relegated for the following season.

===International qualification===
The Série A will serve as a qualifier to [[CONMEBOL]]'s 2010 international tournaments. The top-three teams in the standings will qualify to the Second Stage of the [[2010 Copa Libertadores]], while the fourth place team will qualify to the First Stage. The next eight-best teams will qualify to the [[2010 Copa Sudamericana]]. Should the winner of the [[2009 Copa do Brasil]] finish better than 13th, the next best team(s) will earn the berth(s) it would have qualified for in the league standings.

==Team information==
Like in 2008, twenty teams will compete in this year's Série A. Defending champion [[São Paulo Futebol Clube|São Paulo]] will have a chance to extend two records in Brazilian football should they win this year: first club to win four titles in a row, and first club to win seven titles overall. Of the four teams promoted from the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B 2008|2008 Série B]], one is new to the Série A. [[Grêmio Recreativo Barueri|Grêmio Barueri]] will be competing in the Série A for the first time since turning professional eight years ago. Four-time champion [[Sport Club Corinthians Paulista|Corinthians]] returns after spending a single season in the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B 2008|Série B]]. The other teams promoted are [[Esporte Clube Santo André|Santo André]] (first return since 1984) and [[Avaí Futebol Clube|Avaí]] (first return since 1979). As is becoming common in Brazilian football, one of the country's most important clubs has been relegated after the previous season. For 2009, [[Clube de Regatas Vasco da Gama|Vasco da Gama]], champion in [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A 1974|1974]], [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A 1989|1989]], [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A 1997|1997]] and [[Copa João Havelange|2000]], will play the 2009 season in the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B 2009|Série B]].
