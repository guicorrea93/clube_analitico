The '''2020 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A''' (officially the '''Brasileirão Assaí 2020''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 64th season of the {{Lang|pt|[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]]|italic=no}}, the top level of professional football in Brazil, and the 18th edition in a double round-robin since its establishment in 2003. The competition was originally scheduled to begin on 3 May and end on 6 December,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro/cbf-publica-calendario-de-2020-com-datas-fifas-livres-e-16-datas-para |title=CBF publica calendário de 2020 com Datas FIFA livres |date=3 October 2019 |publisher=CBF |language=pt}}</ref> however due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic in Brazil|COVID-19 pandemic]] the tournament was rescheduled, starting on 8 August 2020 and ending on 25 February 2021.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/index/cbf-publica-calendario-revisado-da-temporada-2020 |title=CBF publica calendário revisado da temporada 2020 |date=9 July 2020 |publisher=CBF |language=pt}}</ref>

The top six teams as well as the [[2020 Copa do Brasil]] champions qualified for the [[2021 Copa Libertadores|Copa Libertadores]]. The next six best-placed teams not qualified for Copa Libertadores qualified for the [[2021 Copa Sudamericana|Copa Sudamericana]] and the last four were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]] for 2021. The match ball for the 2020 season was the Nike Merlin CBF 2020, which was presented by the [[Brazilian Football Confederation]] on 31 January 2020. [[Clube de Regatas do Flamengo|Flamengo]] successfully defended the title, winning their seventh championship on the last day of the season despite losing 2–1 to [[São Paulo FC|São Paulo]], after [[Sport Club Internacional|Internacional]] drew with [[Sport Club Corinthians Paulista|Corinthians]] at home by a 0–0 score.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globoesporte.globo.com/sp/futebol/brasileirao-serie-a/jogo/25-02-2021/sao-paulo-flamengo.ghtml |title=É campeão! Flamengo perde para o São Paulo, conta com tropeço do Internacional e celebra o título brasileiro |date=25 February 2021 |publisher=Globoesporte.com |language=pt}}</ref>

Twenty teams competed in the league – the top sixteen teams from the previous season, as well as four teams promoted from the [[2019 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].

[[Red Bull Bragantino]] became the first club to be promoted, assured of a return to the top flight after 22 years of absence following a 3–1 win against Guarani on 5 November 2019.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/em-noite-de-serie-b-bragantino-carimba-acesso-a-elite-do-brasileirao |title=Em noite de Série B, Bragantino carimba acesso à elite do Brasileirão |date=5 November 2019 |publisher=CBF |language=pt}}</ref> 

[[Sport Club do Recife|Sport]] was promoted on 20 November,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/sport-vence-a-ponte-preta-e-confirma-acesso-a-serie-a-do-brasileirao |title=Sport vence a Ponte Preta e confirma acesso à Série A do Brasileirão |date=20 November 2019 |publisher=CBF |language=pt}}</ref>  making an immediate return to the first division after a season away.

The final two teams to be promoted were [[Coritiba Foot Ball Club|Coritiba]] and [[Atlético Clube Goianiense|Atlético Goianiense]], on 30 November, both returning after a two-year absence.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/coritiba-e-atletico-go-garantem-ultimas-vagas-de-acesso-na-serie-b |title=Coritiba e Atlético-GO garantem últimas vagas de acesso na Série B |date=30 November 2019 |publisher=CBF |language=pt}}</ref>
