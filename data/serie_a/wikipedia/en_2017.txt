The '''2017 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]]''' was the 61st season of the Campeonato Brasileiro Série A, the top level of professional football in Brazil, and the 15th edition in a double round-robin since its establishment in 2003. The season began on 13 May 2017 and ended on 3 December 2017. The top six teams qualified to [[2018 Copa Libertadores|Copa Libertadores]] and the last four were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]] of 2018.

[[Sport Club Corinthians Paulista|Corinthians]] won their seventh title and was the first team in the history of the tournament, since the double round-robin system was established in 2003, to finish the first round undefeated.<ref>{{cite web|title=Jô brilha, Corinthians vira para cima do Fluminense em 3 minutos e é heptacampeão brasileiro|url=http://espn.uol.com.br/noticia/743572_jo-brilha-corinthians-vira-para-cima-do-fluminense-em-3-minutos-e-e-heptacampeao-brasileiro|publisher=ESPN|date=15 November 2017|access-date=16 November 2017}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Mais um recorde! Corinthians se torna primeiro time a virar de turno invicto|url=http://www.goal.com/br/not%C3%ADcias/mais-um-recorde-corinthians-se-torna-primeiro-time-a-virar/10sowv35s1csb1ks18u11vqii2|publisher=Goal|date=5 August 2017|access-date=16 November 2017}}</ref>

==Format==
For the fifteenth consecutive season, the tournament was played in a [[Round-robin tournament|double round-robin]] system. The team with most points at the end of the season was declared champion. The bottom four teams were relegated and will play the [[2018 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|2018 Série B]].

===International qualification===
The Série A served as a qualifier to [[CONMEBOL]]'s [[2018 Copa Libertadores]]. The top four teams in the standings qualified to the group stage of the competition, while the fifth and the sixth placed in the standings qualified to the second stage.

And this change also impacted on [[Copa Sudamericana]] qualification, whose vacancies were again distributed through league placement instead of the [[Copa do Brasil]].
