The '''2023 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A''' (officially the '''Brasileirão Assaí 2023''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 67th season of the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]], the top level of professional football in Brazil, and the 21nd edition in a double round-robin since its establishment in 2003. The competition began on 15 April and ended on 6 December 2023.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/index/brasileirao-sera-paralisado-em-datas-fifa-e-copa-do-brasil-tera-finais |title=Calendário 2023: Brasileirão será paralisado em Datas FIFA pela primeira vez |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese |date=21 September 2022}}</ref>

The top six teams as well as the [[2023 Copa do Brasil]] champions qualified for the [[2024 Copa Libertadores|Copa Libertadores]]. The next six best-placed teams not qualified for Copa Libertadores qualified for the [[2024 Copa Sudamericana|Copa Sudamericana]] and the last four were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]] for 2024.

Defending champions [[SE Palmeiras|Palmeiras]] won their twelfth league championship in this season, clinching the title on the final day of the season with a 1–1 draw against [[Cruzeiro Esporte Clube|Cruzeiro]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/mg/futebol/brasileirao-serie-a/jogo/06-12-2023/cruzeiro-palmeiras.ghtml|title=Dodecacampeão! Palmeiras empata com Cruzeiro e confirma título brasileiro|trans-title=Twelve-time champion! Palmeiras draw with Cruzeiro and confirm Brazilian title|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=6 December 2023|access-date=7 December 2023}}</ref>

==Teams==
Twenty teams competed in the league: the top 16 teams from the previous season and four teams promoted from the [[2022 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].

[[Cruzeiro Esporte Clube|Cruzeiro]] became the first club to be promoted on 21 September 2022 after a 3–0 win against [[CR Vasco da Gama|Vasco da Gama]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/mg/futebol/brasileirao-serie-b/jogo/21-09-2022/cruzeiro-vasco.ghtml |title=O Cabuloso voltou! Cruzeiro domina, vence o Vasco e garante acesso para a série A |date=21 September 2022 |publisher=Globo |language=Portuguese}}</ref> [[Grêmio Foot-Ball Porto Alegrense|Grêmio]] were promoted on 23 October 2022,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/gremio-derrota-o-nautico-e-confirma-retorno-a-serie-a-do-brasileirao |title=Grêmio derrota o Náutico e confirma retorno à Série A do Brasileirão |date=23 October 2022 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref> and [[Esporte Clube Bahia|Bahia]] and [[CR Vasco da Gama|Vasco da Gama]] were promoted on 6 November 2022.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/bahia-e-vasco-da-gama-garantem-vaga-no-brasileirao-assai-2023 |title=Bahia e Vasco da Gama garantem vaga no Brasileirão Assaí 2023 |date=6 November 2022 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>
