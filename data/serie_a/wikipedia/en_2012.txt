The '''2012 [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]]''' (known as the '''Brasileirão [[Petrobras]] 2012''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 56th edition of the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]], the [[Brazilian football league system|top-level of professional football in Brazil]]. [[Sport Club Corinthians Paulista|Corinthians]] come in as the defending champions having won the title in the [[2011 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|2011 season]].
On 11 November 2012, Fluminense won the title for the fourth time.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.goal.com/en-gb/news/2557/news/2012/11/12/3521177/fluminense-crowned-brasileiro-serie-a-champions
|title=Fluminense crowned champions|date=12 November 2012|publisher=Goal|access-date=18 December 2012 }}</ref>

==Format==
For the tenth consecutive season, the tournament will be played in a [[Round-robin tournament|double round-robin]] system. The team with most points at the end of the season will be declared champion. The bottom four teams will be relegated and will play in the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] in the 2013 season.

===International qualification===
The Série A will serve as a qualifier to [[CONMEBOL]]'s [[2013 Copa Libertadores]]. The top-two teams in the standings will qualify to the Second Stage of the competition, while the third and fourth places in the standings will qualify to the First Stage.
