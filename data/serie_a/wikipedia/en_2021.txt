The '''2021 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A''' (officially the '''Brasileirão Assaí 2021''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 65th season of the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]], the top level of professional football in Brazil, and the 19th edition in a double round-robin since its establishment in 2003. The competition began on 29 May and ended on 9 December 2021. [[Clube de Regatas do Flamengo|Flamengo]] were the defending champions.

The top six teams as well as the [[2021 Copa do Brasil]] champions qualified for the [[2022 Copa Libertadores|Copa Libertadores]]. The next six best-placed teams not qualified for Copa Libertadores qualified for the [[2022 Copa Sudamericana|Copa Sudamericana]] and the last four were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]] for 2022.

[[Clube Atlético Mineiro|Atlético Mineiro]] were the champions, winning their third Série A title on 2 December 2021 with two matches to spare after they defeated [[Esporte Clube Bahia|Bahia]] by a 3–2 score.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/ba/futebol/brasileirao-serie-a/jogo/02-12-2021/bahia-atletico-mg.ghtml |title=Soltou o grito: Atlético-MG aplica virada no Bahia na Fonte Nova e é campeão brasileiro |date=2 December 2021 |publisher=ge |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

Twenty teams compete in the league – the top sixteen teams from the previous season, as well as four teams promoted from the [[2020 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].

[[América Futebol Clube (MG)|América Mineiro]] and [[Associação Chapecoense de Futebol|Chapecoense]] became the first two clubs to be promoted on 12 January 2021 after a 0–0 draw against Náutico and a 2–1 win against Figueirense, respectively.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/america-mg-e-chapecoense-garantem-vaga-no-brasileirao-assai-em-2021 |title=América-MG e Chapecoense conquistam acesso ao Brasileirão Assaí em 2021 |date=13 January 2021 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref> Chapecoense had an immediate return to the first division after a season away, while América Mineiro returned after a two-year absence. [[Cuiabá Esporte Clube|Cuiabá]] was promoted for the first time ever on 22 January 2021.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/competicoes/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/2020/370 |title=Série B: Cuiabá perde para o Sampaio Corrêa, mas comemora acesso à elite |date=22 January 2021 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref> The final team to achieve promotion were [[Esporte Clube Juventude|Juventude]] on 29 January 2021, sealing a return to the top flight after a thirteen-year absence.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/competicoes/campeonato-brasileiro-serie-b/2020/374 |title=Juventude vence o Guarani na última rodada da Série B e confirma acesso |date=29 January 2021 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese}}</ref>
