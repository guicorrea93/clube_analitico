The '''2016 {{Lang|pt|Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|italic=no}}''' was the 60th season of the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|Série A]], the [[Brazilian football league system|top level of professional football in Brazil]], and the 14th edition in a double round-robin since its establishment in 2003. The season began on 15 May 2016 and ended on 11 December 2016. Fixtures for the 2016 season were announced on 25 April 2016.<ref>Veja - [http://veja.abril.com.br/esporte/brasileirao-2016-tera-mais-jogos-matinais-e-um-novo-horario-aos-sabados/ Brasileirão 2016 terá mais jogos matinais e um novo horário aos sábados]</ref> [[Sociedade Esportiva Palmeiras|Palmeiras]] won the title, their ninth overall.

==Format and results==
For the fourteenth consecutive season, the tournament was played in a [[Round-robin tournament|double round-robin]] system. The team with most points at the end of the season was declared champion. The bottom four teams were relegated and will play in the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] in the 2017 season.

Atlético Mineiro and Chapecoense did not play their last match due to the [[LaMia Flight 2933|accident]] involving 71 deaths (including 19 Chapecoense players) on 28 November 2016. Due to a lack of squad depth, both teams decided to forfeit the game resulting in Chapecoense finishing 11th and Atlético Mineiro finishing 4th.<ref name=ESPN-1>{{cite web|url=http://www.espnfc.com/futebol-brasileiro/story/3009180/atletico-mineiro-say-they-wont-play-final-round-match-vs-chapecoense|title=Atletico Mineiro say they won't play final-round match vs. Chapecoense|author=ASSOCIATED PRESS|date=1 December 2016|publisher=ESPN|access-date=10 December 2016}}</ref> Match officials declared a double [[Walkover|W.O.]], as CBF refused to cancel the match.<ref name=CBF-UOL-1>{{cite web|url=https://esporte.uol.com.br/futebol/ultimas-noticias/2016/12/05/presidente-da-chape-diz-que-cbf-ja-cancelou-jogo-contra-o-atletico-mg.htm|title=Sem jogo: CBF confirma WO duplo em Chapecoense x Atlético-MG}}</ref>

===International qualification===
The Série A served as a qualifier to [[CONMEBOL]]'s [[2017 Copa Libertadores]]. The top-three teams in the standings qualified to the Second Stage of the competition, while from the fourth to sixth place in the standings qualified to the First Stage.

And this change also impacts on the [[Copa Sudamericana]], whose vacancies are again to be distributed in the stockmarket enot more by [[Copa do Brasil]].

Twenty teams competed in the league – the top sixteen teams from the previous season, as well as four teams promoted from the [[2015 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].

Botafogo became the first club to be promoted after a 1–0 win against [[Luverdense]] on 10 November 2015 meant they were guaranteed an automatic place.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://esportes.terra.com.br/futebol/brasileiro-serie-b/apos-celebrar-acesso-botafogo-garante-foco-no-titulo-da-serie-b,2179c26b9b21a98ffbb72308e8881961lro26jcn.html|title=Após celebrar acesso, Botafogo garante foco no título da Série B|language=pt|author=Gazeta Press|publisher=Terra|date=November 11, 2015}}</ref><ref>Goal.com - [http://www.goal.com/br/match/luverdense-vs-botafogo/2016840/report Luverdense 0 x 1 Botafogo: Fogão vence fora e garante retorno antecipado à Série A]</ref> They returned to the League after only a season's absence.<ref>UOL - [http://esporte.uol.com.br/futebol/campeonatos/brasileiro/serie-b/ultimas-noticias/2015/11/10/luverdense-x-botafogo.htm Botafogo bate Luverdense, garante retorno à Série A e encaminha título]</ref> Vitória became the second club to be promoted, after a 3-0 win with Luverdense. They returned to the League after only a season's absence. [[Santa Cruz Futebol Clube|Santa Cruz]] became the third club to be promoted, after 3-0 win with [[Mogi Mirim]]. They played the ''Brasileirão'' for the first time since the [[2006 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|2006 season]]. [[América Mineiro]] became the fourth and final club to be promoted, following a 1–1 draw over Ceará meant they finished above them on points difference and secured the automatic spot.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://esporte.uol.com.br/futebol/campeonatos/brasileiro/serie-b/ultimas-noticias/2015/11/21/vitoria-bate-luverdense-e-garante-acesso-santa-e-america-mg-tambem-sobem.htm|title=Vitória bate Luverdense e garante acesso; Santa e América-MG também sobem - Futebol - UOL Esporte}}</ref>

The four promoted clubs replaced [[Avaí]], [[Vasco da Gama]], [[Goiás]] and [[Joinville]]. At the end of the season, for the third consecutive year, [[Rio de Janeiro]] did not have its four representatives in the first division, largely due to the inconsistent performance of Vasco da Gama and Botafogo, who alternated in relegation in recent seasons.

===Stadia and locations===
The three clubs based in Rio de Janeiro, Botafogo, Flamengo and Fluminense weren't able to play their home matches in [[Maracanã Stadium|Maracanã]] or [[Estádio Olímpico João Havelange|Engenhão]] Stadiums due to the preparation of these venues for the [[2016 Summer Olympics|Olympic Games]] that were held in the city in August.<ref>http://globoesporte.globo.com/rj/futebol/brasileirao-serie-a/noticia/2016/05/aluga-se-para-temporada-conheca-os-novos-estadios-do-rio-para-o-brasileiro.html (Portuguese)</ref> The clubs used other stadiums for their home matches. Other stadiums used include [[Arena das Dunas]], [[Itaipava Arena Fonte Nova|Arena Fonte Nova]], [[Itaipava Arena Pernambuco|Arena Pernambuco]], [[Estádio Kléber Andrade|Kléber Andrade]], [[Estádio Nacional Mané Garrincha|Mané Garrincha]], [[Estádio Municipal Radialista Mário Helênio|Mário Helênio]], [[Estádio do Pacaembu|Pacaembu]], and [[Estádio Raulino de Oliveira|Raulino de Oliveira]].
