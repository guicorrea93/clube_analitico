The '''2022 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A''' (officially the '''Brasileirão Assaí 2022''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 66th season of the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]], the top level of professional football in Brazil, and the 20th edition in a double round-robin since its establishment in 2003. The competition began on 9 April and ended on 13 November 2022.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbf.com.br/futebol-brasileiro/noticias/index/cbf-apresenta-calendario-do-futebol-brasileiro-em-2022 |title=CBF apresenta calendário do futebol brasileiro em 2022 |publisher=CBF |language=Portuguese |date=4 November 2021}}</ref>

The top six teams as well as the [[2022 Copa do Brasil]] champions qualified for the [[2023 Copa Libertadores|Copa Libertadores]]. The next six best-placed teams not qualified for Copa Libertadores qualified for the [[2023 Copa Sudamericana|Copa Sudamericana]] and the last four were relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]] for 2023.

[[Sociedade Esportiva Palmeiras|Palmeiras]] won the competition, securing their eleventh league title with three rounds to go after [[Sport Club Internacional|Internacional]]'s 1–0 defeat to [[América Futebol Clube (MG)|América Mineiro]] on 2 November.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/futebol/times/palmeiras/noticia/2022/11/02/e-campeao-palmeiras-conquista-o-titulo-do-brasileirao-apos-derrota-do-inter-para-o-america-mg.ghtml|title=É campeão! Palmeiras conquista o título do Brasileirão após derrota do Inter para o América-MG|trans-title=It's champion! Palmeiras win the ''Brasileirão'' title after Inter's defeat to América-MG|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=2 November 2022|access-date=2 November 2022}}</ref> [[Clube Atlético Mineiro|Atlético Mineiro]] were the defending champions.

==Teams==
Twenty teams competed in the league – the top sixteen teams from the previous season, as well as four teams promoted from the [[2021 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].

[[Botafogo de Futebol e Regatas|Botafogo]] and [[Coritiba Foot Ball Club|Coritiba]] became the first two clubs to be promoted on 15 November 2021, after the former achieved a 2–1 home win against Operário Ferroviário,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.gazetaesportiva.com/campeonatos/brasileiro-serie-b/botafogo-vence-o-operario-pr-de-virada-e-confirma-acesso-a-serie-a/|title=Botafogo vence o web|url=https://ge.globo.com/pr/futebol/times/coritiba/noticia/vice-lider-coritiba-se-beneficia-de-tropeco-do-crb-e-garante-acesso-a-serie-a.ghtml|title=Coritiba se beneficia de tropeço do CRB e garante acesso antecipado à Série A|trans-title=Coritiba profit from slip from CRB and assure anticipated promotion to the Série A|publisher=ge|language=pt-BR|date=15 November 2021|accessdate=18
