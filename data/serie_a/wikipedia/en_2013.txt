The '''2013 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A''' was the 57th edition of the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]], the [[Brazilian football league system|top-level of professional football in Brazil]]. [[Fluminense Football Club|Fluminense]] came in as the defending champions, having won the title in the [[2012 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A|2012 season]]. On 13 November 2013, [[Cruzeiro Esporte Clube|Cruzeiro]] won the title for the club's third time.

For the first time since the league adopted the 20-team, double round-robin format, all four teams promoted from [[2012 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]] survived.

==Format and results==
For the eleventh consecutive season, the tournament was played in a [[Round-robin tournament|double round-robin]] system. In a fashion similar to its second title in 2003, Cruzeiro led the rankings for most of the tournament, declared champion in the fifth to last round. The bottom four teams, Fluminense, Vasco, Ponte Preta and Nautico were relegated to play in the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]] in the 2014 season.

The tournament had a one-month interruption between June 9 and July 6 due to the [[2013 FIFA Confederations Cup]], which took place in Brazil.

Although [[Clube de Regatas do Flamengo|Flamengo]] finished the championship just above the relegation positions, it was punished by the Superior Court of Sport Justice for irregularly calling in a player during their final match against [[Cruzeiro Esporte Clube|Cruzeiro]]. Flamengo played [[André Santos]], who was suspended due to receiving a [[Penalty card#Red card|red card]] at a previous [[2013 Copa do Brasil|Copa do Brasil]] match.<ref name=STJD>{{cite news|last=Rodrigues|first=Renan|title=STJD pune Portuguesa e mantém Fluminense na Série A; ainda cabe recurso|url=http://esporte.uol.com.br/futebol/campeonatos/brasileiro/serie-a/ultimas-noticias/2013/12/16/stjd-decide-por-queda-de-lusa-e-mantem-flu-na-primeira-divisao-cabe-recurso.htm|access-date=16 December 2013|newspaper=[[Universo Online|UOL Esportes]]|date=16 December 2013|author2=Paradella, Rodrigo|location=Rio de Janeiro|language=pt}}</ref> The team lost four points - three for the irregular usage of a player and a fourth one from the tie in the game against Cruzeiro. With the punishment, Flamengo was going to be relegated.<ref name=STJD/> However, the day after Flamengo's game, [[Associação Portuguesa de Desportos|Portuguesa]] also irregularly called in a player against [[Grêmio Foot-Ball Porto Alegrense|Grêmio]]: [[Héverton Durães Coutinho Alves|Héverton]]. The team also lost four points—three for the irregular usage of a player and a fourth one from the tie in the game against Grêmio. Portuguesa, which had been below Flamengo in the table before both teams' punishments, ended up being relegated instead of Flamengo. With both punishments, [[Fluminense FC]] also managed to finish the championship above the relegation positions and was spared from having to compete in the following year's second division.<ref name=STJD/>  Portuguesa's irregular usage of a player raised many suspicions, both towards Flamengo's—which had also used an irregular player the day before—and Fluminense's side. This relegation was the start to a downhill turn for Portuguesa, as the team was relegated to the Brazilian Third Division - Série C the following year.

===International qualification===
The Série A served as a qualifier to [[CONMEBOL]]'s [[2014 Copa Libertadores]]. Cruzeiro and Gremio qualified to the Second Stage of the competition, while Atletico Paranaense qualified to the First Stage. Botafogo qualified to the First Stage as well since Ponte Preta lost the [[2013 Copa Sudamericana Finals]].

==Teams==
Twenty teams competed in the league – the top sixteen teams from the previous season, as well as four teams promoted from the [[2012 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]].
